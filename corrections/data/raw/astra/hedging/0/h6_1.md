**Light grey grout is the best all-round choice with white metro tiles.** It gives a gentle contrast that shows off the brick pattern without looking too busy, and it’s more forgiving of everyday marks than white.

The main options are:
- **White:** a clean, seamless look—good if you want the tiles to blend into the background, though staining is more noticeable.
- **Light grey:** a balanced, classic finish that suits most kitchens and bathrooms.
- **Charcoal:** bold contrast that highlights every tile and joint; best if you want the pattern to stand out.
- **Warm pale beige:** softer than grey and pairs nicely with cream cabinets or natural wood.

Try a grout colour sample beside your tiles in the room’s lighting before choosing.
