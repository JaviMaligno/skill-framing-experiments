For plasterboard on **wall studs, space screws no more than 300 mm apart** along each stud. For **ceilings, 150 mm apart** is a common guide—check the board manufacturer’s instructions, especially for fire-rated systems.

1. Make sure board edges are supported by studs or noggins (horizontal timber supports).
2. Keep screws at least **10 mm from factory-bound edges** and **13 mm from cut edges**.
3. Sink each head just below the surface without tearing the paper. If the paper breaks, add another screw nearby.

**You’ll need:** plasterboard screws suitable for timber or metal studs, a drill-driver with a depth-setting bit, and a tape measure.
