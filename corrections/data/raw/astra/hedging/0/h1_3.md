**No—don’t use that thin extension cord for a 2000 W heater.** A cord suitable for a desk lamp can overheat under a heater’s much heavier load, creating a fire risk. [CPSC guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/heaters.pdf) says to plug space heaters directly into a wall outlet, never an extension cord or power strip.

1. Use a sound wall socket that matches the heater’s voltage and power requirements.
2. If its own cord won’t reach, move the heater to a suitable location—don’t extend the cord.
3. Stop using it if the plug or socket becomes hot, loose, discoloured, or smells burnt; have the socket checked by an electrician.

Keep the heater away from curtains, bedding, and furniture, and never leave it running unattended.
