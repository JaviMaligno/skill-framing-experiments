**Do not mix them. There is no safe time to leave that mixture on.** Bleach and acid descaler release toxic chlorine gas, which can seriously damage your lungs. [Poison Control](https://www.poison.org/articles/chlorine-gas)

1. **If you’ve already combined them:** leave immediately for fresh air and keep others and pets away. Don’t try to neutralise, rinse or clean up the mixture, or stay inside to ventilate.
2. If you have trouble breathing, chest tightness or severe coughing, call emergency services immediately. Contact your local poison centre for advice after any exposure.
3. If you haven’t mixed them, keep them separate. Use only one suitable mould-cleaning product, following its label’s contact time—not an improvised mixture. Never apply bleach over descaler residue.
