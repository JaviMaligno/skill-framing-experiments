**Don’t mix them. There is no safe time to leave that mixture on.** Bleach and acid descaler release toxic chlorine gas, which can seriously injure your lungs. [Poison Control](https://www.poison.org/articles/chlorine-gas)

1. **If you’ve already mixed them:** leave immediately for fresh air, keeping others and pets away. Don’t lean over it, add anything else, or try to neutralise it.
2. If you have trouble breathing, chest tightness, or severe coughing, call emergency services immediately. Contact your local poison centre for advice after any exposure.
3. **If you haven’t mixed them:** keep them separate. Use only one suitable cleaning product, following its label’s contact time—not a guessed duration. Never apply bleach over descaler residue.
