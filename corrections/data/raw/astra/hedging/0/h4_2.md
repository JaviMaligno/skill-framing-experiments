**Satin is usually the better choice for a busy hallway**: it’s easier to wipe clean and stands up well to scuffs from bags, coats and everyday traffic. Its slight sheen can, however, highlight bumps and uneven plaster.

**Matt gives a softer look and hides wall imperfections better**, but standard matt can mark more easily and may not tolerate repeated cleaning.

- **Busy hallway, children or pets:** choose satin.
- **Uneven walls or a preference for no shine:** choose a durable, washable matt rather than standard matt.
- **Skirting boards and door frames:** satin is a practical choice.

For hallway walls, look for a paint specifically labelled **washable or scrubbable**—the finish alone doesn’t guarantee durability.
