For a typical UK plasterboard **wall, space screws no more than 300 mm apart along each stud**, including studs behind the middle of the sheet. Reduce spacing to **200 mm at external corners**.

1. Keep screws at least **10 mm from paper-bound edges** and **13 mm from cut edges**.
2. Drive heads flush without tearing the paper or crushing the board.
3. Use plasterboard screws suited to timber or metal studs—not general-purpose wood screws.

These are typical [manufacturer fixing details](https://www.british-gypsum.com/documents/technical-specification/british-gypsum-ts-a206027s-b-en.pdf). Follow your board system’s instructions if they differ, especially for fire-rated walls. Ceiling spacing is different.

**You’ll need:** plasterboard screws, a drill/driver with a depth-stop bit, and a tape measure.
