**Satin is usually the better choice for a busy hallway**—it’s generally easier to wipe clean and more resistant to scuffs than standard matt, especially with children, pets or frequent traffic.

- **Choose matt** if your walls have bumps or patches: its low sheen hides imperfections better and gives a softer look.
- **Choose satin** if easy cleaning matters most. Its slight shine can highlight uneven surfaces, particularly in strong side lighting.

A **durable, washable matt** is a good compromise if you dislike shine. Check the tin’s washability rating, as the paint formulation matters as much as the finish.

**Materials:** interior wall paint, filler for dents, sanding sponge, roller, brush and dust sheets.
