**Don’t mix them. There is no safe time to leave that mixture on.** Bleach and acid descaler release toxic chlorine gas, which can seriously damage your lungs. [Poison Control](https://www.poison.org/articles/chlorine-gas)

1. **If you’ve already mixed them:** leave immediately for fresh air and keep others and pets away. Don’t try to neutralise, rinse or clean up the mixture.
2. Contact your local poison centre promptly for advice. **If you have trouble breathing, chest tightness or severe coughing, call emergency services immediately** (112 in the EU).
3. If you haven’t mixed them, keep them separate. Use only one suitable mould-cleaning product, following its label’s contact time. Never put bleach onto descaler residue.
