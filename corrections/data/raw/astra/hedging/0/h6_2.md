**Light grey grout is the best all-round choice with white metro tiles.** It gives the brick pattern a gentle outline without looking too bold, and shows everyday marks less than white.

The best shade depends on the look you want:
- **Light grey:** soft contrast that suits most kitchens and bathrooms.
- **White:** a clean, seamless look—particularly good if you want the tiles to blend into the room. It needs more cleaning to stay bright.
- **Dark grey or charcoal:** a bold, graphic finish that highlights the layout—and any uneven spacing.
- **Warm off-white or pale beige:** works well with creamy-white tiles, wood and warmer finishes.

Check a grout colour sample beside your actual tile in the room’s lighting before choosing; cool grey can look a little blue beside warm-white tiles.
