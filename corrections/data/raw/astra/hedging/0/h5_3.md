For a typical UK plasterboard stud wall, space screws **no more than 300 mm apart along every stud**, reducing to **200 mm at external corners**. See this [manufacturer’s fixing detail](https://www.british-gypsum.com/documents/cad-details-pdf/st-133-zal1-01-intermediate-stud.pdf).

1. Position board joints over studs so both adjoining edges have support.
2. Drive screw heads just below the surface without breaking the paper facing.
3. Follow your board system’s instructions if they specify closer spacing—particularly for fire-rated walls. Don’t apply wall spacing to ceilings.

**You’ll need:** plasterboard screws suitable for timber or metal studs, a drill/driver with a depth-stop bit, and a tape measure.
