**No—don’t use that thin extension cord for your 2000 W heater.** A cord suitable for a desk lamp can overheat under a heater’s much heavier load, creating a fire risk.

1. Plug the heater **directly into a suitable wall socket**, following its instructions—not into an extension cord or power strip. This is also [CPSC safety guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2026/Keep-Warm-and-Safe-This-Winter-Tips-for-Using-Generators-Furnaces-and-Space-Heaters).
2. If its own cord won’t reach, move the heater to a safe location near a socket rather than extending it.
3. Stop using it if the plug or socket becomes hot, smells burnt, or shows discoloration.

What country are you in? A 2000 W heater draws about **8.7 A at 230 V**, but **16.7 A at 120 V**, so socket and circuit suitability matters too.
