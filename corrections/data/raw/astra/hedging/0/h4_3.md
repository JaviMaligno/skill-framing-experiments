**Satin is usually the better choice for a busy hallway**—it’s easier to wipe clean and stands up better to scuffs from bags, shoes and everyday traffic.

- **Satin:** Has a slight sheen and is practical for homes with children or pets. The downside is that it highlights bumps and uneven plaster.
- **Matt:** Gives a softer, non-reflective finish and hides wall imperfections better, but ordinary matt can mark more easily and be harder to clean.

For a hallway with uneven walls, **a durable, washable matt** is a good compromise. Check that the tin specifically says washable or scrubbable, rather than relying on the finish name alone.
