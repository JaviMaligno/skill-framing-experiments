**No—don’t plug a 2000 W space heater into a thin lamp extension cord.** It can overload the cord, causing overheating or a fire.

1. Plug the heater directly into a suitable wall outlet, following its manufacturer’s instructions. Don’t use a power strip or multi-plug adapter.
2. If the heater’s cord won’t reach, move the heater closer to the outlet while keeping it at least 1 metre from curtains, bedding and furniture.
3. Stop using it if the plug or outlet becomes hot, smells burnt or shows discoloration. Have the outlet checked by an electrician.

A cord working fine for a desk lamp does **not** mean it can safely handle a heater.
