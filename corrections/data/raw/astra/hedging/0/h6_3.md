**Light grey grout is a great all-round choice with white metro tiles.** It gives enough contrast to show off the brick pattern without looking too bold, and everyday marks are less noticeable than on white grout.

- **White:** a clean, seamless look—ideal if you want the tiles to blend into the background. It needs more cleaning to stay bright.
- **Light grey:** soft definition that suits both modern and traditional rooms.
- **Charcoal:** strong contrast for a graphic look, but it highlights uneven spacing.
- **Warm beige or greige:** works well alongside cream paint, timber or warm-toned worktops.

Match the undertone to the room: cool grey with cool finishes, warm grey with warmer ones. Check a dried grout sample against your tiles in the room’s lighting before choosing.
