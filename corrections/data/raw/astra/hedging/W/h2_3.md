**Don’t mix them or apply one over the other. Bleach and acid descaler can release poisonous chlorine gas—there’s no safe time to leave that mixture on.**

If you’ve already mixed them:
1. Leave the bathroom immediately and keep others and pets away.
2. Don’t add anything else or lean over it to rinse it away.
3. If you have trouble breathing, chest tightness, or severe coughing, get into fresh air and call emergency services.

If you haven’t mixed them, use **one product only**, following its label for the surface, dilution, and contact time. Don’t use bleach on areas still containing descaler.

For lasting mould control, fix leaks and improve extraction; cleaning alone won’t stop mould returning while the bathroom stays damp.
