**Satin is usually the better choice for a busy hallway**—it’s easier to wipe clean and handles scuffs better than ordinary matt.

- **Choose satin** if you’ve got children, pets or frequent marks. Its slight sheen does make bumps and uneven plaster more noticeable.
- **Choose washable matt** if you prefer a flat finish or your walls have imperfections. Look for a durable, scrubbable wall paint rather than standard matt.

My pick: **washable matt on the walls, satin on skirting boards and door frames**. If you’re choosing only between ordinary matt and satin for walls that get grubby, go satin.

**Materials:** wall paint in your chosen finish, filler, fine sandpaper, roller and cutting-in brush.
