For a typical UK plasterboard **wall, space screws no more than 300 mm (12 inches) apart along every stud**, including those behind the middle of the sheet. Reduce spacing to 200 mm at external corners. [Fixing-spacing guidance](https://www.british-gypsum.com/technical-support/self-help-tools/faqs/what-are-screw-fixing-centres-partitions-ceilings-encasements)

1. Position board joints over the centre of a stud so both sheet edges have support.
2. Keep screws at least **10 mm from factory-wrapped edges and 13 mm from cut edges**.
3. Seat heads just below the surface without tearing the paper. [Fixing details](https://www.british-gypsum.com/documents/technical-specification/british-gypsum-ts-a306033-b-en.pdf)

Check for cables and pipes before driving screws.

For ceilings or a specified fire-rated wall, follow that board system’s fixing pattern rather than assuming 300 mm.

**You’ll need:** plasterboard screws suited to timber or metal studs, a drill-driver with a depth-stop bit, and a tape measure.
