**Light grey grout is the best all-round choice for white metro tiles.** It gives the brick pattern a gentle outline without looking too busy, and shows everyday marks less than white.

- **White** gives a clean, seamless look—great if you want the tiles to blend into the background. It takes more cleaning to keep looking fresh.
- **Light grey** suits most kitchens and bathrooms and works with both modern and traditional fittings.
- **Charcoal** creates a bold, graphic look, but makes uneven spacing more noticeable.
- **Warm pale grey or ivory** works nicely alongside cream cupboards, timber and warm-toned worktops.

Check a grout colour sample against your actual tiles under the room’s lighting; a cool grey can look blue beside a warm white tile.
