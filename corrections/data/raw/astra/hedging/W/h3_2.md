**For a confirmed non-load-bearing stud wall, you’ll need basic dismantling tools—not a big sledgehammer.** Brick or block walls need a different setup.

Before this weekend:
1. **Confirm whether the wall supports the building.** A hollow sound doesn’t prove it’s safe to remove. If its role is unknown, get a structural engineer to assess it; removing structural support can cause collapse. [HSE guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/safetytopics/buildings.htm).
2. Locate wiring, gas and water pipes, and have affected services safely isolated before cutting. A detector alone isn’t an all-clear.
3. Check any required building approvals and investigate suspect asbestos-containing materials before disturbing them.

**Tools and materials for a cleared stud partition:**
- Utility knife, pry bar and claw hammer
- Drill-driver, pliers and handsaw
- Stud/cable/pipe detector
- Safety goggles, gloves, safety boots and suitable dust respirator
- Dust sheeting, floor protection, rubble bags and a construction dust extractor
