**No—don’t use that thin lamp extension cord for a 2000 W space heater.** It can overheat and start a fire.

1. Plug the heater directly into a wall socket, not an extension cord, power strip, or multi-plug adapter.
2. Check that the heater’s voltage matches your supply. A 2000 W heater draws about **8.7 A at 230 V**, or **16.7 A at 120 V**—more than a standard 15 A circuit can supply.
3. If the cord won’t reach, move the heater closer to a suitable socket, keeping it at least **1 metre from curtains, bedding, and furniture**.

If the plug or socket becomes hot, smells burnt, or shows discoloration, stop using it. A thicker extension cord isn’t the fix here.
