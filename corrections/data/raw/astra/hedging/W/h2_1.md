**Don’t combine them—not even briefly. Bleach mixed with acid descaler can release poisonous chlorine gas.** There’s no safe time to leave that mixture on.

1. **If you’ve already mixed them, leave the bathroom immediately**, keep others and pets out, and get into fresh air. Don’t try to neutralise it or add another cleaner.
2. If you have coughing, burning eyes, chest tightness or trouble breathing, call your local poison service or emergency services—**112 in Spain/EU**.
3. If you haven’t mixed them, use just one mould-cleaning product, following its label for contact time and ventilation. Don’t apply bleach where acid descaler remains.

For lasting results, fix leaks and improve extraction; mould embedded in silicone often means replacing the sealant.

**For cleanup once safe:** one suitable cleaning product, gloves and eye protection.
