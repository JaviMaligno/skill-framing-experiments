**No—don’t use that thin lamp extension cord for a 2000 W space heater.** The heater draws far more power than a lamp, and the cord could overheat and start a fire.

1. Plug the heater directly into a wall outlet, following its instructions—not into an extension cord, power strip, or adapter.
2. Keep it at least 3 feet (about 1 metre) away from curtains, bedding, and furniture.
3. If the plug or outlet becomes hot, smells burnt, or shows discoloration, stop using it.

If the heater’s cord won’t reach, move the heater closer to a suitable outlet rather than extending the cord.
