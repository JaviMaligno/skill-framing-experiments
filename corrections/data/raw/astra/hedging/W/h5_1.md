For a typical **plasterboard wall, space screws about 300 mm apart along each stud**, including studs supporting board edges. Check the board manufacturer’s fixing pattern—ceilings and fire-rated walls can need closer spacing.

1. Make sure board joints land on a stud or another suitable support.
2. Keep screws at least **10 mm from factory-bound edges** and **13 mm from cut edges**.
3. Drive each head just below the surface without tearing the paper. If it breaks through, add another screw nearby.

Watch for cables and pipes behind the studs—don’t use unnecessarily long screws.

**You’ll need:** plasterboard screws suited to timber or metal studs, a drill/driver with a depth-setting bit, and a tape measure.
