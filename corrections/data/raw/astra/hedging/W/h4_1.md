**Satin is usually the better choice for a hallway**—it’s easier to wipe clean and stands up well to scuffs, fingerprints and passing bags. The slight sheen does highlight bumps and uneven plaster, though.

**Matt is better for hiding imperfections** and gives a softer, non-shiny finish. If that’s the look you prefer, choose a **washable, durable matt** rather than standard matt, particularly around doorways and light switches.

My pick: satin for a busy hallway with fairly smooth walls; durable matt for uneven walls or if you dislike sheen. Check the tin specifically says washable or scrubbable—finish alone doesn’t guarantee toughness.

**For painting:** wall paint, filler, fine sandpaper, roller, cutting-in brush and dust sheets.
