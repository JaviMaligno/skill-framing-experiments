**Light grey grout is the best all-round choice with white metro tiles.** It shows off the brick pattern without looking too stark, and it’s more forgiving of everyday marks than white.

- **Light grey:** a soft, classic contrast that suits most kitchens and bathrooms.
- **White:** a clean, seamless look—great if you want the tiles to blend into the room, though marks show more readily.
- **Charcoal:** a bold, graphic look that makes each tile stand out. It also highlights uneven spacing.
- **Warm off-white or pale beige:** works well alongside cream cupboards, wood and warm-toned worktops.

Check a grout colour sample against your actual tile in the room’s lighting—warm and cool whites can look surprisingly different together.
