For a typical plasterboard **wall**, space screws **no more than 300 mm (12 inches) apart along every stud**, including those behind board joints. Reduce that to **200 mm (8 inches) at external corners**. [Fixing guidance](https://www.british-gypsum.com/general-search?query=screw)

1. Position board joints over the centre of a stud so both boards have support.
2. Drive screw heads just below the surface, without breaking the paper.
3. For timber studs, use plasterboard screws long enough to penetrate the timber by at least **25 mm**.

Check for cables and pipes before driving screws.

These spacings are for ordinary wall boarding; ceilings and specified fire-rated systems have different fixing patterns.

**You’ll need:** plasterboard screws suited to timber or metal studs, a drill-driver with a depth-stop bit, and a tape measure.
