**No—don’t use that thin lamp extension cord for a 2000 W space heater.** The heater draws far more power than a desk lamp, and an undersized cord can overheat and start a fire.

1. Plug the heater **directly into a wall socket**, following its manual—not into an extension cord, power strip, or adapter.
2. If the cable won’t reach, move the heater closer to a socket, keeping it at least 1 metre from curtains, bedding, and furniture.
3. If the plug or socket gets hot, smells scorched, or shows discoloration, stop using it.

**What to buy:** No extension cord for this setup; use a suitable wall socket within reach.
