**Satin is usually the better choice for a busy hallway**—it’s easier to wipe clean and stands up well to scuffs from bags, shoes and passing traffic. The slight sheen does show up wall bumps and patch repairs, though.

**Matt is better for disguising uneven walls** and gives a softer, non-shiny finish. For a hallway, choose a **washable, durable matt**, rather than standard matt, so you can clean marks without spoiling the finish.

My pick: durable matt on the walls if you prefer a flat finish; satin if easy cleaning matters most. Satin also works well on skirting boards and door frames—use paint specifically intended for woodwork.
