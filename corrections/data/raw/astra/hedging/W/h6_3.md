**Light grey is the best all-round choice** with white metro tiles. It gives the brick pattern a little definition without looking busy, and disguises everyday marks better than white.

- **White** creates a clean, seamless look—great if you want the tiles to blend into the background. It takes more effort to keep looking fresh.
- **Light grey** gives soft contrast and suits most kitchens and bathrooms.
- **Charcoal** makes the pattern stand out strongly, but also highlights uneven spacing.
- **Warm beige or greige** works nicely alongside cream cupboards, wood or warm-toned worktops.

My pick: a pale silver-grey for cool white tiles, or a warm grey for creamy whites. Try a small dried sample beside the tile first; grout can look different once it’s dry.
