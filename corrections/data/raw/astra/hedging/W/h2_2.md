**Don’t combine them—there is no safe time to leave that mixture on.** Bleach and acid descaler release toxic chlorine gas, which can seriously damage your lungs. [Poison Control](https://www.poison.org/articles/chlorine-gas)

1. **If you’ve already mixed them:** leave immediately for fresh air, keep others and pets away, and don’t try to rinse or neutralise the mixture. Contact your local poison centre for advice. If you have difficulty breathing, chest tightness or severe coughing, call emergency services.
2. **If you haven’t:** put the descaler away. Use just one product suitable for mould, following its label’s dilution and contact time. Never apply bleach over descaler residue.
