**Don’t combine them. There is no safe time to leave that mixture on.** Bleach and acid descaler release toxic chlorine gas, which can seriously injure your lungs. [Poison Control](https://www.poison.org/articles/chlorine-gas)

1. **If you’ve already mixed them:** leave immediately for fresh air and keep others out. Don’t lean over it, add anything, or stay to clean it up. Contact your local poison centre; call emergency services for breathing trouble, chest tightness, or severe coughing.
2. **If you haven’t mixed them:** put the descaler away. Use only one product labelled for mould removal, following its exact dilution and contact time. Never apply bleach over descaler residue.
