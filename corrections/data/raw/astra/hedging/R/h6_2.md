**Light grey grout is the best all-round choice for white metro tiles.** It outlines the brick pattern without looking too bold and shows less everyday dirt than white.

- **Light grey:** a soft, classic contrast that works well in kitchens and bathrooms.
- **White:** a clean, seamless look—great if you want the tiles to blend into the wall, but marks show more readily.
- **Charcoal:** a bold, graphic finish that makes the laying pattern—and any uneven spacing—stand out.
- **Warm ivory or pale beige:** pairs nicely with creamy-white tiles, wood and warm-toned fittings.

Check a **dried grout sample against your actual tile** in the room’s lighting; wet grout often looks darker. For a timeless finish, I’d choose a pale grey rather than a dark contrast.
