For a standard plasterboard stud wall, space screws **no more than 300 mm (about 12 inches) apart along every stud**, including those behind the middle of the sheet. Reduce spacing to **200 mm at external corners**. [Fixing-spacing guidance](https://www.british-gypsum.com/technical-support/self-help-tools/faqs/what-are-screw-fixing-centres-partitions-ceilings-encasements)

1. Position board joints over studs so both sheet edges have support.
2. Drive screw heads just below the surface without tearing the paper.
3. Use the specified fixing pattern for fire-rated or multilayer wall systems.

For **ceilings**, use closer spacing: 230 mm along supports and 150 mm at board ends, under the same guidance.

**You’ll need:** drywall screws suited to timber or metal studs, a drill/driver with a depth-stop bit, and a tape measure.
