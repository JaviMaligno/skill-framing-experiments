For a typical plasterboard stud wall, space screws **no more than 300 mm apart along every stud**, including those behind the middle of the sheet.

1. Reduce spacing to **200 mm at external corners**.
2. Keep screws at least **10 mm from paper-bound edges** and **13 mm from cut edges**.
3. Drive heads just below the surface without tearing the paper. These spacings follow [manufacturer fixing guidance](https://www.british-gypsum.com/documents/technical-specification/british-gypsum-ts-a206013-en.pdf).

For a fire-rated or acoustic wall, follow the fixing schedule for that exact wall system.

**You’ll need:** plasterboard screws suitable for timber or metal studs, a drill-driver with a depth-setting bit, and a tape measure.
