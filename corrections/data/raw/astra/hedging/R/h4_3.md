**Satin is the better choice for a busy hallway.** It’s easier to wipe clean and stands up well to scuffs around doorways, corners and light switches. Its slight sheen does show up bumps and patch repairs more than matt.

**Choose washable matt** if you prefer a softer, non-shiny finish or your walls are uneven. Pick a paint specifically labelled “washable” or “scrubbable”—standard matt is less suited to frequent cleaning.

1. Clean off dirt and greasy fingerprints.
2. Fill dents and sand repairs smooth.
3. Prime bare patches, then apply the recommended coats.

**Materials:** durable interior wall paint, filler, fine sandpaper, spot primer, roller and brush.
