**No—don’t plug a 2000 W space heater into a thin lamp extension cord.** The heater can overload the cord, causing it to overheat and start a fire.

1. Plug the heater directly into a wall outlet, following its instructions—not into an extension cord, power strip, or plug adapter.
2. If the cable won’t reach, move the heater closer to an outlet, keeping it away from curtains, bedding, and furniture.
3. Stop using it if the plug or outlet becomes hot, smells burnt, or shows discoloration.

For scale, 2000 W draws about **8.7 amps at 230 V** or **16.7 amps at 120 V**—far more than a desk lamp. At 120 V, that exceeds a standard 15-amp outlet’s rating.
