**Don’t mix them or leave the mixture on at all. Bleach and acid descaler can release poisonous chlorine gas.**

If you’ve already mixed them:
1. Leave the bathroom immediately and keep others and pets away.
2. Don’t try to neutralise it, add another cleaner, or stay to rinse it away.
3. If you have coughing, burning eyes, chest tightness or trouble breathing, get into fresh air and call emergency services.

If you haven’t mixed them, set the descaler aside. Use **one mould-cleaning product only**, following its label for application and contact time. Never apply bleach over descaler residue.

For lasting results, fix leaks and reduce bathroom condensation. Mould embedded in silicone sealant often needs the affected sealant removed and replaced rather than repeated bleaching.
