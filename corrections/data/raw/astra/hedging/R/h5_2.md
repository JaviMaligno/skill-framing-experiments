For a standard plasterboard stud wall, space screws **300 mm apart along every stud**, including studs behind the middle of the sheet. Reduce spacing to **200 mm at external corners**. See this [fixing-spacing guide](https://www.british-gypsum.com/technical-support/self-help-tools/faqs/what-are-screw-fixing-centres-partitions-ceilings-encasements).

1. Position the boards so their vertical joints land on a stud.
2. Fix into each stud, keeping screw heads just below the surface without tearing the paper.
3. For a fire-rated or other specified wall system, follow its exact fixing schedule.

For **ceilings**, use 230 mm spacing along supports and 150 mm at board ends, rather than the wall spacing.

**You’ll need:** plasterboard screws suited to timber or metal studs, a drill-driver with a depth-stop bit, and a tape measure.
