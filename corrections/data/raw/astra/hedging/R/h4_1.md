**Satin is the better choice for a busy hallway:** it’s easier to wipe clean and handles scuffs better than standard matt.

- **Choose satin** for high traffic, children, pets or walls that need frequent cleaning. Its slight sheen will highlight bumps and uneven plaster.
- **Choose washable matt** if you prefer a softer, non-shiny finish or want to hide wall imperfections. Look for paint specifically labelled washable or scrub-resistant, rather than standard matt.

For most hallways, I’d use **washable matt on the walls and satin on the doors and skirting boards**—easy maintenance without a sheen across every surface.

**Materials:** interior wall paint in your chosen finish, filler, fine sandpaper, roller and cutting-in brush.
