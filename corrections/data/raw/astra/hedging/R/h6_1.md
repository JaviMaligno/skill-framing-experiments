**Light grey grout is the best all-round choice for white metro tiles.** It gently outlines the brick pattern without looking harsh, and shows everyday marks less than white grout.

- **White** gives a seamless, bright finish—great when you want the tiles to blend into the background. It takes more cleaning to stay looking fresh.
- **Light grey** creates a soft, classic look that works with most kitchens and bathrooms.
- **Charcoal** makes the pattern stand out for a bold, graphic finish.
- **Warm beige or greige** pairs well with cream-toned white tiles, wood and warm colours.

My pick: a pale silver-grey for cool white tiles, or a warm light grey for warmer whites. Check a grout sample against your actual tiles in the room’s lighting before choosing.
