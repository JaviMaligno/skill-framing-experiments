**Satin is the better choice for a busy hallway.** It’s generally easier to wipe clean and more resistant to scuffs than standard matt—handy near doors, coat hooks and shoe racks.

- **Choose satin** for a durable finish with a slight sheen. It will highlight bumps and uneven plaster more than matt.
- **Choose washable matt** if you prefer a flat finish or want to make wall imperfections less noticeable. Look for paint specifically labelled washable or scrubbable, rather than standard matt.

My pick: **washable matt for the walls, satin for doors and skirting boards**. If you’re choosing strictly between standard matt and satin for the walls, go satin.

**Materials:** interior wall paint in your chosen finish, filler for dents, fine sandpaper, roller and edging brush.
