**No—don’t use that thin lamp extension cord for a 2000 W space heater.** It can overheat and start a fire.

1. Plug the heater directly into a wall outlet—not an extension cord, power strip, or adapter.
2. Check the heater’s label matches your supply voltage. A 2000 W heater draws about **8.7 A at 230 V** or **16.7 A at 120 V**; the latter exceeds a standard 15 A outlet’s rating.
3. If the plug or outlet becomes hot, smells burnt, or shows discoloration, stop using it.

If the heater’s cable won’t reach, move the heater closer to a suitable outlet while keeping it clear of curtains, bedding, and furniture.
