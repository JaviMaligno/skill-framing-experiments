**Before buying demolition tools, get the wall checked for structural support and hidden gas, electrical and water lines.** A wall’s thickness or sound when tapped won’t tell you whether it’s load-bearing. Removing it without the right support can cause a collapse.

1. **Confirm what can be removed.** Have a structural professional assess the wall. If it supports loads, you’ll need a designed beam and temporary supports—not just demolition tools.
2. **Identify and isolate services.** Arrange for any wiring, pipes or gas lines to be disconnected or rerouted before demolition. Check suspect older materials for asbestos before disturbing them.
3. **Choose tools for the confirmed wall type.** For a cleared, non-load-bearing plasterboard partition, the basic kit is below. Brick or block needs a different demolition plan.

**Tools and materials:** utility knife, pry bar, claw hammer, drill/driver, suitable dust extractor, heavy-duty rubble bags, floor protection, dust sheeting, goggles, gloves, hearing protection and a properly fitted particulate respirator.
