**Before buying demolition tools, establish whether the wall is load-bearing and whether it contains wiring, gas pipes or plumbing.** Don’t start knocking it down until those checks are complete—removing structural support can cause a collapse.

1. **Confirm the wall’s role.** Get a structural assessment; appearance or a tap test won’t establish whether it supports the building. A load-bearing wall needs a designed support and temporary-propping plan.
2. **Locate services.** A wall scanner helps, but it cannot prove the wall is clear. Identify and isolate services before cutting.
3. **Check suspect materials.** Older textured coatings, boards and insulation may contain asbestos; have suspect materials tested before disturbing them.

**For a confirmed non-load-bearing plasterboard partition:** wall scanner, utility knife, plasterboard saw, pry bar, claw hammer, drill/driver, rubble bags, dust barriers, suitable dust extractor, eye protection, gloves and a dust respirator.
