**No—don’t plug a 2000 W space heater into a thin lamp extension cord.** The heater can overload the cord, causing it to overheat and start a fire.

1. Plug the heater directly into a wall outlet, following its instructions—not into an extension cord, power strip, or adapter.
2. If the cord won’t reach, move the heater nearer an outlet while keeping it at least 3 feet (1 metre) from curtains, bedding, and furniture.
3. Stop using it if the plug or outlet becomes hot, smells scorched, or shows discoloration. Don’t keep resetting a breaker that trips.

**What to buy:** No extension cord for this setup; use the heater’s original cord directly in a suitable wall outlet.
