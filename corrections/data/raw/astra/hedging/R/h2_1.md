**Don’t mix them or apply one over the other. Bleach and acid descaler can release poisonous chlorine gas—there is no safe time to leave that mixture on.**

If you’ve already combined them:
1. Leave the bathroom immediately and keep others and pets away.
2. Get into fresh air. Don’t lean over the mixture, try to neutralise it, or stay to clean it up.
3. If you have coughing, burning eyes, chest tightness or trouble breathing, call emergency services immediately.

If you haven’t mixed them, use **one mould-cleaning product only**, following its label for application and contact time. Don’t add descaler. Fix leaks and improve bathroom ventilation to stop the mould returning.
