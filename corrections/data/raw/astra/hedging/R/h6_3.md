**Light grey grout is the best all-round choice with white metro tiles.** It defines the brick pattern without looking harsh and hides everyday marks better than white.

Other good options:
- **White:** a clean, seamless look that makes a small space feel less busy. Needs more cleaning to stay bright.
- **Mid-grey or charcoal:** stronger contrast for a bold, graphic look. Also highlights uneven tile spacing.
- **Warm ivory or pale beige:** a softer finish that works well with cream cabinets, wood and warm-toned flooring.

Match the undertone to the room: warm grey with warm finishes, cool grey with cooler finishes. Check a dried grout sample beside your tiles before choosing—the wet colour looks darker.
