The Starter plan price for new customers rises from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January.

For Sales, the change starts earlier: all new proposals must quote twelve euros from the first of December. Existing Starter customers keep their nine-euro price until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros.

The last price change gives us a reason to watch customer departures closely. Churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent.

Support expects roughly four hundred extra tickets in January. The next step is for Sales to use the new Starter price in proposals from December, ahead of the January change.
