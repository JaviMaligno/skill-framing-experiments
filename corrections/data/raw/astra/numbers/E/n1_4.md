The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four out of 791 migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number.

For teams using the new customer relationship management system, the bigger issue is missing postcodes, not VAT numbers.

Around one in seven records had no postcode. That is up from about one in ten in the second quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, from about 95 percent to about 94 percent. Duplicates affected roughly five percent of records.

So the feared VAT issue was small, but gaps in the customer data remain. Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
