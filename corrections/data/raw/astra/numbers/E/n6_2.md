The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up slightly from last year. The main requests concern how we spend our time, how promotions work, and the laptops we use.

More than three quarters of employees responded. Satisfaction was seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year.

Fewer recurring meetings was the top request, raised by about four in ten respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, followed by better laptops.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month. Those steps address the two most common requests in the survey.
