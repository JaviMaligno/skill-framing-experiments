The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up slightly from last year.

The findings highlight what people want to change at work, with fewer recurring meetings topping the list. Clearer promotion criteria came next, followed by better laptops.

Around eight in ten employees responded, so the results reflect feedback from a large majority of the team. Satisfaction averaged seven point four out of ten. Last year, it was seven point one.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays from next month, addressing the most common request in the survey.
