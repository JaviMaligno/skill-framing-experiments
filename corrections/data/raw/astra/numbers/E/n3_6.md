Checkout was down for a little over two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The outage affected customers trying to pay and left work for our support team. Around forty-eight thousand euros in revenue was at risk, rather than confirmed lost.

Nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most were retried automatically, but two hundred and thirteen still need manual follow-up. Those payments are the next task for support.

To address the cause, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That change is designed to prevent another checkout outage caused by an expired certificate.
