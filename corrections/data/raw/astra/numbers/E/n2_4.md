The Starter plan price will rise from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers.

For Sales, the change starts earlier: all new proposals must quote twelve euros from the first of December. Support also expects a busier January, with roughly three hundred and fifty extra tickets.

Existing customers will keep paying nine euros until the end of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros a month.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent.

The next step is for Sales to use the new Starter price in December proposals, ahead of the January change.
