The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had a wrong VAT number.

The main issue to focus on now is missing postcodes, rather than VAT errors. Around one in seven records lacked a postcode, up from roughly one in ten in the second quarter.

The audit also found 37 duplicate records. Average record completeness slipped to about 94 percent, compared with about 95 percent in the previous quarter.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November. That puts the focus on the more widespread gap identified by the audit.
