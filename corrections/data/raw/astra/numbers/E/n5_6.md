The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend. The main thing to plan around is search: it will be down for about six hours on Saturday. If you own pages with macros, there’s also a check coming your way.

The migration covers roughly three thousand pages. Most will make the move automatically: eighty-seven percent of pages convert without an owner check. But the three hundred and seventy-nine pages with macros need their owners to check them.

Those owners will get an email on Monday. Links to old pages will redirect for twelve months, so the move won’t mean losing those links straight away.
