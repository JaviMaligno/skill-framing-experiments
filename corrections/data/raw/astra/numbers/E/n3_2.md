Checkout was down for a little over two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The outage left around forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk. For the support team, there is still follow-up work to do.

Nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most were retried automatically, but two hundred and thirteen payments need manual follow-up. That is the remaining action for support.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS certificate. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That change addresses the certificate expiry that caused this outage.
