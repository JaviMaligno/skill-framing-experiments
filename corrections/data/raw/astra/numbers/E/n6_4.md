The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up slightly from last year.

The main requests are about how we spend our time, how promotions work, and the laptops we use. Leadership has announced next steps on two of those areas.

Nearly four in five employees responded. Satisfaction scored seven point four out of ten, compared with seven point one last year.

Fewer recurring meetings was the top request, raised by about forty percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, followed by better laptops.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month.
