The Starter plan price will rise from nine to twelve euros a month for new customers on the first of January.

For Sales, the change starts earlier: all new proposals must quote twelve euros from the first of December. Existing customers keep their nine-euro price until the end of June. Pro stays unchanged at twenty-nine euros a month.

The last price change gives us something to watch. Churn rose to two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent.

Support expects roughly three hundred and fifty extra tickets in January. The immediate next step is for Sales to use the new Starter price in December proposals, ahead of the January change.
