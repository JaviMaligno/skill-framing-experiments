The Starter plan price for new customers rises from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January.

For employees, the immediate changes are in sales proposals and the support workload. Existing customers keep the nine-euro price until the end of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros a month.

The last price change gives us a reason to watch customer departures closely. Churn reached two point three percent after that change. The baseline was one point eight percent. Support expects roughly three hundred and fifty extra tickets in January.

The first action comes before the price rise itself. From the first of December, Sales must quote twelve euros for Starter in all new proposals.
