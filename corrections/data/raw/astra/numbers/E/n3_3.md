Checkout was down for a little over two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The outage affected customer payments and left about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk. Support now needs to follow up manually on two hundred and thirteen failed payments.

In total, nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most were retried automatically, but those retries should not be confused with confirmed successful payments.

To prevent the same certificate expiry problem from happening again, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. The remaining customer follow-up sits with the support team.
