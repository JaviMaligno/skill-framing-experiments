The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the feared problem with value added tax, or VAT, numbers barely happened: just four out of 791 migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number.

That means the main concern around the migration was rare, while missing postcodes need more attention.

Around one in seven records had no postcode, up from about one in ten in the second quarter. Average record completeness also fell slightly, to about 94 percent. Duplicates accounted for roughly five percent of records.

So the next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November. The focus now shifts from the feared VAT issue to the more common postcode gaps.
