The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing nearly three thousand pages with it.

The main thing to plan around is search. It will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. If you own pages that need checking, look out for an email on Monday.

Most pages will convert automatically. But 379 pages contain macros and need their owners to check them. Those are the pages the Monday emails will flag.

You won’t need to replace every old link straight away. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base for twelve months after the move.
