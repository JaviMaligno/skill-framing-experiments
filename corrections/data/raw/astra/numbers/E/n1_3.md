The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had a wrong VAT number.

The main issue to focus on is missing postcodes, rather than VAT errors. Around one in seven records lacked a postcode, up from around one in ten in the second quarter.

The audit also found 37 duplicate records. Overall record completeness slipped to about 94 percent, from about 95 percent in the previous quarter.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November. That will address the audit’s biggest gap.
