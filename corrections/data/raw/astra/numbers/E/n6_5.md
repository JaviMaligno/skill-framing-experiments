The team survey results are in, with satisfaction up slightly from last year and fewer recurring meetings topping the list of requests.

The follow-up includes changes to meeting schedules and clearer information about promotion criteria.

Nearly four in five colleagues responded. Satisfaction scored seven point four out of ten, compared with seven point one last year.

Around forty percent asked for fewer recurring meetings. Clearer promotion criteria came next, followed by better laptops.

Leadership will trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. It will also publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Those are the two next steps announced in response to the survey.
