Facilities has replaced the plants on floors two, three and five, with just under fifty new plants now in place.

The main thing to remember is that you don’t need to water them. The supplier will take care of that, so please leave the watering to them.

There are fourteen species in the new planting, with three plants in each meeting room. The supplier’s contract runs for a year and a half. Watering visits are scheduled every seven to ten days.

Floor four is still to come. It will get its plants once the refurbishment ends. For now, the replacement covers the other three floors.
