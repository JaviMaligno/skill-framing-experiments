The team survey results are in, with satisfaction slightly higher than last year and fewer recurring meetings topping the list of requests.

The results point to changes that could affect your working week and make promotion expectations clearer.

Around four in five employees responded, giving leadership feedback from across the team. Satisfaction was seven point four out of ten. That’s up from seven point one last year.

Fewer recurring meetings was the most common request, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays from next month. The survey update includes no next step on laptops.
