The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the 791 migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number.

The bigger issue is missing address information, so that’s where the next clean-up effort will focus. Around one in seven records had no postcode, up from about one in ten in the second quarter.

Overall record completeness also slipped, from about 95 percent to about 94 percent. Duplicates accounted for roughly five percent of records.

So the main takeaway is reassuring on VAT numbers, but there’s still work to do on data quality. The postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
