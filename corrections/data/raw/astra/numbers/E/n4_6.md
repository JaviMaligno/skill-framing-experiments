Facilities has replaced the office plants on floors two, three and five.

The main thing to remember is that you do not need to water them. The supplier will take care of that, so please leave watering to them.

There are around fifty new plants across those floors, with three plants in each meeting room. The selection includes fourteen species.

The supplier’s contract runs for eighteen months. Watering is scheduled every seven to ten days, rather than every day.

Floor four is still to come. It will get its plants once the refurbishment ends. For now, the replacement covers only floors two, three and five.
