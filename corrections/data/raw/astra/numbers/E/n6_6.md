The team survey results are in, with satisfaction at seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year.

The results highlight what employees want to change, and leadership has plans on two of the biggest requests.

Seventy-eight percent of employees responded. The most common ask was fewer recurring meetings, chosen by about four in ten respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at just over a third. Better laptops were also a priority, with nearly three in ten asking for them.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays from next month.
