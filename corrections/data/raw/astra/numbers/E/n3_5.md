Checkout was down for just over two hours on the fourteenth of October after a security certificate expired on the payment proxy.

The outage left some payments needing manual follow-up by support. It also put about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk.

Nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most were retried automatically, but two hundred and thirteen still need the support team’s attention.

The root cause was the expired certificate. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire, addressing the cause of this outage.

The remaining follow-up is for support to handle the payments that were not retried automatically.
