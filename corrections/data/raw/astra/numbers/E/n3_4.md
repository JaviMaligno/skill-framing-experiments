Checkout was down for a little over two hours on the fourteenth of October because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The immediate priority is support follow-up on payments that were not retried automatically. The incident put about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk. That is revenue at risk, not a confirmed loss.

Around thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most were retried automatically, but two hundred and thirteen still need manual follow-up by the support team.

To prevent the same certificate problem from happening again, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. The remaining work is the manual payment follow-up.
