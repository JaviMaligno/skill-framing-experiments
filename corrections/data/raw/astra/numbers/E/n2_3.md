The Starter plan price rises from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers.

The key point for teams is that sales proposals must change before the new price takes effect. Existing customers keep their nine-euro price until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays unchanged at twenty-nine euros a month.

There is reason to watch customer reactions. After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects around three hundred and sixty extra tickets in January.

The first action comes on the first of December. From that date, Sales must quote twelve euros for Starter in all new proposals.
