Checkout was down for a little over two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The main follow-up is for support: two hundred and thirteen failed payments still need manual attention. Those are separate from the payments that were retried automatically.

The outage affected around thirteen hundred payments in total. Just over a thousand were retried automatically. Revenue at risk was about forty-eight thousand euros, though that is not a confirmed loss.

Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That change addresses the cause of this outage. Support’s remaining task is to follow up on the payments that need manual attention.
