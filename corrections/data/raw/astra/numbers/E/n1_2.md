The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four out of 791 migrated customer records had a wrong number.

That means the main concern with the move to the new customer relationship management system was rare. But the audit also shows where clean-up is needed.

Missing postcodes were the biggest issue, affecting about one in seven records. That’s up from roughly one in ten in the second quarter.

Duplicates accounted for about five percent of records. Average record completeness also slipped, from about 95 percent to about 94 percent.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
