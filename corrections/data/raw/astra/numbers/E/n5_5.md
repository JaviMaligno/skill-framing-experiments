The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing across about three thousand pages.

The main thing to plan for is a search outage. Search will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Existing links to old pages will redirect for twelve months.

Most pages will convert automatically. But some need their owners to check them because they contain macros. There are 379 pages in that group.

If you own a page that needs checking, you’ll get an email on Monday. So watch for that message after the move, and remember that search will be temporarily unavailable on Saturday.
