The Starter plan will rise from nine to twelve euros a month for new customers on the first of January.

For teams, this means a change to sales proposals and an expected increase in support requests. Sales must quote twelve euros in all new proposals from the first of December, a month before the price change takes effect.

Existing customers keep the nine-euro price until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent.

Support expects around three hundred and sixty extra tickets in January.
