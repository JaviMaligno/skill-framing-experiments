The Starter plan price will rise from nine to twelve euros a month on January first for new customers.

The key thing for teams is that sales proposals need to change before the price takes effect. Sales must quote twelve euros in all new proposals from December first. Existing customers will keep paying nine euros until June thirtieth. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros.

There are two things to watch. After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects around three hundred and forty to three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January.

Next comes the December proposal update, followed by the new customer price in January.
