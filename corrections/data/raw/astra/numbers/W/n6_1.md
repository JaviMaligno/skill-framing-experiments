The team survey results are in, with satisfaction up slightly from last year. For employees, the follow-up includes clearer promotion criteria and a trial of meeting-free Wednesdays, addressing the two most common requests.

Seventy-eight percent of the team responded, giving us four hundred and twelve responses. Satisfaction averaged seven point four out of ten, compared with seven point one last year.

Fewer recurring meetings topped the requests at forty-one percent. Clearer promotion criteria came next at thirty-six percent, followed by better laptops at twenty-nine percent.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. The meeting-free Wednesday trial starts next month. No next step for laptops was announced in these results.
