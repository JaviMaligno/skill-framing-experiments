Checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The outage affected customers trying to pay and left just over forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk. That is revenue at risk, not a confirmed loss.

Nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Just over a thousand were retried automatically, but two hundred and thirteen still need manual follow-up from the support team.

Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That change addresses the cause of this outage. The remaining customer follow-up is with support.
