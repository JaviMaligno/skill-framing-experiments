The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing nearly three thousand pages with it.

The main thing to plan around is search. It will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages will redirect for the next twelve months.

Most of the move is automatic. Eighty-seven percent of pages will convert without manual work. The migration covers sixty-one spaces and just over twelve gigabytes of attachments.

But three hundred and seventy-nine pages with macros need their owners to check them. Those owners will get an email on Monday. If you receive one, your next step is to check the pages it identifies.
