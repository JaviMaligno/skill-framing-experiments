Checkout was down for a little over two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The immediate priority is support follow-up for just over two hundred failed payments. Around forty-eight thousand euros in revenue was at risk, though that is not a confirmed loss.

Nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Just over a thousand were retried automatically, leaving two hundred and thirteen that need manual follow-up by the support team.

To address the cause, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. The remaining customer follow-up is the next step for support.
