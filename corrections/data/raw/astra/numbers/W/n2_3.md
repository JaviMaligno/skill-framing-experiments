The Starter plan price rises from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers.

For Sales, the change starts earlier: all new proposals must quote twelve euros from the first of December. Support also needs to prepare for more questions, with around three hundred and forty to three hundred and eighty extra tickets expected in January.

Existing Starter customers will keep their nine-euro price until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros a month.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent.

The next step is for Sales to use the new Starter price in every new proposal from December.
