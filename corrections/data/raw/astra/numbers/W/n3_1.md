Checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes on October fourteenth after a security certificate on the payment proxy expired.

The outage put about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk. That is revenue at risk, not a confirmed loss. For our support team, there are still payments that need manual follow-up.

Nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Just over a thousand were retried automatically, leaving two hundred and thirteen for support to follow up on. An automatic retry does not itself confirm a successful payment.

Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That change addresses the expired certificate issue behind this outage.
