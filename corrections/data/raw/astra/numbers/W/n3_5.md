On October fourteenth, checkout was down for a little over two hours because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The immediate priority is support follow-up: two hundred and thirteen failed payments still need manual attention. The incident put about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk.

In total, nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most of those, just over a thousand, were retried automatically. That leaves the remaining payments for the support team to follow up on.

To prevent a repeat of this certificate expiry, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. The outstanding work is the manual follow-up on those remaining payments.
