The Starter plan price will rise from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers.

For Sales, the change starts earlier: all new proposals must quote twelve euros from the first of December. Existing customers will keep paying nine euros until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros a month.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects around three hundred and forty to three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January.

The next step is for Sales to use the new price in every new proposal from December, ahead of the January change.
