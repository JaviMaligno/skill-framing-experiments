The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up slightly from last year. The main requests were fewer recurring meetings, clearer promotion criteria and better laptops, with changes planned on the first two.

Seventy-eight percent of the team responded, giving us four hundred and twelve responses. Satisfaction came in at seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year. Fewer recurring meetings topped the requests at forty-one percent. Clearer promotion criteria followed at thirty-six percent, and better laptops at twenty-nine percent.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month.
