Facilities has replaced the office plants on floors two, three and five.

The main thing for everyone to know is that you don’t need to water them. The supplier will take care of that, so please don’t water them yourself.

There are forty-six new plants in total, covering fourteen species, with three plants in each meeting room. The supplier’s contract runs for eighteen months, and they’ll water the plants every seven to ten days.

Floor four is still to come. It will get its plants once the refurbishment ends. For now, the new plants are on floors two, three and five, and watering is covered by the supplier.
