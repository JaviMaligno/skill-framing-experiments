The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing across nearly three thousand pages from sixty-one spaces.

The main thing to plan around is search. It will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages will redirect for twelve months, so you won’t need to replace them all straight away.

Most of the move is automatic. Eighty-seven percent of pages convert without intervention. But three hundred and seventy-nine pages contain macros and need their owners to check them.

If you own one of those pages, you’ll get an email on Monday asking you to take a look.
