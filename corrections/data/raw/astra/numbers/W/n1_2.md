The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT, or value added tax, number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the seven hundred and ninety-one migrated customer records had a wrong number.

The main concern now is missing postcodes, rather than VAT errors. Around one in seven records lacked a postcode, up from roughly one in ten in the second quarter.

There were also thirty-seven duplicate records. Overall, average record completeness slipped to just under ninety-four percent, from just over ninety-five percent in the previous quarter.

So the feared VAT issue was small, but there is still clean-up work to do. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
