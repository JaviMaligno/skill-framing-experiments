The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing across nearly three thousand pages from sixty-one spaces, plus about twelve gigabytes of attachments.

The main thing to plan around is search. It will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages will redirect for twelve months, so those links will still get you to the content after the move.

Most pages, eighty-seven percent, will convert automatically. But three hundred and seventy-nine pages with macros need their owners to check them.

If you own one of those pages, you’ll get an email on Monday asking you to check it.
