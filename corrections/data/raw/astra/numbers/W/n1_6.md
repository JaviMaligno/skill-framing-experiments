The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the seven hundred and ninety-one migrated customer records had a wrong value added tax number.

The takeaway is reassuring on VAT, but missing postcodes need more attention in the new customer relationship management system.

About one in seven records had no postcode, up from about one in ten in the previous quarter. There were also thirty-seven duplicate records. Average record completeness slipped from just over ninety-five percent to just under ninety-four percent.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
