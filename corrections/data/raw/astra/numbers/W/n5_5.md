The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing nearly three thousand pages with it.

The main thing to plan around is search: it will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages will redirect for a year.

Most of the move is automatic. Eighty-seven percent of pages convert without manual work. The migration covers sixty-one spaces and just over twelve gigabytes of attachments.

But three hundred and seventy-nine pages contain macros and need their owners to check them. If you own one of those pages, you’ll get an email on Monday asking you to take a look.
