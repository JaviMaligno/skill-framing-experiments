The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up slightly from last year.

The feedback points to three things people want improved: recurring meetings, promotion criteria and laptops. Leadership has announced next steps on the first two.

Seventy-eight percent of the team responded. Satisfaction averaged seven point four out of ten, compared with seven point one last year.

Fewer recurring meetings was the top request, raised by forty-one percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at thirty-six percent. Better laptops followed, at twenty-nine percent.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month.
