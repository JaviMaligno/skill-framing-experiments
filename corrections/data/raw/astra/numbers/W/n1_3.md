The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four out of seven hundred and ninety-one customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had a wrong VAT number.

The main takeaway is that the bigger issue is missing postcodes, not VAT numbers.

Around one in seven records lacked a postcode, up from about one in ten in the second quarter. The audit also found thirty-seven duplicates. Average record completeness slipped to just under ninety-four percent, from just over ninety-five percent in the previous quarter.

Next up is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
