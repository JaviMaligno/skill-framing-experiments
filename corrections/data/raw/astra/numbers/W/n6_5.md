The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up slightly from last year. If you’ve been asking for fewer meetings or clearer guidance on promotions, leadership has announced steps on both.

Nearly four hundred and twelve colleagues responded, giving the survey a response rate of seventy-eight percent. Satisfaction averaged seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year.

Fewer recurring meetings was the top request, raised by forty-one percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next at thirty-six percent, followed by better laptops at twenty-nine percent.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter and will trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month.
