The Starter plan rises from nine to twelve euros a month on January first for new customers.

The key thing to watch is the timing: Sales must use twelve euros in all new proposals from December first, a month before the price takes effect. Existing customers keep paying nine euros until June thirtieth. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros a month.

The last price change gives us some context. Churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects around three hundred and forty to three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January.

The next step is for Sales to start quoting the new Starter price in December, ahead of the January change.
