The third-quarter data-quality audit found just four wrong VAT numbers among the seven hundred and ninety-one customer records migrated to the new CRM.

The takeaway is reassuring: the VAT problem everyone feared barely happened. The bigger clean-up task is missing postcodes, not VAT numbers.

About one in seven records had no postcode, up from roughly one in ten in the second quarter. That’s a hundred and twelve records needing attention. The audit also found thirty-seven duplicates. Overall record completeness slipped to just under ninety-four percent, from about ninety-five percent in the previous quarter.

Next up is the postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
