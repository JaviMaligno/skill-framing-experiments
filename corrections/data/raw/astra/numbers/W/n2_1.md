The Starter plan is going from nine to twelve euros a month on January first for new customers.

The key thing for teams is that sales proposals need to change before the price takes effect. Sales must quote twelve euros in all new proposals from December first.

Existing customers will keep paying nine euros until June thirtieth. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros.

There are two figures to keep in mind. After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects roughly three hundred and forty to three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January.

The first deadline is December first, when the new proposal pricing starts.
