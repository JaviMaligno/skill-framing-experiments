Checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The outage put about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk. That’s revenue at risk, not a confirmed loss. For our support team, the work now is to follow up manually on two hundred and thirteen failed payments.

In total, nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most of those were retried automatically, but the remaining payments still need attention.

To prevent the same certificate issue from happening again, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. Manual payment follow-up is the next step for support.
