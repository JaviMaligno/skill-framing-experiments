The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing nearly three thousand pages with it.

The main thing to plan around is search being down for about six hours on Saturday. Your old page links will still work through redirects for the next twelve months.

Most of the move is automatic. Eighty-seven percent of pages will convert without a manual check. But three hundred and seventy-nine pages contain macros and need their owners to check them. The migration also covers sixty-one spaces and just over twelve gigabytes of attachments.

If you own a page that needs checking, you’ll get an email on Monday.
