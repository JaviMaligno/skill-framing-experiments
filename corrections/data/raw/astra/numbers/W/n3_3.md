Checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes on October fourteenth after a security certificate expired on the payment proxy.

The immediate priority is the payments that still need attention. Support has two hundred and thirteen failed payments to follow up manually. The incident put around forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk. That is revenue at risk, not a confirmed loss.

In total, nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Just over a thousand were retried automatically.

Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire, addressing the cause of this outage. The remaining follow-up is for support to work through the payments that were not automatically retried.
