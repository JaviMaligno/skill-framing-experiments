The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up slightly from last year.

The main message is about how we work: fewer recurring meetings, clearer promotion criteria and better laptops topped the list of requests. Leadership has announced plans on the first two.

About four in five colleagues responded, giving us just over four hundred responses. Satisfaction rose to seven point four out of ten, from seven point one last year. Fewer recurring meetings was the biggest ask, at forty-one percent. Clearer promotion criteria followed at thirty-six percent, and better laptops at twenty-nine percent.

Next, leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter and trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month.
