The team survey results are in, with satisfaction up slightly and fewer recurring meetings topping the list of requests.

For employees, the changes coming next are clearer promotion criteria and a trial of meeting-free Wednesdays.

Just over three quarters of the team responded, with four hundred and twelve people taking part. Satisfaction averaged seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year. Forty-one percent asked for fewer recurring meetings, thirty-six percent wanted clearer promotion criteria, and twenty-nine percent asked for better laptops.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. The meeting-free Wednesday trial starts next month. The update does not announce any action on laptops.
