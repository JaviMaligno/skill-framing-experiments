The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the nearly eight hundred customer records migrated to the new CRM had a wrong VAT number.

The takeaway is that VAT errors were rare, but missing address details need attention.

Around one in seven records had no postcode, up from about one in ten in the second quarter. That’s a hundred and twelve records needing postcode clean-up. The audit also found thirty-seven duplicates.

Overall record completeness slipped to just under ninety-four percent, from about ninety-five percent in the previous quarter.

Next up is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
