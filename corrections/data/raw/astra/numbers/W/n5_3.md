The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing across nearly three thousand pages from sixty-one spaces.

The main thing to plan around is search. It will be down for about six hours on Saturday. If you use old page links, those will redirect to the new knowledge base for the next twelve months.

Most of the move is automatic: eighty-seven percent of pages convert without an owner check. But three hundred and seventy-nine pages contain macros and need their owners to check them.

Those owners will get an email on Monday. Watch for that message to find out whether you have a page to review.
