The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the seven hundred and ninety-one migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number.

The bigger issue is missing postcodes, so that’s where the next clean-up effort will focus. About one in seven records lacked a postcode, up from roughly one in ten in the second quarter.

Overall record completeness also slipped, from about ninety-five percent to just under ninety-four percent. The audit found thirty-seven duplicates, too.

So the reassuring news is that wrong VAT numbers were rare. Postcode gaps are the more common problem, and the postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
