The Starter plan price will rise to twelve euros a month for new customers on the first of January.

For Sales, the change starts earlier: all new proposals must quote the new price from the first of December. Existing Starter customers will keep paying nine euros a month until the end of June. The Pro plan price is unchanged.

The last price change saw churn rise by half a percentage point above the baseline. Support expects roughly three hundred and sixty extra tickets in January.

The immediate next step is for Sales to use the new Starter price in December proposals, ahead of the January change for new customers.
