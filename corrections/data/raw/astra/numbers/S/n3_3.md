Checkout was down for more than two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The impact was failed payments for customers and follow-up work for our support team. About forty-eight thousand euros in revenue was at risk. That is revenue at risk, not a confirmed loss.

Nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most were retried automatically, but that does not mean every payment was recovered.

The next step is for support to follow up manually on the remaining two hundred and thirteen payments. To prevent a repeat, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire.
