Checkout was down for a little over two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The immediate priority is following up on payments that were not retried automatically. For the support team, that means manual follow-up on just over two hundred failed payments.

The outage affected around thirteen hundred payments in total. Most were retried automatically, but that does not mean they were all completed. Revenue at risk was about forty-eight thousand euros.

To prevent the same certificate problem from happening again, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. The remaining support follow-up is still needed.
