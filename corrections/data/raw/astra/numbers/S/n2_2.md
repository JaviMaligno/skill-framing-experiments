The Starter plan price rises to twelve euros a month for new customers on the first of January.

For Sales, the change starts earlier: all new proposals must quote that price from the first of December. Support should also prepare for a busier January.

Existing Starter customers will keep paying nine euros a month until the end of June. Pro pricing is unchanged.

The last price change saw churn rise above its baseline. Support now expects several hundred extra tickets in January.

The immediate next step is for Sales to use the new Starter price in December proposals, ahead of the January change for new customers.
