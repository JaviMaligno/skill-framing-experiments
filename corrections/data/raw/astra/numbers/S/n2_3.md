The Starter plan price for new customers will rise to twelve euros a month on the first of January.

For Sales, the deadline comes earlier. All new proposals must quote the new price from the first of December. Support also expects more work in January, with roughly three hundred and sixty extra tickets anticipated.

Existing Starter customers will keep paying nine euros a month until the end of June. Pro pricing is unchanged at twenty-nine euros.

After the last price change, churn was half a percentage point above the baseline. That is the context for this change.

The next step is for Sales to use the new Starter price in December proposals, ahead of the January increase.
