The Starter plan price rises to twelve euros a month for new customers on the first of January.

Existing customers will keep their current nine euro rate until the end of June. Pro pricing is unchanged.

For Sales, the action comes before the price change takes effect. All new proposals must quote the higher Starter price from the first of December.

There is also a support impact to prepare for. Support expects roughly three hundred and fifty extra tickets in January. After the last price change, churn was slightly above baseline, reaching a little over two percent.

The next step for Sales is to use the new price in December proposals, ahead of the January change for new customers.
