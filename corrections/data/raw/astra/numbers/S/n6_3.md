The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is slightly higher than last year. The results matter because leadership is acting on two of the main requests: fewer recurring meetings and clearer promotion criteria.

Nearly four in five employees responded, giving a broad view of what the team thinks. Satisfaction rose to just over seven out of ten. Fewer recurring meetings was the most common request. Clearer promotion criteria came next, followed by better laptops.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month. The update does not include a next step on laptops.
