Checkout was down for a little over two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The immediate priority is support follow-up for just over two hundred failed payments that still need manual attention. Around forty-eight thousand euros in revenue was at risk during the outage. That is revenue at risk, not a confirmed loss.

In total, roughly thirteen hundred payments failed. Most were retried automatically, but those retries should not be confused with confirmed successful payments.

Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That change addresses the cause of this outage. The remaining payment follow-up sits with the support team.
