The Starter plan price is rising to twelve euros a month for new customers from January first.

If you work in sales, the key deadline comes earlier. All new proposals must quote the new price from December first. Existing customers keep their current monthly price of nine euros until June thirtieth. The Pro plan price is unchanged.

The last price change offers some context. Customer churn was slightly higher than the baseline, so that’s worth keeping in mind as this change approaches. Support expects around three hundred and fifty extra tickets in January.

The next step for sales is to use the new Starter price in December proposals, ahead of the January change for new customers.
