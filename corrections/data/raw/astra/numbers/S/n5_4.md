The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing nearly three thousand pages with it.

The main thing to plan around is search. It will be down for about six hours on Saturday. If you own pages with macros, you’ll also need to check them after the move.

Most pages will convert automatically. But around four hundred pages with macros need an owner’s check. Owners of those pages will get an email on Monday.

Existing links won’t stop working straight away. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base for twelve months. For now, watch for the Saturday search outage and Monday’s owner emails.
