Checkout was down for a little over two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

For the support team, the next step is manual follow-up on two hundred and thirteen failed payments. Those are the payments that were not retried automatically.

The outage caused roughly thirteen hundred failed payments in total. Most were retried automatically, but that does not confirm they were completed. Revenue at risk was about forty-eight thousand euros.

The expired certificate was a Transport Layer Security certificate. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That change addresses the cause of this outage, while the remaining payments still need support follow-up.
