Facilities has replaced the office plants on the second, third and fifth floors.

The main thing to remember is that you don’t need to water them. The supplier will take care of that, so please leave watering to them, including in meeting rooms.

There are just under fifty new plants in total, with a mix of fourteen species. Each meeting room has three plants. The supplier will water them roughly every week to ten days, under an eighteen-month contract.

The fourth floor is still to come. It will get its plants once the refurbishment is finished. For now, the replacement covers the other three floors.
