Checkout was down for a little over two hours on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

For employees, the main takeaway is that the outage left some payments needing support follow-up. It also put around forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk.

Nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most were retried automatically, but that does not mean every failed payment has been resolved.

The support team now needs to follow up manually on just over two hundred payments. To prevent another certificate expiry, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire.
