The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the feared problem with value added tax numbers barely happened during migration to the new customer relationship management system.

For teams using those customer records, the main issue is missing postcodes, rather than wrong tax numbers.

Just four of the nearly eight hundred migrated records had a wrong tax number. Missing postcodes, by contrast, affected about one in seven records, up from roughly one in ten in the previous quarter. The audit also found duplicate records. Overall record completeness fell slightly, to about ninety-four percent.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
