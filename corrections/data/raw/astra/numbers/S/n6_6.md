The team survey results are in, with satisfaction slightly higher than last year and clear requests for changes at work.

The results matter because leadership is taking action on two of the top asks: fewer recurring meetings and clearer promotion criteria. Better laptops were the other leading request.

Around four in five employees responded to the survey. Satisfaction averaged seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year. Fewer recurring meetings was the most common ask.

Next, leadership will trial meeting-free Wednesdays from next month. It will also publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. The survey update does not include a plan for laptops.
