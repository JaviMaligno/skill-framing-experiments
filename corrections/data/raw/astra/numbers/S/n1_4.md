The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the value added tax, or VAT, number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of nearly eight hundred migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number.

That’s reassuring for teams using the new customer relationship management system. The bigger issue is missing postcodes, not incorrect VAT numbers.

Around one in seven records lacked a postcode, a higher share than in the previous quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, and the audit found some duplicate records.

The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint starting on the third of November. That puts the focus on the more common gap the audit uncovered.
