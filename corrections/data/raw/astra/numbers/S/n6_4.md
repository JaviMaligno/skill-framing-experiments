The team survey results are in, with satisfaction slightly higher than last year and clear requests for changes at work.

The findings point to things that affect your working week and career: fewer recurring meetings, clearer promotion criteria and better laptops.

Nearly four out of five employees responded. Satisfaction averaged seven point four out of ten. That’s up from seven point one last year.

Fewer recurring meetings was the most common request, followed by clearer promotion criteria, then better laptops.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. Those are the two announced next steps responding to the survey.
