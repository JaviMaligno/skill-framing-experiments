The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend, bringing nearly three thousand pages with it.

The main thing to plan around is search. It will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages will redirect for a year, so those links will still take you to the pages after the move.

If you own one of those pages, you’ll get an email on Monday. That’s the next step for owners: checking the pages that need attention after the migration.
