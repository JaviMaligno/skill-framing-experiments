The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened during the move to the new CRM.

The main takeaway is reassuring: wrong VAT numbers were rare. The bigger clean-up issue is missing postcodes.

Just four of the nearly eight hundred migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number. By contrast, around one in seven records had no postcode, a higher share than in the previous quarter.

Overall record completeness also fell slightly, to about ninety-four percent. The audit found duplicate records too, but missing postcodes were the most common of these issues.

Next up is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
