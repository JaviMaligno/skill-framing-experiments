The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is slightly higher than last year.

The main requests are fewer recurring meetings, clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership has announced action on meetings and promotion criteria, so those are the areas where changes are coming.

Nearly eight in ten employees responded to the survey. Fewer recurring meetings was the most common request, raised by about four in ten respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, followed by better laptops.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month. The survey update does not include a plan for laptops.
