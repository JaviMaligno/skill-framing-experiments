The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the feared problem with wrong VAT numbers barely happened during the move to the new customer records system.

The main takeaway is that postcode gaps, rather than VAT errors, need attention. Just four of nearly eight hundred migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number.

Missing postcodes were much more common, affecting about one in seven records. That’s up from roughly one in ten in the previous quarter. Average record completeness also fell slightly, and the audit found some duplicate records.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November. That will tackle the biggest gap identified in the audit.
