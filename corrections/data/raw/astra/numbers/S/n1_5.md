The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the feared problem with value-added tax, or VAT, numbers barely happened during the move to the new customer relationship management system.

The takeaway is that VAT errors are not the main clean-up issue. Missing postcodes need more attention.

Only four of nearly eight hundred migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number. Missing postcodes were much more common, affecting about one in seven records. That is up from about one in ten in the previous quarter.

The audit also found duplicate records, and overall record completeness fell slightly.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
