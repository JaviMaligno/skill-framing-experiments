The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up slightly from last year. The main requests were fewer recurring meetings, clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership has announced action on meetings and promotion criteria.

Around four in five employees responded, giving a broad view of the team’s feedback. Satisfaction was just over seven out of ten, a small improvement on last year. Fewer recurring meetings was the most common request, followed by clearer promotion criteria, then better laptops.

Next, leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month. The update does not include a plan for laptops.
