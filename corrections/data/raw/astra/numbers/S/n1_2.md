The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the feared problem with value added tax numbers barely happened: just four of nearly eight hundred migrated customer records had a wrong number.

The takeaway is that the main clean-up issue in the new customer relationship management system is missing postcodes, rather than tax numbers.

About one in seven records lacked a postcode, a higher share than in the previous quarter. Overall record completeness also slipped slightly. Duplicate records were another issue, affecting roughly five percent of the total.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November. That puts the focus on the most common problem found in the audit.
