The Starter plan price rises to twelve euros a month for new customers on the first of January.

For Sales, the change starts earlier: all new proposals must quote the new price from the first of December. Existing Starter customers keep paying nine euros a month until the end of June. The Pro plan stays unchanged at twenty-nine euros.

The last price change saw churn half a percentage point above the baseline. That is an important detail as teams prepare for this increase. Support expects roughly three hundred and sixty extra tickets in January.

The next step is for Sales to use the new Starter price in December proposals, ahead of the January change.
