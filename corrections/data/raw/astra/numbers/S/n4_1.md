Facilities has replaced the office plants on the second, third and fifth floors.

The main thing for everyone to remember is simple: please don’t water them yourself. The supplier will take care of watering, so leave that job to them, including in meeting rooms.

There are now nearly fifty new plants across those floors. The selection includes fourteen species, with three plants in each meeting room. The supplier will water them roughly every week to ten days. That care is covered by an eighteen-month supplier contract.

The fourth floor will get its plants once the refurbishment there is finished.
