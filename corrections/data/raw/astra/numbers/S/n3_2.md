On October fourteenth, checkout was down for a little over two hours because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The outage stopped customers from completing payments and left some needing follow-up from our support team. Around forty-eight thousand euros in revenue was at risk. That is revenue at risk, not confirmed lost revenue.

Nearly thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most were retried automatically, but two hundred and thirteen still need manual follow-up from support.

Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That change addresses the cause of this incident. The remaining customer follow-up is the next task for the support team.
