The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is slightly higher than last year. The findings point to changes that matter in day-to-day work: fewer recurring meetings and clearer promotion criteria.

More than three quarters of the team responded, so the results reflect a broad range of employee feedback. Satisfaction rose to seven point four out of ten. Fewer recurring meetings was the most common request, followed by clearer promotion criteria. Better laptops also featured among the top asks.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays from next month. The source does not announce any next steps on laptops.
