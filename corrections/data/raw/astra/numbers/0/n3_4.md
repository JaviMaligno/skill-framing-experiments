Checkout was down on the fourteenth of October for two hours and seventeen minutes, from nine forty-one to eleven fifty-eight Central European Time.

The outage put forty-eight thousand, three hundred and twenty euros in revenue at risk. That is revenue at risk, not confirmed lost revenue. For the support team, there are still payments that need manual follow-up.

In total, one thousand, two hundred and eighty-four payments failed. Of those, one thousand and seventy-one were retried automatically. The remaining two hundred and thirteen need support follow-up.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire.
