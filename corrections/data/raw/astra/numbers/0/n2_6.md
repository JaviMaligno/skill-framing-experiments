The Starter plan price will rise from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers.

For teams, the key points are an earlier change to sales quotes and extra demand for support. Sales must quote twelve euros in all new proposals from the first of December. Support expects between three hundred and forty and three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January.

Existing Starter customers will keep the nine-euro price until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros a month.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent.

The first action is the December update to new sales proposals, ahead of the January price change.
