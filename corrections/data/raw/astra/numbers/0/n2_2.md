The Starter plan is moving from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers.

For Sales, the change starts earlier: all new proposals must quote twelve euros from the first of December. Existing customers keep the nine euro price until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros a month.

The last price change gives us a useful comparison. Churn afterward was two point three percent, against a baseline of one point eight percent.

Support expects between three hundred and forty and three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January. The next step is for Sales to use the new price in December proposals, ahead of the January change.
