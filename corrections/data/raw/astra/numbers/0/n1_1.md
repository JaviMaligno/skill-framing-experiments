The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had a wrong VAT number.

The main issue to focus on is missing postcodes, rather than VAT numbers.

Wrong VAT numbers affected about half a percent of records. By comparison, 112 records were missing postcodes. That’s 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter.

The audit also found 37 duplicates, or 4.7 percent. Average record completeness fell from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent.

Next up is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
