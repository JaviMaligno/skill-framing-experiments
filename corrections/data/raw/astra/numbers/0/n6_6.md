The team survey results are in, and satisfaction has risen from seven point one last year to seven point four out of ten.

For employees, the results point to three priorities: fewer recurring meetings, clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership has announced next steps on two of those priorities.

Seventy-eight percent of the team responded, or four hundred and twelve out of five hundred and twenty-eight people. Fewer recurring meetings was the top ask, at forty-one percent. Clearer promotion criteria followed at thirty-six percent, and better laptops at twenty-nine percent.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month.
