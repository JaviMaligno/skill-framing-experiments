The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up from last year.

The main asks are about how we spend our time, how promotions work, and the laptops we use. Leadership has announced next steps on two of those topics.

The survey had a response rate of seventy-eight percent, with four hundred and twelve people taking part out of five hundred and twenty-eight. Satisfaction scored seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year.

Fewer recurring meetings topped the requests at forty-one percent. Clearer promotion criteria followed at thirty-six percent, and better laptops at twenty-nine percent.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter and trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month.
