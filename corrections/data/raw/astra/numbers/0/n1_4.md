The third-quarter data-quality audit found just four wrong value-added tax numbers among the 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system.

That’s the main takeaway: the tax-number problem everyone feared barely happened. The bigger issue is missing postcodes.

Four incorrect tax numbers means an error rate of about half a percent. By comparison, 112 records were missing postcodes, or 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. The audit also found 37 duplicates.

Overall record completeness fell from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent. So, while the feared problem was small, there’s still clean-up work ahead.

Next up is the postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
