The old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base this weekend.

The main thing to plan for is a search outage of about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages will redirect for twelve months, so those links will still help you find the content after the move.

The migration covers two thousand nine hundred and fourteen pages across sixty-one spaces, plus twelve point four gigabytes of attachments. Eighty-seven percent of pages will convert automatically.

Another three hundred and seventy-nine pages contain macros and need a check by their owners. Those owners will get an email on Monday. If you own one of those pages, that’s the next step to watch for.
