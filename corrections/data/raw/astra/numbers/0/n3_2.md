Checkout was down on 14 October for two hours and seventeen minutes, from nine forty-one to eleven fifty-eight Central European Time.

The outage affected customers trying to pay, with forty-eight thousand, three hundred and twenty euros in revenue at risk. That figure is revenue at risk, not confirmed lost revenue.

In total, one thousand, two hundred and eighty-four payments failed. Of those, one thousand and seventy-one were retried automatically.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before expiry.

Support still needs to follow up manually on the remaining two hundred and thirteen failed payments.
