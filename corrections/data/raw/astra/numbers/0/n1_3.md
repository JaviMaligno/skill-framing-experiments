The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the feared problem with value added tax, or VAT, numbers barely happened: just four of the 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had a wrong VAT number.

The main takeaway is that the bigger issue is missing postcodes, not VAT numbers.

Wrong VAT numbers affected about half a percent of records. By comparison, 112 records were missing postcodes. That’s 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. The audit also found 37 duplicates, or 4.7 percent of records. Average record completeness fell from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
