The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had a wrong value added tax number.

The main takeaway is that the feared issue was rare, while missing postcodes were more common.

Wrong VAT numbers affected about half a percent of records. There were 37 duplicates, or 4.7 percent. Postcodes were missing from 112 records, or 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Average record completeness also fell, from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
