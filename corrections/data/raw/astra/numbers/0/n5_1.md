The old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base this weekend. For anyone looking up information, the main disruption is search. It will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages will redirect for twelve months.

The migration covers two thousand nine hundred and fourteen pages across sixty-one spaces, plus twelve point four gigabytes of attachments. Eighty-seven percent of pages convert automatically. But three hundred and seventy-nine pages with macros need a check by their owners.

Those owners will get an email on Monday. So if you own wiki pages with macros, watch for that email about the check.
