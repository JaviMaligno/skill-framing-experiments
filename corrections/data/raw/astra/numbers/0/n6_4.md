The team survey results are in, with satisfaction at seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year.

The results highlight what employees want to change, and leadership has announced steps on two of the top requests.

Four hundred and twelve of five hundred and twenty-eight employees responded. That’s a response rate of seventy-eight percent. Fewer recurring meetings topped the list of requests at forty-one percent. Clearer promotion criteria followed at thirty-six percent, and better laptops at twenty-nine percent.

Next, leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month.
