The old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base this weekend, including all two thousand nine hundred and fourteen pages across sixty-one spaces.

For anyone using the wiki, the main interruption is search. It will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages will redirect for twelve months.

The migration also includes twelve point four gigabytes of attachments. Eighty-seven percent of pages convert automatically. But three hundred and seventy-nine pages contain macros and need a check by their owners.

Those owners will get an email on Monday. If you own one of those pages, that email is your prompt to check it after the move.
