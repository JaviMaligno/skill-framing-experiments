The Starter plan price will rise from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers.

For employees, the key points are when to quote the new price and the extra support demand expected in January. Sales must use twelve euros in all new proposals from the first of December, a month before the change takes effect.

Existing customers keep the nine-euro price until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects between three hundred and forty and three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January.
