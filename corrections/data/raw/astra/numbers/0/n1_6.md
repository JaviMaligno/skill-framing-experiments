The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened: just four of the 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had a wrong value added tax number.

The main takeaway is that the bigger issue is missing postcodes, not VAT numbers. Wrong VAT numbers affected about half a percent of records. Postcodes were missing from 112 records, or 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. The audit also found 37 duplicate records, or 4.7 percent.

Average record completeness fell to 93.6 percent, from 95.1 percent in the second quarter.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
