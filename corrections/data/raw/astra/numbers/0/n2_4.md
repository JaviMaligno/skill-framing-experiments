The Starter plan price will rise from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers.

The key dates differ for sales and existing customers, so keep those distinctions clear in customer conversations. Sales must quote twelve euros in all new proposals from the first of December. Existing customers keep their nine-euro price until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects between three hundred and forty and three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January.

The next step is the December change to sales proposals, ahead of the January price increase.
