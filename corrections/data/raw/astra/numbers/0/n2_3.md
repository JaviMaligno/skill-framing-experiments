The Starter plan price rises from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers.

The key point for teams is that sales proposals need to change before the new price takes effect. Sales must quote twelve euros in all new proposals from the first of December.

Existing customers keep the nine-euro price until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros a month.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects between three hundred and forty and three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January.

The first deadline is the December change to sales quotes.
