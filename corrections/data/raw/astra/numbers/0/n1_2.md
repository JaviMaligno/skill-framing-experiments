The third-quarter data-quality audit found just four wrong value added tax numbers among the seven hundred and ninety-one customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system.

The main takeaway is reassuring: the tax-number problem everyone feared barely happened. It affected about half of one percent of migrated records.

Missing postcodes were the bigger issue. They affected one hundred and twelve records, or fourteen point two percent, up from nine point eight percent in the second quarter. There were also thirty-seven duplicate records, or four point seven percent.

Average record completeness fell from ninety-five point one percent to ninety-three point six percent.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
