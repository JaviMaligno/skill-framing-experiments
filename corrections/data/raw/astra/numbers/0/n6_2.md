The team survey results are in, with satisfaction at seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year.

If you asked for fewer meetings or clearer promotion criteria, leadership has announced steps on both.

Four hundred and twelve of our five hundred and twenty-eight employees responded, a response rate of seventy-eight percent. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, at forty-one percent. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at thirty-six percent, followed by better laptops, at twenty-nine percent.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month. There’s no laptop action announced in this update.
