Facilities has replaced the office plants on floors two, three and five, with forty-six plants across fourteen species.

The main thing for everyone on those floors to know is that you don’t need to water them. The supplier will handle watering, so please don’t water the plants yourself.

The supplier will visit to water them every seven to ten days, under an eighteen-month contract. There are three plants per meeting room as part of the replacement.

Floor four is still waiting for its plants. Those will arrive once the refurbishment ends. For now, the replacement covers floors two, three and five, with watering left to the supplier.
