The team survey results are in, with satisfaction at seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year. If you want fewer meetings or clearer promotion criteria, leadership has announced steps on both. The response rate was seventy-eight percent, with four hundred and twelve of five hundred and twenty-eight people taking part. Fewer recurring meetings topped the requests at forty-one percent. Clearer promotion criteria followed at thirty-six percent, and better laptops at twenty-nine percent. Next, leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month. It is a trial, rather than a permanent change.
