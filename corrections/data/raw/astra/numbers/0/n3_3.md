Checkout was down on the fourteenth of October for two hours and seventeen minutes, leaving more than a thousand payments unsuccessful.

The immediate priority is customer follow-up. The support team needs to follow up manually on two hundred and thirteen failed payments. Revenue at risk totals forty-eight thousand, three hundred and twenty euros.

The outage ran from nine forty-one to eleven fifty-eight in the morning, Central European Time. In total, one thousand, two hundred and eighty-four payments failed. Of those, one thousand and seventy-one were retried automatically.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire.
