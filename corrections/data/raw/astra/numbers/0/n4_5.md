Facilities has replaced the office plants on floors two, three and five, with forty-six plants across fourteen species.

The main thing for everyone to know is that you don’t need to water them. The supplier will take care of that, so please don’t water the plants yourself.

There are three plants per meeting room. The supplier’s contract runs for eighteen months, and watering is scheduled every seven to ten days.

If you’re on floor four, your plants are still to come. That floor will get its plants once the refurbishment ends. For now, the replacements cover floors two, three and five.
