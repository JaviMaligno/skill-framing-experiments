Checkout was down on the fourteenth of October for two hours and seventeen minutes, from nine forty-one to eleven fifty-eight Central European Time.

The impact was failed payments and revenue at risk, with some payments still needing attention from support.

In total, one thousand two hundred and eighty-four payments failed. Of those, one thousand and seventy-one were retried automatically. The remaining two hundred and thirteen need manual follow-up by the support team. Revenue at risk was forty-eight thousand three hundred and twenty euros.

The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before expiry. The outstanding work is support’s manual follow-up on those remaining payments.
