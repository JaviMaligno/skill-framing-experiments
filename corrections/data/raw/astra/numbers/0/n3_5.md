Checkout was down on October fourteenth for two hours and seventeen minutes, from nine forty-one to eleven fifty-eight, Central European Time.

The outage put forty-eight thousand, three hundred and twenty euros in revenue at risk. That is revenue at risk, not a confirmed loss. For the support team, there is still manual follow-up to do.

There were one thousand, two hundred and eighty-four failed payments. Of those, one thousand and seventy-one were retried automatically. The remaining two hundred and thirteen need support follow-up.

The root cause was an expired security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. The outstanding action is manual follow-up on those remaining payments.
