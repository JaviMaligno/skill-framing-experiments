Checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes on October fourteenth after a security certificate expired on the payment proxy.

The outage put forty-eight thousand, three hundred and twenty euros in revenue at risk. Support now needs to follow up manually on two hundred and thirteen failed payments.

The incident ran from nine forty-one to eleven fifty-eight in the morning, Central European Time. In total, one thousand, two hundred and eighty-four payments failed. Of those, one thousand and seventy-one were retried automatically.

The expired certificate was a Transport Layer Security certificate. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before expiry.
