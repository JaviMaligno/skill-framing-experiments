Facilities has replaced the office plants on floors two, three and five.

The main thing for everyone on those floors to remember is simple: please do not water the plants yourself. The supplier will handle watering every seven to ten days, so you can leave that job to them.

There are forty-six replacement plants in total, covering fourteen species. Each meeting room has three plants. The supplier’s contract runs for eighteen months.

Floor four is still waiting for its plants. Those will arrive once the refurbishment ends. For now, the replacement covers floors two, three and five, with watering left to the supplier.
