The latest data-quality audit shows that the feared problem with wrong VAT numbers barely materialised during the move to the new customer relationship management system. That’s reassuring for anyone relying on the migrated customer records, though other data-quality issues still need attention. Just four records had a wrong VAT number. The bigger concern is missing postcodes, which became more common than in the previous quarter. The audit also found duplicate records, and overall record completeness slipped. So the main takeaway is that the VAT concern was limited, but the clean-up work is not finished. Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint in November.
