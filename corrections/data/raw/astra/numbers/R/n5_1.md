The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend. The main thing to plan around is search: it will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. If you need to find something in the wiki that day, keep that interruption in mind.

On Monday, owners whose pages need checking will get an email. If you receive one, your next step is to check those pages.
