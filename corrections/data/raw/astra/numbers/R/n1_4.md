The latest data quality audit found that the feared VAT number problem barely happened during the move to the new customer relationship management system.

That means the main concern about VAT numbers was not borne out by the audit. Only about half a percent of migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number.

The audit did find other problems. Some records were duplicates, and missing postcodes were more common than in the previous quarter. Average record completeness also fell. So the reassuring VAT result does not mean the clean-up is finished.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint in November, focused on those missing postcodes.
