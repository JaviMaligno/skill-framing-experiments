The latest data-quality audit shows that the feared problem with wrong VAT numbers barely happened during the move to the new customer relationship management system.

That means the main concern going into the migration was not the main issue the audit found. Wrong VAT numbers affected only about half a percent of migrated customer records.

The bigger gap was missing postcodes, which became more common than in the previous quarter. Overall record completeness also fell, and the audit found duplicate records.

So the reassuring news on VAT comes with some unfinished clean-up work. Next up is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting in early November.
