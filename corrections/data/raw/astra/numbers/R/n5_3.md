The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend.

The main thing to plan around is search: it will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. If you rely on the wiki to find information, that’s the disruption to keep in mind during the move.

Most pages will convert automatically. But pages that use macros need a check by their owners. So the move won’t require everyone to review their pages, but some owners will have a follow-up task.

Those owners will get an email on Monday. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base for a limited period after the move.
