The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up from last year. The feedback highlights changes employees want in their working day and their career development. The main requests were fewer recurring meetings, clearer promotion criteria and better laptops, with fewer meetings topping the list.

The response rate was seventy-eight percent, so these results reflect feedback from a large majority of the team. Leadership has announced action on meetings and promotion criteria. A trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month. Promotion criteria will be published in the first quarter of next year. Better laptops were also a top request, but the update does not include a next step on that.
