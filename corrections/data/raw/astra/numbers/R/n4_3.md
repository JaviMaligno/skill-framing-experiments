Facilities has replaced the office plants on the floors covered by the current rollout. The main thing for everyone to know is that you do not need to water them. The supplier will take care of watering, so please leave that job to them rather than watering the plants yourself. The replacement includes a mix of plant species, with plants in meeting rooms as well as elsewhere on the completed floors. Watering is part of the supplier’s scheduled care for the plants. Next up is floor four, which will get its plants once the refurbishment there has come to an end.
