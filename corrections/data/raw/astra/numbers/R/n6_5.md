The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up compared with last year. For employees, the main news is that leadership is acting on requests about meetings and promotion criteria. Fewer recurring meetings topped the list of asks. Clearer promotion criteria came next, followed by better laptops. Those requests point to what employees want changed about their working experience, alongside the improvement in satisfaction. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays from next month. So the next step on meetings is a trial, while the promotion criteria have a planned publication window.
