Checkout went down after a security certificate on the payment proxy expired. The outage prevented customers from completing payments and put revenue at risk. Most failed payments were retried automatically, but 213 still need manual follow-up from the support team. That is the remaining customer follow-up work identified in the incident review. The automatic retries do not cover every failed payment, so support still has a role in resolving the incident’s impact. The cause has also been addressed: certificates now renew automatically before they expire. That change is intended to prevent another checkout outage caused by an expired certificate.
