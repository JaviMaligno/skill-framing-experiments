Checkout went down on October fourteenth because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired.

The outage prevented customers from completing payments and put revenue at risk. The remaining work now sits with support, which needs to follow up manually on the payments that were not retried automatically.

Most failed payments were retried automatically. That leaves just over two hundred needing manual follow-up. Those automatic retries should not be confused with confirmed successful payments; the review does not report their final outcome.

To address the cause, certificates now renew automatically ahead of expiry. The next step is for support to follow up on the remaining payments.
