The Starter plan price is rising for new customers in January, while existing customers keep their current price through June.

For Sales and Support, this means updating proposals before the change takes effect and preparing for more customer questions. Pro pricing is unchanged.

The new Starter price will be twelve euros a month. After the last price change, churn was above baseline. Support also expects extra tickets in January, so the impact goes beyond updating the price itself.

Sales must use the new price in all new proposals from the start of December. The price then takes effect for new customers at the start of January.
