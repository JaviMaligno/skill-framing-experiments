The latest data quality audit found that the feared problem with wrong VAT numbers barely happened during the move to the new customer relationship management system.

For everyone concerned about VAT errors, that is reassuring news. But the audit also shows that other parts of record quality still need attention.

Only four migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number. The bigger issue was missing postcodes, which became more common than in the previous quarter. Average record completeness also fell, and the audit found duplicate records.

The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint starting in early November. So the takeaway is reassuring on VAT numbers, with postcode gaps now the focus for clean-up.
