The Starter plan’s monthly price is rising to twelve euros for new customers in January.

For Sales, the change affects proposals before it affects billing. All new proposals must quote the new price from the start of December. Existing Starter customers keep their current price through the end of June. The Pro price is unchanged.

The last price change was followed by churn above the baseline. That’s important context for this increase, though it does not tell us what will happen this time.

Support expects extra tickets in January. The next step is for Sales to use the new price in December proposals, ahead of the January change for new customers.
