The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up from last year. The findings highlight what employees want to change about everyday work and career progression. Leadership has announced action on meetings and promotion criteria.

The survey had a response rate of seventy-eight percent. The biggest request was for fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Satisfaction improved, but those requests show where employees still want changes.

Next, leadership will trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. It will also publish promotion criteria in the first quarter of next year. There is no laptop action announced in this update.
