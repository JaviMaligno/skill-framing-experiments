Checkout went down after a security certificate expired on the payment proxy, causing payments to fail.

The immediate priority is following up with customers whose payments still need attention. Support has 213 failed payments to follow up on manually. Most of the failed payments were retried automatically, but that does not mean those retries were confirmed as successful. The outage also put revenue at risk.

The review identified the expired certificate as the root cause. Certificates now renew automatically ahead of their expiry date. That change addresses the cause of this incident. The remaining customer follow-up sits with the support team.
