The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up compared with last year.

The feedback points to changes that could affect your working week and how you understand promotion opportunities. The main requests were fewer recurring meetings, clearer promotion criteria and better laptops, in that order.

The response rate was seventy-eight percent, so the results reflect feedback from a large majority of the team.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter of the year. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays from next month. That trial addresses the most common request in the survey: fewer recurring meetings. The update does not announce any action on laptops.
