The Starter plan price is rising for new customers at the start of January, while existing customers keep their current price through the end of June.

For sales, the immediate change comes earlier: all new proposals must quote twelve euros a month from the start of December. That means proposals need to reflect the new price before it takes effect.

Pro pricing is unchanged. After the last price change, customer churn was above baseline. Support also expects extra tickets in January, so this change has implications beyond sales.

The next step is for sales to use the new Starter price in proposals, with support anticipating more customer questions when the change takes effect.
