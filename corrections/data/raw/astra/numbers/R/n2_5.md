The Starter plan price is rising to twelve euros a month for new customers from the start of January.

For employees, the key point is that sales proposals need to change before the new price takes effect. Existing customers will keep their current price through the end of June. The Pro plan price is unchanged.

The last price change saw customer cancellations rise above the baseline. Support expects extra tickets in January, so the change also means more work for that team.

Next, sales must use the new Starter price in all new proposals from the start of December. That quoting deadline comes ahead of the January price change.
