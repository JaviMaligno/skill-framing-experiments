The team survey results are in, and satisfaction has improved since last year. For employees, the practical news is that leadership is acting on requests about meetings and career progression. The response rate was seventy-eight percent. Among the top requests, fewer recurring meetings came first, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Those requests show where employees want changes, alongside the improvement in overall satisfaction. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays from next month. Better laptops were another top request, but the survey update does not include a next step on that issue.
