The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened in the customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system.

The takeaway is reassuring on VAT numbers, but there is still work to do on record quality. Only about half a percent of migrated records had a wrong VAT number.

Missing postcodes were a bigger issue, and they became more common than in the previous quarter. Average record completeness also fell. The audit found duplicate records too.

Next comes the postcode clean-up sprint in November. That puts the focus on an area where the audit showed quality getting worse.
