The Starter plan’s monthly price is rising to twelve euros for new customers, while existing customers keep their current price for a limited time.

For sales, the key point is that new proposals must use the higher price before the increase takes effect. Support also needs to prepare for more tickets.

The Pro plan’s price is not changing. After the last price change, churn was above baseline, so that is important context for this increase.

Next, sales must update pricing in all new proposals. The new customer price takes effect after that, with the existing customer price held steady until the transition period ends.
