The latest data-quality audit shows that the feared problem with wrong VAT numbers barely happened during the move to the new customer relationship management system.

For anyone worried about that part of the migration, this is reassuring news. Wrong VAT numbers affected only about half a percent of migrated customer records.

The audit did find other issues. Some records were duplicated, and missing postcodes became more common than in the previous quarter. Average record completeness also fell. So the reassuring VAT result does not mean the data clean-up is finished.

Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint in early November, targeting the gap that worsened this quarter.
