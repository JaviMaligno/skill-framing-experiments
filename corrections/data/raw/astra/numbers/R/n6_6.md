The team survey results are in, and leadership is responding with clearer promotion criteria and a trial of meeting-free Wednesdays.

The changes address the biggest requests from colleagues: fewer recurring meetings and a clearer understanding of what it takes to earn a promotion. Better laptops were also among the top asks.

The survey had a response rate of about eighty percent, and satisfaction was up slightly compared with last year. Fewer recurring meetings topped the list of requests, followed by clearer promotion criteria, then better laptops.

Next, leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. The trial of meeting-free Wednesdays starts next month.
