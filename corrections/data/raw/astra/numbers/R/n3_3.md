Checkout went down after a security certificate on the payment proxy expired, preventing customers from completing payments.

The immediate priority is support follow-up. Most failed payments were retried automatically, but 213 still need manual follow-up by the support team. The outage also put revenue at risk. That is revenue at risk, not a confirmed loss.

The review identified the expired certificate as the root cause. Certificates now renew automatically ahead of their expiry date, rather than being allowed to expire.

The next step is for support to follow up on the payments that were not retried automatically. Automatic certificate renewal is already in place.
