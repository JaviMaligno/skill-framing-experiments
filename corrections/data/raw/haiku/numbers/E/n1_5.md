The third quarter data check is in, and the tax problem everyone feared barely happened. Of 791 customer records moved to our new customer relationship management system, only 4 had a wrong value added tax number. That is about half a percent. The bigger issue is missing postcodes. About one in seven records now lacks one, up from about one in ten last quarter. Overall record completeness also slipped, to about 94 percent from 95. Duplicates came to under 5 percent. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November.
