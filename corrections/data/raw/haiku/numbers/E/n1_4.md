The third-quarter data audit of our new customer relationship management system, or CRM, is finished. The VAT problem everyone feared barely happened. Only four out of 791 migrated customer records had a wrong value added tax, or VAT, number. That is the good news. The bigger concern is postcodes. Missing postcodes rose to about 14 percent, up from about 10 percent last quarter. Duplicates came in at about 5 percent. Average record completeness slipped from about 95 percent to about 94 percent. Those gaps are what we need to fix. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on the 3rd of November.
