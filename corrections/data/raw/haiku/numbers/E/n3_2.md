Checkout went down for just over two hours on 14 October. The outage ran from just before ten in the morning until just before noon, Central European Time. That meant customers could not pay, and about 48,000 euros in revenue was at risk.

About 1,300 payments failed. More than 1,000 retried automatically. The rest, about 200, need manual follow-up from our support team.

The cause was an expired transport layer security certificate on our payment proxy. That is the credential that keeps payment traffic encrypted.

Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. This removes the risk of a missed renewal.
