On 14 October, checkout was down for just over two hours. That meant customers could not pay from about 9:40 until just before noon, Central European Time. Around 1,300 payments failed. About 1,100 of them retried automatically. The other 200 or so need manual follow-up from our support team. About 48,000 euros in revenue was at risk. The root cause was an expired TLS certificate on our payment proxy. TLS, short for Transport Layer Security, is the protocol that secures connections like this one. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That change removes this particular failure point, so it should not come back the same way.
