On 14 October, our checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. Customers trying to pay during that window were affected. For them, it meant orders that did not go through. For the company, it meant revenue at risk. The outage ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. About 1,300 payments failed. Most were retried automatically. The other 213 need manual follow-up from our support team. Revenue at risk was about 48,000 euros. The root cause was an expired TLS certificate on our payment proxy. TLS stands for Transport Layer Security, the encryption that keeps web connections secure. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire. This change addresses that cause.
