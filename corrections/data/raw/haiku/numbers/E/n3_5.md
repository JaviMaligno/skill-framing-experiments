Here is a short review of the checkout outage on October 14. Checkout was down for about two hours. Customers could not pay, and about 48,000 euros in revenue was put at risk. Checkout is how customers pay, so an outage stops sales. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. That is the security credential that protects connections between systems. About 1,300 payments failed. Roughly 1,070 were retried automatically. The other 210 or so need manual follow-up from the support team. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire, so an expiry should not catch us by surprise.
