The team survey results are in, and most of you took part. Nearly four in five people responded. Overall satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up a little from last year. That matters because it shows us what to change. The top request is fewer recurring meetings, from about four in ten people. Next, about a third want clearer promotion criteria. Around three in ten asked for better laptops. Leadership is acting on two of these. They will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, they will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays. Thank you to everyone who answered.
