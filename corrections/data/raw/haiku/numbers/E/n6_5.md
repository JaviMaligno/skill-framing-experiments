The team survey results are in, and satisfaction has gone up. Employees rated it 7.4 out of 10, up from last year.

That matters because it shows leadership what to fix. Nearly four in five of you took part, so the results are a fair picture of the team.

The top request was fewer recurring meetings. About two in five people asked for that. Clearer promotion criteria came next, with over a third. Better laptops followed, from nearly three in ten.

Here is what happens next. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. They are also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays, starting next month.
