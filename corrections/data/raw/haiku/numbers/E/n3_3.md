On 14 October, checkout went down for about two hours. More than a thousand failed payments were retried automatically. About two hundred still need manual follow-up, and the support team will handle those. Revenue at risk is about 48,000 euros. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on our payment proxy. That is the digital credential that keeps payment data secure in transit. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire, so this particular cause is covered.
