The Starter plan is going up in price. From 1 January, new customers will pay 12 euros a month, up from 9. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan does not change.

This matters to more than the people who set prices. After the last price change, the churn rate, meaning the share of customers who cancel, reached 2.3 percent. Normally it sits at 1.8 percent. Support expects between 340 and 380 extra tickets in January, so expect a busy month.

Here is what happens next. From 1 December, the sales team must quote 12 euros in every new proposal.
