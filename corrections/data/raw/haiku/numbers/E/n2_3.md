The Starter plan is getting more expensive. Its monthly price rises from 9 euros to 12 euros on 1 January. That rise applies only to new customers. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

Since the last price change, churn has risen to 2.3 percent. Churn means the share of customers who leave. The usual level is 1.8 percent.

Support expects more than 300 extra tickets in January. Sales has one new rule. From 1 December, every new proposal must quote 12 euros for Starter. Both teams need to prepare for these changes.
