Here is the pricing update. Starter is going up in price. From 1 January, new Starter customers will pay 12 euros a month. Existing customers keep paying 9 until 30 June. Pro stays the same at 29 euros a month.

Why does this matter? Our last price change pushed churn up. Churn means customers who cancel. It is now just over 2 percent. Before the change, it was under 2 percent.

Support expects roughly 350 extra tickets in January. Please plan for a busy month.

For sales, there is one rule to remember. From 1 December, every new proposal must quote 12 euros for Starter.
