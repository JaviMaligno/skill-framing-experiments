Our latest team survey is in, and satisfaction is up. More than 400 people took part, about four in five of everyone asked. Satisfaction reached 7.4 out of 10, up slightly from last year. These answers matter because they show what is weighing on people's working weeks. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, which about four in ten people asked for. Next came clearer promotion criteria, which more than a third of people want. Nearly three in ten asked for better laptops. Leadership is responding to the first two. They will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. And from next month, they will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. Thanks to everyone who took the time to answer.
