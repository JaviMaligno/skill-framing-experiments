The Starter plan is going up from nine euros to twelve euros a month. The new price starts on the first of January, for new customers only. Existing customers keep paying nine euros until the end of June. The Pro plan stays at twenty-nine euros.

This matters because churn, the share of customers who leave, rose to two point three percent after the last price change. Before that, it was under two percent. The support team expects between 340 and 380 extra tickets in January.

Sales has a new rule. From the first of December, every new proposal must quote twelve euros for Starter.

So if you talk to customers, know the new price, and expect a busier support queue in January.
