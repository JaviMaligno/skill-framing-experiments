The Starter plan is going up in price. It moves from 9 euros to 12 euros a month on the first of January. That applies to new customers only. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until the thirtieth of June. Pro stays at 29 euros.

Why does this matter? Churn, meaning customers who cancel, was just over 2 percent after the last price change. The usual baseline is just under 2 percent. Support expects between 340 and 380 extra tickets in January.

For sales, the rule is simple. From the first of December, every new proposal must quote 12 euros for Starter.
