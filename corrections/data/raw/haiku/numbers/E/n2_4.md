Our Starter plan is going up in price. For new customers, it moves from nine euros to twelve euros a month on the first of January. Existing customers keep paying nine euros until the thirtieth of June. The Pro plan stays at twenty-nine euros.

Why does this matter? Churn, which is the share of customers who cancel, reached 2.3 percent after the last price change. Normally it sits at 1.8 percent. Support expects between 340 and 380 extra tickets in January, so expect a busier month.

Sales has one new rule. From the first of December, every new proposal must quote twelve euros for Starter.
