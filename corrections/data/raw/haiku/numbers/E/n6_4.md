Our team survey results are in, and the headline is good news. About four in five people responded, and satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from last year. That matters because it shows what would make work better for all of us. The top request, from about four in ten respondents, is fewer recurring meetings. More than a third asked for clearer promotion criteria, and about three in ten want better laptops. Leadership has responded on two fronts. Promotion criteria will be published in the first quarter. And starting next month, leadership is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays.
