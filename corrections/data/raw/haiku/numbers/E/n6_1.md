Our latest team survey is in, and it shows satisfaction edging up. More than three in four people responded, which gives us a solid picture. Overall satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, from 7.1 last year. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, which about four in ten people asked for. Clearer promotion criteria came next, then better laptops. Leadership is acting on two of these. Promotion criteria will be published in the first quarter. Meeting-free Wednesdays start next month as a trial. If meetings or promotion are on your mind, these are the changes to watch for. Thanks to everyone who took part.
