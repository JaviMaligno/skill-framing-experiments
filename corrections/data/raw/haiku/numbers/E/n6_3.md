The results of our team survey are in. Just under eight in ten of you responded. Satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. This matters because it shows where you want change. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, from about four in ten people. Over a third asked for clearer promotion criteria. Nearly three in ten want better laptops. Here is what happens next. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Starting next month, they are trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. Thanks to everyone who took part.
