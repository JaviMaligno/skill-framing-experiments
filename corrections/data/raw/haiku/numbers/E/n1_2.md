Good news from our third quarter data check. The problem we feared most, wrong tax numbers, barely happened. Only 4 of 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship system had a wrong value added tax number. That is good news for anyone who relies on this customer data.

The weak spot is postcodes. About one in seven records now has no postcode. That is up from about one in ten in the second quarter. Average record completeness also slipped a little, and duplicates still need cleaning up.

What happens next: a postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November.
