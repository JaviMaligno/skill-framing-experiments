The tax number problem everyone feared barely happened. Of 791 customer records moved to our new CRM, only 4 had a wrong value added tax number. That is about half of one percent. This matters because it was the risk people worried about most.

The third quarter audit did find other problems. Duplicate records made up about 5 percent. Missing postcodes rose to about 14 percent, up from about 10 percent in the second quarter. Average record completeness slipped from 95 percent to 94 percent.

So the new system is in better shape than many expected, but it still needs cleaning up. Postcodes come first. A clean-up sprint starts on the 3rd of November.
