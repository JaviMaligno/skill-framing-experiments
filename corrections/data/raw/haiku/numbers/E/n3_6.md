On 14 October, checkout went down for just over two hours. It ran from about 9:40 in the morning until just before noon, Central European Time. That hit payments hard. Each failed payment is a sale that may not come back. About 1,300 payments failed. Over 1,000 of them retried automatically. Around 200 still need manual follow-up from our support team. The revenue at risk is about 48,000 euros. The cause was an expired transport layer security certificate on our payment proxy. The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire.
