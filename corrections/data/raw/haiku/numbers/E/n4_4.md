Facilities has replaced the plants on floors two, three and five. If you work on one of those floors, you will see the difference. There are about fifty plants in total, made up of fourteen different types. Each meeting room now has three of them. The supplier is under an eighteen-month contract, and it waters the plants every seven to ten days. So please do not water them yourself. The supplier does that job, so there is nothing you need to do. Floor four will get its plants once the refurbishment ends. That is the next change to watch for.
