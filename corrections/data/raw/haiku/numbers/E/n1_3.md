The problem everyone feared, wrong value added tax numbers, barely happened. When we moved customer records to our new customer relationship management system, only four out of 791 had a wrong VAT number. That is about half of one percent. A wrong number can cause invoicing and compliance problems, so this news is good.

Duplicates were low too, at under five percent.

The bigger issue is postcodes. About 14 percent of records are now missing one, up from about 10 percent last quarter. Overall record completeness also slipped, from about 95 percent to about 94 percent.

Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
