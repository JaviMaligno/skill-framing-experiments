Our third-quarter data check is in, and the problem everyone feared barely happened. We moved customer records to the new CRM, which is our customer relationship management system. Only four of 791 records had a wrong VAT number. VAT is value added tax. That is about half of one percent.

Duplicates were about 5 percent of records.

The weak spot is postcodes. About 14 percent of records are missing one, up from about 10 percent in the second quarter. Average completeness fell a little, to about 94 percent from 95.

The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on 3 November.
