On 14 October, checkout was down for just over two hours. It ran from shortly before 10 in the morning until just before noon, Central European Time.

That matters because customers could not pay, and about 48,000 euros in revenue was at risk.

The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security, or TLS, certificate on the payment proxy. About 1,300 payments failed. More than 1,000 of them were retried automatically. The remaining 200 or so need manual follow-up from the support team, so staff will be working through them by hand.

Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire.
