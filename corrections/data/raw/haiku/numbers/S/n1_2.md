The third-quarter data audit is in, and the problem everyone feared barely happened. Only four of nearly 800 customer records moved to our new customer relationship management system had a wrong value added tax, or VAT, number. That is about half of one percent.

So the migration mostly went well. The real gap is missing postcodes. About 14 percent of records now have none, up from about 10 percent in the second quarter. A small number of records, about 5 percent, are duplicates. Average record completeness also dipped, from about 95 percent to about 94 percent.

A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
