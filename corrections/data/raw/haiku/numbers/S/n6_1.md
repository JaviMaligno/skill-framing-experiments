Our latest team survey is in, and people are slightly happier at work than last year. About four in five people responded. Satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from last year.

Why does this matter? Because it shows leadership exactly what to fix. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, asked for by about two in five people. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at more than a third. Better laptops followed, at about three in ten.

Leadership has responded in two ways. Promotion criteria will be published in the first quarter. And starting next month, they will trial meeting-free Wednesdays.

If your calendar is packed, that second change is one to watch.
