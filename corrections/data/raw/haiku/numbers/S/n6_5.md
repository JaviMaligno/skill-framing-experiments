Our team survey results are in, and the main story is that satisfaction has gone up. People rated their experience 7.4 out of 10, up from last year. Nearly four in five people responded, so these results reflect most of us. The top requests were fewer recurring meetings, clearer promotion criteria, and better laptops. If you have ever felt buried in calendar invites, this one is for you. Leadership says it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Leadership is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays, starting next month. That is the update for now. Thank you to everyone who took the time to respond.
