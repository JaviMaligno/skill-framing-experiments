The Starter plan is going up in price. From 1 January, new customers will pay 12 euros a month, up from 9 euros. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

Why does this matter? After the last price change, churn, meaning customers who cancel, rose by half a point. It is now just over two percent. Support expects between 340 and 380 extra tickets in January, so they will need to plan for that workload.

Sales has an action to take now. From 1 December, every new proposal must quote the 12 euro price.
