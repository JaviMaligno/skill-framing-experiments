The price of our Starter plan is going up. For new customers, it rises from 9 euros to 12 euros a month on 1 January. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

Why does this matter? A price change can push some customers to leave. Churn, the share of customers who cancel, has risen to just over two percent since the last price change. The usual level is under two percent.

Support expects about 350 extra tickets in January.

Sales has one change to make. From 1 December, every new proposal must quote 12 euros for Starter.
