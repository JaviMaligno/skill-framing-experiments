Over four hundred of our colleagues answered this year's team survey, and leadership is responding. Nearly eight in ten people took part. Overall satisfaction rose slightly from last year. The biggest request was fewer recurring meetings, which about four in ten people asked for. Clearer promotion criteria came next. Leadership will publish those criteria in the first quarter of next year. Leadership is also trying meeting-free Wednesdays, starting next month. So if you have ever wished for a day without back-to-back calls, this one is for you. Watch your calendar and see how it goes.
