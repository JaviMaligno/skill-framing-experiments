The VAT problem everyone feared barely happened. VAT means value added tax. In our third quarter data audit, only 4 of the 791 customer records moved to the new CRM had a wrong VAT number. That is about half a percent. Duplicates were about 5 percent, or 37 records. The bigger issue is postcodes. About 14 percent of records are now missing a postcode, up from about 10 percent in the second quarter. Average record completeness also dipped, from 95 percent to 94. So the VAT fear was largely unfounded, but postcodes need fixing. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November. That is the next step.
