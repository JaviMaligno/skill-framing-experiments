On 14 October, checkout went down for just over two hours. The outage ran from about 9:40 in the morning until just before noon, Central European Time. It put about 48,000 euros of revenue at risk. Around 1,300 payments failed. Most were retried automatically. About 200 still need manual follow-up from the support team, so that work is still open. The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS certificate, on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. If you work on checkout, payments or support, this is the fix to know about.
