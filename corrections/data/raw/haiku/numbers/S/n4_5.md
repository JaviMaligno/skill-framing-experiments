Facilities has replaced the office plants on the second, third and fifth floors. There are about fifty plants in total, made up of fourteen species. This matters because the plants are now part of how these floors look and feel every day. The supplier has a contract for eighteen months, and they handle all the watering. Every meeting room now has three plants. The supplier waters them roughly every week to ten days, so please don't water them yourself. Floor four will get its plants once the refurbishment ends. So if you work on that floor, expect new greenery after the work is finished.
