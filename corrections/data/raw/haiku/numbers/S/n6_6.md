Our latest team survey is in, and leadership is making two changes. Nearly four in five of us responded, and satisfaction edged up to 7.4 out of 10. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, from about four in ten people. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at more than a third. Better laptops followed, at under a third. In the first quarter, leadership will publish promotion criteria. From next month, they will also try out meeting-free Wednesdays. If meetings or promotion have been on your mind, watch for both.
