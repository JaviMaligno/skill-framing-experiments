Here is a quick update on our data check of the new customer relationship management system. The problem everyone feared was wrong value added tax numbers, and it barely happened. Only four of 791 migrated customer records had one. That means the migration went far better than many of us expected. The bigger issue is postcodes. About one in seven records is now missing a postcode, up from about one in ten last quarter. Duplicates came in under five percent. Overall record completeness dipped slightly. If you work with customer addresses, this is where our attention needs to go. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
