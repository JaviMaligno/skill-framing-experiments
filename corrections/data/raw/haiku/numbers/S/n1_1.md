This is the third-quarter data check on our new customer system. The main point is that the tax problem everyone feared barely happened. Of nearly 800 customer records moved over, only four had a wrong value added tax number. That is well under one percent.

But other gaps grew. About five percent of records are duplicates. About one in seven is missing a postcode, up from about one in ten in the second quarter. Average completeness slipped too, from about 95 percent to about 94.

What happens next: a postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
