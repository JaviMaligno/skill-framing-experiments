On October 14, checkout was down for just over two hours. It ran from 9:41 until 11:58 Central European Time. Checkout is the step where customers pay for their orders.

This matters because customers could not complete their payments during that window. Roughly 1,300 payments failed.

About 1,100 of them were retried automatically. The rest need manual follow-up from the support team.

The revenue at risk was about 48,000 euros.

The root cause of the outage was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy.

Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
