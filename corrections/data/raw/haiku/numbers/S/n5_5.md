The company wiki moves to our new knowledge base this weekend. If you use the wiki, this affects you. About 2,900 pages are moving, spread across more than 60 spaces, along with about 12 gigabytes of attachments. Search will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Nearly nine in ten pages will convert automatically. About 380 pages use special macros, so their owners need to check them. Those owners will get an email on Monday. Links to old pages will keep working for a year, because they redirect to the new location. If you own a wiki page, watch for that email and check it when it arrives.
