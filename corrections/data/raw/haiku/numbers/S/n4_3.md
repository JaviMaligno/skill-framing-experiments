Facilities has replaced the plants on floors two, three and five with new ones. If you work on those floors, you will see fresh greenery at your desk and in the meeting rooms. The change is small, but you will notice it when you walk in.

The new plants come from a supplier under an eighteen-month contract. The supplier waters them every seven to ten days, so you do not need to take care of them. Each meeting room has three plants.

Please do not water them yourself.

Floor four will get its plants once the refurbishment ends. If you are on that floor, your turn is still to come.
