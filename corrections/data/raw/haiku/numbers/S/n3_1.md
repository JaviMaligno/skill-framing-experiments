Here is what happened, and what has changed. On October fourteenth, checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. It went down at about ten to ten in the morning and came back just before noon, Central European Time. During that time, customers could not complete purchases. About 1,300 payments failed. Roughly 1,100 of them were retried automatically. That leaves about 200 that the support team has to follow up on by hand. The revenue at risk is about 48,000 euros. The root cause was an expired certificate for Transport Layer Security, known as TLS, on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. This should prevent a repeat.
