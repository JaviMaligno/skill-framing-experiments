The third-quarter data-quality audit is done, and the problem everyone feared barely happened. We moved 791 customer records into the new customer relationship management system. Only four had a wrong VAT number, which means value added tax. That is about half a percent.

The bigger issue is postcodes. About 14 percent of records are now missing a postcode, up from about 10 percent in the second quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, a little. Duplicates are around 5 percent of records.

What happens next: a postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
