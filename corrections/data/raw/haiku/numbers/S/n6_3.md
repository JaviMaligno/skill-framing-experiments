The results of our latest team survey are in, and they point to some changes coming soon. About four in five people responded, which gives us a clear picture. Satisfaction rose slightly, to just over seven out of ten. That matters because it tells us where we should focus next. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, and over four in ten people asked for it. Next came clearer promotion criteria, which about a third of people asked for. Nearly three in ten asked for better laptops. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter of next year. And starting next month, they will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. Thank you to everyone who responded. Your answers are shaping what happens next.
