Here is the third-quarter data check on our new customer relationship management system, or CRM. We moved about 800 customer records across. The big fear was wrong value added tax numbers, or VAT. That problem barely happened. Only four records out of 791 had a wrong VAT number. This matters because the risk we worried about most turned out very small. The bigger issue is missing postcodes. About one record in seven now has none. Last quarter it was about one in ten. Duplicates reached 37 records, around five percent. Overall record completeness also slipped a little. What happens next? A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
