Starter is going up in price, and that affects every team. From 1 January, new customers will pay 12 euros a month for the Starter plan, up from 9. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

After the last price change, customer churn, meaning customers leaving, reached 2.3 percent. The baseline before that was 1.8 percent.

Support expects between 340 and 380 extra tickets in January, so the team is planning for a busy month.

Sales has a deadline too. From 1 December, every new proposal must quote the 12 euro price.
