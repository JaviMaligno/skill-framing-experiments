The old company wiki moves to our new knowledge base this weekend. If you use the wiki, here is what changes for you.

The old wiki holds nearly three thousand pages. They sit in about sixty spaces, with roughly twelve gigabytes of attachments. Most of it moves on its own. Almost nine in ten pages will convert automatically.

The rest need a closer look. About 380 pages use macros, so their owners need to check them. Those owners get an email on Monday.

Search will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Keep that window in mind.

Old links will redirect to the new pages for twelve months, so links you already have will keep working during that time.
