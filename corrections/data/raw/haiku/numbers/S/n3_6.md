Here is the short version of our checkout incident review. Our checkout went down on the morning of 14 October, for just over two hours. Customers could not complete purchases during that time. Every failed payment is a customer who could not buy from us, so this one affected all of us. Because of this, about 48,000 euros in revenue was put at risk. In total, around 1,300 payments failed. About 1,100 of them were retried automatically. The remaining 200 or so need manual follow-up from our support team. The cause was an expired security certificate on the payment proxy. From now on, certificates renew automatically thirty days before they expire.
