The Starter plan is going up in price. Starter rises from nine euros to twelve euros a month on the first of January. That applies to new customers only. Existing customers keep paying nine euros until the end of June. The Pro plan stays at twenty-nine euros.

Why does this matter? Since the last price change, the number of customers leaving, which we call churn, has risen to about two percent. The usual baseline is just under two percent. The support team expects around 350 extra tickets in January, so plan for a busy month.

Sales has one new rule. From the first of December, every new proposal must quote twelve euros for Starter.
