Our latest team survey is in, and satisfaction has edged up. Almost eight in ten employees took part, more than 400 people in all. Overall satisfaction is 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. That is a useful signal about how people feel about working here. The top request was fewer recurring meetings. About four in ten people asked for that. Clearer promotion criteria came next, and better laptops came third. Leadership has responded in two ways. Promotion criteria will be published in the first quarter. And from next month, the company is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays.
