On October 14, the checkout was down for more than two hours. It ran from just before 10 a.m. to just before noon, Central European Time.

That matters because it put money at risk for the business. About 1,300 payments failed. Just over 1,000 were retried automatically. About 200 need manual follow-up from the support team. The revenue at risk is about 48,000 euros.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS, on the payment proxy. A certificate is a digital credential that proves a system is who it says it is. Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire.
