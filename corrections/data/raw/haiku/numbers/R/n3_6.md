On 14 October, checkout was down for more than two hours. That matters because customers could not complete purchases during that time, and about 48,000 euros in revenue was at risk. Most of the failed payments were retried automatically. The rest still need manual follow-up from the support team, so that work is still open. The root cause was an expired transport layer security certificate on the payment proxy. A certificate is the digital credential that keeps data protected while it moves between systems. What happens next is a change to how certificates are managed. From now on, they renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That should stop this kind of failure from happening again.
