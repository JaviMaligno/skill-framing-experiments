Our data check on the move to the new customer system is finished, and the problem everyone feared barely happened. Of the customer records we moved, only four had a wrong value added tax number, known as VAT. VAT numbers matter for invoices and tax reporting, so getting them right is important. That is a very small share. The check did find other gaps. Many records are now missing postcodes, and that problem has grown since the last quarter. Overall record completeness also dipped a little. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
