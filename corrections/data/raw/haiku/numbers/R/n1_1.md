Here is the third-quarter data check on our customer records, from the move to our new customer relationship management system, or CRM.

The problem everyone feared with tax numbers barely happened. Only four of the 791 records we moved had a wrong value added tax number, or VAT number.

So tax data looks largely sound in the new system. The bigger issue is postcodes. More records are missing them than last quarter.

Some records are also duplicates, and overall completeness slipped a little.

Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November. That is the work to watch.
