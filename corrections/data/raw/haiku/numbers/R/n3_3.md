Checkout was down for most of a morning earlier this month. Nearly fifty thousand euros of revenue was at risk.

Checkout is the step where customers pay. When it stops, customers cannot buy anything.

The cause was an expired security certificate on the payment proxy. Most of the failed payments were retried automatically. The rest need manual follow-up from our support team.

The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically before they expire, so this exact failure should not happen again.
