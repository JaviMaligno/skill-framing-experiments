The Starter plan is going up. From 1 January, new customers will pay 12 euros a month. Existing customers keep their current price until 30 June, so nothing changes for them yet. The Pro plan does not change at all.

Why does this matter? Since the last price change, more customers have been cancelling their subscriptions than before. Our team calls this churn. Support expects a clear rise in tickets in January, so they are preparing for a busy month.

What happens next? Sales has one job. From 1 December, every new proposal must quote 12 euros for Starter.
