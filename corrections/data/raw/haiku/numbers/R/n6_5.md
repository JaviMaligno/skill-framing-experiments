Our latest team survey is in, and leadership is already responding to it. Most of the company took part, so these results are a fair picture of how people feel. Overall satisfaction went up slightly on last year. This matters because it shows where your working week could change. The biggest request was fewer recurring meetings. More than four in ten people asked for that. Clearer promotion criteria came next, and better laptops came third. Leadership says it will publish the promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays, starting next month. So if meetings take up much of your week, watch for that change.
