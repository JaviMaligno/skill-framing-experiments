On 14 October, our checkout went down for a little over two hours. It matters because customers could not pay during that time. About 48,000 euros in revenue was at risk. Most failed payments were retried automatically. The support team is now following up by hand on the ones that still need it. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on our payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire, so this specific failure should not happen again. If your team looks after payments or security certificates, you should know about this change.
