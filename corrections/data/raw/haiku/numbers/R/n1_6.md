Here is the result of our third-quarter data audit, and it is mostly good news. The value added tax problem that everyone feared barely happened. Of 791 customer records moved to our new customer relationship management system, only four had a wrong value added tax number. That is the point to take away: the fear was bigger than the problem.

The audit also found weak spots. Missing postcodes went up compared with the second quarter. Duplicate records showed up too, and the overall completeness of our records slipped a little.

What happens next is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on 3 November.
