On October fourteenth, checkout was down for about two hours. Customers who tried to pay during that time saw their payments fail, and revenue was at risk. Most of the failed payments were retried automatically. The support team will now follow up by hand on the rest.

The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on our payment proxy. This certificate is what lets our systems connect securely. To stop this from happening again, certificates now renew automatically, a month before they expire. That change means this exact failure should not happen again.
