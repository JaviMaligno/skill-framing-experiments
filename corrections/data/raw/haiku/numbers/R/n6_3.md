The results of our latest team survey are in, and satisfaction has risen. People rate it 7.4 out of 10, up from last year. The response was strong, with most of the team taking part. The biggest request was fewer recurring meetings. Next came clearer promotion criteria, then better laptops. Leadership is responding in two ways. In the first quarter, it will publish promotion criteria. Starting next month, it will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. If you sit in a lot of recurring meetings, or you are waiting on a promotion decision, these are the changes to watch. Thanks to everyone who answered.
