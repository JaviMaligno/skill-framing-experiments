The Starter plan is going up in price. From 1 January, new customers will pay 12 euros a month. Existing customers keep their current price until 30 June. The Pro plan stays the same.

Why does this matter? After the last price change, more customers cancelled than usual. Cancellations are customers who leave. Support expects several hundred extra tickets in January, so that team will be busy.

Sales has a clear rule for this. From 1 December, every new proposal must quote the new Starter price.

So the change reaches sales first, then new customers in January. Existing customers stay on their current price until the end of June.
