The results of our team survey are in, and overall satisfaction has gone up since last year. Most of you took part, so this gives a good picture of how people feel. It matters because it shows what you want changed. The biggest request, from more than four in ten people who answered, was fewer recurring meetings. Leadership is now trialling meeting-free Wednesdays, starting next month. The second request was clearer promotion criteria, and leadership will publish those in the first quarter. Better laptops came third. The survey gives everyone a clear view of what matters most to colleagues. Thank you to everyone who responded.
