Here is the result of the third quarter data-quality audit, and the news is good on tax. The value added tax, or VAT, problem that everyone feared barely happened. Of nearly 800 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system, only four had a wrong VAT number. That is about half a percent.

The bigger concern is postcodes. More records are now missing a postcode than last quarter, and overall record completeness has slipped a little. A record without a postcode is harder to use, so this is the issue to watch.

What happens next is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
