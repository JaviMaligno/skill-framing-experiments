The results of our team survey are in, and leadership has responded. Most of us took part, and overall satisfaction rose slightly from last year. Why does it matter? Because it shows what is weighing on people most. The top request, from about four in ten respondents, was fewer recurring meetings. Clearer promotion criteria came next, followed by better laptops. Leadership will publish the promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also testing meeting-free Wednesdays, starting next month. If you have wished for fewer meetings, that test is worth watching. Watch for both updates. Thanks to everyone who answered.
