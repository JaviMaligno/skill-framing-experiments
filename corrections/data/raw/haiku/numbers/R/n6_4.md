The latest team survey is in, and it shows where people want change. Satisfaction edged up on last year, and most staff took part. The top request is clear: four in ten people want fewer recurring meetings. Clearer promotion criteria and better laptops also came up often. Leadership is responding in two ways. In the first quarter, it will publish its promotion criteria. Starting next month, it will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. That matters if your week is full of back-to-back calls. Keep an eye out for the Wednesday trial, which starts soon.
