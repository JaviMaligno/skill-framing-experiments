The results of our team survey are in, and they show a small rise in how satisfied we are. This matters because it affects how we work every day. More than three quarters of us responded, so the results carry real weight. The most common request, from about four in ten people, was fewer recurring meetings. Leadership is responding. From next month, they are trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. They will also publish clear promotion criteria in the first quarter, which was another top request. People also asked for better laptops. Thank you to everyone who took part. Your answers are shaping what happens next.
