On 14 October, checkout was down for just over two hours. Payments failed during that time. Most of them were retried automatically. Some still need follow-up from our support team, and some revenue was put at risk. Why does this matter? Checkout is where customers pay, so an outage there hits the business directly. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. A certificate is the digital credential that keeps connections secure, and this one was not renewed in time. Going forward, certificates renew automatically, well before they expire, so this cause is now handled.
