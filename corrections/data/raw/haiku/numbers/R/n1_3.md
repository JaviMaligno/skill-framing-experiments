The new customer system is live, and the tax number problem everyone feared barely happened. We checked 791 customer records that moved into the new CRM, which stands for customer relationship management system. Only 4 had a wrong value added tax number, or VAT number. That is 4 out of 791. Other problems did show up. Some records are duplicates. More records are now missing postcodes, and overall completeness dropped a little. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November to deal with the missing postcodes.
