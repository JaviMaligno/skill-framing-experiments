Checkout was down for most of a morning on October fourteenth, and customers could not pay. The cause was an expired security certificate on our payment proxy. A certificate is a digital file that proves a service is genuine, and this one was not renewed in time. About 48,000 euros in revenue was at risk. Most of the failed payments were retried automatically. The support team will now follow up on the rest, which need manual action. To prevent a repeat, certificates now renew on their own a month before they expire, so this renewal no longer depends on someone remembering it.
