The VAT problem everyone feared barely happened. Moving to a new system always carries risk, so this is good news. In our third-quarter data audit, only four of 791 customer records migrated to the new CRM had a wrong VAT number. VAT stands for value added tax. The audit did find other gaps. There are some duplicate records. Missing postcodes have also grown since last quarter, and overall record completeness has slipped a little. The fix is already planned. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November. That gives us a clear date to tidy up the records that still need work.
