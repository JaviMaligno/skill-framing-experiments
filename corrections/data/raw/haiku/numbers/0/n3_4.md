On 14 October, checkout was down for two hours and 17 minutes. Checkout is the step where customers pay, so an outage there hits sales directly. That put about 48,320 euros of revenue at risk. The outage ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. In total, 1,284 payments failed. Of those, 1,071 were retried automatically. The other 213 need manual follow-up from the support team. Those are the payments that still need attention. The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security, or TLS, certificate on the payment proxy. The new safeguard: certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire.
