Our team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up. Scores rose to 7.4 out of 10, compared with 7.1 last year. Four hundred and twelve of 528 people responded, a response rate of 78 percent. Here is why this matters: the survey shows what you want changed, and leadership is responding. The top request was fewer recurring meetings. Forty-one percent of respondents asked for this. Next came clearer promotion criteria, at 36 percent. Better laptops came third, at 29 percent. So what happens next? Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Starting next month, they will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
