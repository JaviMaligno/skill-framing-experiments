The third-quarter data check on our new customer relationship management system is done. We moved 791 customer records into it. The big fear was wrong value added tax numbers. That problem barely happened. Only four records, about half of one percent, had a wrong tax number. That is good news. The number that moved most is missing postcodes. One hundred twelve records, or 14.2 percent, now have no postcode. That is up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Average record completeness also dropped, from 95.1 to 93.6 percent. We also found 37 duplicate records. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November.
