The team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up. Our score rose to 7.4 out of 10, from 7.1 last year. This matters because the survey guides what leadership changes next.

Four hundred twelve people responded, out of 528. That is a response rate of 78 percent. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, which 41 percent of respondents asked for. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at 36 percent. Better laptops followed at 29 percent.

Leadership is acting on two of these. First, it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Second, starting next month, it will trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
