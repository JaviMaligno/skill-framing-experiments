The third-quarter data audit is in, and the problem everyone feared barely happened. We moved 791 customer records into the new CRM, which is customer relationship management software. Only 4 of them had a wrong VAT number, which is value added tax. That is about half a percent. Duplicates came to 37 records, or 4.7 percent. The weak spot is postcodes. 112 records are missing them, which is 14.2 percent. That is up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on November 3.
