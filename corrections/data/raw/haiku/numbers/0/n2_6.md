The Starter plan is getting more expensive. Its price rises from 9 euros to 12 euros a month on 1 January. That applies to new customers only. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

Why does this matter? After the last price change, churn reached 2.3 percent. Churn is the share of customers who cancel. The usual rate is 1.8 percent.

The detail for the support team: they expect 340 to 380 extra tickets in January.

What happens next: from 1 December, sales must quote 12 euros in every new proposal. If you work in sales, check your proposals before you send them.
