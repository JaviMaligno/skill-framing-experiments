On 14 October, checkout was down for two hours and 17 minutes, from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. Customers could not complete their purchases during that window. Why does that matter? Every failed payment is a customer who may not have finished buying. In total, 1,284 payments failed. Of those, 1,071 were retried automatically. The other 213 need manual follow-up from our support team. Revenue at risk is 48,320 euros. The review found the root cause: an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire. That is the fix now in place.
