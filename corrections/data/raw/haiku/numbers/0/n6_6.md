The latest team survey shows satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. That matters because it shows how people feel about their work, and it points to what needs fixing. Four hundred twelve of 528 people responded, a 78 percent response rate. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, which 41 percent asked for. Next came clearer promotion criteria, at 36 percent, and better laptops, at 29 percent. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, they will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
