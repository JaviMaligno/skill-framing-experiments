Our third-quarter data audit of the new customer relationship management system, or CRM, is finished. The big fear was wrong VAT numbers, short for value added tax. That problem barely happened. Only 4 of 791 migrated records had one, which is 0.51 percent. Here is what else the numbers show. 112 records are missing postcodes. That is 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Duplicates reached 37 records, or 4.7 percent. Average record completeness dropped from 95.1 to 93.6 percent. The postcode gap is the one to watch. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
