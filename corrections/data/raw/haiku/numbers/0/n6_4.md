The team survey results are in, and satisfaction has gone up. Satisfaction now stands at 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. Four hundred and twelve of the 528 people responded, a 78 percent response rate. Here is why this matters to you: your answers are directly shaping what changes next. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, asked for by 41 percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came second, at 36 percent. Better laptops were third, at 29 percent. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Starting next month, leadership is also trying out meeting-free Wednesdays. The first two changes respond directly to the top requests.
