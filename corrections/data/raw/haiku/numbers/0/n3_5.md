On 14 October, our checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. That matters because customers could not complete payments during that time. The outage ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. In total, 1,284 payments failed. Of those, 1,071 were retried automatically. The remaining 213 need manual follow-up from our support team. Revenue at risk is 48,320 euros. That is the value at risk from the failed payments. The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on our payment proxy. To prevent a repeat, certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire.
