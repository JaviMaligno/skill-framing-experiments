The third-quarter data-quality audit of our new customer relationship management system, or CRM, is in. Out of 791 customer records moved over, only four had a wrong VAT number, which is value added tax. That is about half a percent. The VAT problem everyone feared barely happened. If you worried about tax errors after the move, this is reassuring.

The bigger issue is postcodes. 112 records, or 14.2 percent, are missing a postcode. That is up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. We also found 37 duplicate records, 4.7 percent, and average completeness slipped from 95.1 to 93.6 percent.

Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November.
