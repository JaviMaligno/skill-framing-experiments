Our team survey results are in. Satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. These answers show leadership where to focus. Four hundred and twelve of 528 people responded, a 78 percent response rate. Three requests stood out. The top one was fewer recurring meetings, from 41 percent of respondents. Next came clearer promotion criteria, at 36 percent. Better laptops came third, at 29 percent. Here is what happens next. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, they are also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. Thank you to everyone who took the time to respond.
