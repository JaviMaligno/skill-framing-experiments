Our latest team survey results are in, and they show satisfaction going up. Satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, compared with 7.1 last year. Why does this matter? Because the answers show what people want changed. Four hundred twelve of 528 people responded, a 78 percent response rate. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, named by 41 percent of respondents. Next came clearer promotion criteria, at 36 percent, and better laptops, at 29 percent. Those were the three top asks. So what happens next? Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays, starting next month.
