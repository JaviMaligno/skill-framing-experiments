Our Starter plan is getting a price increase. From 1 January, new customers will pay 12 euros a month, up from 9 euros. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

Why does this matter? After our last price change, churn rose to 2.3 percent. Churn means the share of customers who cancel. Our usual rate is 1.8 percent. Support expects 340 to 380 extra tickets in January. That is a heavy load for one month.

What happens next mainly affects sales. Starting 1 December, sales must quote 12 euros in all new proposals.
