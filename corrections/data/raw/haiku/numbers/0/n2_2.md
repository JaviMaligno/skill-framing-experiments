The Starter plan is going up in price. From 1 January, new customers will pay 12 euros a month instead of 9 euros. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

Why does this matter? After the last price change, churn rose to 2.3 percent. Churn means the share of customers who cancel. Our usual level is 1.8 percent. Support expects 340 to 380 extra tickets in January, so the support team will be busy.

Sales has one deadline. From 1 December, every new proposal must quote 12 euros for Starter.
