The third-quarter data audit of our new customer relationship management system is in. The big fear was about VAT numbers, and that problem barely happened. Of 791 customer records moved over, only 4 had a wrong VAT number. VAT means value added tax. That is 4 out of 791, or 0.51 percent. The bigger gap is postcodes. 112 records, or 14.2 percent, are missing one. In the second quarter, that figure was 9.8 percent. Duplicates reached 37 records, or 4.7 percent. Average record completeness fell from 95.1 percent in the second quarter to 93.6 percent now. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint begins on 3 November.
