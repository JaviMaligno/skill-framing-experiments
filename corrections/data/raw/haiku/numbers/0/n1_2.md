Our third-quarter data audit of the new CRM is finished. The problem everyone feared, wrong tax numbers, barely happened. Only 4 of 791 migrated customer records had a wrong value-added tax number. That is 0.51 percent. Good customer records matter to every department, because everyone works from them. The number that needs attention is missing postcodes. 112 records, or 14.2 percent, now lack one. That is up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Duplicates reached 37 records, or 4.7 percent. Average record completeness also fell, to 93.6 percent from 95.1 percent. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on 3 November.
