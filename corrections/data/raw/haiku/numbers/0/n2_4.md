Our Starter plan is going up in price. From the first of January, new customers will pay 12 euros a month, instead of 9 euros. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June, so nothing changes for them yet. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

This matters to all of us, because it touches customers, support, and sales. After the last price change, customer churn rose to 2.3 percent, up from a baseline of 1.8 percent. Churn means customers leaving. Support expects 340 to 380 extra support tickets in January.

What happens next? From the first of December, sales must quote 12 euros in every new proposal.
