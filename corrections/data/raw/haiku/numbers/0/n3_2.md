On 14 October, checkout went down for two hours and seventeen minutes. It ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. Checkout is how customers pay us, so every minute of downtime puts money at risk. In total, 1,284 payments failed. Of those, 1,071 were retried automatically. The other 213 need manual follow-up from the support team. Those 213 payments are still waiting on the support team. Revenue at risk is 48,320 euros. That is money we could lose. The root cause was an expired security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire.
