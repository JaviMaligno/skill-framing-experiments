The Starter plan is going up. It rises from 9 euros to 12 euros a month. New customers pay the higher price from 1 January. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

Here is why this matters. After the last price change, churn was 2.3 percent. That means 2.3 percent of customers left. Our baseline is 1.8 percent.

Support is preparing for more work. The team expects 340 to 380 extra tickets in January.

Sales has one rule to follow. From 1 December, every new proposal must quote 12 euros.
