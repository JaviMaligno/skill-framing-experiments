Here is what happened on October 14. Checkout was down for two hours and 17 minutes. It ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. This matters because payments failed during that window. In total, 1,284 payments failed. Of those, 1,071 were retried automatically. That leaves 213 payments that need manual follow-up from our support team. Support will follow up on those 213 payments. The revenue at risk is 48,320 euros. The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire. That is the change now in place.
