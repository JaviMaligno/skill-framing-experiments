The company wiki moves to our new knowledge base this weekend. Here is what that means for you.

Most of the content moves on its own. Of the 2,914 pages in 61 spaces, 87 percent convert automatically. The other 379 pages use macros, so their owners need to check them. Those owners get an email on Monday. If you own a page, watch for it.

Search will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Plan for that.

The migration also moves 12.4 gigabytes of attachments. Links to old pages keep working. They redirect to the new pages for 12 months.

So the short version: the wiki moves this weekend, search is down for part of Saturday, and your old links will still work for a year.
