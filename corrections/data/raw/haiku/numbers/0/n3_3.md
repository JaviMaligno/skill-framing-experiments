On October 14th, checkout was down for two hours and 17 minutes, from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. Checkout is where customers pay, so this outage hit revenue directly. In total, 1,284 payments failed. Of those, 1,071 were retried automatically. The remaining 213 need manual follow-up from our support team. Revenue at risk was 48,320 euros. The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. That certificate is the digital credential that lets systems connect securely. Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire. Renewal is now automatic, so no one has to remember this date.
