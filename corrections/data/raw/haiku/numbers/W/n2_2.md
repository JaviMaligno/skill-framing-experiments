Starter is going up in price, and the new price starts on 1 January. For new customers, the monthly price moves from 9 euros to 12 euros. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros, so Pro customers are not affected.

Why does this matter? After the last price change, more customers left. Churn reached 2.3 percent, against a usual 1.8 percent. Support expects between 340 and 380 extra tickets in January.

If you write proposals for new customers, use 12 euros from 1 December. Sales must quote that price in every proposal from that date.
