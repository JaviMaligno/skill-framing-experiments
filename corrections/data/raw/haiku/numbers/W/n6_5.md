The team survey results are in, and they show where your feedback is landing. Seventy-eight percent of you responded, 412 people out of 528. Overall satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The biggest request was fewer recurring meetings, named by 41 percent. Clearer promotion criteria came second, at 36 percent. Better laptops followed at 29 percent. Leadership has committed to acting on two of the top three asks. They will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. They will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. For anyone whose calendar is full of recurring meetings, that could be real relief. Thank you to everyone who took the time to answer.
