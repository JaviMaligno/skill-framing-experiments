The third-quarter data check on our new customer system is in, and the big fear didn't come true. We worried about wrong value-added tax numbers. Only four of the 791 customer records we moved had one. That's about one in two hundred.

The bigger change is postcodes. 112 records are missing one, about one in seven. That's up from under one in ten in the second quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent.

We also found 37 duplicate records, just under five percent. The migration wasn't the disaster we feared, but the data still needs cleaning. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on November third.
