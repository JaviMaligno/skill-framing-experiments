Facilities has replaced the plants on floors 2, 3 and 5. If you work on one of those floors, you will see the difference. There are 46 plants in total, from 14 different species. Each meeting room gets three plants. The supplier will water them every seven to ten days, and the contract runs for 18 months. That means the plants are looked after without anyone on staff having to do it. So please don't water them yourself, and leave that to the supplier. Floor 4 gets its plants once the refurbishment ends, so that is the next change to watch for.
