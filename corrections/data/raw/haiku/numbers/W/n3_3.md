Checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes on 14 October. It ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. Every failed payment is a customer who could not pay, so this one mattered. In total, 1,284 payments failed. Of those, 1,071 were retried automatically. The other 213 need manual follow-up from our support team. Revenue at risk is 48,320 euros. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security, or TLS, certificate on our payment proxy. A certificate is the digital credential that keeps connections secure. Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire.
