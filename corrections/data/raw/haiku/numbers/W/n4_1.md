Facilities has replaced the plants on floors two, three and five. If you work on one of those floors, you will notice the change. There are 46 plants in total, from 14 different species. Each meeting room on those floors now has three of them. A supplier looks after the plants under an 18-month contract, and waters them every seven to 10 days. So please don't water them yourself. For most of us, the only change is what we see when we walk into a meeting room. Floor four will get its plants once the refurbishment there ends.
