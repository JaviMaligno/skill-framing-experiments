On October 14th, our checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. Customers could not pay on the site for most of a morning. The outage ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time.

Some 1,284 payments failed. About 1,071 of them retried automatically. The other 213 need manual follow-up from our support team. Revenue at risk is 48,320 euros.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on our payment proxy. That is the digital ID that lets payments connect securely. Once it expired, payments stopped going through.

Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire. Those 213 cases are with the support team for follow-up.
