Our latest team survey is in, and the results are encouraging. Satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. More than three quarters of us responded, 412 people out of 528. That matters because it shows where people want change. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, from 41 percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at 36 percent. Better laptops followed at 29 percent. Here is what happens next. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. And starting next month, they are trying out meeting-free Wednesdays. Thanks to everyone who took the time to respond.
