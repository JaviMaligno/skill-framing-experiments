Here's the third quarter data-quality audit for our move to the new customer relationship management system, and the news on tax numbers is good. The problem everyone feared barely happened. Of 791 customer records moved over, only 4 had a wrong value added tax number. That's about half a percent. Duplicates came in at 37, under five percent. The real concern is postcodes. 112 records are missing them, 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Overall record completeness slipped too, from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent. So the migration held up on tax numbers and duplicates, but postcodes need attention. Next up is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting November third.
