Our latest team survey is in, and satisfaction has edged up. Four hundred and twelve of our 528 colleagues responded, a 78 percent response rate. Satisfaction averaged 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. Why does this matter? Because you told us what to fix. The biggest ask, from 41 percent of respondents, was fewer recurring meetings. Next came clearer promotion criteria, at 36 percent, and better laptops, at 29 percent. Here is what happens next. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. And starting next month, they're trialling meeting-free Wednesdays.
