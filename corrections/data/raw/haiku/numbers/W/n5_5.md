The old company wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend. If you use the wiki, here's what changes for you. The old wiki holds nearly three thousand pages across 61 spaces, with about 12 gigabytes of attachments. About 87 percent of pages convert automatically. The other 379 pages use macros, so their owners need to check them. Those owners get an email on Monday. Search will be down for about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages redirect for 12 months, so existing links keep working. If you own pages, watch for Monday's email, and plan around Saturday's search outage.
