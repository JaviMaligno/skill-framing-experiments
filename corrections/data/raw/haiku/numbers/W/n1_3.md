The third-quarter data audit has one clear headline. The problem everyone feared, a wrong value added tax number, barely happened. Only 4 of our 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system, or CRM, had a wrong VAT number. That is about half of one percent.

The bigger issue is postcodes. 112 records are missing one. That is 14 percent, up from under 10 percent last quarter. Overall record completeness also slipped, from 95 percent to just under 94 percent. We also found 37 duplicate records, about 5 percent.

The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint starting 3 November.
