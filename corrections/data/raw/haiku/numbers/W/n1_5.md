The third quarter data-quality audit on our new customer system is done, and the VAT problem everyone feared barely happened. Only 4 of 791 migrated customer records had a wrong VAT number. VAT stands for value added tax. That is about half of one percent. For anyone who works with customer records, that is a relief.

Postcodes are the weak spot. 112 records are missing one, which is 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. We also found 37 duplicate records, or 4.7 percent. Average completeness slipped too, from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November.
