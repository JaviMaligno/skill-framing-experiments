The latest team survey results are in, and the response was strong. Seventy-eight percent of people answered: 412 out of 528. Overall satisfaction is 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. If you've felt buried in recurring meetings, you're not alone. That was the top request, named by 41 percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next at 36 percent, and better laptops at 29 percent. Leadership says it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. And starting next month, it will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. If your calendar is packed, that second change is one to watch.
