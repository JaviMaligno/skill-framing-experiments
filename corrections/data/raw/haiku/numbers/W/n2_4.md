Our Starter plan goes from 9 euros to 12 euros a month. The new price starts on the first of January, but only for new customers. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until the thirtieth of June. This matters to all of us, because price changes affect how many customers stay and how much work reaches support. After the last price change, churn, which means customers cancelling, reached 2.3 percent. Our usual rate is 1.8 percent. Support expects between 340 and 380 extra tickets in January. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros. For sales, starting on the first of December, every new proposal should quote 12 euros.
