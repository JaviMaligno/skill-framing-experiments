The Starter plan is getting more expensive. On 1 January, its price rises from 9 euros to 12 euros a month for new customers. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

This affects two teams right away. Sales must quote 12 euros in every new proposal from 1 December. Support expects 340 to 380 extra tickets in January.

Churn, the share of customers who leave, was 2.3 percent after the last price change. The usual baseline is 1.8 percent. That is the number to watch.

Next, sales starts quoting the new price on 1 December, and the increase for new customers takes effect on 1 January.
