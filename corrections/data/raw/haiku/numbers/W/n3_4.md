Checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes on October 14th. It ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. Checkout is where revenue happens, so an outage there touches every team. For customers, it meant no way to pay. Revenue at risk is about 48,320 euros.

The cause was an expired security certificate on our payment proxy. Of the 1,284 payments that failed, 1,071 were retried automatically. The other 213 need manual follow-up from our support team, and that work is still ahead.

The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. That closes the gap that caused this outage.
