The third quarter data audit of our new customer relationship management system is finished, and the problem everyone feared barely happened. Of 791 customer records moved over, only four had a wrong value added tax number. That's about half of one percent. The point is simple: that tax risk is small, so our attention belongs elsewhere. The gap that grew is postcodes. 112 records are missing them, about 14 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. We also found 37 duplicate records, about 4.7 percent. Average record completeness dropped to 93.6 percent, from 95.1 percent. Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November.
