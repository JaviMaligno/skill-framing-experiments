Checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes on October 14th. It ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. Checkout is where customers pay, so every minute of that outage put money and trust at risk. During the outage, 1,284 payments failed. Most recovered on their own. 1,071 were retried automatically. The other 213 need manual follow-up from our support team. About 48,320 euros of revenue was at risk. The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS certificate, on our payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire. That is the fix we've put in place.
