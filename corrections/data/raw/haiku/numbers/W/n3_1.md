On October 14th, checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. It ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time, and customers could not pay during that window. In total, 1,284 payments failed. Of those, 1,071 retried automatically. The other 213 still need manual follow-up from our support team. Revenue at risk is more than 48,000 euros. The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS certificate, on our payment proxy. That is the security credential that encrypts traffic between systems. Certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire.
