Our third-quarter data check on the move to the new customer relationship system is in, and the problem everyone feared barely happened. Only four of the 791 migrated customer records had a wrong value-added tax number. That's about half a percent. Why it matters: the tax risk we worried about most is largely gone. The real gap is postcodes. 112 records now have no postcode, about one in seven, up from about one in ten last quarter. We also found 37 duplicate records, just under five percent. Average record completeness slipped from 95 percent to just under 94 percent. What happens next: a postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November.
