The team survey results are in, and satisfaction has gone up. People rated it 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. That matters because it tells us what to fix next. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, from 41 percent of respondents. Next came clearer promotion criteria, at 36 percent, then better laptops, at 29 percent. Leadership is acting on two of these. They will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. And from next month, they will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. Seventy-eight percent of people responded, 412 out of 528, so these results reflect most of us.
