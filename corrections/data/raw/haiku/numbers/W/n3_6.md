On 14 October, checkout went down for two hours and seventeen minutes, from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. That meant customers could not pay. About 1,300 payments failed. Just over 1,000 of them retried automatically. The other 213 need manual follow-up from the support team, so if you work in support, that work is coming your way. The company had around 48,000 euros of revenue at risk. The root cause was an expired Transport Layer Security, or TLS, certificate on the payment proxy. The good news is that certificates now renew automatically, 30 days before they expire. That is the fix that came out of this review.
