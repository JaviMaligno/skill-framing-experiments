The Starter plan is getting more expensive. From 1 January, new customers will pay 12 euros a month, up from 9. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until 30 June. The Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

This matters because more customers are leaving since the last price change. Churn, the share of customers who cancel, is now 2.3 percent. Our normal level is 1.8 percent. Support expects 340 to 380 extra tickets in January. Tickets are the requests for help that customers send in.

Here is what happens next. Starting 1 December, sales must quote 12 euros in every new proposal.
