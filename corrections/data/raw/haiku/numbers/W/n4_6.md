Facilities has replaced the plants on floors two, three and five with new ones. If you work on one of those floors, you will notice some new greenery in your workspace. In total, there are 46 plants, and they come from 14 different species. Every meeting room gets three of them. The supplier's contract for the plants runs for 18 months. The supplier also takes care of the watering, which happens every seven to 10 days. So please don't water them yourself. Leave that job to them. Floor four will get its plants once its refurbishment ends.
