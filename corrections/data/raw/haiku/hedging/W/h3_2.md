Knocking out a non-load-bearing wall is a solid weekend project. You'll mostly be cutting, prying, and hauling drywall and framing.

Before you swing anything, check whether the wall is load-bearing. Look at which way the ceiling joists run, and check the attic or basement for beams or posts under the wall. If the wall carries weight, don't remove it without a structural plan, because a ceiling can sag or fall. Then switch off the breakers and use a voltage tester to check for wires, pipes, and vents inside the wall.

Rough steps:

1. Remove the trim, switch plates, and outlet covers.
2. Cut a small test hole in the drywall to see what's behind it.
3. Pull off the drywall in sections.
4. Cut out the studs and the top and bottom plates.
5. Haul debris out as you go.

Tools and supplies:

- Utility knife and drywall saw
- Reciprocating saw or oscillating multi-tool
- Flat pry bar and claw hammer
- Stud finder and non-contact voltage tester
- Drop cloths, painter's tape, and a dust mask
- Safety glasses and work gloves
- Shop vacuum and heavy-duty contractor bags
- Sawhorses or a stepladder
