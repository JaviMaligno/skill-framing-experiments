You'll need a demolition kit, a way to find what's inside the wall, and dust protection.

**Main tools:**
- Reciprocating saw (or a multi-tool) for cutting through studs and lath
- Club hammer and cold chisel, or a pry bar, for plaster and drywall
- Stud finder and non-contact voltage tester, to locate wires and pipes
- Tape measure and level
- Shop vacuum and drop cloths
- Headlamp
- Safety glasses, gloves, and a dust mask or respirator

**Before you swing anything:** Check whether the wall is load-bearing. Look at which way the joists run in the floor above, or whether the wall sits near the middle of the house or under another wall upstairs. If it might be structural, you'll need a temporary support and a beam, and it's worth having a structural engineer or building inspector take a look first. Also trace any wiring and plumbing with the voltage tester. Shut off the circuits you find, and if there are gas lines or supply pipes in the wall, stop and call a plumber before cutting.

If the wall turns out to be non-structural, you can likely finish it in a weekend.
