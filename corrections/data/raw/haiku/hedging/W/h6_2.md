For white metro tiles, a light grey grout is the safest and most popular choice. It keeps the wall looking clean and bright, and it hides the dirt and marks that show up on pure white grout. Warm greige or soft cream also works well if you want a slightly softer look.

Avoid pure white grout unless you're prepared to clean it often. It shows every mark, and it tends to darken over time in kitchens and bathrooms.

For a sharp, modern contrast, dark charcoal grout makes the tile pattern stand out. It's bold, but it shows less dirt than white and looks striking with white tiles.

Whichever colour you pick, choose a cement-based grout with a good stain-resistant finish, or seal it after it cures. That will make it much easier to keep clean.

For this job you'll need:
- Grout (cement-based, in your chosen colour)
- Rubber grout float
- Sponge and bucket of water
- Grout sealer (if the grout isn't pre-sealed)
