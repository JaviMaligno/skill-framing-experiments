**Don't mix them.** Bleach and acid descaler react to produce chlorine gas, which can seriously hurt your lungs and eyes, and a stronger mix won't clean better anyway.

For the black mould:

1. Open the window and run the extractor fan. Wear gloves and eye protection.
2. Spray bleach (a bleach-based mould remover is fine) onto the mould and leave it for about 15 minutes. Don't let it dry out.
3. Scrub with an old brush, then rinse well with plenty of water.
4. Dry the area with a towel or a fan.

Keep the descaler for limescale around taps and the shower head. It won't remove mould. If you use it, rinse the bathroom thoroughly first, then wait until the surface is dry before using bleach.

If the mould keeps coming back, it's usually condensation. Run the fan during and after showers, and wipe down the walls and silicone sealant afterwards.
