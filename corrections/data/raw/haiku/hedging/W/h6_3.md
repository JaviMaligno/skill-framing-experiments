For white metro tiles, a **light to mid grey grout** is the classic choice. It defines the grid so the tiles look neat, without the starkness of a high-contrast line. If you want the tiles to read as one smooth surface, go with a **bright white or warm white** grout, which blends in. A **charcoal** grout makes a bold, graphic look, but it shows any slight misalignment in the tile lines.

For a kitchen backsplash or bathroom, choose a **sanded or unsanded cement grout with a sealer**, or an **epoxy grout** if you want something more stain-resistant and waterproof. Epoxy is harder to work with, so it's easier for a first-timer to stick with a standard cement grout.

Whichever you pick, buy a little extra and test a sample on a spare tile. Grout dries noticeably lighter or darker than it looks when wet.

**What you'll need:**
- Grout (cement or epoxy, in your chosen colour)
- Rubber grout float
- Sponge and bucket of clean water
- Grout sealer (for cement grout)
- Soft cloth for buffing off haze
