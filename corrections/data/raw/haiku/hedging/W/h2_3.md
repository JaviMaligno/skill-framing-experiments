Don't mix them. Bleach and acid descaler release chlorine gas when combined, and in a small bathroom that can seriously damage your lungs.

For black mould, bleach on its own is the right product. Leave the descaler out of it, since it's made for limescale and can damage grout and tiles.

1. Open the window and run the extractor fan.
2. Spray diluted bleach (roughly one part bleach to two parts water) onto the mould. Leave it for about 15 minutes, no longer.
3. Scrub with a stiff brush, then wipe the area clean.
4. Rinse with clean water and dry the surface.
5. If you can still see staining, repeat once. Faint marks are often left after the mould itself is gone.

Keep the bleach and descaler stored apart so you don't grab the wrong one mid-job. If the mould keeps coming back, the room probably needs more ventilation or the fan needs upgrading.

You'll need a spray bottle, a stiff brush, rubber gloves, eye protection, and cloths or paper towels.
