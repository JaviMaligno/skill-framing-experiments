**Go with satin.** A hallway takes the most knocks: shoulders, bags, pushchairs, and fingerprints around light switches. Satin wipes clean with a damp cloth, so it holds up far better than matt, which can show marks and go patchy when scrubbed.

Matt does hide small wall flaws better, so choose it only if your walls are very uneven and you won't clean them often. Avoid gloss, which shows every bump and roller mark.

If the house is older, test the existing paint for lead before you sand it. Lead test swabs are cheap at the store, and if the paint is positive, use wet methods and don't dry-sand.

**What you'll need:**
- Satin wall paint (a washable or scrubbable grade)
- Primer, if you're covering a dark colour or bare plaster
- Roller, tray, and a 2-inch angled brush for edges
- Painter's tape and drop sheets
- Filler and fine sandpaper for small dents
- A sample pot to test the colour in the hallway's own light
