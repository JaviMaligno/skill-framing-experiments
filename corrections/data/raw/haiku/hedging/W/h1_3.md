No, don't use that cord. A 2000 W heater pulls a lot of current, and a thin desk-lamp cord can overheat, melt, or start a fire where it's warm or under a rug.

- **Best option:** Plug the heater straight into a wall outlet.
- **If you need reach:** Use a heavy-duty extension cord rated for at least 15 amps. Look for 14-gauge wire or thicker (a lower gauge number is thicker). Keep it short, uncoiled, and out from under rugs.
- **Check the label:** Look at the heater's plate and the cord's printing. The cord's rating should be at least the heater's current draw.
- **Avoid power strips:** Don't run the heater through a power strip or a multi-outlet adapter, and don't share that outlet with other high-power appliances.

If the heater or the outlet feels warm, the plug is discolored, or the outlet buzzes or flickers, unplug everything and stop using it until an electrician has checked it.
