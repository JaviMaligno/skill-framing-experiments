No, don't plug it into that cord. A 2000 W heater pulls about 16–17 amps on a standard 120 V outlet. A thin lamp cord is usually rated for 7–10 amps, so it will overheat under that load. That can melt the insulation and start a fire, often where the cord runs under a rug or behind furniture.

Plug the heater straight into a wall outlet. Use an outlet that isn't shared with other big appliances, and check that the outlet isn't warm or buzzing.

If you need a longer reach, a heavy-duty outdoor-rated extension cord marked 12 AWG (or at least 14 AWG) and rated for 15 amps or more can handle the load. Keep it as short as possible, uncoil it fully, and never run it through a power strip or plug another cord into it.
