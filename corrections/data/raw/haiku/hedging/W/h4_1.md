**Go with satin.** A hallway takes a lot of knocks from shoulders, bags, and little hands, and satin wipes clean much better than matt. Matt hides bumps in the wall a little better, but it marks easily and can look dull after a scrub. Pick a washable, water-based wall paint in satin.

If the walls are in good shape, one coat of primer-sealer and two coats of satin should do. Fill any dents and sand them smooth first, and wipe off grease near the light switches and door frames before you paint.

**The one warning:** if your house was built before about 1978, the old paint may contain lead. Don't sand or scrape it dry. Dampen the surface first, or have a test kit check it before you start.

**What you'll need:**
- Washable satin wall paint
- Primer-sealer (for patches or bare plaster)
- Filler and a putty knife
- Sandpaper or a sanding sponge
- Roller, tray, and a brush for edges
- Painter's tape and dust sheets
- A damp sponge for cleaning
