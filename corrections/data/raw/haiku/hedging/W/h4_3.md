**Go with a satin finish**, or a washable matt if you want a softer look. A hallway takes a lot of knocks from shoes, bags, and hands, and satin wipes clean without much effort.

Matt hides wall imperfections well, but it marks easily and is harder to clean without leaving a shiny patch. Satin is more forgiving. Gloss is too reflective for walls and shows every dent.

If you're painting over bare plaster or a patched area, use a primer first so the topcoat goes on evenly. Paint a test patch on the wall and look at it in both daylight and evening light before you commit, since hallways are often dim.

**What you'll need:**
- Satin or washable matt emulsion (paint)
- Mould-resistant primer, if the wall is new or patched
- Roller and tray, plus a brush for edges
- Dust sheets and painter's tape
- Filler and sanding block, for small dents and cracks
- Fine-grit sandpaper, for smoothing the filler
