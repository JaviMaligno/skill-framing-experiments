Most of this job needs a standard demolition kit, but first check whether the wall is **load-bearing**. Removing one can make the floor or ceiling above sag. Look for joists running perpendicular to the wall, or a beam or wall directly above it. If you're unsure, ask a structural engineer or your local building department before you swing a hammer.

Once you've confirmed it's safe to open, scan for wiring and pipes with a stud finder that detects AC wires, then shut off power to that area.

**Tools you'll need:**
- Stud finder with AC wire detection
- Non-contact voltage tester
- Drywall saw or utility knife
- Pry bar and claw hammer
- Reciprocating saw with wood and metal blades
- Drill/driver
- Level, tape measure, and pencil
- Shop vac and plastic sheeting to contain dust
- Safety glasses, dust mask, and work gloves
- Headlamp
- If load-bearing: adjustable steel posts and a header beam for temporary and permanent support
