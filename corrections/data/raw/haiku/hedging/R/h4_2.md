**Go with satin.** Hallways take a lot of knocks, scuffs, and fingerprints, and satin wipes clean far more easily than matt. It has a soft sheen without looking shiny. Matt hides wall imperfections better, but marks are harder to clean off it. Eggshell is a good middle ground if you want a little less sheen.

Look for a paint labeled "washable" or "scrubbable," and choose a hard-wearing finish for woodwork like the skirting boards and door frames. Satin or semi-gloss works well there.

For painting, you'll need:
- Roller, tray, and extension pole
- Brush for cutting in at edges and corners
- Painter's tape and dust sheets
- Filler and sandpaper for any holes or cracks
- Primer, if you're painting over a dark colour or bare plaster
