For a classic look, go with **light to mid grey grout**. It keeps the tiles looking clean and bright without the stark contrast of dark grout.

If you want the grid pattern to stand out, which is part of the appeal of metro tiles, choose a **charcoal or dark grey**. It makes the layout look bold and modern, but it shows any haze left on the tiles after grouting, so wipe the faces clean as you go.

Pure white grout gives the most seamless finish, but it stains and darkens quickly, especially behind a sink or around a cooker.

Some practical tips:
- Use a **sanded grout** for joints wider than about 3 mm, and **unsanded** for narrower ones.
- Pick an **epoxy grout** for kitchens or bathrooms, since it resists stains and water better.
- Test a small patch on a spare tile or in a hidden corner first, because grout looks darker when wet and lighter once dry.

You'll need a rubber grout float, a damp sponge, a bucket, and a clean microfibre cloth for buffing the tiles.
