**Light grey** is the safest choice. It keeps the look clean and bright without making every stain stand out.

- **Light or warm grey:** a soft, classic look that hides dirt well.
- **White or off-white:** a seamless, gallery-style finish, but it shows dirt quickly, especially in kitchens and bathrooms.
- **Charcoal or dark grey:** strong contrast that makes the tiles pop. It hides stains well but can look harsh with a lot of grout lines.

If you want a bit of warmth, try a greige (grey with beige undertones). For a dark room, a slightly darker grey keeps the space from feeling cold.

Whatever you choose, use a sealed or stain-resistant grout, and ask for a sample to test against your tiles in daylight before you buy in bulk.

For grouting, you'll need:
- Grout (sanded for gaps wider than about 3 mm, unsanded for narrower joints)
- A rubber grout float
- A damp sponge and a bucket
- Grout sealer
- Spacers, if you're still setting tiles
