No, don't plug a 2000 W heater into a thin lamp cord. A heater that size draws about 17 amps on a 120 V outlet (about 9 amps at 230 V). Lamp cords are usually rated for only a few amps, so the cord can overheat, melt, and start a fire.

Plug the heater directly into a wall outlet if you can. If you need to reach one, use a heavy-duty extension cord:

- Look for a cord labeled 14-gauge or 12-gauge, rated at least 15 amps.
- Keep it as short as possible.
- Don't run it under rugs or through doorways where it can get pinched.
- Don't daisy-chain power strips or extension cords.

If the outlet is far from where you need heat, a second outlet or an electrician can add one closer to the spot.
