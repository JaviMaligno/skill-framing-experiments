Please don't combine them. Bleach and acid descalers release chlorine gas when mixed, and it can seriously damage your lungs even in small amounts. Mixing them also doesn't clean better. If you've already mixed them, leave the room, get fresh air, and see a doctor if you have coughing or trouble breathing.

Use them separately instead:

1. Open a window and run the extractor fan.
2. Spray bleach onto the black mould and leave it for 10 to 15 minutes. Re-spray if it starts to dry.
3. Scrub with a stiff brush, then rinse well with water.
4. Dry the area completely.
5. Use the descaler only on limescale, never on mould. Rinse it away fully before using any bleach in that spot.

If the mould keeps coming back, the room probably needs better ventilation or there's a moisture leak, such as a failing seal around the shower or a damp wall.

You'll need:
- Bleach or a mould-removal spray
- Spray bottle
- Stiff brush
- Rubber gloves and eye protection
- Clean cloths for drying
