**Go with satin.** Hallways take a lot of scuffs from shoulders, bags, and shoes, and satin wipes clean much better than matte. Matte hides wall flaws well, but it shows marks and is harder to clean without leaving shiny patches.

Satin has a soft sheen that's easy on the eyes and works in most hallways. If your walls are very uneven, an eggshell finish is a middle ground. Semi-gloss is the most washable, but it shows every bump in the wall.

For the paint, pick a scrubbable interior acrylic in satin, and use a primer first if you're painting over a dark color or bare drywall.

**What you'll need:**
- Satin or eggshell interior paint
- Primer (if needed)
- 2-inch angled brush for edges
- 9-inch roller with a medium-nap cover
- Roller tray
- Painter's tape
- Drop cloths
- Sanding sponge
