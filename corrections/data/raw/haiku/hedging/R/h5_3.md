Space the screws about **300 mm (12 inches)** apart along each stud on walls, and closer, around **200 mm (8 inches)**, on ceilings. Keep every screw about 10 mm in from the board's edges and ends.

1. Find the studs with a stud finder and mark their centres.
2. Position the board so its edges sit on the middle of a stud, and check it's level.
3. Start at the centre and work outwards so the board stays flat.
4. Drive each screw so the head sits just below the surface, without tearing the paper. A depth-set screw gun makes this easy.
5. Keep screws in a straight line along each stud, and don't place them where two boards meet unless the stud is there.

You'll need:
- Drywall screws (coarse-thread, bugle-head)
- Screw gun or drill with a depth-setting nose
- Stud finder
- Spirit level
- Tape measure and pencil
