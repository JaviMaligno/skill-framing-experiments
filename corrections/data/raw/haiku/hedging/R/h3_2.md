Most interior walls can be opened with basic demolition tools, but first confirm the wall isn't load-bearing. If it holds up the ceiling or floors above, removing it needs a beam and a structural engineer, so check with your local building office before you start.

Once you've confirmed it's safe to remove:

1. Scan the wall for pipes and wires, and switch off the circuits in that area.
2. Mark the opening and score the drywall with a utility knife.
3. Pry the drywall off in sections.
4. Cut the exposed studs with a reciprocating saw, then pull out the nails.
5. Sweep up the debris and vacuum the dust.

**Tools you'll need:**
- Stud finder with wire and pipe detection
- Non-contact voltage tester
- Utility knife and drywall saw
- Pry bar and hammer
- Reciprocating saw with wood and metal-cutting blades
- Safety glasses, dust mask, and work gloves
- Drop cloths and a shop vac
- Tape measure, level, pencil, and headlamp
