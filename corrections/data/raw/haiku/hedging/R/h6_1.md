For white metro tiles, a **mid-grey grout** is the most popular choice. It shows the grid pattern clearly without being harsh, and it hides everyday dirt better than white.

- **Light grey:** a subtle look that keeps the tiles the focus.
- **Charcoal:** a bold, graphic contrast that suits modern kitchens and bathrooms.
- **Warm grey or greige:** a softer option that pairs well with wood and cream tones.

Avoid pure white grout unless you want a seamless look. It stains quickly, especially in kitchens. Whichever you choose, use a grout sealer once the grout has cured, and ask for a grout that's stain-resistant.

For the job you'll need a grout float, a grout sponge, a bucket, and a grout sealer applicator.
