Fix screws about **200mm (8 inches) apart** along each stud on walls, and about **150mm (6 inches)** on ceilings. Never go wider than **300mm (12 inches)**. Keep screws roughly 10mm in from the board edges, and use the same spacing on the middle studs.

1. Find the studs with a stud finder, and mark their centres on the board.
2. Start in the middle of the board and work outwards so the board sits flat.
3. Drive each screw until the head sits just below the surface, without breaking the paper. Overdriven screws weaken the board.
4. Keep screws in straight lines along each stud, with the rows about 300mm apart.

You'll need:
- Drywall screws (about 25mm long for single-layer 12.5mm board, longer for thicker board)
- A drill with a clutch or a screwdriver with a drywall bit
- A stud finder
- A spirit level and tape measure
- A plasterboard saw or knife for trimming
