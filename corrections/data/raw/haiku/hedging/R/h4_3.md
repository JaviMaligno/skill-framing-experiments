**Go with satin.** Hallways take a lot of knocks, scuffs, and handprints, and satin wipes clean with a damp cloth. Matt hides wall imperfections better, but it's harder to clean and shows marks more once it's been scrubbed. Choose matt only if your hallway walls are very rough or uneven.

For the best finish, use a washable satin in a water-based formula, which is easier to work with indoors.

You'll need:
- Roller and tray
- Angled brush for edges and corners
- Painter's tape
- Drop sheets
- Filler and a sanding sponge, for any dents or patches
- Primer, if you're painting bare plaster or a large repair
