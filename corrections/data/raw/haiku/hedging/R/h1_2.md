**No.** A 2000 W heater pulls about 16–17 amps on a standard 120V US outlet (about 9 amps on 230V). A thin desk-lamp cord is usually rated for 2–10 amps, so it can overheat, melt its insulation, and start a fire. The heater should plug straight into a wall outlet.

If you need the heater farther from the outlet:

1. Move the heater closer to the outlet, or move the outlet's load elsewhere.
2. If you must use a cord, buy a heavy-duty one rated at least 15 amps (look for 14 AWG or thicker on the label), and keep it short.
3. Check that the cord stays cool and that the plug doesn't get warm. If either happens, stop using it.
4. Use a dedicated outlet, not a power strip or a splitter with other devices on the same circuit.

Make sure the heater has tip-over and overheat shut-off features, and keep it at least 3 feet from curtains, bedding, and furniture.
