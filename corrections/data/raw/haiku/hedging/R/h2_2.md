**Don't mix bleach with an acid descaler.** Together they release chlorine gas, which can seriously hurt your lungs. Use each one separately instead, and you don't need the descaler for mould anyway.

**To clean the mould with bleach:**

1. Open a window and run the extractor fan.
2. Wear rubber gloves and eye protection.
3. Spray or brush undiluted bleach (or the strength on the label) onto the mould, especially grout and silicone.
4. Leave it for 10 to 15 minutes. Don't let it dry out, so re-spray if needed.
5. Scrub with an old brush, then rinse well with water.
6. Dry the area with a towel and keep the fan running for a while.

If the black marks are still there after one round, repeat the process. Stubborn stains on silicone may need a mould-remover gel, which is designed to stay on vertical surfaces. Rinse the descaler away fully before using bleach on the same surface, or use it on a different day.

**You'll need:** bleach, rubber gloves, eye protection, a stiff brush or old toothbrush, a spray bottle, and towels.
