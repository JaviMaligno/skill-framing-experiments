**Don't mix bleach and an acid descaler.** Together they release chlorine gas, which can cause serious lung damage, even in a small bathroom. Use one product at a time, and never in the same session without rinsing thoroughly.

For black mould, bleach is the right choice:

1. Open the window and run the extractor fan.
2. Mix one part bleach with four parts water, or use a ready-made mould spray.
3. Spray or sponge it onto the mould and leave it for 15 to 30 minutes. Don't let it dry out.
4. Scrub with a stiff brush, then rinse with clean water.
5. Dry the area fully. Repeat if faint marks remain.

If the mould keeps returning, it's usually from condensation. Improve ventilation and check for leaky seals.

**You'll need:** bleach or a mould spray, a spray bottle or sponge, a stiff brush, rubber gloves, eye protection, and a cloth for drying.
