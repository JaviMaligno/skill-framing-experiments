No, don't plug it into that cord. A 2000 W heater pulls about 17 amps on a standard 120-volt outlet (about 9 amps on 230-volt). Thin lamp cords are usually rated for only 2 to 10 amps, so the cord can overheat and start a fire, even if nothing trips.

Safer options:

1. **Plug the heater directly into a wall outlet.** This is the best choice.
2. **Use a heavy-duty extension cord** if you need reach. Look for a cord labeled 12 or 14 gauge, rated for at least 15 amps, and keep it as short as possible.
3. **Skip power strips and multi-outlet adapters.** Many aren't rated for a heater's load.
4. **Check the outlet.** If it's warm, buzzes, or the breaker trips often, have an electrician look at it.

Also, keep the heater at least 3 feet from anything that can burn, and use one with an automatic shutoff for tip-over and overheating.
