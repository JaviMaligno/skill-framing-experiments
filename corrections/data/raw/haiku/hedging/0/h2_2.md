**Please don't mix them.** Bleach and acid descaler release chlorine gas when combined. The gas can seriously damage your lungs, even in a small room, so keep them apart.

Bleach works well on black mould by itself:

1. Open the window and run the extractor fan.
2. Put on rubber gloves and eye protection.
3. Spray the mould with household bleach or a bleach-based mould remover, and leave it on for **15 to 30 minutes**.
4. Scrub gently with a stiff brush, then rinse well with water.
5. Dry the area. Repeat if any marks remain.

Acid descaler is for limescale, not mould, so you don't need it here. If you do use it another time, rinse all the bleach away first and let the surface dry.

**What you'll need:**
- Rubber gloves and safety glasses
- Bleach or a bleach-based mould remover
- Spray bottle and stiff brush
- Paper towel or a cloth
- Anti-mould paint or mould-resistant silicone, if the mould keeps returning
