**Please don't combine them.** Bleach mixed with an acid descaler releases chlorine gas, which can seriously damage your lungs. Use each product separately, and never use the descaler for mould at all.

To clean the mould safely:

1. Open the window and run the extractor fan.
2. Wear rubber gloves and eye protection.
3. Spray bleach (or a mould-remover spray) directly on the mould and leave it for 10 to 15 minutes. Don't let it dry out.
4. Scrub with an old brush, then rinse well with clean water.
5. Wipe the area dry and keep the fan running for a while after.

If the mould is on silicone sealant and won't come off, it may need to be cut out and replaced. Mould often returns unless the room is ventilated, so use the fan during and after showers.

If you've already mixed the two, leave the room, keep the door closed behind you, and get fresh air. If you have coughing, chest tightness, or trouble breathing, call NHS 111 or your local emergency number.
