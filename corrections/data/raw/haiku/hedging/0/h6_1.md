For white metro tiles, a mid-to-dark grey grout is the most popular and forgiving choice. It shows the tile edges clearly without looking harsh, and it hides everyday dirt better than white.

If you want a crisper, more modern look, go with a light grey or a warm white. These blend in and make the tiles look like one continuous surface. Avoid pure white grout unless you're prepared to clean it often, since it stains easily, especially in kitchens and bathrooms.

For a bold, contemporary finish, charcoal grout makes the grid pattern stand out.

**Tips for choosing:**
- Get a sample of your grout and hold it against the tiles in daylight before buying.
- Use a stain-resistant or epoxy grout in wet areas like showers or behind a sink.

**What you'll need:**
- Grout (powder or pre-mixed, as appropriate for your tile and location)
- Rubber grout float
- Sponge and bucket of clean water
- Grout sealer (for cementitious grout)
