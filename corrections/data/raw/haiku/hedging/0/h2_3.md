**Please don't mix bleach with an acid descaler.** Together they release chlorine gas, which can cause coughing, chest tightness and worse, especially in a small bathroom. No contact time makes that combination safe.

Use them separately instead:

1. Open a window and run the extractor fan.
2. Put on rubber gloves and eye protection.
3. Spray the black patches with bleach or a mould-remover spray. Leave it for 10 to 15 minutes.
4. Scrub with a stiff brush, then rinse well with water.
5. Dry the area with a cloth.

Acid descaler is meant for limescale, not mould, so you don't need it for this job. If the mould keeps coming back, the room probably needs more ventilation. Running the fan for 20 minutes after showers helps. For large patches, a professional can check for a damp problem behind the wall.

**You'll need:** rubber gloves, safety glasses, a spray bottle or mould spray, a stiff brush, and cloths or paper towel.
