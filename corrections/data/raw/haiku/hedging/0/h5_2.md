Space the screws about **300 mm (12 inches)** apart along each stud for walls. On ceilings, use closer spacing, about **150-200 mm (6-8 inches)**.

1. Find the studs with a stud finder, and mark their centres on the board.
2. Drive screws along every stud, keeping each one about 10 mm (3/8 inch) in from the board edges.
3. Sink each screw head just below the paper surface, without tearing the paper or breaking the board.
4. Use screws about 25 mm (1 inch) long for 9.5 mm board, or about 30-32 mm (1¼ inches) for 12.5 mm board.
5. Stagger the screw rows at the joints so the board doesn't crack.

You'll need:
- Drywall screws (coarse-thread, with a bugle-head or a drywall-specific head)
- A cordless drill or screwdriver with a drywall depth-setting nose
- A stud finder
- A pencil and tape measure
- A drywall saw or utility knife, if you need to trim the board
