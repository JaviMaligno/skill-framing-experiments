**Go with satin.** Hallways get scuffs, fingerprints, and bumps from shoes, bags, and furniture, and satin wipes clean with a damp cloth far more easily than matt.

Matt hides wall imperfections better, so it can suit a hallway with very rough or patched walls. But it marks more easily and is harder to clean. Eggshell is a good middle ground if you want something slightly less shiny than satin.

For the best finish, use a paint labeled for hallways or high-traffic areas, and choose a washable type.

If you're painting it yourself, you'll need:
- Paint in a satin or eggshell finish
- Primer, if the walls are bare, patched, or a very dark color
- Roller, tray, and a roller cover suited to your wall texture
- Angled brush for edges and corners
- Painter's tape and drop cloths
- Sandpaper or a sanding sponge for rough spots
