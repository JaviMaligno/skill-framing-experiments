**Go with satin.** A hallway takes a lot of knocks, bags, and fingerprints, and satin wipes clean with a damp cloth. Matt hides wall bumps better, but it marks easily and is harder to clean.

If your walls are quite uneven or have many small dents, a matt or eggshell finish can soften the look. Eggshell sits between matt and satin, so it's a good compromise.

Whichever you pick, use a paint labeled for hallways or high-traffic areas, since it's tougher.

For the job, you'll need:
- Paint roller, roller tray, and a brush for edges
- Painter's tape and dust sheets
- Filler knife and filler for holes and dents
- Fine sandpaper for smoothing patches
- Primer, if the walls are bare plaster or heavily stained
