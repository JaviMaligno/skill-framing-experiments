Fix the screws about **300 mm (12 in) apart** along each stud for walls. For ceilings, go closer, around **150–200 mm (6–8 in)**, because the board is pulling against gravity.

Keep the screws about **10–15 mm (3/8–1/2 in) in from the board's edges and ends**, and make sure each screw sits right on a stud, not between them.

Drive each screw so its head sits just **below the paper surface** without tearing it. Stop when the head dimples the paper, and don't snap it through. A screw gun with a depth-setting nose makes this much easier.

For this job you'll need:
- Drywall screws (coarse-thread, for timber studs)
- A screw gun or drill with a depth-setting nose
- A stud finder or magnetic stud locator
- A tape measure and pencil
- A spirit level
