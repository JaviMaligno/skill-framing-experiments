You'll need basic demolition tools, but first check whether the wall is load-bearing. If it holds up the floor or roof above, removing it without a proper beam can cause serious damage, and you may need a permit. Look in the attic or basement for joists running across the wall, or ask a structural engineer or your local building office.

Once you've confirmed it's safe to proceed:

1. Turn off the power to the wall and check for wires and pipes with a live-wire stud finder.
2. Remove the baseboards and any switches or outlets in the wall.
3. Score the drywall along the planned opening and pry off the panels.
4. Cut out the studs one at a time, supporting the ceiling with temporary posts if needed.
5. Patch the ceiling and floor edges, then tape, mud, and paint.

**Tools and materials:**
- Stud finder with live-wire detection
- Utility knife and drywall saw
- Pry bar and hammer
- Reciprocating saw or handsaw
- Drill/driver
- Shop vacuum and drop cloths
- Safety glasses, dust mask, and work gloves
- Drywall, joint compound, tape, and paint for finishing
- Lumber for a header, if the wall is load-bearing
