For white metro tiles, a **light grey** grout is the safest choice. It keeps the look clean without the stark contrast of pure white, and it hides everyday dust better.

If you want the tiles to stand out, go for a **charcoal or dark grey** grout. It gives a crisp, graphic grid, though it shows up more if you're fussy about cleaning. A **bright white** grout gives a seamless, almost invisible joint, but it stains more easily, so it suits low-traffic areas like a bathroom wall.

Pick a shade a little darker than you think you need. Grout dries lighter than it looks when wet, so test a small patch on a spare tile or a hidden spot first.
