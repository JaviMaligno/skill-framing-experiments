For white metro tiles, **light to mid grey grout** is the most popular and forgiving choice. It keeps the tiles looking clean without showing every smudge.

- **Light grey:** a subtle, classic look that blends in.
- **Charcoal or dark grey:** bold contrast that makes the grid pattern stand out.
- **Bright white:** a seamless, almost invisible joint, but it shows dirt more easily.
- **Warm grey or greige:** softens the look if your room has warm tones.

Metro tiles usually have narrow joints (about 2 to 3 mm), so pick **unsanded grout**, which is designed for thin gaps. Use a **grout sealer** after it cures to make cleaning easier.

**You'll need:**
- Unsanded grout in your chosen colour
- Rubber grout float
- Sponge and bucket of clean water
- Grout sealer (optional but recommended)
- Grout haze remover (if the tile surface gets cloudy)
