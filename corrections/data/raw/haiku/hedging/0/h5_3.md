Fix plasterboard screws about **300 mm (12 in) apart** along each stud on walls, and about **150-200 mm (6-8 in)** apart on ceilings, where the board sags more if it's loosely fixed. Keep screws about 10 mm (3/8 in) in from the board edges and 15 mm (1/2 in) from the ends.

1. Find the studs with a stud finder and mark their centres in pencil.
2. Start screws at the middle of the board and work outward, so the board stays flat.
3. Drive each screw so its head sits just below the surface, leaving a small dimple without tearing the paper.
4. Check that each screw bites firmly into the stud. If it spins without gripping, move it a few millimetres.

You'll need:
- Drywall screws, coarse-thread for timber studs (about 32 mm / 1 1/4 in for 12.5 mm board)
- A drill with a drywall or screw-gun bit that stops at the right depth
- A stud finder
- A tape measure and pencil
