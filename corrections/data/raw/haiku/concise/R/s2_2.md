**CSV export dates fixed for customers outside the UK**

A bug applied the timezone offset twice in CSV exports for UTC+ accounts. It's fixed and covered by a new test. No existing data was changed, so nothing needs backfilling.

**Impact:** CSV exports created 6–17 October for customers outside the UK may show dates one day late.

**Actions:**
- **Support:** Tell affected customers to run their export again.
- **Finance:** Re-run your September close export before Monday.
