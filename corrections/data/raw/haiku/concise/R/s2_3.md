**Subject: CSV export timezone bug fixed (affected exports from 6–17 Oct)**

A bug in the CSV exporter applied the timezone offset twice for accounts in UTC+ timezones. It is now fixed and covered by a new test. No data was changed, so nothing needs backfilling.

**Impact:** CSV exports created between 6 and 17 October for customers outside the UK showed dates one day late.

**Actions:**
- **Support:** Tell affected customers to run their export again.
- **Finance:** Re-run your September close export **before Monday**.
