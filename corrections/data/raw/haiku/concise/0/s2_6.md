**Date bug in CSV exports for customers outside the UK: fixed**

Some CSV exports showed dates one day late for customers outside the UK. The cause was a time zone adjustment that was applied twice. We've fixed it and added a test to catch it going forward.

**Who is affected:** Exports created from 6 to 17 October for customers outside the UK may show dates one day late. Exports from other dates and UK customers are not affected. We have not corrected existing export files, so affected ones need to be run again.

**What to do:**
- **Support: tell affected customers to run their export again.**
- **Finance: re-run your September close export before Monday.**

Questions go to the engineering channel.
