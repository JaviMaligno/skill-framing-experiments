**CSV export bug: dates were one day late for some exports (fixed)**

We found and fixed a bug in the CSV export. For accounts outside the UK, dates in exports were shifted forward by one day. The fix is live, and a test now covers this case. We did not change any existing data.

**Affected:** Exports created from 6 to 17 October for customers outside the UK show dates one day late.

**Support:** Please tell affected customers to run their export again. **Do this as customers reach out, and use the fixed export.**

**Finance:** Please re-run your September close export **before Monday**. Exports from before 6 October are not affected.
