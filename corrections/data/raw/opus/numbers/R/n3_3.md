On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for more than two hours. During that time, customers could not pay. If you deal with customers, this may come up. Most of the failed payments were retried automatically. But some still need manual follow-up from the support team. So if a customer mentions a failed payment that morning, send them to support. The cause was a security certificate that had expired. It sat on the server that routes our payments. Once it expired, payments could not go through. A lasting fix is now in place. Certificates now renew automatically, well before they expire. That should stop the same problem from happening again.
