The Starter plan is getting more expensive. From the first of January, new customers will pay twelve euros a month. Existing customers keep their current price until the end of June. The Pro plan does not change.

Why does this matter to you? The last time we raised prices, more customers left than usual. So we expect questions. Support is preparing for several hundred extra tickets in January. Anyone who talks to customers should be ready for them.

Sales moves first. From the first of December, every new proposal must quote the new price. That means twelve euros a month for Starter, not the old price. So if you write a proposal from December on, check the price before it goes out.
