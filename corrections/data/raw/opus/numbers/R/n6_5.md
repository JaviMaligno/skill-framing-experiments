Our team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up on last year. Most of you took part, so thank you. Your answers point to what changes next. Three requests stood out. You want fewer recurring meetings. You want clearer promotion criteria. And you want better laptops. Meetings topped the list, with about four in ten of you asking for fewer of them. So here is what happens now. Starting next month, leadership is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. And in the first quarter, leadership will publish the promotion criteria, so everyone can see what it takes to move up. That covers two of your three top asks. Keep the feedback coming.
