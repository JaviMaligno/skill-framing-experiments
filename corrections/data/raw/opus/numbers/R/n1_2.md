The problem everyone feared with value-added tax numbers in our new customer system barely happened. If you work with invoices, billing or customer accounts, that's good news. It means the details we bill customers with made the move intact. Our third-quarter data-quality audit checked every customer record we migrated. Only four had a wrong VAT number, out of nearly eight hundred. That's a tiny fraction. Not everything is as clean, though. More records are missing a postcode than last quarter. And records overall are a little less complete. So that's where the work goes next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
