On October fourteenth, our checkout was down for just over two hours. That matters to all of us, because when checkout stops, customer payments stop with it. During the outage, many payments failed, and revenue was put at risk. Most of those payments were retried automatically. The rest need manual follow-up, and the support team will handle those. So if a customer mentions a failed payment from that morning, please point them to support. The cause was an expired security certificate on our payment proxy. Here is what changes. Certificates now renew automatically, well before they expire. That closes the gap behind this outage.
