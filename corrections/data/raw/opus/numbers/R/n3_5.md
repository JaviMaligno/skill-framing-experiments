On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. During that time, many customer payments failed. That matters to everyone, because checkout is where our revenue comes in. When it stops, the whole company feels it. Most of the failed payments were retried automatically. The rest need manual follow-up from the support team. So what went wrong? A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. It is called a Transport Layer Security, or TLS, certificate. Once that certificate expired, checkout stopped working. The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically, well before they expire. That closes the gap that caused this outage.
