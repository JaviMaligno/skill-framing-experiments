This weekend, our old wiki moves to the new knowledge base. If you rely on the wiki to find answers, a few things change for you. On Saturday, search will be down for about six hours. If you need to look something up, plan around that. Most pages will convert automatically. You will not need to do anything for those. Some pages use macros, and those need a check by their owners. If you own one of them, you will get an email on Monday asking you to review it. Your saved links will keep working. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base. So if you own one of those pages, watch for that email on Monday. And from next week, look for your answers in the new knowledge base.
