Facilities has replaced the office plants on floors two, three and five. If you work on one of those floors, you have probably already noticed them. There is one simple request that comes with them. Please do not water the plants yourself. The supplier takes care of that. Watering is part of their contract, and their team comes in on a regular schedule to do it. So you can enjoy the greenery and leave the care to them. You will also find them in the meeting rooms, which each have a few of their own. There is a real mix of species, so take a look around. And if you are on floor four, your turn is coming. Floor four gets its plants as soon as the refurbishment there is finished.
