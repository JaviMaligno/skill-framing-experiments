Our third-quarter data audit is in, and the tax number problem we all feared barely happened. When we moved customer records into the new customer relationship management system, the big worry was wrong value-added tax numbers. If you sell to, bill or support customers, these records are what you rely on. Here is the result. Only four records out of nearly eight hundred had a wrong tax number. That is a tiny share. Not everything went as well. Some records came across twice. And more records are missing a postcode than last quarter. Overall, our records are a little less complete than they were. So the next step is clear. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
