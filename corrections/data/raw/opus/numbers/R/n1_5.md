The tax number problem we all feared in the move to our new customer system barely happened. Our third-quarter data-quality audit checked the customer records we moved into the new customer relationship management system, or CRM. The big worry was wrong value added tax numbers, known as VAT numbers. In the end, just four records out of nearly eight hundred had one. That is a tiny share. The audit did find other things to fix. Some records are duplicated. More records are missing a postcode than last quarter. And overall, records are a little less complete than they were. So postcodes are now the priority. A clean-up sprint starts on the third of November. Until then, the message is simple. The VAT scare is over, and the work now is on postcodes.
