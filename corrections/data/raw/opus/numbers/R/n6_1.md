Our team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up on last year. Most of you took part, so this is a clear picture of how we feel. It matters because leadership is acting on what you asked for. Your top request was fewer recurring meetings. About four in ten of you raised it. Many of you also want clearer promotion criteria. Better laptops came third. Here is what happens next. Starting next month, leadership is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. So one day a week will be free of meetings. And in the first quarter, leadership will publish the promotion criteria. So everyone can see how promotions are decided. Thanks to everyone who answered. Your feedback is shaping how we work.
