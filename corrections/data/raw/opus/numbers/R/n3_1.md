On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. That matters to everyone here. While checkout was down, customers could not complete their payments, and revenue was put at risk. Many payments failed during that window. The good news is that most of them were retried automatically. A smaller group could not be recovered that way. Our support team is now following up on each of those by hand. So what went wrong? A security certificate had expired on the payment proxy, the system that handles our payments. When that certificate ran out, payments stopped going through. Here is what changes. Certificates now renew automatically, well before they expire. That should stop this same failure from happening again.
