Last Wednesday morning, our checkout went down for more than two hours.

If you work with customers, this may reach you. During the outage, payments failed. Some revenue was put at risk as well. Most of those payments were retried automatically. But some could not be handled that way. The support team will follow those up by hand. So if a customer mentions a failed payment that morning, that is likely why.

The cause was simple. A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired.

Here is what has changed. Certificates now renew automatically, well before they expire. That closes the gap behind last week's outage.
