Last Wednesday, our checkout went down for just over two hours. If you work with customers or revenue, this affected you. While checkout was down, customer payments failed. Most of those payments were retried automatically. The rest need someone to follow up by hand, and the support team is handling them. Until that work is done, some revenue is still at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate on the payment proxy. That's a Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS for short. When it expired, checkout stopped working. Here is what has changed. Certificates now renew automatically, well before they expire. That closes the gap that caused this outage.
