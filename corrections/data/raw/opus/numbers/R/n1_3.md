The tax number problem we all feared in the move to our new customer system barely happened. If you work with customers or invoices, that is good news. It means the value-added tax numbers on our customer records came through almost entirely correct. The third-quarter data-quality audit looked at the customer records we moved across. Only four out of nearly eight hundred had a wrong tax number. That is a tiny share. The audit did find other gaps worth knowing about. Some customer records are duplicated. More records are missing postcodes than last quarter. And overall, records are slightly less complete than they were. Postcodes are the biggest of these gaps. So that is where the work goes next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
