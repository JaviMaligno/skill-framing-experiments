The results of our team survey are in, and satisfaction is up on last year. This matters because you told leadership what to change, and they are acting on it. More than three quarters of us took part, so this is a clear picture. Three requests stood out. People want fewer recurring meetings. They want clearer criteria for promotion. And they want better laptops. Here is what happens next. From next month, leadership will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. Leadership will also publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. So what it takes to move up should soon be clearer. Thank you to everyone who filled in the survey. Your answers are shaping how we work.
