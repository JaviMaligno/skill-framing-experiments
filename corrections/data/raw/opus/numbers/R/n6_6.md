The results of our team survey are in, and satisfaction is up on last year. This matters because leadership heard what you asked for, and they are acting on it. Most of us took part, so this is a clear picture of how the team feels. Your biggest ask was fewer recurring meetings. About four in ten people raised it. Clearer promotion criteria came next, and better laptops came third. Here is what happens now. From next month, leadership is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. Leadership will also publish promotion criteria in the first quarter of the year. That should make it clearer what it takes to move up. Thank you to everyone who responded. Your answers are already shaping how we work.
