Our third-quarter data audit is in, and the VAT number problem we all feared barely happened. That matters to anyone who works with customer records, because those records now live in our new CRM. When we moved them over, just four out of nearly eight hundred had a wrong VAT number. That is a tiny share. Not everything went as well, though. Missing postcodes went up compared with last quarter. We also found some duplicate records. And records overall are a little less complete than they were. So the next job is postcodes. A clean-up sprint starts on the third of November. For now, the big worry is off the table.
