Our biggest fear about the new customer system turned out to be almost nothing. In the third quarter, we audited the customer records we moved into our new customer relationship management system, or CRM. The worry everyone had was wrong VAT numbers, the value added tax numbers on customer accounts. If you work with customer records, this is the result you were hoping for. Only four records out of nearly eight hundred had a wrong VAT number. That is a tiny share. The audit did find other issues. Some records were duplicated. And more records were missing a postcode than last quarter. That is the one to watch. So the next step is a postcode clean-up sprint. It starts on the third of November.
