The Starter plan is getting more expensive. From the first of January, new customers will pay twelve euros a month instead of nine. This matters if you sell to customers, support them or talk to them. Existing customers are protected for now. They keep their current price until the end of June. The Pro plan price stays the same. Why the extra care? The last time we raised prices, more customers left than usual. Churn, the share of customers who cancel, went above two percent. Normally it sits below two. Support is getting ready for a busy January. They expect around three hundred and sixty extra tickets. Sales has an earlier deadline. From the first of December, every new proposal must quote the new Starter price. If you work with customers, start getting ready now.
