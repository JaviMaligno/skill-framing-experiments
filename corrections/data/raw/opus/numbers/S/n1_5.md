Our third-quarter data audit found that the VAT number problem we all feared barely happened. If you work with customer data or invoices, that is good news. The audit checked the customer records we moved into the new CRM, our customer relationship management system. Only four out of nearly eight hundred had a wrong VAT number. The audit did find other issues. About one record in twenty is a duplicate. Missing postcodes are the bigger worry. About one record in seven now has no postcode. Last quarter, it was closer to one in ten. Overall, records are slightly less complete than they were. So here is what happens next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
