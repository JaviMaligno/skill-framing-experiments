The tax number problem everyone feared in our customer data migration barely happened. Only four records out of nearly eight hundred had a wrong VAT, or value added tax, number. So if you were bracing for a big tax clean-up, you can relax. This comes from the third-quarter data-quality audit of the records moved into our new CRM, the customer relationship management system. Not everything looks as good, though. The real gap is postcodes. About one record in seven is missing one. Last quarter it was about one in ten. Around one record in twenty is a duplicate. And overall, records are slightly less complete than they were last quarter. So the next step is a postcode clean-up sprint. It starts on the third of November.
