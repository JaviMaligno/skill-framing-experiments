Our third-quarter data audit has good news about the problem we feared most. When customer records moved to the new CRM, the worry was wrong VAT numbers. That barely happened. Only four records out of nearly eight hundred had a wrong VAT number. So if you were bracing for VAT trouble, you can relax. Not everything is as tidy, though. Missing postcodes are the real issue. About one record in seven has no postcode. Last quarter it was about one in ten, so it is getting worse. We also found some duplicates, roughly one record in twenty. And overall, records are a little less complete than they were in the second quarter. Here is what happens next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
