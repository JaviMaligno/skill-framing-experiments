On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for a little over two hours. That matters to all of us, because when checkout stops, customers can't pay. About thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most of them were retried automatically. But around two hundred still need a manual follow-up, and the support team is handling those. In total, close to fifty thousand euros in revenue is at risk. So what went wrong? A security certificate had expired. It's called a TLS certificate, and it sat on our payment proxy, the system that passes payments along. When it expired, payments stopped going through. The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. That means this kind of outage should not catch us out again.
