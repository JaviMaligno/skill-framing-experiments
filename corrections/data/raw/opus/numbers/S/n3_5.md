On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. That matters to all of us, because when checkout stops, customers can't pay. About thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most of them were retried automatically. But just over two hundred still need manual follow-up, and the support team is handling those. In total, close to fifty thousand euros in revenue is at risk. So what went wrong? A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. It's known as a T L S certificate, which is short for Transport Layer Security. The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically, a month before they expire. That way, they shouldn't run out without anyone noticing again.
