On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. If your work touches customers or revenue, this one matters to you. For that whole stretch of the morning, customers could not pay. About thirteen hundred payments failed. Most of them were retried automatically. Around two hundred still need manual follow-up from the support team. In total, close to fifty thousand euros of revenue was at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate. It's called a T L S certificate, short for Transport Layer Security. It sat on the server that handles our payments. When it expired, payments stopped. Here is what changes. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. That should stop this from happening again.
