Our Starter plan is getting more expensive. From the first of January, new customers will pay twelve euros a month instead of nine.

If you work with customers, this affects your conversations. Existing Starter customers are protected for now. They keep their current price until the end of June. The Pro plan does not change.

We know price changes have a cost. After our last one, the share of customers leaving rose by about half a percentage point above normal. Support is preparing for that. They expect between three and four hundred extra tickets in January.

Here is what happens next. Sales starts quoting the new Starter price from the first of December. That applies to every new proposal. So if you are drafting one now, use twelve euros, not nine.
