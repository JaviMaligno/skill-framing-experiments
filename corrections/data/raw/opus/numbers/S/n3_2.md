Checkout went down for just over two hours on the morning of October fourteenth. If you deal with customers, this one matters to you. Close to thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most of them were retried automatically. But a little over two hundred still need manual follow-up from the support team. Until those are resolved, nearly fifty thousand euros of revenue is at risk. So what went wrong? A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. That's a TLS certificate, short for Transport Layer Security. The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. Next, the support team will work through the remaining payments.
