Our team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up. If you filled it in, thank you. Nearly four in five of us did. That means these results really reflect how we feel. Satisfaction rose from last year, to around seven and a half out of ten. So what do people want changed? The biggest ask was fewer recurring meetings. About two in five people raised it. Next came clearer promotion criteria. Better laptops came third. Leadership is acting on the top two. Starting next month, we'll trial meeting-free Wednesdays. And leadership will publish clear promotion criteria in the first quarter. So keep an eye on your Wednesday calendar, and watch for those criteria soon.
