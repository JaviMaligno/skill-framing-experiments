The value added tax problem we all feared in our customer data move barely happened. Our third-quarter audit checked nearly eight hundred customer records moved into the new CRM, our customer relationship management system. Only four had a wrong VAT number. That was the risk everyone worried about most, and it turned out to be tiny. The audit did find other gaps. About one record in twenty is a duplicate. Missing postcodes are the bigger issue. About one record in seven has no postcode, up from about one in ten last quarter. Overall record completeness also slipped a little since last quarter. So next comes a postcode clean-up sprint. It starts on the third of November.
