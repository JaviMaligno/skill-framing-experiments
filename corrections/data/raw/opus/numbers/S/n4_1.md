Facilities has put new plants on floors two, three and five. If you work on those floors, you will see them around you, including in the meeting rooms. Here is the one thing you need to know. Please do not water them yourself. The supplier looks after that.

There are close to fifty new plants in total. They cover more than a dozen different species. Each meeting room has three of them. The supplier has a contract that runs for a year and a half. Their team comes in to water the plants every week or so. So if a plant looks thirsty, leave it to them.

And if you are on floor four, your plants are coming too. They will arrive once the refurbishment on your floor is finished. Until then, enjoy the green on the other floors.
