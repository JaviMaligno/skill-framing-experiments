On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. If you deal with customers, you may hear about it. The outage ran from about twenty to ten until just before noon, Central European Time. Almost thirteen hundred payments failed during that time. Most of them were retried automatically. A little over two hundred were not, and the support team needs to follow up on those by hand. In total, close to fifty thousand euros in revenue was at risk. The cause was simple. A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. That's a TLS certificate, short for Transport Layer Security. To stop this happening again, certificates now renew automatically, a month before they expire. The last step is with support, who are following up on the remaining failed payments.
