On the fourteenth of October, checkout was down for just over two hours. That meant customers could not pay us. Around thirteen hundred payments failed. Most of them were retried automatically. About two hundred still need manual follow-up from our support team. Until that's done, nearly fifty thousand euros in revenue is at risk. So what went wrong? A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. It's called a TLS certificate, short for Transport Layer Security. When it lapsed, payments stopped going through. Here's what has changed. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. That leaves a full month of margin.
