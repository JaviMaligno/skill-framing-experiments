Good news from the third-quarter data audit. The tax number problem everyone feared barely happened. We moved almost eight hundred customer records into our new customer relationship management system. Only four had a wrong value added tax number, or VAT number. If you invoice customers or rely on customer data, that is the result you were hoping for. The weaker spot is postcodes. About one record in seven is now missing a postcode. Last quarter it was closer to one in ten. We also found duplicates in roughly one record in twenty. Overall, our records are slightly less complete than last quarter. So what happens next? A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November. And the VAT numbers? Four out of almost eight hundred. That is one worry we can put down.
