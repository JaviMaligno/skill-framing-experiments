Our Starter plan is getting more expensive. From the first of January, new customers will pay twelve euros a month instead of nine. The change affects support, sales and anyone who talks to customers. Customers already on Starter are protected for now. They keep paying nine euros until the end of June. The Pro plan does not change. Last time, some customers left after a price rise. After our last change, the share of customers who cancelled rose about half a percentage point above normal. Support is getting ready for a busy January. They expect between three and four hundred extra tickets. For sales, the change starts sooner. From the first of December, every new proposal must quote twelve euros for Starter.
