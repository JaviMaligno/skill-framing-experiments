Our team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up. This matters because you told leadership what to fix, and they are acting on it. Nearly four in five of us responded. That is a strong response rate. Satisfaction rose to about seven and a half out of ten. Last year it was just over seven. Three requests stood out. The biggest was fewer recurring meetings. About two in five people asked for that. Next came clearer promotion criteria. Then came better laptops. So here is what happens next. Leadership is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays, starting next month. And it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Thank you to everyone who took part.
