The results of this year's team survey are in, and satisfaction is up. If you took part, thank you. You told leadership what to fix, and they are acting on it. Nearly four in five of us responded. That is a strong showing. Overall satisfaction rose to just over seven out of ten, a small step up from last year. Three requests stood out. The biggest was fewer recurring meetings, raised by about two in five people. Next came clearer promotion criteria. Then better laptops. So here is what happens next. Starting next month, leadership will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. And in the first quarter, they will publish promotion criteria. Keep an eye out for both.
