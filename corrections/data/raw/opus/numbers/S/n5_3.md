This weekend, our old wiki moves to the new knowledge base. If you look things up in the wiki, this affects you. Nearly three thousand pages are moving, along with their attachments. Almost nine in ten pages will convert automatically. But nearly four hundred pages use macros, and those need a human check. If you own one of those pages, watch for an email on Monday. It will ask you to check your pages. There is one short disruption. Search will be down for about six hours on Saturday. So if you need to find something, try to do it before then. Your old bookmarks will still work. Links to old pages will redirect for a full year. So nothing breaks overnight. The move happens this weekend, and page owners get their email on Monday.
