Facilities has put new plants on floors two, three and five. There's one thing to remember: please don't water them yourself. The supplier takes care of that. They come in every week or so to water every plant. There are nearly fifty new plants in total. They cover fourteen different species. Each meeting room has three of them, so you'll likely spot them in your next meeting. The supplier contract runs for a year and a half. So the plants will be looked after for a good while. If you work on floor four, your plants are coming too. They'll arrive once the refurbishment is finished. Until then, enjoy the greenery on the other floors. And leave the watering to the supplier.
