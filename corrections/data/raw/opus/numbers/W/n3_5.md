On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. It went down at about twenty to ten in the morning and came back just before noon. If you deal with customers, this one matters to you. Around thirteen hundred payments failed while checkout was down. Most of them, more than a thousand, were retried automatically. But about two hundred still need a manual follow-up from the support team. In total, roughly forty-eight thousand euros of revenue is at risk. So what went wrong? A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. When it lapsed, payments couldn't go through. The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically, a full month before they expire. If a customer asks you about a failed payment from that morning, point them to support.
