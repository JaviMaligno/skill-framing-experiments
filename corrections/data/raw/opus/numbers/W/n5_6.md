Our old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend. If you rely on the wiki to find things, here's what that means for you. On Saturday, search will be down for about six hours, so plan around that. Your old links will keep working. They'll redirect to the new pages for a full year. This is a big move. We're talking nearly three thousand pages across sixty-one spaces. Almost nine in ten of those pages convert automatically. Just under four hundred pages use macros, and those need a check by the people who own them. If one of those pages is yours, you'll get an email about it on Monday. So, the short version. Expect search to be offline for part of Saturday, keep using your old links, and watch your inbox on Monday if you own wiki pages.
