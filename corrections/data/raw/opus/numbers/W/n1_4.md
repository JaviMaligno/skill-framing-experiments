The value added tax problem we all feared in our customer data migration barely happened. Our third-quarter data-quality audit checked seven hundred and ninety-one customer records moved into the new customer relationship management system. Only four had a wrong VAT number. Four. That's barely half a percent. So if you deal with invoices or billing, you can breathe out on that one. The real gap is somewhere else. Postcodes. About one record in seven is now missing one. Last quarter it was closer to one in ten. There are also some duplicates, roughly one record in twenty. Overall, our records are a little less complete than they were back in the second quarter. So here's what happens next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
