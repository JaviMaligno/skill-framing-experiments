On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. It ran from about twenty to ten until just before noon, Central European Time. If you deal with customers or revenue, this one matters to you. About thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most of them went through on an automatic retry. But just over two hundred still need manual follow-up from the support team. In total, close to fifty thousand euros in revenue was at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate on our payment proxy. That's a Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS. Here's the fix. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
