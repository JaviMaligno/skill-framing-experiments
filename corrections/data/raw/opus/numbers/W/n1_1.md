Good news from our third-quarter data-quality audit. The VAT number problem we all worried about barely happened. If you work with customers, that's a real relief. When we moved our customer records into the new CRM, only four out of nearly eight hundred had a wrong VAT number. That's about half a percent. Not everything is as tidy, though. About one in seven records is now missing a postcode. Last quarter it was roughly one in ten. We also found a few dozen duplicate records. Overall, our records are still about ninety-four percent complete. That's slightly down from last quarter. So the next job is postcodes. A clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
