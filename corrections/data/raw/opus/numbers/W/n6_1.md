Our team survey results are in, and people are a bit happier than last year. That matters because almost eight in ten of you took part, so this really is your voice. Overall satisfaction rose to seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one. Three requests stood out. The biggest was fewer recurring meetings, raised by about four in ten of you. Next came clearer promotion criteria, from more than a third. Better laptops were third, mentioned by nearly three in ten. Leadership is acting on the first two. Starting next month, we'll trial meeting-free Wednesdays. And in the first quarter, leadership will publish the promotion criteria. So a quieter midweek is almost here.
