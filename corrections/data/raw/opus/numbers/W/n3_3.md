On the fourteenth of October, checkout was down for just over two hours. If your work touches customers or revenue, this one matters to you. From about twenty to ten until just before noon, customers couldn't pay. Almost thirteen hundred payments failed. Most went through on an automatic retry. But around two hundred still need the support team to follow up by hand. All told, about forty-eight thousand euros of revenue was at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate, known as a TLS certificate, on our payment proxy. Here's what changed. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. Support team, those two hundred or so payments are the open item.
