The results of our team survey are in, and people are a bit happier than last year. That matters because your answers are already changing how we work. More than three quarters of you responded. Overall satisfaction rose to seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one. When we asked what you want, three things stood out. The biggest was fewer recurring meetings, which about four in ten of you asked for. Next came clearer promotion criteria, mentioned by over a third of you. Better laptops came third. Leadership is acting on the top two. Starting next month, we're trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. And promotion criteria will be published in the first quarter.
