Our third-quarter data-quality audit is in, and the problem everyone feared barely happened. We moved almost eight hundred customer records into the new CRM, our customer relationship management system. The fear was wrong value added tax numbers, or VAT numbers. Just four records had one. That's about half of one percent. For anyone who works with invoices or customer tax details, that's good news. The real problem is postcodes. About one record in seven is missing a postcode, and that's worse than last quarter. We also found duplicates, roughly one record in twenty. And overall, records are a little less complete than they were last quarter. So next up is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
