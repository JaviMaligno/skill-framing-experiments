Our third-quarter data audit is in, and the VAT problem we were all bracing for barely happened. Of nearly eight hundred customer records moved into the new CRM, just four had a wrong VAT number. That's about half a percent. For anyone who relies on customer tax details, the migration held up where it mattered most. The audit did find a different gap. About one record in seven is now missing a postcode, up from roughly one in ten last quarter. That pulled overall completeness down a little, from about ninety-five percent to just under ninety-four. There were also a few dozen duplicates to tidy up. So what's next? Starting the third of November, there's a clean-up sprint to fill in those missing postcodes.
