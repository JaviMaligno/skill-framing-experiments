On the fourteenth of October, checkout was down for just over two hours. That meant customers couldn't pay, and real revenue was on the line. About thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most of them, just under eleven hundred, went through on an automatic retry. But just over two hundred did not. The support team is now following up on each of those by hand. In all, close to fifty thousand euros in revenue was at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate on our payment proxy. It's what's known as a TLS, or Transport Layer Security, certificate. Here's what changes. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. So this kind of failure shouldn't catch us out again.
