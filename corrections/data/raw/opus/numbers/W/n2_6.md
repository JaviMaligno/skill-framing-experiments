Our Starter plan is getting more expensive. From the first of January, new customers will pay twelve euros a month instead of nine.

Why does this matter to you? The last time we raised prices, more customers left. Churn hit two point three percent, against a normal level of one point eight. So how we handle this change really counts.

Here's the detail. Existing Starter customers keep paying nine euros until the end of June. The Pro plan doesn't change. It stays at twenty-nine euros.

What happens next? If you're in Sales, every new proposal from the first of December should quote twelve euros. If you're in Support, get ready for a busy January. The team expects about three hundred and sixty extra tickets. And if a customer asks you, keep it simple. New customers pay twelve from January. Current customers keep their price through June.
