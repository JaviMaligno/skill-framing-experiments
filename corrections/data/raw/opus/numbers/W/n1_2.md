The value added tax problem everyone feared in our move to the new CRM barely happened. That's the headline from the third-quarter data-quality audit. We moved nearly eight hundred customer records. Only four came across with a wrong VAT number. Just four. So the tax details in the new system are in good shape. The audit did flag other work. About one record in twenty is a duplicate. And postcodes are the real gap. One record in seven has no postcode, up from about one in ten last quarter. Overall, records are a little less complete than before. Completeness slipped from about ninety-five percent to just under ninety-four. Here's what happens next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
