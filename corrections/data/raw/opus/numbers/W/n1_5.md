The VAT number problem everyone feared in our CRM migration barely happened. Our third-quarter data-quality audit checked the customer records we moved to the new CRM. Just four out of nearly eight hundred had a wrong VAT number. So if you were bracing for a mess there, you can relax. The audit did find other gaps. About one record in twenty is a duplicate. And postcodes are now the bigger worry. Roughly one record in seven has no postcode, up from about one in ten last quarter. Overall, records are a little less complete than they were last quarter, at just under ninety-four percent. Next up is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November. The VAT worry is behind us. Postcodes are next.
