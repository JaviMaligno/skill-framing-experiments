This weekend, the old wiki moves to our new knowledge base. If you ever look things up in the wiki, this affects you. The move covers almost three thousand pages and all their attachments. Nearly nine in ten pages will convert on their own. Just under four hundred pages use macros, and those need a check by whoever owns them. If that's you, look out for an email on Monday. On Saturday, search will be down for about six hours. If you need to find something that day, plan around it. Your old links will keep working, too. They'll redirect to the new pages for a full year. So, to recap: the move happens this weekend, search takes a break on Saturday, and page owners hear from us on Monday.
