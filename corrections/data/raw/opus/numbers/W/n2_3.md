Our Starter plan is getting more expensive. From the first of January, new customers will pay twelve euros a month instead of nine. Customers already on Starter keep paying nine euros until the end of June. The Pro plan doesn't change. It stays at twenty-nine euros. Why does this matter to you? The last time we changed prices, more customers left than usual. Churn, the share of customers who cancel, went up to two point three percent, compared with a usual one point eight. Support is getting ready for a busy January, with roughly three hundred and sixty extra tickets expected. And for Sales, there's one date to remember. From the first of December, every new proposal must quote twelve euros.
