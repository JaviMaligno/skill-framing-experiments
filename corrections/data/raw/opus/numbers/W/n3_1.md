On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours in the middle of the morning. If you deal with customers or revenue, this one matters to you. About thirteen hundred payments failed while checkout was down. The good news is that most of them went through on an automatic retry. But around two hundred still need a manual follow-up from the support team. In total, close to fifty thousand euros of revenue was at risk. The cause was simple. A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. Here is what changed. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
