On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for a little over two hours. That meant customers could not pay us for most of a morning. It ran from about twenty to ten until just before noon, Central European Time. Around thirteen hundred payments failed in that window. Most of them went through on an automatic retry. But just over two hundred still need someone from the support team to follow up by hand. Until those are sorted out, close to fifty thousand euros in revenue is at risk. The cause was a single expired certificate. It was a Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS, on our payment proxy. When it expired, payments stopped going through. That has now been fixed. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire. So the support team's follow-ups are the last open piece.
