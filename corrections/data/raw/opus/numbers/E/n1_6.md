Our third-quarter data audit is in, and the problem everyone feared barely happened. We moved 791 customer records into the new customer relationship management system, or CRM. Only 4 had a wrong value added tax number, or VAT number. So if you were bracing for tax number errors, you can relax. The audit did find other gaps. About one record in twenty is a duplicate. Postcodes are a bigger issue. Around one record in seven now has no postcode. Last quarter it was about one in ten. Overall, records are a little less complete than last quarter. Here's what happens next. A clean-up sprint for postcodes starts on the third of November. But the headline is this. Four bad VAT numbers out of 791.
