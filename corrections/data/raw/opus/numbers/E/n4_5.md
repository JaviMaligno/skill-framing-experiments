Facilities has put new plants on floors two, three and five. If you work on one of those floors, there is one thing to know. Please do not water them yourself. The supplier handles all the watering, roughly once a week. There are close to fifty new plants, from more than a dozen species. Each meeting room has three of them. The supplier looks after them under a contract that runs for a year and a half. And if you are on floor four, your plants are coming too. They will arrive once the refurbishment there is finished. So enjoy the new greenery. Just leave the watering to the supplier.
