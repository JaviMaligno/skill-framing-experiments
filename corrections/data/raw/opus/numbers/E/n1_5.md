Our third-quarter data quality audit is in, and the VAT number problem we all feared barely happened. That is good news for anyone who works with customer data. We moved seven hundred and ninety-one customer records into the new CRM. Only four had a wrong VAT number. Four out of seven hundred and ninety-one. That is about half of one percent. The picture is not all good, though. Around one record in twenty is a duplicate. And missing postcodes went up. About one record in seven now has no postcode. Last quarter it was about one in ten. Overall, our records are slightly less complete than last quarter. So the next step is a postcode clean-up sprint. It starts on the third of November.
