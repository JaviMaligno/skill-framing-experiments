Our third-quarter data audit brings good news on the risk we feared most. We moved our customer records into the new customer relationship management system, the CRM. The worry was wrong value added tax numbers, or VAT numbers. That problem barely happened. Only four records out of seven hundred and ninety-one had a wrong VAT number. The issue everyone was bracing for turned out to be tiny. There is still work to do. About one record in twenty is a duplicate. And missing postcodes are rising. Roughly one record in seven now has no postcode, up from about one in ten last quarter. Overall, records are a little less complete than last quarter. So here's what's next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
