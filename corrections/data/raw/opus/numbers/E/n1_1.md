Our third-quarter data audit is in, and the VAT number problem everyone feared barely happened. If you were bracing for bad tax details on customer records, you can relax. We moved 791 customer records into the new CRM, our customer relationship management system. Only 4 had a wrong VAT number. That's 4 out of 791. Well under one percent. There is one area that needs work. Missing postcodes went up. About one record in seven now has no postcode. Last quarter it was closer to one in ten. We also found some duplicate records, around five percent. And overall, records are slightly less complete than before. So here's what's next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
