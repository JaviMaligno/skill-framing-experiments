Our team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up. This matters because you told us what to fix, and leadership is acting on it. Nearly four in five of us responded. Satisfaction rose to seven point four out of ten. Last year it was seven point one. The biggest ask was fewer recurring meetings. About two in five of you raised it. Next came clearer promotion criteria. More than a third asked for that. Better laptops came third, mentioned by nearly three in ten. So here is what happens next. Starting next month, leadership is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. And in the first quarter, they will publish the promotion criteria. Thanks to everyone who took part.
