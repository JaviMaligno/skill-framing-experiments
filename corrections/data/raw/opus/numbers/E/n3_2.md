On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. That matters to all of us. Customers could not pay, and some of that money is still at stake. About thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most of them were retried automatically. But two hundred and thirteen still need manual follow-up from the support team. Around forty-eight thousand euros of revenue is at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate. It sat on the payment proxy, the server that passes our payments along. When the certificate expired, checkout stopped working. The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. Next, the support team will work through those two hundred and thirteen payments.
