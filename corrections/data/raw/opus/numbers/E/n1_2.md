Good news from the third-quarter data audit. The tax number problem everyone feared barely happened. We moved 791 customer records into the new customer relationship management system, our CRM. Only 4 of them had a wrong VAT number. VAT means value added tax. That's 4 out of 791. So the risk we planned for turned out to be tiny. There is a weaker spot, though. It's postcodes. About one record in seven is missing its postcode. That's worse than last quarter, when it was about one in ten. We also found some duplicates. That's roughly one record in twenty. And overall, records are a little less complete than they were last quarter. Next comes a postcode clean-up sprint. It starts on the third of November.
