The Starter plan is going up from 9 euros to 12 euros a month. If you deal with customers, this changes what you tell them, and when. Here is how it works. New customers pay 12 euros from the first of January. Existing customers keep paying 9 euros until the end of June. The Pro plan does not change. It stays at 29 euros. The last time we changed prices, more customers left than usual. Churn, meaning the share of customers who cancel, rose from 1.8 percent to 2.3 percent. So we need to be ready. Support is planning for about 360 extra tickets in January. And Sales, this starts sooner for you. From the first of December, every new proposal must quote 12 euros.
