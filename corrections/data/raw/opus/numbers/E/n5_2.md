This weekend, our old wiki moves to the new knowledge base. If you use the wiki to find answers or share your team's work, this affects you. The move covers nearly three thousand pages across about sixty spaces. Almost nine in ten pages will convert automatically. Just under four hundred pages use macros, and their owners need to check them. If you own one of those pages, you'll get an email on Monday. On Saturday, search will be down for about six hours, so plan around that. Your links to old pages will keep working. They will redirect to the new pages for twelve months. Most of you don't need to do anything. If you get that email on Monday, please check your pages.
