On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. That matters to all of us, because it put real revenue at risk and left work for our support team. The outage ran from about twenty to ten until just before noon. In that time, around thirteen hundred payments failed. Most were retried automatically. But two hundred and thirteen still need manual follow-up by support. Around forty-eight thousand euros of revenue is at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate, known as a TLS certificate, on our payment proxy. Here's what changed. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
