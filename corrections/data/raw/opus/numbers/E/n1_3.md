The value added tax number problem we all feared barely happened. Our third-quarter data audit checked 791 customer records moved into the new CRM, our customer relationship management system. Only 4 had a wrong VAT number. That's 4 out of 791. This was the risk everyone worried about going into the move. It turned out to be tiny. On tax numbers, the migration went well. The audit did find other things to fix. About one record in twenty is a duplicate. Missing postcodes are the bigger issue. About one record in seven now has no postcode. Last quarter it was closer to one in ten. Overall, records are slightly less complete than last quarter. That's where the work goes next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
