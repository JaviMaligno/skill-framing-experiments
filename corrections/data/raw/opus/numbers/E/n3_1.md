On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. If your work touches customers, sales or revenue, this matters to you. The outage ran from about twenty to ten until just before noon, Central European Time. In that window, close to thirteen hundred payments failed. Most of them were retried automatically. But two hundred and thirteen still need manual follow-up from the support team. In total, about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue is at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate, known as a TLS certificate, on our payment proxy. Here is what has changed. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. If a customer asks you about a failed payment that day, the support team is following up.
