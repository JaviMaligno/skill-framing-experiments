Our team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up. Here's what you told us, and what leadership is doing next. Nearly four in five of us took part. Our overall satisfaction score rose to 7.4 out of 10. Last year it was 7.1. So what do people want? The biggest ask was fewer recurring meetings. About four in ten of us raised it. Next came clearer promotion criteria, from more than a third of us. Better laptops came third. Here's what happens next. From next month, leadership is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. And in the first quarter, leadership will publish the promotion criteria. Thanks to everyone who filled it in.
