On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. If you deal with customers, this matters to you. From around twenty to ten until just before noon, Central European Time, payments could not go through. About thirteen hundred payments failed. Most of them succeeded on an automatic retry. But just over two hundred still need a manual follow-up from the support team. In total, about forty-eight thousand euros of revenue is at risk. The cause was simple. A security certificate, known as a T L S certificate, had expired on our payment proxy. So what happens next? The support team will follow up on the remaining payments. And certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
