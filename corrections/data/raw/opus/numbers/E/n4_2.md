Facilities has put new plants on floors two, three and five. If you work on one of those floors, you may have already noticed. There is one thing to know. Please do not water them yourself. The supplier takes care of that. They come in every week or so to water them.

In total there are nearly fifty new plants. They cover more than a dozen species. Each meeting room now has three of them. The supplier contract runs for a year and a half.

And floor four? It gets its plants once the refurbishment is finished. Until then, enjoy the new green on the other floors. And leave the watering to the professionals.
