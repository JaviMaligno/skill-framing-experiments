On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. Whatever team you're on, that was a morning when customers couldn't pay us. Almost thirteen hundred payments failed. Most of them were retried automatically. But two hundred and thirteen still need manual follow-up, and that work falls to the support team. In total, about forty-eight thousand euros of revenue is at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate, known as a TLS certificate, on our payment proxy. Nothing more complicated than that. Here's the fix. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. If you're in support and working through those two hundred and thirteen payments, thank you.
