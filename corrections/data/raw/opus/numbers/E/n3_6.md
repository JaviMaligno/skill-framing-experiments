On the morning of 14 October, our checkout was down for just over two hours. That matters to all of us, because checkout is where our revenue comes in. Around thirteen hundred payments failed during the outage. Most were retried automatically. But just over two hundred still need manual follow-up from the support team. In total, about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue is at risk. So what went wrong? A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. It's known as a Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS for short. The fix is now in place. These certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. And the support team will follow up on the remaining failed payments.
