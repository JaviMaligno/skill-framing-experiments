Our team survey is in, and satisfaction is up on last year. That matters because your answers are already changing how we work. Almost four in five of you responded. That's more than four hundred people. Satisfaction rose to seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one. You also told us what you want. The top ask was fewer recurring meetings, from about four in ten of you. Next came clearer promotion criteria, from just over a third. Then better laptops, from nearly three in ten. Leadership is acting on the first two. Starting next month, we're trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. And promotion criteria will be published in the first quarter. Thanks to everyone who took the time to respond.
