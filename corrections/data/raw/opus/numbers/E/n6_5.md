Our team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up. That matters because leadership is already acting on what you told them. Nearly four in five of you responded. Satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of ten, up from 7.1 last year. So what do you want changed? The biggest ask was fewer recurring meetings. About four in ten of you said that. Next came clearer promotion criteria, from more than a third of you. Better laptops came third, at close to three in ten. Here is what happens next. Starting next month, leadership will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. And in the first quarter, they will publish the promotion criteria.
