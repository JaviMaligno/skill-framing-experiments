On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. That was from nine forty-one to eleven fifty-eight, Central European Time. This matters because customers could not pay us, and money is still at stake. During the outage, one thousand two hundred eighty-four payments failed. One thousand seventy-one of them were retried automatically. The other two hundred thirteen need manual follow-up from our support team. In total, forty-eight thousand three hundred twenty euros of revenue is at risk. The root cause was an expired security certificate on our payment proxy. That is a TLS certificate, short for Transport Layer Security. Here is what changes. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
