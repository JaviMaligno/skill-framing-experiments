The Starter plan goes up from nine euros to twelve euros a month on the first of January. That new price is for new customers. Existing customers keep paying nine euros until the thirtieth of June. The Pro plan stays at twenty-nine euros. Why does this matter? Because price changes can cost us customers. Churn is the share of customers who leave. After our last price change, churn was two point three percent. Our normal baseline is one point eight percent. Support expects between three hundred forty and three hundred eighty extra tickets in January. Here is what happens next. From the first of December, sales must quote twelve euros in all new proposals. Pro customers will see no change.
