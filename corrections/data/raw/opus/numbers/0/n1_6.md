The VAT problem we all worried about in the move to the new CRM barely happened. Only four of seven hundred and ninety-one customer records had a wrong VAT number. That's about half of one percent. So if you work with invoices or tax, you can breathe easier. But the third-quarter data-quality audit found a different problem. Postcodes. One hundred and twelve records are missing one. That's fourteen point two percent, up from nine point eight percent in the second quarter. The audit also found thirty-seven duplicate records, or four point seven percent. And average record completeness slipped to ninety-three point six percent, down from ninety-five point one. What happens next? A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
