On the fourteenth of October, checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. It went down at 9:41 in the morning, Central European Time, and came back at 11:58. If you work with customers, this one matters to you. During the outage, 1,284 payments failed. Most of them, 1,071, were retried automatically. But 213 still need manual follow-up from the support team. The revenue at risk is 48,320 euros. So what went wrong? A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. That's a TLS certificate, short for Transport Layer Security. Here's the fix. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire. Next up for support: following up on those 213 payments.
