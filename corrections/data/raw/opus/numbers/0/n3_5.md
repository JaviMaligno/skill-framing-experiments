On the fourteenth of October, checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. It went down at nine forty-one in the morning, Central European Time, and came back at eleven fifty-eight. This matters because real customer payments failed, and some of them still need attention. In total, one thousand two hundred and eighty-four payments failed. One thousand and seventy-one were retried automatically. The other two hundred and thirteen need manual follow-up. Forty-eight thousand, three hundred and twenty euros in revenue is at risk. The root cause was an expired certificate on our payment proxy. It was a Transport Layer Security certificate, often called TLS. So what happens next? The support team will follow up on those two hundred and thirteen payments by hand. And certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
