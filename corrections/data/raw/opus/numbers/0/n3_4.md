On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. It went down at nine forty-one in the morning, Central European Time, and came back at eleven fifty-eight. That matters to all of us, because for that whole time, customers could not pay. One thousand two hundred and eighty-four payments failed. One thousand and seventy-one of them were retried automatically. The other two hundred and thirteen need manual follow-up from our support team. In total, forty-eight thousand three hundred and twenty euros of revenue is at risk. The cause was an expired security certificate on our payment proxy. That's a Transport Layer Security certificate, or TLS. The fix is already in place. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
