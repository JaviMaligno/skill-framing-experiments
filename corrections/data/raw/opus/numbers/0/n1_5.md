The tax number problem we all feared barely happened. Our third-quarter data-quality audit checked 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system, the CRM. Only 4 had a wrong value added tax, or VAT, number. That is about half a percent. If you work with invoices or customer accounts, that is good news. The migration barely touched VAT details. The real gap is postcodes. 112 records are missing one. That is 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent last quarter. The audit also found 37 duplicate records, or 4.7 percent. And average record completeness slipped to 93.6 percent, down from 95.1. So what happens next? A postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November.
