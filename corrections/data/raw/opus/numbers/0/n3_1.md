On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. That means customers could not pay us, and some of that money is still at risk. The outage ran from nine forty-one to eleven fifty-eight in the morning, Central European Time. In that window, one thousand two hundred eighty-four payments failed. Our systems retried one thousand and seventy-one of them automatically. That leaves two hundred and thirteen payments. The support team will follow up on each of those by hand. In total, forty-eight thousand three hundred and twenty euros of revenue is at risk. The cause was simple. A security certificate on our payment proxy had expired. Engineers call it a TLS certificate. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
