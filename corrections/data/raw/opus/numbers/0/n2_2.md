Our Starter plan is going up from nine euros to twelve euros a month. This affects Sales, Support and our customers, so here is what you need to know. The new price applies to new customers from the first of January. Existing customers keep paying nine euros until the thirtieth of June. The Pro plan does not change. It stays at twenty-nine euros. We have been here before. After our last price change, churn, meaning the share of customers who cancel, was two point three percent. Our normal level is one point eight percent. Support expects between three hundred forty and three hundred eighty extra tickets in January. So what happens next? From the first of December, Sales must quote twelve euros in every new proposal.
