Our latest team survey is in, and satisfaction is up. It matters because leadership is already acting on two of your biggest requests. Seventy-eight percent of us responded. That's four hundred and twelve people out of five hundred and twenty-eight. Overall satisfaction rose to seven point four out of ten. Last year it was seven point one. So what do people want? The top ask was fewer recurring meetings. Forty-one percent of respondents said that. Next came clearer promotion criteria, at thirty-six percent. Then better laptops, at twenty-nine percent. Here's what happens next. Starting next month, leadership is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. And in the first quarter, leadership will publish the promotion criteria.
