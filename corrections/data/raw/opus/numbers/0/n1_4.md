The VAT number problem everyone feared in our new CRM barely happened. Out of seven hundred and ninety-one customer records we migrated, only four had a wrong VAT number. That's about half of one percent. So the risk most of us were bracing for turned out to be tiny.

Those numbers come from the third-quarter data-quality audit. Not everything looked as good. Thirty-seven records were duplicates. That's just under five percent. Missing postcodes are the bigger issue. A hundred and twelve records have no postcode. That's fourteen point two percent, up from nine point eight percent last quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, from ninety-five point one percent to ninety-three point six.

So here's what's next. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
