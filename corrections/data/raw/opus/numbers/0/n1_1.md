The VAT problem we all feared in the CRM migration barely happened. Our third-quarter data-quality audit checked the 791 customer records moved to the new CRM. Only 4 had a wrong VAT number. That is about half of one percent. So if you were worried about wrong VAT numbers on customer records, you can let that go. The bigger gap is postcodes. In total, 112 records are missing one. That is 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. We also found 37 duplicates, or 4.7 percent. Average record completeness slipped to 93.6 percent, from 95.1 percent last quarter. What happens next? A postcode clean-up sprint starts on 3 November.
