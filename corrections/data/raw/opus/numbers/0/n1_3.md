The third-quarter data-quality audit is in, and the problem we feared most barely happened. We moved 791 customer records into the new CRM. Only 4 had a wrong value added tax number, or VAT number. That's about half of one percent. So if you bill customers or handle tax, that worry can be put to rest. Not everything improved, though. One hundred and twelve records are missing a postcode. That's 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent last quarter. We also found 37 duplicates, or 4.7 percent. And average record completeness slipped to 93.6 percent, down from 95.1. So what's next? A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
