Our team survey results are in, and satisfaction is up. Your answers are leading to two concrete changes. 412 of 528 of you responded. That's a response rate of 78 percent. Overall satisfaction scored 7.4 out of 10. Last year it was 7.1. Three requests stood out above the rest. Fewer recurring meetings came top, at 41 percent. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at 36 percent. Then better laptops, at 29 percent. Leadership is acting on the first two. From next month, we'll trial meeting-free Wednesdays. And in the first quarter, leadership will publish the promotion criteria. Thanks to everyone who took part.
