It also carries twelve point four gigabytes of attachments. Eighty-seven percent of pages will convert automatically. But three hundred and seventy-nine pages use macros. Those pages need a check by their owners. If you own one, you'll get an email on Monday. Search will be down for about six hours on Saturday. So plan around that. Your old bookmarks will still work.
