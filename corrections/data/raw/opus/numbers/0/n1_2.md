Our third-quarter data-quality audit found that the VAT number problem we all feared barely happened. Only 4 of the 791 customer records moved to the new CRM had a wrong VAT number. That's about half of one percent. That matters to anyone who bills customers or handles tax. Other numbers need attention, though. The audit found 37 duplicate records. That's 4.7 percent. Missing postcodes are the bigger concern. 112 records have none. That's 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent last quarter. Overall, records are 93.6 percent complete. Last quarter, that figure was 95.1 percent. So what happens next? A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the 3rd of November. Until then, the headline is simple. Four wrong VAT numbers out of 791.
