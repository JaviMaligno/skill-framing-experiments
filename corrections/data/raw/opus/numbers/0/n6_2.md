Our latest team survey is in, and satisfaction is up. It rose to seven point four out of ten, from seven point one last year. Why does this matter? You told leadership what to change, and they are acting on two of your top requests. Four hundred and twelve of you responded, out of five hundred and twenty-eight. That is a seventy-eight percent response rate. Three requests stood out. Forty-one percent of you want fewer recurring meetings. Thirty-six percent want clearer promotion criteria. And twenty-nine percent want better laptops. Here is what happens next. From next month, leadership is trialling meeting-free Wednesdays. And in the first quarter, they will publish the promotion criteria. If you took the survey, thank you.
