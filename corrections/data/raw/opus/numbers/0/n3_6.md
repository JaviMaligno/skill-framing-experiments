On the fourteenth of October, our checkout was down for two hours and seventeen minutes. That matters because forty-eight thousand three hundred and twenty euros in revenue was at risk. The outage ran from nine forty-one to eleven fifty-eight, Central European Time. In that window, one thousand two hundred and eighty-four payments failed. One thousand and seventy-one of them were retried automatically. The other two hundred and thirteen need manual follow-up from the support team. The cause was an expired certificate on the payment proxy. It was a Transport Layer Security certificate, known as TLS. Here's what changes. Certificates now renew automatically, thirty days before they expire.
