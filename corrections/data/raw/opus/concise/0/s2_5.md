**CSV export bug: some dates were one day late (now fixed)**

We found a bug in CSV export. For accounts in time zones ahead of UTC, the time zone adjustment was applied twice, which moved dates forward by a day. The bug is fixed and we've added a test so it doesn't come back.

Existing exports haven't been corrected. Any export created between 6 and 17 Oct for a customer outside the UK may show dates one day late. Exports created now come out correct.
