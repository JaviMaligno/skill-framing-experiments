**CSV export: dates one day late for some customers (now fixed)**

We found a bug in CSV export. For accounts in time zones ahead of UTC, the time zone adjustment was applied twice, so dates came out one day late. The fix is live, and we added a test so this doesn't happen again.

*Who's affected:* exports created between 6 and 17 October by customers outside the UK. Their dates are one day late. Files exported in that period have **not** been corrected automatically.

*What to do:*
• **Support: tell affected customers to run their export again.** New exports will have the correct dates.
• **Finance: re-run your September close export before Monday.**
