**Dashboard is now much faster**

We reworked how the dashboard pulls its numbers. Instead of crunching everything each time someone opens it, the totals are now pre-calculated and refreshed every 5 minutes.

The dashboard now loads much faster for customers and for us. Even the slowest loads (all but the slowest 5%) take about 0.6 seconds, down from 4.2. Because totals refresh every 5 minutes, figures can be up to 5 minutes behind live data.

No action needed from anyone.
