It depends on the look you want, but **light grey** is the most popular choice and the safest bet.

- **Light grey:** Gives a soft outline to each tile and hides dirt and staining much better than white. It suits most kitchens and bathrooms.
- **White:** Gives a clean, seamless, bright look. It works best on walls that don't get splashed much, because white grout shows grime and discolours over time, especially behind a hob or sink.
- **Dark grey or charcoal:** Makes the brick pattern stand out for a bolder, industrial or Victorian feel. It hides dirt very well. Bear in mind it shows every uneven joint, so your tiling needs to be neat.

If you're unsure, pick light to mid grey. It looks smart and is easy to live with.

Put a little grout on a spare tile and let it dry before you commit, because colours dry lighter than they look when wet.

**You'll need:**
- Wall grout in your chosen colour (a stain-resistant or anti-mould type for wet areas)
- Rubber grout float
- Sponge and bucket
- Grout finishing tool
- Rubber gloves, because wet grout irritates the skin
