**Go for satin, or a "durable/scrubbable" matt if you really want a flat look.**

Hallways get knocked by bags, shoes, hands and the vacuum, so the paint needs to survive being wiped. Ordinary matt hides wall bumps well, but it marks easily and can go shiny or patchy where you scrub it. Satin has a soft sheen that wipes clean easily and holds up much better.

**A common setup:**
1. **Walls:** satin, or a washable matt if your walls are uneven. Sheen shows every dent and filler patch.
2. **Woodwork (skirting, doors, frames):** satin or gloss. These take the most kicks.
3. **Ceiling:** plain matt. Nobody touches it, and matt hides flaws.

If you have kids or pets, or the hallway is narrow, satin is the safer choice.

**You'll need:**
- Satin or durable matt emulsion
- Filler and fine sandpaper
- Sugar soap or mild degreaser for cleaning the walls
- Masking tape and dust sheets
- Roller (short-to-medium pile), tray and an angled brush for cutting in
