**Don't mix them.** Bleach plus an acid descaler gives off chlorine gas, which can seriously hurt your lungs even in a small bathroom with the window open. It also won't clean any better. Use them separately, on different days, and rinse well in between.

**For the black mould, use the bleach on its own:**

1. Open a window and turn on the extractor fan. Put on gloves.
2. Mix about 1 part bleach to 4 parts water in a spray bottle, or use a ready-made mould spray.
3. Spray the affected grout or sealant and leave it for **10–15 minutes**.
4. Scrub with an old toothbrush or grout brush, rinse with clean water, and dry the area.
5. Repeat if marks remain. If the stain has gone deep into silicone sealant, cut the old sealant out and reseal, because bleach won't reach it.

**To stop it coming back:** run the fan for 20 minutes after showers and wipe down wet surfaces.

Save the descaler for limescale on taps and shower heads, and only once the bleach has been fully rinsed away.

**You'll need:** rubber gloves, spray bottle, grout brush, cloths, and possibly sealant remover, a sealant gun and mould-resistant bathroom silicone.
