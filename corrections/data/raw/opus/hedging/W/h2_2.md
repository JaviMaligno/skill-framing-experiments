**Don't mix them. Bleach plus an acid descaler gives off chlorine gas, which can seriously hurt your lungs even in a small bathroom.** The mix isn't any stronger on mould either. Use one product at a time.

**For black mould on grout and sealant:**

1. Open a window and run the extractor fan.
2. Put on gloves and eye protection.
3. Apply diluted bleach or a mould spray. Leave it on for **10–15 minutes**. On stubborn spots, lay a bleach-soaked paper towel over the area for a few hours.
4. Scrub with a stiff brush, rinse well and dry the area.
5. Keep the descaler for limescale, and use it on a different day after the bleach is fully rinsed off.

**If it comes back:** mould that's grown into silicone sealant usually won't clean out. Cut the old bead out with a sealant remover tool and run a new bead of mould-resistant silicone. To stop it returning, ventilate after showers and wipe surfaces down.

**You'll need:** bleach or mould spray, gloves, goggles, a stiff brush, and maybe a sealant remover tool and mould-resistant silicone.
