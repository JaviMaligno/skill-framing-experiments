**Go for satin, or a durable "washable" matt if you prefer a flat look.** Hallways get a lot of knocks from bags, shoes, prams and hands, so the walls need to be easy to wipe clean.

**How to choose:**
- **Satin (or silk/eggshell):** has a low sheen, is tough and wipes clean easily. The downside is that the shine shows up dents and patchy plaster more.
- **Durable/scrubbable matt:** gives a flat, modern look that hides wall imperfections, and it can still be wiped clean. Ordinary matt marks easily and scuffs if you scrub it, so don't use that in a hallway.
- **Woodwork (skirting, doors, banisters):** use satin or gloss whichever wall finish you pick, because these get kicked the most.

**Rule of thumb:** if the walls are smooth, use satin. If they're a bit bumpy, use durable matt.

**You'll need:**
- Durable matt or satin wall paint
- Satin or gloss paint for the woodwork
- Sugar soap and a sponge
- Filler and fine sandpaper
- Masking tape and dust sheets
- Roller, tray and an angled brush
