It depends on the look you want, but **light grey is the safest all-round choice** for white metro tiles. It looks clean, hides dirt far better than pure white, and still gives a soft outline to each tile.

**Your main options:**

1. **White**: a seamless, bright look that suits small bathrooms. The downside is that it shows stains quickly, especially behind a hob or around a shower.
2. **Light or mid grey**: subtle definition, low maintenance, and it works in almost any room.
3. **Charcoal or black**: a bold, graphic look that makes the brick pattern stand out. It suits kitchens and industrial styles. Your tiling needs to be neat, because every uneven joint will show.

**Tip:** grout dries lighter than it looks when wet. Do a test patch on a spare tile first.

**What you'll need:**
- Grout in your chosen colour (go for a stain-resistant or epoxy type in kitchens and showers)
- Grout float
- Grouting sponge and bucket
- Grout sealer (if you're using cement-based grout)
