For a classic look, go with **light grey grout** on white metro tiles. It shows off the brick pattern a little, looks crisp, and hides dirt much better than white.

**Your main options:**

1. **White or bright white.** Gives a seamless, minimal look. It's the brightest choice, but it shows staining quickly, especially on floors or behind a hob.
2. **Light or mid grey.** This is the most popular choice. It suits nearly any kitchen or bathroom and is easy to live with.
3. **Charcoal or black.** Makes a bold, graphic grid that works well in industrial or Victorian-style rooms. Your tiling needs to be neat, though, because dark grout shows every uneven line.

**Tips:**
- Before you commit, grout a spare tile or a bit of board and let it dry fully. Grout dries lighter than it looks when wet.
- On splashbacks and in showers, use an epoxy or stain-resistant grout, which stays clean much longer.

**You'll need:** grout, a rubber grout float, a grout sponge, a bucket, a mixing paddle and grout sealer (not needed with epoxy).
