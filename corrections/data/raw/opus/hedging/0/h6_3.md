**For a clean, classic look, go with white or bright white grout.** The tiles and lines blend together, so the wall looks fresh and seamless, which works well in small bathrooms and kitchens.

Other good options, depending on the look you want:

1. **Light grey:** This is the most practical choice. You still get a soft, subtle look, but it hides dirt and staining much better than pure white. It's a good pick for splashbacks behind hobs and sinks.
2. **Mid or dark grey:** This brings out the brick pattern and gives an industrial or modern feel. It also hides dirt the best.
3. **Black or charcoal:** This gives a bold, graphic contrast. It looks great, but every uneven tile or grout line will show, so neat tiling matters.

A tip: if you can, test a little grout on a spare tile first. Grout often dries a shade lighter or darker than it looks in the tub.

**You'll need:**
- Grout in your chosen colour (a stain-resistant or epoxy type is good for kitchens)
- Rubber grout float
- Grout sponge and bucket
- Grout sealer (for cement-based grout)
