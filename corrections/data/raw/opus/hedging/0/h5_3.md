For walls, put screws **about 200–300 mm (8–12 in) apart** along each stud. On ceilings, space them closer, **about 150–200 mm (6–8 in)**, because gravity pulls the board down. The board maker's instructions take priority, so check the pack for their spacing.

**How to fix the board:**

1. Make sure each board edge lands on the middle of a stud. Studs are usually 400 or 600 mm apart.
2. Keep screws **10–15 mm in from the board edges** so the edges don't crumble.
3. Use plasterboard screws long enough to go at least 20 mm into the wood. That's about 32 mm screws for 12.5 mm board, or 38 mm for 15 mm board.
4. Drive each screw so the head sits just below the surface. It should make a slight dimple without tearing the paper face. A dimpler bit stops the screw at the right depth for you.
5. Put screws into every stud the board crosses, not just the edges.

**You'll need:**
- Plasterboard sheets
- Plasterboard screws (coarse thread for wood studs)
- Cordless drill/driver with a dimpler bit
- Tape measure, pencil and a straightedge
- Utility knife for cutting boards
- Stud finder, if you're fixing to existing framing
