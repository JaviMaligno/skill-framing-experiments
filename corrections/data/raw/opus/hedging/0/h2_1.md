**Please don't mix them.** Bleach plus an acid descaler gives off chlorine gas. Even a small amount in a closed bathroom can damage your lungs. It also won't clean any better. If you've already mixed some, open the window, leave the room, and come back only once the air has cleared. Then flush the mixture away with plenty of water.

**Using one product at a time works well:**

1. Open a window or turn on the extractor fan, and put on gloves.
2. Use bleach on its own, either diluted about 1 part to 4 parts water or as a ready-made mould spray. Apply it to the black mould.
3. Leave it for **10–15 minutes**, then scrub with a stiff brush and rinse with clean water.
4. If you want to descale too, do it on a different day. At the very least, rinse well first so no bleach is left.

If the mould is deep in silicone sealant, it won't come out. Cut the old sealant away and put in fresh mould-resistant sealant. After that, run the fan during showers and for a while afterwards.

**You'll need:** rubber gloves, eye protection, a stiff brush, a spray bottle, mould remover or bleach, and possibly a sealant remover tool and mould-resistant silicone.
