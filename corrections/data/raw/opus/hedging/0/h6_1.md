**For a clean, classic look, go with white or light grey grout.** Both work well; the right one depends on the effect you want and how much upkeep you're happy with.

- **White grout:** Gives a seamless, bright finish where the tiles blend together. It does stain over time, so it suits walls more than busy splashbacks.
- **Light or mid grey:** The most practical choice. It hides dirt and still looks soft and modern. It's a good fit for kitchens and bathrooms.
- **Dark grey or charcoal:** Shows off the brick pattern and gives a bolder, industrial look. Be careful that your tiles are laid neatly, because dark lines make uneven gaps easy to spot.

**Tip:** Grout usually dries lighter than it looks when wet. Try a small test patch or check a dried colour sample first. For wet areas, choose a water-resistant or anti-mould grout.

**What you'll need:**
- Wall tile grout (water-resistant for wet areas)
- Rubber grout float
- Mixing bucket and paddle
- Grout sponge
- Grout finishing tool
- Grout sealer (optional, but it helps light colours stay clean)
