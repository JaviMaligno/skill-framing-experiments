**Please don't mix them.** Bleach plus an acid descaler gives off chlorine gas. It isn't stronger at killing mould, and even a small amount in a closed bathroom can burn your eyes and lungs. If you've already mixed some, leave the room, open the windows, and come back once the smell has completely gone.

Bleach on its own works well on black mould on hard surfaces. Here's how to use it:

1. Open a window and turn on the extractor fan.
2. Mix bleach with water, about 1 part bleach to 10 parts water, or use a ready-made mould spray.
3. Spray or wipe it on and **leave it for 10–15 minutes**.
4. Scrub with a stiff brush, rinse with clean water and dry the area.
5. If the mould is deep in silicone sealant, it won't come out. Cut out the old sealant and apply new mould-resistant sealant.

Keep the descaler for limescale and use it on a different day. Rinse well between the two products.

**You'll need:** rubber gloves, eye protection, a mask, a stiff brush, a spray bottle and cloths. For old sealant you'll also want a sealant removal tool and mould-resistant silicone.
