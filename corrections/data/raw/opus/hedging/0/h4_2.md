**For most hallways, go with satin.** Hallways get a lot of wear from bags, shoes, hands and the vacuum cleaner. A satin finish has a slight sheen, so it's tougher and easier to wipe clean. You can usually sponge off a scuff mark without leaving a shiny patch.

**When matt makes sense:**
- Your walls have dents, patches or uneven plaster. Matt doesn't reflect light, so it hides flaws better.
- You prefer a soft, modern look.

If you want the matt look, choose a **durable or scrubbable matt** wall paint. It's made to handle cleaning much better than standard matt.

**A popular combination:**
1. Ceiling: standard matt
2. Walls: satin or scrubbable matt
3. Woodwork (skirting, doors, frames): satin or gloss, because it takes the most knocks

**What you'll need:**
- Wall paint in satin or scrubbable matt
- Satin or gloss paint for the woodwork
- Medium-pile roller and tray
- 2" angled brush for edges
- Masking tape and dust sheets
- Filler and fine sandpaper for any dents
