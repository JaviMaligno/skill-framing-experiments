**Please don't mix them.** Bleach plus an acid descaler gives off chlorine gas, which can badly hurt your lungs even in a small bathroom with the window open. It doesn't make the cleaner work any better, and it's one of the most common ways people get hurt cleaning at home. Use one product at a time. For mould, bleach is the right one.

**How to treat the mould with bleach on its own:**

1. Open a window and turn on the extractor fan.
2. Put on rubber gloves and eye protection.
3. Mix about 1 part bleach to 4 parts water, or use a ready-made mould spray.
4. Spray or sponge it onto the mould and leave it for **10–15 minutes**.
5. Scrub with a stiff brush, rinse with clean water, and dry the area well.
6. On silicone sealant, if the black marks are still there after two goes, the mould has grown into the sealant. Cut the old sealant out and apply new.

If you want to use the descaler for limescale, do that on a different day, after rinsing the bleach off completely.

If you ever smell a sharp, swimming-pool smell while cleaning, get out and let fresh air in.

**You'll need:** rubber gloves, safety glasses, a spray bottle, a stiff brush, cloths. For bad sealant, add a sealant removal tool and mould-resistant silicone.
