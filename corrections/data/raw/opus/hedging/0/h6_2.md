**For a clean, classic look, go with white or a soft light grey.** Both are popular for different reasons:

- **White grout** blends in, so the wall reads as one smooth, bright surface. It's ideal in small bathrooms. The downside is that it shows dirt and discolours over time, especially on floors or behind a hob.
- **Light or mid grey** is the most forgiving choice. It still looks fresh, picks out each tile gently and hides everyday grime far better than white.
- **Charcoal or black** gives a bold, graphic look that suits kitchens and industrial styles. Lay tiles carefully, because dark grout shows up every uneven gap.

**Tips:**
1. Test a small patch on a spare tile first. Grout usually dries a shade lighter than it looks when wet.
2. In wet areas, choose a flexible, water-resistant wall grout.
3. Apply grout sealer once it has cured so it stays clean longer.

**You'll need:** wall grout, a rubber grout float, a grout sponge, a bucket, grout sealer and a spare tile for testing.
