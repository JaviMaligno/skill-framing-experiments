**Don't mix them.** Bleach plus an acid descaler gives off chlorine gas, which can seriously hurt your lungs even in a small bathroom. The mix also doesn't kill mould any better. Use one product at a time.

**What to do instead:**

1. Open a window or turn on the extractor fan.
2. Scrub off any loose mould with a stiff brush and a little soapy water, then let it dry.
3. Apply diluted bleach (about 1 part bleach to 4 parts water) or a mould-removal spray. Leave it for **15–20 minutes**. On grout or sealant, let it sit up to an hour.
4. Scrub again, rinse with clean water and dry the area well.
5. If you want to use the descaler for limescale, do it on another day, after you've rinsed the bleach away completely.

If the mould is inside the silicone sealant, it won't come out. Cut the sealant out and replace it.

**You'll need:** rubber gloves, a stiff brush or old toothbrush, a spray bottle, a cloth, and anti-mould silicone sealant with a sealant knife if you're redoing the seal.
