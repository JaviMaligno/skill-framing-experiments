For white metro tiles, **light grey** is usually the best choice. It gives the tiles a crisp outline and hides dirt much better than pure white.

**Your main options:**

1. **Light or mid grey:** The safe, popular choice. The grid shows softly, it suits both modern and classic rooms, and it stays looking clean for longer.
2. **White:** Gives a smooth, seamless look that makes a small room feel bigger. It discolours over time, especially behind sinks and hobs, so it works better on walls than in splash zones.
3. **Charcoal or black:** Makes the brick pattern stand out strongly for an industrial or café look. Lay the tiles neatly, because dark grout shows every uneven joint.

**Tip:** In kitchens and showers, use an epoxy or stain-resistant grout. It soaks up less grease and water, so the colour stays even for longer.

**What you'll need:**
- Grout in your chosen colour (cement-based or epoxy)
- Rubber grout float
- Grout sponge and bucket
- Grout finishing tool or jointer
- Microfibre cloth for buffing off haze
- Grout sealer (for cement-based grout)
