**Don't mix them.** Bleach plus an acid descaler releases chlorine gas, which can seriously damage your lungs even in a small bathroom. It doesn't make the cleaner stronger, just dangerous. Use them separately, on different days.

**For black mould, use the bleach on its own:**

1. Open a window or run the extractor fan.
2. Dilute bleach about 1 part to 10 parts water in a spray bottle (or use a ready-made mould spray).
3. Spray the mouldy grout or sealant and **leave it 10–15 minutes**.
4. Scrub with a stiff brush, rinse with clean water, and dry the area.
5. Repeat for stubborn stains.

**Save the descaler** for limescale on taps and shower screens another time. Rinse thoroughly after the bleach first so no residue is left to react.

If the mould has gone deep into silicone sealant, cleaning won't fully shift it. Cut the old sealant out and reseal with mould-resistant silicone.

**You'll need:**
- Bleach or mould remover spray
- Spray bottle
- Stiff brush or old toothbrush
- Rubber gloves
- Cloth for drying
