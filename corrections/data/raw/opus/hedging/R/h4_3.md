For a hallway, go with **satin**. Hallways take a lot of knocks from bags, shoes, hands, pets and vacuum cleaners. Satin has a slight sheen and a harder surface, so scuffs and fingerprints wipe off without leaving a shiny patch or taking the paint with them.

**When matt makes more sense:** Matt hides dents, filler patches and uneven plaster much better than satin, which picks up the light and shows every flaw. If your walls are in rough shape, choose a **durable or scrubbable matt**. It looks flat like normal matt but stands up to cleaning much better.

**A common split that works well:**
1. **Walls:** satin, or durable matt if the walls are uneven
2. **Woodwork (skirting, door frames, doors):** satin or gloss for the toughest finish
3. **Ceiling:** standard matt, since nobody touches it

**What you'll need:**
- Satin or durable matt emulsion for the walls
- Satin or gloss trim paint for the woodwork
- Filler and sandpaper
- Sugar soap for cleaning the walls first
- Roller and tray, plus an angled brush for cutting in
- Masking tape and dust sheets
