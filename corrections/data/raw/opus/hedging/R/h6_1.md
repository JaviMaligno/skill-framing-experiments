**Light grey is the safest choice.** It looks clean, hides dirt much better than pure white, and shows just enough of the tile shape to give metro tiles their classic look.

**Other options, depending on the look you want:**

1. **White grout:** A seamless, bright look that works well in small bathrooms. It does stain over time, especially on floors and around sinks and showers.
2. **Mid or charcoal grey:** Shows off the brick pattern more. It suits kitchens and gives a slightly industrial feel.
3. **Black:** A strong, graphic contrast. Your tiling has to be very neat, because every uneven gap will stand out.

**Tips:**
- Before you commit, spread a little grout on a spare tile and let it dry. Dry grout is often lighter than it looks wet.
- In wet areas or behind a hob, a stain-resistant grout (epoxy or a "stain-block" cement grout) is worth paying a bit more for.

**You'll need:**
- Grout in the colour you've chosen
- Rubber grout float
- Grout sponge and a bucket
- Grout finishing tool, or the end of a dowel, to smooth the joints
- Grout sealer (for cement grout)
