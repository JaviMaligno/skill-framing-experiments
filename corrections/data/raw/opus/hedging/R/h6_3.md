**Light grey is the safest choice for white metro tiles.** It keeps the clean look and shows each tile's shape, and it hides dirt much better than white grout does.

Other good options, depending on the look you want:

1. **White grout**: the surface reads as one smooth, bright wall. It looks very clean, but it stains fastest, so it's a poor fit behind a hob or sink.
2. **Light or mid grey**: the best all-rounder. It gives soft definition between tiles and is easy to keep clean.
3. **Charcoal or black**: a bold, graphic grid with a classic "Paris metro" or industrial feel. Choose this one only if your tiling is neat, because it shows every uneven gap.

For kitchens and bathrooms, choose a grout labelled **anti-mould** or **water-resistant**, and seal it once it has cured. That keeps any colour looking fresh for longer.

**What you'll need:**
- Grout in your chosen colour (anti-mould for wet areas)
- Rubber grout float
- Mixing bucket and paddle
- Grout sponge and clean water
- Grout sealer and a small brush
