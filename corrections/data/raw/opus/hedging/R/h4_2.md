**Go with satin.** Hallways get scuffed by bags, shoes, hands and pets more than any other room, and satin's slight sheen means you can wipe off marks without leaving a shiny patch or taking the paint off.

**When matt is the better choice:** if your walls are uneven, patched or old plaster, matt hides those flaws much better because it doesn't reflect light. In that case, use a **scrubbable (durable) matt**. It's made to handle washing while keeping the flat look.

**A good combination:**
1. **Walls:** satin, or scrubbable matt if the walls are bumpy.
2. **Woodwork (skirting boards, door frames, banisters):** satin or gloss. These take the most knocks and need the toughest finish.
3. **Ceiling:** standard matt. Nobody touches it, and matt reduces glare from lights.

**What you'll need:**
- Satin or scrubbable matt wall paint
- Satin or gloss paint for the woodwork
- Medium-pile roller and tray
- 50 mm angled brush for cutting in (painting the edges and corners a roller can't reach)
- Filler and fine sandpaper
- Masking tape and dust sheets
