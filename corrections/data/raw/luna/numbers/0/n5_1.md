This weekend, the old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base. That means our shared reference material will be in one place, though search will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. The migration covers 2,914 pages across 61 spaces, plus 12.4 gigabytes of attachments. Eighty-seven percent of pages will convert automatically. The remaining 379 pages use macros and need to be checked by their owners. Those owners will get an email on Monday with what to do. Links to old pages will redirect to the new pages for 12 months, so existing bookmarks and references should continue to work during that period.
