The third-quarter data-quality audit found that only four of 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had the wrong value-added tax number. That is the issue many people were most concerned about, and it barely happened. The bigger problem is missing postcodes: 112 records, or 14.2 percent, compared with 9.8 percent in the second quarter. There were also 37 duplicate records, and average completeness fell to 93.6 percent from 95.1 percent last quarter. These figures show where clean-up work can have the greatest impact. A postcode clean-up sprint is scheduled to start on the third of November.
