The team survey shows satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. This matters because the results point to practical changes that could improve employees’ day-to-day work. The response rate was 78 percent, with 412 of 528 people taking part. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, named by 41 percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next at 36 percent, followed by better laptops at 29 percent. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. The company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month.
