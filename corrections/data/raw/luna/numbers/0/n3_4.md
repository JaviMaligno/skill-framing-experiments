On 14 October, checkout was unavailable for two hours and 17 minutes. This affected payments and left revenue at risk. The outage ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. There were 1,284 failed payments. The system retried 1,071 automatically, but the support team still needs to follow up manually on 213. The incident put 48,320 euros in revenue at risk. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. To prevent this from happening again, certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
