The third-quarter data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system. That is just over half a percent.

That matters because the VAT problem people feared barely happened. The bigger issue is missing postcodes: 112 records, or 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent last quarter.

There were also 37 duplicate records, and average record completeness fell to 93.6 percent from 95.1 percent in the second quarter.

Next, a postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November. The audit shows the VAT concern was limited, but postcode quality and completeness need attention.
