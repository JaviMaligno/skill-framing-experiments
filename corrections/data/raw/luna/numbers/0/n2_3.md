The Starter plan price will rise from 9 euros to 12 euros per month on 1 January for new customers. Existing customers will keep paying 9 euros until 30 June, and the Pro plan stays at 29 euros.

This matters if you work with new customers or support them. Sales must quote the new 12-euro price in all proposals issued from 1 December. After the last price change, churn was 2.3 percent, compared with a baseline of 1.8 percent. Support expects 340 to 380 extra tickets in January.

So, teams should prepare for the new pricing and the likely increase in customer questions.
