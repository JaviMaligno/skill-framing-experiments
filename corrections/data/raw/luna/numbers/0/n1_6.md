The Q3 data-quality audit found that only four of 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had a wrong value-added tax number. That is just over half a percent, so the problem many people feared barely happened. For anyone working with customer information, the bigger issue is missing postcodes. They were absent from 14.2 percent of records, up from 9.8 percent in Q2. The audit also found 37 duplicate records, or 4.7 percent. Average record completeness was 93.6 percent, down from 95.1 percent in Q2. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
