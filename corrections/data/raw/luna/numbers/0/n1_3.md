The Q3 data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system. That is 0.51 percent, so the VAT problem many people feared barely happened. For teams relying on customer data, the bigger issue is missing postcodes. They were missing from 14.2 percent of records, up from 9.8 percent in Q2. There were also 37 duplicate records, or 4.7 percent. Average record completeness fell to 93.6 percent, compared with 95.1 percent in Q2. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on 3 November.
