On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for two hours and seventeen minutes, from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time.

That meant customers could not complete payments during the outage, and some transactions still need attention.

There were 1,284 failed payments. The system retried 1,071 automatically, while 213 need manual follow-up from the support team. Revenue at risk was 48,320 euros.

The cause was an expired certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
