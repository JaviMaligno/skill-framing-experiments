This weekend, the old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base. That means a temporary search outage, and a small number of pages will need owners to check them after the move.

The old wiki has 2,914 pages across 61 spaces, with 12.4 gigabytes of attachments. Eighty-seven percent of the pages will convert automatically. The remaining 379 pages use macros, so their owners will need to review them. Those owners will get an email on Monday.

Search will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base for 12 months.
