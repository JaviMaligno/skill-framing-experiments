This weekend, the old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base. That means employees will have a new place to find company information, but search will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. The migration covers 2,914 pages across 61 spaces, plus 12.4 gigabytes of attachments. Most pages—87 percent—will convert automatically. The remaining 379 pages use macros and need to be checked by their owners. Those owners will get an email on Monday with next steps. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base for 12 months, so existing bookmarks and references should keep working during that period.
