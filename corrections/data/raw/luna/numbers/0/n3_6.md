Checkout was unavailable on 14 October for two hours and 17 minutes. If you tried to pay during that time, your payment may have failed or need follow-up. There were 1,284 failed payments. The system retried 1,071 automatically, while 213 need manual follow-up from the support team. The incident put 48,320 euros in revenue at risk. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
