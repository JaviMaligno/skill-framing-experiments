The team survey shows satisfaction has risen to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. That’s encouraging, and the feedback also points to practical changes employees want to see. The survey had a 78 percent response rate, with 412 of 528 people taking part. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, cited by 41 percent. Clearer promotion criteria came next at 36 percent, followed by better laptops at 29 percent. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays. These steps address two of the most common requests, and the trial will give teams a chance to see how a meeting-free day works in practice.
