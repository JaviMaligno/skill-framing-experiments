Checkout was unavailable for two hours and 17 minutes on 14 October, from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. This matters because the outage disrupted payments and put revenue at risk. In total, 1,284 payments failed. The system retried 1,071 automatically, but 213 still need follow-up from the support team. The incident put 48,320 euros in revenue at risk. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire. That change addresses the cause of the outage and should prevent this specific certificate issue from recurring.
