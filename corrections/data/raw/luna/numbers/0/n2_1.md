Starting January first, the monthly price of the Starter plan will rise from nine euros to twelve euros for new customers. This matters for anyone preparing customer proposals or supporting the transition. Existing customers will keep paying nine euros until June thirtieth, and the Pro plan remains at twenty-nine euros. After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects three hundred forty to three hundred eighty extra tickets in January. Sales teams must quote the new twelve-euro price in all proposals for new customers starting December first.
