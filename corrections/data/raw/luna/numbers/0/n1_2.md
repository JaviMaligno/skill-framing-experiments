The third-quarter data-quality audit found four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system. That’s just over half a percent, so the VAT problem many people feared barely happened. Duplicates were more common: 37 records, or 4.7 percent. The bigger concern is missing postcodes. They affected 112 records, or 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent. This matters because missing details and duplicates can make customer records harder to use reliably across teams. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
