The team survey drew responses from 78 percent of employees, or 412 people out of 528. That gives us a broad view of how people are feeling and what would make work better. Satisfaction came in at 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, named by 41 percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at 36 percent, followed by better laptops at 29 percent. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
