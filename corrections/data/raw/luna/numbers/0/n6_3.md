The team survey had a 78 percent response rate, with 412 of 528 people taking part. That gives us a broad snapshot of how colleagues are feeling. Satisfaction scored 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, raised by 41 percent of respondents. Next came clearer promotion criteria at 36 percent, and better laptops at 29 percent. Leadership says it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays. That trial will show whether a protected day without meetings can help address one of the team’s top requests.
