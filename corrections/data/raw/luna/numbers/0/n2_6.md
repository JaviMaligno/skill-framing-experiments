The Starter plan’s monthly price is rising from 9 euros to 12 euros for new customers on 1 January. This matters if you prepare customer proposals or support customers on Starter. Existing customers keep their 9-euro price until 30 June, and the Pro plan remains at 29 euros. After the last price change, churn was 2.3 percent, compared with a baseline of 1.8 percent. Support expects 340 to 380 extra tickets in January, so teams should be ready for more questions. Sales must quote the new 12-euro price in all new proposals from 1 December.
