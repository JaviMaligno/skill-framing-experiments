This weekend, the old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base. That means a temporary change to where teams find information, but most pages will move automatically. The old wiki has 2,914 pages across 61 spaces, with 12.4 gigabytes of attachments. Eighty-seven percent of pages will convert automatically. The remaining 379 pages use macros and need to be checked by their owners. Those owners will get an email on Monday with more information. Search will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday, so plan around that if you’ll need to find something during the migration. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base for 12 months.
