The team survey shows satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The results point to a few practical changes employees want, alongside a stronger overall satisfaction score. The survey had a 78 percent response rate, with 412 responses out of 528. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, raised by 41 percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at 36 percent, followed by better laptops at 29 percent. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. And from next month, it will trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
