The team survey had a 78 percent response rate, with 412 of 528 people taking part. That gives us a broad view of how colleagues are feeling and what they want to improve. Overall satisfaction is 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, chosen by 41 percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at 36 percent, followed by better laptops at 29 percent. Leadership says it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. The company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. Those steps address two of the top concerns, and the trial should give teams a chance to see whether a protected day makes a difference.
