The third-quarter data-quality audit found just four wrong value-added tax numbers among 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system. That is 0.51 percent, so the problem many people feared barely happened. But there are other gaps worth attention. The audit found 37 duplicate records, or 4.7 percent. Missing postcodes affected 112 records, or 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent. That matters because incomplete records can make customer information less reliable across teams. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on 3 November.
