This weekend, the old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base. That means employees will have a new place to find company information, though search will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. The old wiki contains 2,914 pages across 61 spaces, along with 12.4 gigabytes of attachments. Eighty-seven percent of pages will convert automatically. The remaining 379 pages use macros and need to be checked by their owners. Those owners will get an email on Monday with next steps. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base for 12 months, so existing bookmarks and references should continue to work during that period.
