The Starter plan price is increasing from nine euros to twelve euros per month on January first for new customers. If you already have the plan, your price stays at nine euros until June thirtieth, and Pro remains at twenty-nine euros.

For teams that speak with customers, the change means new proposals must show the higher Starter price. Sales should quote twelve euros starting December first.

There’s a reason to prepare for questions. After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a one point eight percent baseline. Support expects three hundred forty to three hundred eighty extra tickets in January.

So, update proposals from December first, and expect more customer questions as the new price takes effect in January.
