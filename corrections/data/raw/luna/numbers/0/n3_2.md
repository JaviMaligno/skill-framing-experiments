On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for two hours and seventeen minutes. That meant customers could not complete payments, and some orders now need attention from support.

There were 1,284 failed payments during the outage. The system automatically retried 1,071 of them. The support team still needs to follow up on the remaining 213. Revenue at risk was 48,320 euros.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire. This change is intended to prevent the same certificate issue from disrupting checkout again.
