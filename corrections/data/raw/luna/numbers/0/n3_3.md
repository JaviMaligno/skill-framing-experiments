On 14 October, checkout was unavailable for two hours and 17 minutes.

That meant customers could not complete payments during the incident, and some transactions need follow-up.

There were 1,284 failed payments. The system automatically retried 1,071 of them. The support team needs to follow up manually on the remaining 213. Revenue at risk was 48,320 euros.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security, or TLS, certificate on the payment proxy.

Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire. That change is intended to prevent the same issue from happening again.
