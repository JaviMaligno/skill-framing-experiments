The team survey shows satisfaction has improved since last year. That matters because the results also point to practical changes that could make work better across the company. Nearly eight in ten people responded, and satisfaction reached 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership says it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It is also trialling meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. The survey gives leadership a clear view of what employees want, and the next step is to see whether those changes make a difference.
