The team survey shows that overall satisfaction has risen since last year. This matters because the results point to practical changes that could improve people’s day-to-day work. The strongest request was for fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. That gives teams a chance to see whether setting aside one day for focused work helps. The survey also drew responses from more than three-quarters of the team, giving leadership a broad set of views to consider.
