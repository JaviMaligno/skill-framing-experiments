Checkout was unavailable on October 14 because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired. This matters because customers couldn’t complete purchases, and some payments still need attention from our support team. The outage lasted a little over two hours. Most failed payments were retried automatically, but 213 need manual follow-up. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk. To reduce the chance of this happening again, certificates now renew automatically before they expire.
