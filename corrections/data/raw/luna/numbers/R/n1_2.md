The third-quarter audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among the 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system. That’s reassuring, because the VAT problem many people feared barely happened. But the audit also points to a bigger data-quality concern: missing postcodes have increased since last quarter. More complete records help teams across the company rely on the CRM and reach customers with accurate information. The audit found duplicates too, and overall record completeness was lower than in the second quarter. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
