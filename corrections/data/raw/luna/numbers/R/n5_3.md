This weekend, the old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base. The migration affects everyone who uses wiki pages, so expect search to be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. Most pages will convert automatically, but some use macros that need a check from their owners. Those owners will get an email on Monday with what to review. Links to old pages will keep redirecting for a year, giving teams time to update bookmarks and references. After the migration, the new knowledge base will be the place to search for company information.
