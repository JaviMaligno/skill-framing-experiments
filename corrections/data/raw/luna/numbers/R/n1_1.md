The third-quarter data-quality audit found just four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system. That’s reassuring, because the VAT problem people feared barely happened. But missing postcodes are a bigger issue, and overall records are less complete than last quarter. The audit found duplicates too, though the main concern is the postcode gap. Missing postcodes rose from about one in ten records last quarter to about one in seven this quarter. Average completeness also slipped, so teams may need to check customer details more carefully when using the new system. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
