The team survey shows satisfaction is up, and leaders are responding to what employees asked for. That matters because the results point to practical changes in how we work and how people can grow here. Just over three quarters of the team responded, and satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from last year. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays. Those steps address two of the main themes in the feedback. The survey also highlights equipment as an area employees want improved, though no action on that was announced.
