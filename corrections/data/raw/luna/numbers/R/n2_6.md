The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers on the first of January. This matters if you’re preparing proposals or helping customers plan their budgets. From the first of December, Sales must quote the new monthly price of twelve euros in all new proposals. Existing customers will keep paying nine euros until the end of June, and the Pro plan price is unchanged. After the last price change, churn was slightly higher than its usual level.
