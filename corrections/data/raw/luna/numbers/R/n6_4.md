The latest team survey shows satisfaction is up from last year. That matters because the feedback helps us see what’s working and where teams want change. The strongest request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. In response, leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. The company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. That gives teams a chance to see whether a quieter midweek schedule helps, while the new criteria should make promotion expectations clearer.
