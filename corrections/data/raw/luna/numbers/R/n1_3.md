The third-quarter data-quality audit found very few wrong VAT numbers in records moved to the new customer system. That matters because this was the problem many people feared, and it barely happened. Just four of the 791 records had an incorrect VAT number.

The bigger issue is missing postcodes. They were missing from about one in seven records, up from roughly one in ten last quarter. Overall, record completeness also slipped slightly.

The audit found duplicates too, but the main priority now is postcode clean-up. A clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
