The latest team survey shows satisfaction has improved, and employees have two clear priorities for change. That matters because the results point to practical steps that could improve people’s day-to-day work and career clarity. Satisfaction rose to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. Those steps address two of the top concerns; the survey also gives leaders a signal to keep the laptop request in view.
