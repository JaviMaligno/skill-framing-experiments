Facilities has replaced the office plants on floors two, three, and five. The new plants are there to make shared work and meeting spaces more welcoming, and the supplier will take care of watering them. There are 46 plants across 14 species, with three plants in each meeting room. The supplier will visit every week or so, under an 18-month contract. Please don’t water the plants yourself, even if they look thirsty; that could mean they get too much water. Floor four will get its plants after the refurbishment is complete.
