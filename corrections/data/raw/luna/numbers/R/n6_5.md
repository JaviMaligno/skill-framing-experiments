The team survey shows satisfaction has risen, and staff want clearer promotion criteria and fewer recurring meetings. This matters because the results point to practical changes that could improve the day-to-day experience across teams. Satisfaction scored 7.4 out of 10, up from last year. The leading request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria; better laptops also came up. Leadership says it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. The trial should show whether setting aside one day a week without meetings helps people focus.
