The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers on the first of January. This matters if you’re preparing proposals or supporting customers through the change. New customers will pay twelve euros a month, while existing customers keep their current price until the end of June. Pro remains at twenty-nine euros. After the last price change, churn was slightly higher than its usual baseline. Support expects a noticeable rise in January tickets. Sales should quote the new Starter price in all proposals issued from the first of December.
