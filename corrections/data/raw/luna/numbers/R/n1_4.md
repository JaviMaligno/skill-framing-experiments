The Q3 data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among the customer records moved to the new CRM. That is reassuring for teams who were worried about VAT errors, but postcodes need attention. Missing postcodes rose since the previous quarter, and average record completeness also slipped. The audit also found duplicate records, which can make customer information harder to trust and use. The VAT issue affected just four of 791 records. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November. That is the next step toward improving the quality of the migrated data.
