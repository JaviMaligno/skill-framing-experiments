The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the feared VAT-number problem barely happened: only four customer records had the wrong number. That’s reassuring, but the audit also points to a more common issue that could affect teams relying on accurate customer data. Missing postcodes rose to about fourteen percent, while overall record completeness slipped compared with the previous quarter. There were also duplicate records, so the data still needs attention beyond VAT. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
