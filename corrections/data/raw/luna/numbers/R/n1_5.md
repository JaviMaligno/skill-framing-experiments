The Q3 data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among the customer records moved to the new CRM. That is good news for everyone who was worried about VAT errors. The bigger issue is missing postcodes, which have increased since the previous quarter. Overall record completeness has also slipped. The audit found duplicates too, but the main takeaway is that the feared VAT problem barely happened. The team’s next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on 3 November.
