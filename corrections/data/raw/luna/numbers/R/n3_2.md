Checkout was unavailable on 14 October because the payment proxy’s security certificate had expired. This matters because some payments did not go through, and a portion still needs help from our support team. Of the failed payments, most were retried automatically, while 213 need manual follow-up. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk. We’ve changed certificate renewal so certificates now renew automatically a month before they expire. That addresses the cause of this outage and should help prevent the same kind of interruption. Support will need to follow up on the remaining affected payments.
