The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers, while existing customers get a transition period.

If you’re preparing proposals or supporting customers, the timing matters. Sales should quote the new price from the start of December, and support may see more questions in January.

The monthly price rises from 9 euros to 12 euros on 1 January for new customers. Existing customers keep their current price until the end of June. Pro remains at 29 euros. After the last price change, churn was higher than its usual baseline, and support expects several hundred extra tickets in January.

The change takes effect on 1 January.
