On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired. This matters because customers could not complete purchases during the outage, and some payments still need attention. Of the failed payments, most were retried automatically, but 213 require manual follow-up from the support team. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk. The cause has been addressed: certificates on the payment proxy now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
