The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers on the first of January. This matters if you’re preparing proposals or supporting customers through the change. From the first of December, Sales must quote twelve euros a month for Starter in all new proposals. Existing customers will keep paying nine euros until the end of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros. After the last price change, customer cancellations were higher than the usual baseline, though the source doesn’t say what will happen this time. Support expects several hundred extra tickets in January.
