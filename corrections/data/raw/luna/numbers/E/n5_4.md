The old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base this weekend.

For employees, that means a short search outage and a few pages that need a human check. Search will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday.

Most pages will convert automatically. That’s 87 percent. The remaining 379 pages use macros and need to be checked by their owners. Those owners will get an email on Monday.

The migration covers nearly three thousand pages across 61 spaces, along with 12.4 gigabytes of attachments. Links to old pages will redirect to the new location for 12 months.
