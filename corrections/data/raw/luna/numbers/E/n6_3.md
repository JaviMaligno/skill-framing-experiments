The team survey shows satisfaction has risen to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year.

That’s encouraging, but the responses also point to changes employees want. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops.

The results reflect a strong response: 78 percent of the team took part. Fewer recurring meetings led the list, at 41 percent. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at 36 percent, and better laptops at 29 percent.

Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. The company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month.
