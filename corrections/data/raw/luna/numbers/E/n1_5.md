The third-quarter data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system. That is far fewer than people feared. For most teams, the bigger issue is missing postcodes, which can make customer records less useful for everyday work. The audit found 37 duplicate records, and 112 records were missing postcodes. That’s about 14 percent, up from about 10 percent in the second quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, from about 95 percent to about 94 percent. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
