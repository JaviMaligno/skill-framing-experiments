The team survey shows satisfaction has risen to seven point four out of ten, and leadership is responding to two of the main requests.

That matters because the results point to practical changes that could improve people’s day-to-day work and career clarity.

Nearly eight in ten employees responded. Satisfaction was up from seven point one last year. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, it will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
