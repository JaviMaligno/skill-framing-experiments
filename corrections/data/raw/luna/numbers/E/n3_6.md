Checkout was unavailable on October fourteenth for just over two hours because a payment proxy certificate expired. This matters because payments failed, and some customers still need help completing their purchases. There were 1,284 failed payments. Most, 1,071, were retried automatically. The support team needs to follow up manually on the remaining 213. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk. The outage ran from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. To prevent a repeat, certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
