The third-quarter data-quality audit found very few wrong VAT numbers in records moved to the new customer system. That matters because this was the problem people were most worried about, and it barely happened: just four out of 791 records had the wrong VAT number.

The audit did find other issues. There were 37 duplicate records, and 112 records were missing postcodes. That’s about one in seven, and it’s up from about one in ten last quarter. Average record completeness also slipped, from 95 percent in the second quarter to about 94 percent in the third.

The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
