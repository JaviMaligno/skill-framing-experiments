The team survey shows satisfaction is up, and employees want fewer recurring meetings and clearer promotion criteria.

That matters because the results point to practical changes that could improve how people work and grow here.

Seventy-eight percent of the team responded. Satisfaction averaged 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops.

Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month.
