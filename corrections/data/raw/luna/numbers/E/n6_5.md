The team survey shows satisfaction has risen to seven point four out of ten, and more than three quarters of the team responded.

That matters because the results give us a broad view of what employees value and where they want change. The response rate was seventy-eight percent, or 412 of 528 people. Last year, satisfaction was seven point one.

The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Starting next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.

We’ll see whether those steps address the team’s priorities.
