Checkout was unavailable on 14 October because the payment proxy’s security certificate expired. This matters because some payments failed and a portion still needs personal follow-up from support. The outage lasted a little over two hours, from 9:41 to 11:58 Central European Time. Of 1,284 failed payments, 1,071 were retried automatically, while 213 need manual follow-up. The revenue at risk was about 48,000 euros. To prevent a repeat, certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
