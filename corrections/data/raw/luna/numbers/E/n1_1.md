The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the feared VAT-number problem barely happened: only four of 791 customer records had the wrong number. That is reassuring, but the audit also points to a bigger clean-up job. Nearly one in seven records is missing a postcode, and that share has risen since the second quarter. Overall record completeness also slipped, from about 95 percent to just under 94 percent. The audit found 37 duplicate records as well. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
