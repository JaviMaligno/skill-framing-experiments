The third-quarter audit found only four incorrect value-added tax, or VAT, numbers among the 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system. That’s good news for teams who were most concerned about VAT errors. But other data-quality issues need attention. The audit found 37 duplicate records, and 112 records were missing postcodes. That postcode gap has grown since the second quarter, while average record completeness has slipped to just under 94 percent. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
