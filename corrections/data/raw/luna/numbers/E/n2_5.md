The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers on January first. If you’re preparing a proposal or supporting customers, the key date is December first: from then on, Sales must quote the new monthly price of twelve euros in every new proposal. Existing Starter customers keep paying nine euros until June thirtieth, and the Pro plan remains at twenty-nine euros a month. The change follows a previous price increase that was associated with churn of two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects roughly three hundred to four hundred extra tickets in January, so teams may need to plan for that additional volume. The new price takes effect for new customers on January first; no other next steps were specified.
