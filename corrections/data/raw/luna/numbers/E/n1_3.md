The third-quarter data-quality audit found only a handful of wrong VAT numbers in records moved to the new customer system. That’s reassuring, because VAT errors were the problem people feared most. Just four of the 791 migrated records had a wrong VAT number.

The broader picture is less positive. More records were missing postcodes than in the previous quarter, and average record completeness also fell. There were 112 missing postcodes, or about 14 percent, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Average completeness was 93.6 percent, compared with 95.1 percent.

There were also 37 duplicate records, around 5 percent of the total. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
