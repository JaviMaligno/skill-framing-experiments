On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a payment proxy’s security certificate had expired. This affected customers trying to pay, and left revenue at risk. There were 1,284 failed payments. Most, 1,071, were retried automatically, but 213 still need follow-up from the support team. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk. The certificate renewal process has now been changed, so certificates renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
