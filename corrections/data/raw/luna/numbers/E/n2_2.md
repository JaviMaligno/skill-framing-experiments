The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers on the first of January. This matters if you’re preparing proposals or supporting customers around the change. New customers will pay twelve euros a month, while existing customers keep their nine-euro rate until the end of June. The Pro plan stays at twenty-nine euros. After the last price change, churn was two-point-three percent, compared with a baseline of one-point-eight percent. Support expects roughly three hundred to four hundred extra tickets in January. Sales teams should start quoting the new twelve-euro price in all new proposals from the first of December.
