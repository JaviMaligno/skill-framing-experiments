The team survey shows satisfaction is up, and employees want fewer recurring meetings and clearer promotion criteria. That matters because the results point to practical changes people are asking for, and leadership is already acting on two of them. Satisfaction averaged seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year. The survey had a seventy-eight percent response rate. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month.
