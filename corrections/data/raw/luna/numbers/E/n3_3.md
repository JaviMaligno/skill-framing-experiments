On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a certificate on the payment proxy expired.

This matters because some payments failed, and a portion still needs attention from Support.

There were 1,284 failed payments. Most, 1,071, were retried automatically. The remaining 213 need manual follow-up. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
