The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend. This affects everyone who uses wiki pages or search, so plan for a short interruption on Saturday. Search will be unavailable for about six hours. Most pages will convert automatically, but 379 pages with macros need their owners to check them. Those owners will get an email on Monday with what to do. The migration includes 61 spaces and about 12 gigabytes of attachments, as well as nearly 3,000 pages. Links to old pages will redirect to the new location for 12 months. You do not need to update saved links right away, but keep an eye out for the owner-check email if you maintain pages with macros.
