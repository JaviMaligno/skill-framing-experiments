The team survey shows satisfaction is up, and people want clearer promotion paths and fewer recurring meetings. This matters because the results point to changes leadership is already planning, as well as concerns the team wants addressed. The survey received responses from 78 percent of the team, or 412 people out of 528. Satisfaction averaged 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria. Better laptops also came up. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, the company will trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
