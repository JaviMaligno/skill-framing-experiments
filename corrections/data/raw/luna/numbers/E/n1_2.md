The Q3 data-quality audit found that the feared VAT-number problem barely happened: only four customer records had the wrong number.

That’s reassuring, but the audit also found other gaps that need attention. Of the 791 records migrated to the new customer relationship management system, 37 were duplicates, and 112 were missing postcodes.

Missing postcodes rose from about 10 percent in Q2 to about 14 percent in Q3. Average record completeness also slipped, from about 95 percent to about 94 percent.

The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
