On October 14, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a certificate on the payment proxy had expired. This matters because customers couldn’t complete purchases, and some failed payments still need help from our support team.

There were 1,284 failed payments. Most, 1,071, were retried automatically. The remaining 213 need manual follow-up. Revenue at risk was about 48,000 euros.

The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security, or TLS, certificate. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
