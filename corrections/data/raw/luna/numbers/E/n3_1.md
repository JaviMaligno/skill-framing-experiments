On the fourteenth of October, checkout was unavailable for a little over two hours because a security certificate had expired. This matters because some payments did not go through, and a portion still needs the support team’s attention. In total, one thousand two hundred eighty-four payments failed. Most were retried automatically, but two hundred thirteen need manual follow-up. The incident put about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk. The cause was an expired transport layer security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire.
