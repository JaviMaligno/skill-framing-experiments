The team survey shows satisfaction is up, and leadership is acting on two of the main requests.

That matters because the results point to practical changes employees want, not just a score.

Nearly eight in ten people responded: 412 out of 528. Satisfaction came in at 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, raised by 41 percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, at 36 percent, followed by better laptops at 29 percent.

Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month.
