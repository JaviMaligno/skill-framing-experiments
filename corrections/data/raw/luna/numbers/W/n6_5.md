The latest team survey shows satisfaction has risen to seven point four out of ten, up from seven point one last year. That’s encouraging, but the responses also point to practical changes people want. Nearly eight in ten colleagues took part, giving us a broad view across the team. The biggest request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. And from next month, the company will trial meeting-free Wednesdays. That trial should give teams a chance to see whether setting aside one day without meetings helps create more focus. The survey gives us a clear picture of what colleagues value and where they’d like to see improvements.
