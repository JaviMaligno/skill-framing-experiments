The Starter plan is going up from nine to twelve euros a month for new customers on January first. Existing customers keep their nine-euro price until the end of June, and Pro stays at twenty-nine euros.

For teams across the company, the change means updating customer conversations and preparing for a possible rise in support demand. Sales should quote the new twelve-euro price in every new proposal starting December first.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects between three hundred forty and three hundred eighty extra tickets in January.

The new price takes effect for new customers on January first.
