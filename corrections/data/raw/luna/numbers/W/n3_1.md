Checkout was unavailable on October fourteenth for just over two hours because a certificate on the payment proxy had expired. This matters because customers couldn’t complete payments, and some affected orders still need attention. Of the 1,284 failed payments, 1,071 were retried automatically. The remaining 213 need manual follow-up from the support team. The incident put 48,320 euros in revenue at risk. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire, which should prevent this specific issue from recurring.
