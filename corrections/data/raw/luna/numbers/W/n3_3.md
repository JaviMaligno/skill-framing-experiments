On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a payment proxy certificate had expired.

This matters because customers couldn’t complete payments, and some orders still need attention from support.

There were 1,284 failed payments. Most, 1,071, were retried automatically, but 213 need manual follow-up. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk.

The certificate renewal process has now been changed to renew automatically 30 days before expiry.
