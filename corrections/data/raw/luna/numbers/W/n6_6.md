The team survey had a seventy-eight percent response rate, and overall satisfaction rose to seven point four out of ten.

That gives us a useful read on how colleagues are feeling and what could make work better across teams.

The biggest request was fewer recurring meetings, raised by about four in ten respondents. Next came clearer promotion criteria, followed by better laptops. Satisfaction was up from seven point one last year.

Leadership says it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
