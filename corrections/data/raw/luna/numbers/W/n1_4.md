The third-quarter data-quality audit found that only four of the 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system had the wrong value-added tax number. That’s the issue many people were worried about, and it barely happened. For anyone relying on these records, the bigger concern is postcodes: more than one in seven are missing, up from about one in ten last quarter. There were also 37 duplicate records, and average completeness slipped to 93.6 percent from 95.1 percent in the second quarter. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
