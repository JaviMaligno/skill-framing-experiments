The Starter plan is increasing from nine to twelve euros a month on the first of January for new customers. If you work in sales, make sure new proposals quote twelve euros starting the first of December.

Existing customers will keep paying nine euros until the end of June. Pro stays at twenty-nine euros a month. That gives customers time to adjust, while making the new price clear to anyone considering Starter.

There’s a reason to prepare carefully. After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects between three hundred and forty and three hundred and eighty extra tickets in January.

So sales needs to update proposals from December first, and support should be ready for the January increase in questions.
