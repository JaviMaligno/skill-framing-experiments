Facilities has replaced the plants on floors two, three, and five. That means greener meeting spaces for teams using those floors, with no change to your routine: please don’t water the plants yourself. The supplier will take care of watering every week or so, and the contract runs for 18 months. There are three plants in each meeting room, chosen from 14 species. In total, 46 plants have been installed. Floor four will get its plants after the refurbishment is finished.
