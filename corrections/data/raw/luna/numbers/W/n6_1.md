The team survey shows satisfaction has risen to seven-point-four out of ten, up from seven-point-one last year. This matters because the results point to both what’s working and what could make day-to-day work better. Nearly eight in ten people responded, giving us a strong read on the team’s views. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. And from next month, the company will trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
