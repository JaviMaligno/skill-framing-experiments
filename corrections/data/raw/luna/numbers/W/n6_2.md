The team survey shows satisfaction is up, with a response rate of 78 percent. That matters because the results point to changes employees want, and leadership has already committed to two actions. Satisfaction reached 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year, with 412 of 528 people responding. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, raised by 41 percent of respondents. Clearer promotion criteria came next, followed by better laptops. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter and trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month.
