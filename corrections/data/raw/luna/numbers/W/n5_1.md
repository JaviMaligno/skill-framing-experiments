The old wiki moves to the new knowledge base this weekend. For anyone who relies on it, search will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday, but links to old pages will redirect for the next year.

Most pages will move automatically. About 379 pages use macros and need their owners to check them. Those owners will get an email on Monday. The migration includes more than 2,900 pages across 61 spaces, along with 12.4 gigabytes of attachments.

So, expect a short search outage on Saturday, and watch for an email if you own a page with macros.
