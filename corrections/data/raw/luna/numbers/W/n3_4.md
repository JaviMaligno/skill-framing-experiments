On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a payment proxy’s security certificate had expired.

That matters because customers couldn’t complete purchases, and some payments still need attention.

There were 1,284 failed payments. Most were retried automatically, but 213 need manual follow-up from the support team. About 48,000 euros in revenue was at risk.

The certificate renewal process has now been automated, and certificates will renew 30 days before they expire.
