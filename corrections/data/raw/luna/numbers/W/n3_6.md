Checkout was unavailable on October fourteenth for just over two hours. If you tried to pay during that time, your payment may have failed, and some customers still need help from support.

There were 1,284 failed payments. Most were retried automatically, but 213 need manual follow-up by the support team. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk.

The cause was an expired security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
