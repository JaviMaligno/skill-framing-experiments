The Starter plan is going up in price for new customers, from 9 euros to 12 euros a month, starting January first. If you’re on the existing plan, your price stays at 9 euros through June thirtieth. Pro remains at 29 euros.

This matters if you’re preparing customer proposals or helping customers with questions. Sales should quote the new 12-euro price in every new proposal from December first. The last price change saw churn rise to 2.3 percent, compared with a baseline of 1.8 percent. Support is planning for between 340 and 380 extra tickets in January.

So, the new quote takes effect in December, and the new Starter price begins in January.
