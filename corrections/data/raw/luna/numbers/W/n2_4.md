The Starter plan is going up from nine to twelve euros a month on January first for new customers. If you already have the plan, you’ll keep paying nine euros until June thirtieth.

This matters because the change affects both customer conversations and sales proposals. The Pro plan stays at twenty-nine euros a month, so only Starter is changing.

After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support is expecting three hundred forty to three hundred eighty extra tickets in January.

Sales teams should start quoting twelve euros in all new proposals from December first.
