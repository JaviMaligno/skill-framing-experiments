The third-quarter data-quality audit found that the feared VAT-number problem was rare: only four of the 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system had the wrong number.

That’s good news, but the audit also points to a bigger issue for teams relying on these records. More than one in seven records was missing a postcode, up from about one in ten last quarter. Duplicates affected about one in twenty records, and average completeness slipped from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent.

A postcode clean-up sprint is due to start on the third of November.
