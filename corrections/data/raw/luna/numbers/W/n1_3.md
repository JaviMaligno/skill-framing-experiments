The Q3 data-quality audit found that the VAT-number problem we were worried about barely happened: just four of 791 customer records had the wrong number. That’s good news for anyone working with customer accounts or finance data. But other gaps need attention. There were 37 duplicate records, and 112 records were missing postcodes. That’s 14.2 percent, up from 9.8 percent in Q2. Average record completeness also slipped, from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting on the third of November.
