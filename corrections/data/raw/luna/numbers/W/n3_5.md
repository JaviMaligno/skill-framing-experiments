On the fourteenth of October, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours. This matters because customers couldn’t complete purchases, and some payments still need attention. There were 1,284 failed payments. Most were retried automatically, but 213 need manual follow-up from the support team. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk. The cause was an expired transport layer security certificate on the payment proxy. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
