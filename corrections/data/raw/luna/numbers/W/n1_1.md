The third-quarter data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system. That’s good news on the issue many people were worried about. But postcodes need attention: 14 percent of records are missing one, up from about 10 percent last quarter. Duplicates affected nearly 5 percent of records, and average completeness slipped to 93.6 percent from 95.1 percent. So while the feared VAT problem barely happened, the broader data-quality picture has weakened, especially on postcodes. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
