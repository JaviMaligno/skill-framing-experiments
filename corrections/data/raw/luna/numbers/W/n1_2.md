The Q3 data-quality audit found that the feared VAT-number problem barely happened: just four out of 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system had the wrong number.

That’s reassuring, but other data gaps need attention. Missing postcodes are the clearest concern, especially for teams who rely on accurate customer records.

One hundred and twelve records were missing postcodes, or about fourteen per cent, up from just under ten per cent in Q2. Duplicates affected 37 records, and average completeness slipped to 93.6 per cent from 95.1 per cent last quarter.

A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
