The old wiki is moving to the new knowledge base this weekend. For employees, that means a short search outage, but old links will keep working while teams adjust. Most pages will move automatically. About three hundred and eighty pages that use macros need their owners to check them, and those owners will get an email on Monday. Search will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. The migration covers nearly three thousand pages across sixty-one spaces, along with more than twelve gigabytes of attachments. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base for twelve months. After the migration, watch for that Monday email if you own a page with macros.
