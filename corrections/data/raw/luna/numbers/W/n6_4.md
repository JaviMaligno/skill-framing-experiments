The team survey shows satisfaction has risen to 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. That’s a positive signal, but employees also pointed to practical changes that could make work better. The survey had a 78 percent response rate, with 412 of 528 people taking part. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays. Those steps address two of the top requests; the survey also highlights laptop improvements as an area to watch.
