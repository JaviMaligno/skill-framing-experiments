The Q3 data-quality audit found just four records with a wrong value-added tax, or VAT, number among the 791 customers moved to the new customer relationship management system. That’s a relief on the issue many people were worried about. For teams working with customer records, the bigger concern is postcodes: 112 records were missing one, up from 9.8 percent in Q2 to 14.2 percent in Q3. There were also 37 duplicate records, and average completeness slipped from 95.1 percent to 93.6 percent. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November. The audit suggests the feared VAT problem was rare, but the wider record-quality gaps still need attention.
