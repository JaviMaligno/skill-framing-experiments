On the fourteenth of October, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired. This affected customers trying to pay, and left revenue at risk. There were 1,284 failed payments. The system automatically retried most of them, but 213 still need the support team to follow up manually. The amount of revenue at risk was 48,320 euros. To prevent the same issue from happening again, certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire.
