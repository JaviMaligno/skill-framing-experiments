The third-quarter data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among the 791 customer records moved to the new customer relationship management system. That matters because the VAT problem people were worried about barely happened. The bigger issue is missing postcodes: they were missing from about one in seven records, up from about one in ten in the previous quarter. Duplicate records affected about one in twenty. Overall completeness also slipped slightly, from about 95 percent to about 94 percent. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint, starting November third.
