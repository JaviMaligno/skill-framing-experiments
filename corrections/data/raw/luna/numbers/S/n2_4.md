The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers, while Pro stays the same. If you work with customers or proposals, the change takes effect on different dates depending on the customer. From the first of January, new Starter customers will pay 12 euros a month instead of 9 euros. Existing customers keep their current price until the end of June. Pro remains at 29 euros. After the last price change, churn was 2.3 percent, compared with a baseline of 1.8 percent. Support expects around 360 extra tickets in January. Sales teams should quote the new 12-euro price in all new proposals starting the first of December.
