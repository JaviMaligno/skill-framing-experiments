On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for a little over two hours because a certificate on the payment proxy had expired. This matters because customers could not complete purchases during the outage, and some payments still need attention. There were about twelve hundred failed payments. Most were retried automatically, but just over two hundred need manual follow-up from the support team. The incident put about forty-eight thousand euros in revenue at risk. The cause was an expired Transport Layer Security certificate. To prevent a repeat, certificates now renew automatically thirty days before they expire.
