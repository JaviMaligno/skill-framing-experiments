The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers on January first. Existing customers will keep their current price until June thirtieth, while Pro pricing stays the same.

If you work in Sales, use the new Starter price in every proposal issued from December first. Support teams should also prepare for more customer questions in January.

The key dates are December first for new proposals, January first for the price change, and June thirtieth for the end of existing customers’ old rate.
