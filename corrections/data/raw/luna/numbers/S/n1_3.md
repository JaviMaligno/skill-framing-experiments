The third-quarter audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system. That is far fewer than people feared. For most teams, the bigger concern is missing postcodes, which can affect how reliably customer records are used. The audit found 112 records without postcodes, or about fourteen percent, up from about ten percent in the second quarter. Duplicate records were also found, and average completeness slipped slightly to about ninety-four percent. The next step is a postcode clean-up sprint starting on the third of November.
