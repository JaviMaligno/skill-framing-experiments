The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers on January first. This matters if you’re preparing a proposal or supporting customers who may have questions. New customers will pay twelve euros a month, while existing customers keep their nine-euro rate through June thirtieth. The Pro plan stays at twenty-nine euros. After the last price change, churn was two-point-three percent, compared with a one-point-eight percent baseline. Support expects roughly three hundred to four hundred extra tickets in January. Sales should quote the new twelve-euro price in all proposals from December first.
