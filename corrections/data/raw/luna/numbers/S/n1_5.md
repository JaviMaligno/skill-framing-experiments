The third-quarter data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system. That matters because the VAT problem many people feared barely happened. The bigger issue is missing postcodes, which have increased since the previous quarter and could affect how teams use customer records. The audit also found 37 duplicate records, while average record completeness slipped to about 94 percent from about 95 percent in the second quarter. The postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
