The Starter plan price is increasing for new customers, while existing customers will pay the current rate for six more months. This matters if you’re preparing a proposal or supporting customers through the change. From the first of January, new customers will pay twelve euros a month instead of nine. Existing customers keep the nine-euro rate until the end of June. The Pro plan remains at twenty-nine euros. After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a baseline of one point eight percent. Support expects roughly three hundred and fifty extra tickets in January. Sales should quote the new twelve-euro price in all new proposals starting the first of December.
