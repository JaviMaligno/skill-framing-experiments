On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a payment proxy certificate had expired.

This matters because customers could not complete payments during the outage, and some cases still need support follow-up.

There were about twelve hundred failed payments. Most were retried automatically, but just over two hundred need the support team to follow up. Revenue at risk was about forty-eight thousand euros.

The certificate renewal process has now been updated to renew automatically a month before expiry.
