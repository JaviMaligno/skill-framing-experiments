On October 14, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a certificate on the payment proxy had expired. This meant customers could not complete payments during the outage, and some transactions need further attention. There were 1,284 failed payments. Most were retried automatically, but 213 need manual follow-up from the support team. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk. The root cause was an expired transport layer security certificate. Certificates now renew automatically 30 days before they expire, reducing the chance of this happening again. The support team will follow up on the remaining affected payments.
