The Q3 data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system. That matters because the VAT problem people feared barely happened. The bigger issue is missing postcodes: they affected about one in seven records, up from about one in ten last quarter. There were also 37 duplicate records, and average record completeness slipped slightly, to about 94 percent from 95 percent in Q2. The team will start a postcode clean-up sprint on the third of November. That work should address the clearest gap in the audit, while the VAT result suggests the migration avoided the feared problem.
