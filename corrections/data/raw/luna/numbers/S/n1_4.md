The third-quarter data-quality audit found only four wrong VAT numbers among 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system. That’s the reassuring part: the VAT problem people feared barely happened. The bigger issue is missing postcodes, which affected about one in seven records. The audit also found duplicates, and overall record completeness slipped compared with the previous quarter. That matters because incomplete or duplicated records can make customer information less reliable across teams. Postcodes were missing from 14.2 percent of records, up from 9.8 percent in the second quarter. Average completeness was 93.6 percent, down from 95.1 percent. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November.
