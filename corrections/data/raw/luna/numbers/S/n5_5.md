This weekend, we’re moving the old wiki into the new knowledge base. The migration affects everyone who uses wiki content, and search will be unavailable for about six hours on Saturday. Most pages will convert automatically. Three hundred seventy-nine pages that use macros need to be checked by their owners. Those owners will get an email on Monday with next steps. Links to old pages will redirect to the new knowledge base for a year, so existing bookmarks and shared links should continue to work during the transition.
