The Starter plan’s monthly price will rise for new customers on January first.

This affects teams preparing proposals and supporting customers, while current subscribers have more time before the change reaches them.

From January, new customers will pay twelve euros a month instead of nine. Existing customers keep the nine-euro price until the end of June. The Pro plan remains at twenty-nine euros. After the last price change, churn was two-point-three percent, compared with a one-point-eight percent baseline. Support expects roughly three hundred to four hundred extra tickets in January.

Sales must quote twelve euros in all new proposals starting December first.
