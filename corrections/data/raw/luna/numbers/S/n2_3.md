The Starter plan’s monthly price is increasing for new customers on the first of January. This matters for anyone preparing proposals or supporting customers through the change. New customers will pay twelve euros a month, while existing customers keep their nine-euro rate until the end of June. The Pro plan stays at twenty-nine euros. After the last price change, churn was two point three percent, compared with a one point eight percent baseline. Support expects roughly three hundred to four hundred extra tickets in January. Sales should quote the new twelve-euro price in all new proposals starting the first of December.
