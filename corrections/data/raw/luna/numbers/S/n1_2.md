The third-quarter audit found four incorrect value-added tax numbers among 791 customer records migrated to the new customer relationship management system. That matters because the VAT issue many people feared barely happened. The bigger data-quality concern is missing postcodes: they affected about one in seven records, up from about one in ten last quarter. Overall record completeness also slipped, from about 95 percent to around 94 percent. The audit found duplicates in roughly five percent of records, so there is still clean-up work beyond postcodes. A postcode clean-up sprint starts on the third of November. The audit gives teams a clearer picture of where to focus, while confirming that incorrect VAT numbers were rare.
