The team survey shows satisfaction has risen to 7.4 out of 10, and leaders are planning changes in response. That matters because the results point to practical ways to improve people’s day-to-day work. The survey had a 78 percent response rate, and satisfaction is up from 7.1 last year. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leaders will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Starting next month, they’ll also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
