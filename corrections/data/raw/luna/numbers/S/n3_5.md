Checkout was unavailable on 14 October because a security certificate on the payment proxy had expired. This matters because some customer payments failed, and support still needs to follow up on a smaller group.

The outage lasted just over two hours. About 1,300 payments failed. Most were retried automatically, but 213 need manual follow-up from the support team. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk.

The certificate now renews automatically a month before it expires.
