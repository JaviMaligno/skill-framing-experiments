The team survey shows satisfaction is up, and employees want fewer recurring meetings and clearer promotion criteria. That matters because the results point to practical changes that could improve the day-to-day experience across the company. The survey received responses from about eight in ten employees. Satisfaction averaged 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria, then better laptops. Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Starting next month, it will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
