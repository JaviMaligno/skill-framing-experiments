The team survey shows satisfaction has risen, and leadership is responding to two of the main requests.

That matters because the results point to practical changes employees want, especially around meetings and career progression.

Nearly eight in ten people responded. Satisfaction scored 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria. Better laptops also came up, but less often.

Leadership plans to publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
