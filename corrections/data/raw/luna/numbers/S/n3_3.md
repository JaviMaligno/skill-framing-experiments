On October fourteenth, checkout was unavailable for just over two hours because a certificate on the payment proxy had expired. This matters because some payments failed, and a portion still needs support team follow-up. Of the 1,284 failed payments, most were retried automatically. The remaining 213 need manual attention. The incident put about 48,000 euros in revenue at risk. The certificate renewal process has now been changed so certificates renew automatically a month before they expire.
