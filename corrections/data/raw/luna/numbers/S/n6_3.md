The team survey shows satisfaction has risen since last year, with a strong response from employees.

That matters because the results point to practical changes people want, as well as one action already underway.

About eight in ten employees responded. Satisfaction averaged 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops.

Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. From next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
