The team survey shows satisfaction has risen since last year, with a response rate of 78 percent. That matters because the results give us a useful read on what’s working and what employees want changed. Satisfaction reached 7.4 out of 10, up from 7.1 last year. The top request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria and better laptops. Leadership says it will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. It will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays starting next month. The trial and the new criteria are the next steps to watch.
