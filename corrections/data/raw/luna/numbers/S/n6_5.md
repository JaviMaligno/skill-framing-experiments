The latest team survey shows satisfaction has risen to 7.4 out of 10. That matters because the results point to practical changes that could improve day-to-day work. Just over three-quarters of the team responded, and satisfaction is up from 7.1 last year. The most common request was fewer recurring meetings, followed by clearer promotion criteria. Better laptops were another frequent ask. Leadership will publish promotion criteria in the first quarter. Starting next month, the company will also trial meeting-free Wednesdays.
