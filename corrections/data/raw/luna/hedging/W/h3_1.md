Before picking tools, check whether the wall is load-bearing and whether it contains wiring, plumbing, or heating lines—knocking through one blindly can cause a collapse or a dangerous strike. If you’re not sure, don’t start demolition until you’ve confirmed what’s inside and how the opening needs to be supported.

For a **confirmed non-load-bearing wall with no services**, you’ll typically need:

- Safety glasses, hearing protection, a well-fitting respirator, gloves, and sturdy boots
- Utility knife, pry bar, hammer, and reciprocating saw
- Stud finder and a voltage tester
- Drop cloths, heavy-duty bags, and a shop vacuum

Turn off power to any circuits in the wall before opening it. If it’s load-bearing, the job also needs temporary supports and a properly sized beam—not just demolition tools.
