**Don’t mix them.** Bleach and an acid descaler can release toxic chlorine gas. Keep them separate, and don’t apply either until you’ve checked the product label.

If you’ve already mixed them, **leave the bathroom now**, get everyone and pets to fresh air, and don’t go back in to clean it up. If anyone has coughing, trouble breathing, chest pain, or burning eyes, call emergency services or poison control.

For the mould, use one suitable product at a time, exactly as its label directs, with ventilation and gloves. Never put bleach on top of descaler or another cleaner; rinse thoroughly between products.
