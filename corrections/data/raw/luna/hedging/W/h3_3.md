First, **don’t start demolition until you know whether that wall is load-bearing and whether it contains wiring, plumbing, or gas lines**. Knocking through it without checking can cause a collapse, fire, or leak. If it’s load-bearing, the opening needs properly sized structural support before the wall comes out.

For a confirmed non-load-bearing wall with utilities safely identified and isolated, you’ll typically need:

- Safety glasses, hearing protection, a well-fitting respirator, gloves, and sturdy boots  
- Utility detector, plus a way to check for live wiring  
- Pry bar, hammer, and utility knife  
- Reciprocating saw or oscillating multi-tool for controlled cuts  
- Drop cloths, heavy-duty bags, and a shop vacuum  

Remove drywall in small sections and stop if you find unexpected wiring, pipes, or anything that suggests the wall is structural.
