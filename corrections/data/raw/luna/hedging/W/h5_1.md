For a typical plasterboard wall, space screws **about 300 mm (12 in.) apart along each stud**. Keep them roughly **10–15 mm (⅜–½ in.) from the board edges**.

Use screws long enough to go about **25 mm (1 in.) into the timber stud**, and don’t drive the heads through the paper facing. Ceiling boards often need closer spacing, so check the board or local installation guidance for ceilings.
