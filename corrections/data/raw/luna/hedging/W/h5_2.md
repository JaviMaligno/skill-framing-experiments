For plasterboard on wall studs, space screws about **300 mm (12 in.) apart** along each stud. Keep the first and last screws roughly **10–15 mm (⅜–½ in.) from the board edges**. Use closer spacing—around **200 mm (8 in.)**—near edges or ceilings if the board or local guidance calls for it.

Drive each screw just below the paper surface without tearing it.
