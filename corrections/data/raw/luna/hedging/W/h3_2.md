Before choosing tools, check whether the wall is load-bearing and whether it contains wiring, plumbing, or ducts—cutting into those can cause serious damage or injury. Don’t start demolition until you’ve confirmed what’s inside and how the ceiling is supported.

For a **non-load-bearing wall**, typical gear includes:

- **PPE:** safety glasses, a well-fitting respirator, hearing protection, work gloves, and sturdy boots.
- **Prep:** plastic sheeting, painter’s tape, drop cloths, and a shop vacuum.
- **Hand tools:** utility knife, pry bar, hammer, and a drywall saw.
- **Cutting tools:** an oscillating multi-tool or reciprocating saw, with blades suited to the material.
- **Cleanup:** heavy-duty rubble bags or bins.

If it’s load-bearing, you’ll need a properly designed beam and temporary supports—not just demolition tools. Tools and materials depend on what the wall is made of and what’s inside it.
