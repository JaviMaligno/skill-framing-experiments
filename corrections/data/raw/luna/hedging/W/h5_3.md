For standard plasterboard on timber studs, space the screws **about 300 mm (12 in) apart** along each stud. Keep them roughly **10–15 mm (⅜–⅝ in) from the board edges**, and don’t drive them so deep that they break the paper.

If the board is on a ceiling, or you’re using a particular board thickness or stud spacing, follow the board maker’s fixing guide—ceilings often need closer spacing.
