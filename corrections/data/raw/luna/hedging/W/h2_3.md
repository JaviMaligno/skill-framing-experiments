**Don’t mix them.** Bleach and an acid descaler can release toxic chlorine gas. If you’ve already combined them, leave the bathroom now, keep others and pets away, and call emergency services or poison control for advice. Don’t try to clean it up or add anything else.

Use only one product at a time, exactly as its label directs, with good ventilation. Never use the descaler on top of bleach residue—rinse thoroughly with water and let the area dry before switching products.
