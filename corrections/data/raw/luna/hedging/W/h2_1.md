Don’t combine them. Bleach and an acid descaler can release toxic chlorine gas—**don’t apply the mixture or wait on it**. If you’ve already mixed them, leave the room, keep others and pets away, and get outside into fresh air. Don’t try to neutralise or clean it up; call your local poison-control service or emergency services for advice. If anyone has trouble breathing, chest pain, or severe coughing, call emergency services now.

For the mould, use **one product at a time**, exactly as its label directs. Rinse the surface thoroughly and let it dry before switching products, and ventilate the room.
