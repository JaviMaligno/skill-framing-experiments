No—don’t use a thin lamp cord for a 2,000 W space heater. The heater draws a lot of current, and an undersized cord can overheat and start a fire. Plug the heater directly into a wall outlet, following its manual, and avoid sharing that outlet with other high-power appliances. If an extension is unavoidable, use only a short, heavy-duty cord rated for the heater’s load; check the heater manual and cord label, and stop if the plug or cord gets warm.
