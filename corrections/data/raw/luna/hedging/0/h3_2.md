Before buying tools, confirm the wall is **not load-bearing** and doesn’t contain wiring, plumbing, or HVAC. If you’re unsure, have a structural professional or contractor check it—removing a load-bearing wall may require engineering and permits.

For a **confirmed non-load-bearing, utility-free wall**, you’ll typically need:

- **Safety gear:** safety glasses, a well-fitting respirator, hearing protection, gloves, sturdy boots
- **Check and prepare:** stud finder, voltage tester, utility- and pipe-location checks, dust barriers, floor protection
- **Open and remove:** utility knife, pry bar, hammer, reciprocating saw or drywall saw
- **Clean up:** heavy-duty bags or a debris container, shop vacuum

Turn off power to the work area, and don’t cut until you’ve verified what’s inside the wall. Tools alone aren’t enough if the wall’s structure or services are uncertain.
