**Don’t mix them.** Bleach and an acid descaler can release toxic chlorine gas. If you’ve already combined them, leave the bathroom immediately, avoid breathing the fumes, and don’t try to clean it up or add anything else. Get everyone and pets outside; call emergency services or your local poison-control service for advice, especially if anyone has coughing, burning eyes, or trouble breathing.

For the mould, use **one product at a time**, exactly as its label directs. Never combine cleaners, and rinse and ventilate between products.
