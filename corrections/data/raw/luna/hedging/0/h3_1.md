Before picking up tools, have a qualified professional check whether the wall is load-bearing and whether it contains wiring, plumbing, or gas lines. You may need permits, and removing a structural wall can require engineered supports—don’t start demolition until that’s sorted.

If it’s confirmed non-load-bearing and clear of services, typical tools include:

- Stud finder and utility scanner
- Safety glasses, hearing protection, gloves, and a well-fitting respirator
- Utility knife, pry bar, hammer, and reciprocating saw
- Dust barriers, floor protection, and a shop vacuum
- Work light and heavy-duty trash bags or a debris bin

Turn off power to affected circuits before opening the wall, and have an electrician handle wiring. Tools and materials depend on the wall’s construction and what’s behind it.
