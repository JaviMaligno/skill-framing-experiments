**Don’t mix them.** Bleach and an acid descaler can release toxic chlorine gas. Don’t apply the mixture or leave it on.

If you’ve already combined them, **leave the area immediately**, keep others and pets away, and don’t try to clean it up or add anything else. Get fresh air; if anyone has coughing, trouble breathing, chest pain, or eye irritation, call emergency services or poison control now.

For mould, use **one product at a time**, following its label, with good ventilation and gloves. Rinse thoroughly before using any different cleaner.
