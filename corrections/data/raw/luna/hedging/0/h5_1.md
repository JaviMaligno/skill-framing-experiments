For plasterboard fixed to timber studs, space screws about **300 mm (12 in) apart** along each stud. Keep screws at least **10 mm (⅜ in) from tapered edges** and **13 mm (½ in) from cut edges**.

Use the board manufacturer’s guidance if it differs, and make sure each screw just dimples the face paper without tearing it. For ceilings or metal studs, spacing can vary, so check the board instructions.
