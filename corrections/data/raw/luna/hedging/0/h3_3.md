Before choosing demolition tools, **confirm the wall isn’t load-bearing and check for wiring, plumbing, or gas lines**. If you’re unsure, have a qualified contractor or structural engineer inspect it; cutting a structural wall can cause serious damage, and electrical or gas work may need a licensed professional.

For a **confirmed non-load-bearing wall**, you’ll typically need:

- Safety glasses, hearing protection, a well-fitting respirator, gloves, and sturdy boots
- Utility knife, pry bar, hammer, and a reciprocating saw with suitable blades
- Stud finder and a voltage tester (use the tester only if you know how to isolate and verify circuits safely)
- Drop cloths, plastic sheeting, and a shop vacuum for dust and debris

Turn off relevant utilities before opening the wall, and don’t cut into hidden services. Check local permit requirements before starting.
