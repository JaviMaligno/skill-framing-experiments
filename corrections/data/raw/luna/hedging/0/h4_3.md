Satin is usually the better choice for a hallway: it’s more durable and easier to wipe clean than matte, which helps with scuffs and fingerprints. It has a slight sheen, though, so it may show wall imperfections more.

Choose matte if the walls are uneven and you’d rather hide flaws; choose satin for easier cleaning. For a middle ground, consider eggshell. Use a washable interior wall paint, and check the label for the recommended sheen and cleaning instructions.
