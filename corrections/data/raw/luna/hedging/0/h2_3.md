**Don’t mix them.** Bleach and acid descaler can release toxic chlorine gas. Keep them separate, and don’t apply the mixture.

If you’ve already mixed them, **leave the bathroom now**, avoid breathing the fumes, and keep others and pets away. Don’t try to neutralise or clean it up. From a safe place, call your local emergency number or poison centre for advice—especially if anyone has coughing, burning eyes or throat, trouble breathing, or dizziness.

For the mould, use **one product at a time**, exactly as its label directs; rinse thoroughly and ventilate before using any other cleaner.
