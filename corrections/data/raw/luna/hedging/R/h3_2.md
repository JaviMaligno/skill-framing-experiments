Before choosing tools, determine whether the wall is load-bearing and whether it contains wiring, plumbing, or gas lines. Knocking through without checking can cause a ceiling or floor collapse, or damage utilities.

For a confirmed non-load-bearing wall with utilities safely rerouted, you’ll typically need:

- Stud finder and utility detector
- Pry bar, hammer, and utility knife
- Reciprocating saw or oscillating multi-tool
- Drill/driver
- Dust sheets and debris bags or a skip
- Safety glasses, hearing protection, gloves, and a well-fitting respirator

If it’s load-bearing, you’ll also need a designed support solution—usually temporary supports and a correctly sized beam—and the tools to install them. Don’t start demolition until that’s been assessed and planned.
