**Don’t mix them.** Bleach and acid descaler can release toxic chlorine gas. If you’ve already combined them, leave the area immediately, keep others and pets out, and don’t try to clean it up or add anything else. If anyone has coughing, trouble breathing, chest pain, or eye irritation, get outside and call emergency services or poison control.

For the mould, use **one product at a time**, following its label. Rinse the surface thoroughly with water and let it dry before using a different cleaner.
