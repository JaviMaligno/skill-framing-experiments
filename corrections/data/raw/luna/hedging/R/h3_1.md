Before choosing tools, find out whether the wall is load-bearing and whether it contains wiring, plumbing, or gas lines. Knocking through either without planning can cause a collapse, fire, or gas leak; don’t start demolition until those are identified and any needed structural support is planned.

For a **confirmed non-load-bearing wall with utilities cleared**, you’ll need:

- Stud finder and a utility detector
- Pry bar, hammer, and reciprocating saw
- Drywall knife and utility knife
- Drill/driver
- Dust sheets, heavy-duty bags, and a shop vacuum
- Eye protection, hearing protection, gloves, and a well-fitting respirator

If it’s load-bearing, the job also needs engineered support and temporary shoring—not just demolition tools.
