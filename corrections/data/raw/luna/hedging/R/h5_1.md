For standard plasterboard fixed to timber studs, space screws **about 300 mm (12 in) apart** along each stud. Keep them roughly **10–15 mm (⅜–⅝ in) from the board edges**.

Use closer spacing—around **200 mm (8 in)**—at board edges or ceilings if required by your board manufacturer or local guidance. Don’t overtighten: the screw head should sit just below the surface without breaking the paper.
