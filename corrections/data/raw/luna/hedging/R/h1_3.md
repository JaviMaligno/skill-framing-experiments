No—don’t use that thin lamp cord for a 2000 W heater. At typical household voltage, the heater draws about 17 A at 120 V or 8.7 A at 230 V, which can overheat an undersized cord and start a fire.

Plug the heater directly into a wall outlet. If an extension is unavoidable, use a short, heavy-duty cord rated for the heater’s current and follow the heater’s instructions; don’t run it coiled or under rugs.
