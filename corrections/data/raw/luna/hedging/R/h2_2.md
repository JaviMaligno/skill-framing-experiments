**Don’t mix them.** Bleach and an acid descaler can release toxic chlorine gas. If you’ve already combined them, leave the area immediately, get everyone and pets outside, and call your local emergency service or poison centre. Don’t try to clean it up or ventilate by going back in.

For the mould, use **one product only**, following its label. Rinse the surface thoroughly with water before switching products, and never mix cleaners.
