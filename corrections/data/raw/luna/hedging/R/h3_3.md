First, confirm the wall isn’t load-bearing and check for wiring, plumbing, or gas lines inside it before cutting—removing the wrong wall can cause serious structural damage or injury. If it is load-bearing, you’ll need a designed support plan before demolition.

For a **confirmed non-load-bearing wall**, gather:
- Stud finder with live-wire detection; utility plans if available
- Tape measure, pencil, level, and chalk line
- Utility knife and pry bar
- Drill/driver and reciprocating saw with wood and metal blades
- Hammer or small sledgehammer
- Dust-control plastic, drop cloths, and a shop vacuum
- Eye protection, hearing protection, gloves, and a well-fitting respirator
- Heavy-duty bags or a debris container

Turn off power to any circuits in the wall before opening it. Once it’s open, inspect for hidden services before cutting studs.
