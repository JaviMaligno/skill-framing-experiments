No—don’t use a thin lamp cord for a 2000 W heater. The heater draws about 17 A on 120 V (or 9 A on 230 V), which can overheat a light-duty cord and start a fire. Plug it directly into a wall outlet. If the heater’s instructions allow an extension cord, use a heavy-duty cord rated for the heater’s current and keep it fully uncoiled.
