**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Two-factor authentication:** you can now protect your account with a code from an authenticator app. Turn it on under **Settings → Security**.
- **CSV export:** your exports now include a **Paid on** column, so you can see when each invoice was paid.
- **Fixes:** edits made with Ledgerly open in two tabs were sometimes lost, and this no longer happens. The footer on Spanish invoices now reads "Factura" instead of "Fatura".
