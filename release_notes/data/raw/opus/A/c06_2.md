**Ledgerly update: Google sign-in for teams, plus fixes**

- **New (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in with Google Workspace using single sign-on (SSO).
- **Fixes:** Valid Irish VAT numbers (such as IE1234567FA) are now accepted instead of being rejected. Late in the evening, the dashboard chart sometimes showed last month's revenue as this month's. It now shows the correct month.
