**Ledgerly update: stronger passwords, Microsoft sign-in, and fixes**

- **Action needed:** Passwords now need at least 12 characters. If yours is shorter, change it before November 20, 2026. After that date, you'll be asked to change it the next time you sign in.
- You can now sign in with your Microsoft account.
- Fixes: you're no longer signed out without warning and your unsaved invoice drafts aren't lost, and the Settings page now displays in German.
