**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Get paid by SEPA Direct Debit:** clients can now pay your invoices by SEPA Direct Debit. To turn it on, go to Settings → Payments.
- **More up-to-date currency conversions:** totals converted to another currency now use exchange rates that are refreshed every hour.
- **Fixes:** the Send button no longer crashes when a client has no email address. The date picker now starts the week on Monday for UK users.
