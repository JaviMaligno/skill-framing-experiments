**What's new in Ledgerly this week**

- **Payment QR code on invoices:** Your invoices now include a payment QR code (EPC QR / Girocode). Clients can scan it with their banking app to pay, with your bank details and the amount already filled in.
- **New Zapier trigger, "Invoice overdue":** You can now start a Zap automatically when an invoice becomes overdue, for example to send a reminder or alert your team.
- **Fixes:** Large invoices (100+ lines) no longer time out when you generate the PDF. The **Duplicate invoice** button no longer copies the old due date to the new invoice.
