**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Payment QR code on invoices:** Invoices can now include an EPC QR code (Girocode). Your clients can scan it with their banking app to pay, with the payment details already filled in.
- **New Zapier trigger, "Invoice overdue":** You can now start a Zap automatically when an invoice becomes overdue.
- **Fixes:** Large invoices with 100 or more lines no longer fail to generate as PDFs. The **Duplicate invoice** button no longer copies the old due date to the new invoice.
