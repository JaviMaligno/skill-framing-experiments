**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Multi-currency invoicing:** You can now send an invoice in one currency and get paid in another. Your PDF invoice shows the conversion.
- **Archive clients:** You can archive a client instead of deleting them. Archived clients no longer show up in your client lists.
- **Fixes:** CSV files that use semicolons as separators now import correctly. These files are common in Spain and Germany. Email previews now show your client's name instead of a raw {{client_name}} placeholder.
- **Speed:** Ledgerly should feel a little faster to load.
