**What's new in Ledgerly this week**

- **Two-factor authentication:** Add an extra layer of protection to your account with an authenticator app. Turn it on under **Settings → Security**.
- **"Paid on" in CSV export:** Your CSV exports now include a **Paid on** column, so you can see when each invoice was paid.
- **Fixes:** Edits are no longer lost when you have Ledgerly open in two tabs at once, and the Spanish invoice footer now correctly reads "Factura."
