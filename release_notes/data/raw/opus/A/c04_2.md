**Ledgerly update: last-day billing for recurring invoices, plus fixes**

- **Action required:** XLS export will be removed on December 1, 2026. Before then, switch to XLSX or CSV for your exports.
- Recurring invoices can now be billed on the last day of the month.
- Fixes: clicking "Send reminder" twice no longer sends your client two emails, and SVG logos larger than 1MB now upload.
