# Ledgerly update: last-day-of-month billing and fixes

- **Action required:** XLS export will be removed on December 1, 2026. Before then, switch your exports to XLSX or CSV.
- Recurring invoices can now be billed on the last day of the month.
- Fixes: clicking "Send reminder" twice no longer sends your client two emails, and you can now upload SVG logos larger than 1MB.
