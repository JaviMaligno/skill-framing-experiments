**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Multi-currency invoicing:** you can now invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The conversion appears on the invoice PDF.
- **Archive clients:** you can now archive clients instead of deleting them. Archived clients no longer appear in your client lists.
- **Fixes:**
  - CSV import now works with files that use semicolons as separators, which are common in Spain and Germany.
  - The email preview now shows your client's name instead of the text "{{client_name}}".
