# What's new in Ledgerly

- **Action needed by November 20, 2026:** Passwords now need at least 12 characters. If your password is shorter, change it before November 20. After that date, you'll be asked to change it the next time you sign in.
- **Sign in with Microsoft:** You can now sign in to Ledgerly with your Microsoft account.
- **Fixes:**
  - You're no longer signed out without warning, so unsaved invoice drafts aren't lost anymore.
  - The Settings page now shows correctly in German.
