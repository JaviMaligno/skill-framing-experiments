# Ledgerly update: action needed for bank feeds

- **Action required by 30 November 2026:** Reconnect your bank in **Settings → Bank feeds**. Bank feeds now connect through TrueLayer, and you'll see TrueLayer when you reconnect. Until you reconnect, new transactions won't be imported from your bank.
- Your IBAN now appears in the footer of your invoice PDFs, so clients can see your bank details when they pay.
- Fixed: after reconnecting a bank, some matched payments showed up twice in the Transactions list.
