# What's new in Ledgerly

- **Action needed by December 1, 2026:** XLS export is going away on that date. Switch to XLSX or CSV for your exports before then.
- **Recurring invoices on the last day of the month:** You can now set recurring invoices to go out on the last day of each month.
- **Fixes:**
  - Clicking **Send reminder** twice no longer sends your client two emails.
  - Uploading an SVG logo larger than 1MB no longer fails.
