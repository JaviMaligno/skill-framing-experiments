## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Action needed by December 1, 2026:** XLS export is going away. After that date, export to XLSX or CSV instead.
- **Recurring invoices:** You can now choose to bill on the last day of the month.
- **Fixes:**
  - Clicking **Send reminder** twice no longer sends your client two emails.
  - SVG logos larger than 1MB now upload correctly.
