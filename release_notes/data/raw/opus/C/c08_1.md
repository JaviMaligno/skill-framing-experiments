# What's new in Ledgerly

- **Payment QR code on invoices:** Your invoices can now include an EPC QR code (also called Girocode). Clients scan it with their banking app and the payment details are filled in for them.
- **New Zapier trigger "Invoice overdue":** Start a Zap automatically when an invoice passes its due date, for example to send a reminder or alert your team.
- **Fixes:**
  - PDFs for large invoices (100+ lines) no longer fail to generate.
  - The "Duplicate invoice" button no longer copies the original invoice's due date.
