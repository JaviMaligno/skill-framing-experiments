# Ledgerly update: October 2026

- **Action required by 30 November 2026:** Reconnect your bank in **Settings → Bank feeds**. We've moved to a new bank connection provider, TrueLayer. Until you reconnect, your bank transactions won't sync.
- Your IBAN now appears in the footer of your invoice PDFs, so clients can see where to pay you.
- Fixed: after reconnecting a bank, some matched payments showed up twice in the Transactions list.
