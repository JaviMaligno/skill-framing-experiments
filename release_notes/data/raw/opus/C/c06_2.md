# What's new in Ledgerly

- **Sign in with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now use single sign-on (SSO) with Google Workspace, so your team can log in with their work Google accounts.
- **Safer public invoice links:** Shared invoice links are now better protected against misuse. If a link is opened many times in a short period, the viewer may be asked to wait a moment before trying again.
- **Fixes:**
  - Valid Irish VAT numbers (such as IE1234567FA) are no longer rejected.
  - The dashboard revenue chart no longer shows last month's revenue as this month's around the start of a new month.
