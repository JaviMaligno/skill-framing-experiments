# What's new in Ledgerly

- **Multi-currency invoices:** You can now invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The PDF invoice shows the conversion.
- **Archive clients:** You can now archive a client instead of deleting it. Archived clients no longer show up in your client lists.
- **Fixes:**
  - CSV files that use semicolons to separate values now import correctly. Before, these files failed to import. They are common in Spain and Germany.
  - The email preview now shows your client's name. Before, it showed `{{client_name}}` instead.
