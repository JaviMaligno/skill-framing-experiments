# What's new in Ledgerly

- **Payment QR code on invoices:** Invoices can now include a payment QR code (EPC QR / Girocode). Your clients can scan it with their banking app to pay, with your bank details and the amount already filled in.
- **New Zapier trigger, "Invoice overdue":** Start a Zap automatically when an invoice passes its due date. For example, you can send a reminder or alert your team.
- **Fixes:**
  - PDFs for large invoices (100+ lines) no longer time out or fail to generate.
  - The **Duplicate invoice** button now sets a new due date instead of copying the one from the original invoice.
