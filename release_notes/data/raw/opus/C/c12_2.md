# What's new in Ledgerly

- **Multi-currency invoicing:** You can now send an invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The PDF shows the conversion.
- **Archive clients:** You can now archive a client instead of deleting it. Archived clients are hidden from your client lists.
- **Fixes:**
  - CSV files that use semicolons to separate values, which is common in Spain and Germany, now import correctly. Before, these imports failed.
  - Email previews now show your client's name. Before, they showed the placeholder text {{client_name}}.
