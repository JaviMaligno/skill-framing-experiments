# What's new in Ledgerly

- **New (beta): Sign in with Google Workspace.** Team accounts can now use single sign-on (SSO) with Google Workspace, so your team can log in with their work Google accounts.
- **Safer invoice links:** If someone opens a public invoice link many times in a short period, Ledgerly now limits further access for a while. This helps protect your invoices from misuse. Your clients can still open their invoice links as usual.
- **Fixes:**
  - Valid Irish VAT numbers, such as IE1234567FA, are no longer rejected.
  - Your dashboard revenue chart now shows the current month's revenue. Before, late in the day it could show last month's revenue as the current month.
