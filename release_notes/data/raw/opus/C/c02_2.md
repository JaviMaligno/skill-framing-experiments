## Ledgerly update: two-factor authentication, CSV export and fixes

- **Two-factor authentication:** You can now add an extra layer of security to your account. Turn it on under **Settings → Security**.
- **CSV export:** Exported files now include the **Paid on** column.
- **Fixes:**
  - When Ledgerly was open in two tabs, edits could disappear after autosave. Your edits are now kept.
  - Spanish invoices now spell "Factura" correctly in the footer. It used to read "Fatura".
