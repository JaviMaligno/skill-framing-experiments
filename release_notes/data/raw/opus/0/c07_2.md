# Ledgerly Release Notes: Week of October 19, 2026

## ⚠️ Action required: reconnect your bank by November 30, 2026

We've moved bank feeds to a new provider, TrueLayer, which replaces Plaid. Your bank connection won't carry over, so you need to reconnect it.

**How to reconnect:** Go to **Settings → Bank feeds** and follow the prompts to connect your bank again.

**Deadline: November 30, 2026.** If you haven't reconnected by then, new transactions from your bank will stop syncing into Ledgerly.

## New

- **IBAN on invoice PDFs:** Your IBAN now appears in the footer of invoice PDFs, so customers have your payment details right on the invoice.

## Fixes

- **No more duplicate matched payments:** Fixed a bug where matched payments could show up twice in the Transactions list after you reconnected a bank.

## Improvements

- **Better payment matching:** We've reworked how Ledgerly matches incoming payments to invoices and bills.
- **Behind the scenes:** We made platform and monitoring upgrades to keep Ledgerly fast and reliable.
