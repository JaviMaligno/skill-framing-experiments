# Ledgerly Release Notes

## New

**Payment QR codes on invoices**
Invoices can now include an EPC QR code (also called a Girocode). Your customers scan it with their banking app, and the payment details are filled in for them. They don't need to type an IBAN or reference by hand, so there are fewer payment mistakes.

**Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger**
You can now start a Zapier workflow when an invoice becomes overdue. For example, you could send a reminder, alert your team in Slack, or log the invoice in a spreadsheet.

## Fixes

- **PDFs for large invoices:** Invoices with 100 or more line items no longer time out when you create the PDF.
- **Duplicate invoice:** The "Duplicate invoice" button no longer copies the original invoice's due date. The new invoice now gets a new due date.

## Behind the scenes

We also made general improvements to the codebase and added automated tests to keep Ledgerly fast and reliable.
