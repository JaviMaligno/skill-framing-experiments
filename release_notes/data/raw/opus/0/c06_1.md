# Ledgerly release notes: week of October 19, 2026

## New

**Google Workspace single sign-on (beta)**
Team accounts can now sign in to Ledgerly with Google Workspace. Your team gets one less password to manage, and admins get simpler access control. This feature is in beta, and we'd like to hear your feedback.

## Improvements

**Better protection for public invoice links**
Public invoice links now have rate limiting. This helps protect your invoices from automated abuse. Your clients can still open and pay invoices the same way as before.

## Fixes

- **Irish VAT numbers are accepted.** Valid Irish VAT numbers in the `IE1234567FA` format were being rejected by mistake. You can now enter them without errors.
- **The dashboard shows revenue for the correct month.** After midnight UTC, the revenue chart could show last month's revenue as the current month's. It now shows the correct period whatever your time zone.
