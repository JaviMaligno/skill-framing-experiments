# Ledgerly release notes: week of October 19, 2026

## ⚠️ Action required: reconnect your bank by November 30, 2026

We've moved our bank feed connection to a new provider, TrueLayer. To keep your transactions syncing, reconnect your bank in **Settings → Bank feeds** by **November 30, 2026**. It only takes a minute. If you don't reconnect by then, new transactions will stop importing.

## New

- **IBAN on invoices:** Your IBAN now appears in the footer of invoice PDFs, so customers have your payment details right on the invoice.

## Fixed

- After you reconnected a bank, matched payments sometimes showed up twice in the Transactions list. They now appear only once.

## Improvements

- We made several behind-the-scenes improvements to payment matching, performance and reliability.
