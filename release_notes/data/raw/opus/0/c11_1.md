# Ledgerly Release Notes

## ⚠️ Action required: new password requirements

Passwords must now be **at least 12 characters long**. If your current password is shorter, you can keep signing in with it until **November 20, 2026**. After that date, Ledgerly will ask you to choose a new password the next time you sign in. To skip the prompt, update your password now in **Settings**.

## New

- **Sign in with Microsoft.** You can now sign in to Ledgerly with your Microsoft account.

## Improvements

- **Stronger password protection.** Stored passwords are now secured with a more modern hashing method (argon2id). You don't need to do anything.

## Fixes

- **No more lost invoice drafts when your session expires.** Previously, a session could expire without warning and you'd lose any unsaved changes to an invoice draft. This has been fixed.
- **German translation on the Settings page.** The Settings page was missing some German text. It's now fully translated.
