# Ledgerly Release Notes

## New

**Single sign-on with Google Workspace (Beta)**
Team accounts can now sign in to Ledgerly with Google Workspace. Admins can turn on SSO so members use their existing Google credentials, with no separate password to manage. This feature is in beta, so we'd welcome your feedback.

## Improvements

**Better protection for public invoice links**
Public invoice links now have rate limiting. This helps stop abuse and automated scraping, and your clients can still open and pay invoices as usual.

## Fixes

- **Irish VAT numbers:** Valid Irish VAT numbers in the `IE1234567FA` format are no longer rejected.
- **Dashboard revenue chart:** After midnight UTC, the dashboard sometimes showed last month's revenue as the current month's. That timezone bug is fixed, so the chart now shows the correct period at all times.
