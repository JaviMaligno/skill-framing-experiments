# Ledgerly Release Notes

## ⚠️ Deprecation notice: XLS export
Legacy XLS export is deprecated and **will be removed on December 1, 2026**. Please switch to **XLSX** or **CSV** export before then. Update any workflows, scripts, or integrations that depend on XLS files.

## New
- **Bill recurring invoices on the last day of the month.** Recurring invoices can now be scheduled for the last day of each month, whether the month has 28, 30 or 31 days.

## Fixes
- **No more duplicate reminder emails.** Clicking "Send reminder" twice in a row used to send the customer two emails. Now it sends only one.
- **SVG logo uploads over 1 MB now work.** You can now upload SVG logos larger than 1 MB.
