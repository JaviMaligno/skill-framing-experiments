# Ledgerly Release Notes

## New

- **Two-factor authentication.** You can now protect your account with an authenticator app (TOTP). Turn it on under **Settings → Security**.

## Improvements

- **"Paid on" column in CSV export.** CSV exports now include the date each invoice was paid.

## Fixes

- **Autosave no longer loses edits when you have two tabs open.** Before this fix, editing the same document in two tabs at once could overwrite your changes.
- **Spanish invoice footer typo fixed.** The footer now reads "Factura" instead of "Fatura."
