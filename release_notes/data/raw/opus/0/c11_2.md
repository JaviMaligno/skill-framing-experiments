# Ledgerly Release Notes

## New

**Sign in with Microsoft**
You can now sign in to Ledgerly with your Microsoft account. Choose **Sign in with Microsoft** on the login page.

## Security

**Stronger password requirements**
Passwords must now be at least 12 characters long.
- **Action required:** If your current password is shorter than 12 characters, you'll be asked to change it the next time you log in after **November 20, 2026**. To avoid the interruption, you can update your password now in your account settings.

**Improved password protection**
Passwords are now stored using argon2id, a modern hashing algorithm that offers stronger protection against attacks. You don't need to do anything.

## Fixes

- **No more lost invoice drafts:** Previously, your session could expire without warning, and any unsaved invoice drafts were lost. This has been fixed.
- **German translation:** The Settings page is now fully translated into German.

## Under the hood

- Upgraded to TypeScript 5.7.
