# Ledgerly Release Notes: Week of October 19, 2026

## New

**Payment QR codes on invoices**
Invoices can now include an EPC QR code (also called a Girocode). Your customers scan it with their banking app, and the payment form fills in with your IBAN, the amount and the invoice reference. They don't have to type anything, so you'll see fewer payment mistakes and get paid sooner.

**Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger**
You can now start Zapier workflows when an invoice becomes overdue. For example, you can send a reminder email, post an alert in Slack or create a follow-up task in your CRM.

## Fixes

- **Large invoices generate reliably again.** PDF generation could time out on invoices with 100 or more line items. We've fixed the cause, and large invoices now generate normally.
- **Duplicated invoices get a new due date.** The "Duplicate invoice" button used to copy the original invoice's due date. Duplicates now get a fresh due date based on your payment terms.

## Under the hood

We updated parts of the app's codebase and added automated tests for the onboarding flow. Nothing changes for you, but future releases should be more stable.
