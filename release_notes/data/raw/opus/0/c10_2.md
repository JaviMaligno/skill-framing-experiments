# Ledgerly: what's new this week

## New

**Send e-invoices over Peppol.** You can now send invoices to public-sector clients through the Peppol e-invoicing network, right from Ledgerly. Your invoices reach government and public-sector buyers in the format they require, so you don't need a separate portal.

## Fixes

- **Credit notes now reduce the client's balance.** Issuing a credit note now subtracts it from the client's outstanding balance, so the balance you see is correct.
- **"Mark as paid" now works on mobile Safari.** The button was disabled on iPhone and iPad and can now be tapped as normal.
