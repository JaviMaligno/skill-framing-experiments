# Ledgerly Release Notes: Week of October 19, 2026

## ⚠️ Deprecation notice
**Legacy XLS export is deprecated and will be removed on December 1, 2026.** Please switch to **XLSX** or **CSV** export before then. If you have reports, integrations or workflows that rely on XLS files, update them to one of these formats so they keep working.

## ✨ New
**Recurring invoices can bill on the last day of the month.** You can now schedule a recurring invoice for the last day of each month. It adjusts automatically to month length, so it goes out on the 28th, 29th, 30th or 31st as needed.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **No more duplicate reminder emails.** Clicking "Send reminder" twice in quick succession used to send your customer two emails. Now only one goes out.
- **SVG logos over 1MB now upload.** Uploading an SVG logo larger than 1MB used to fail. It now works as expected.

## 🔧 Under the hood
We added better monitoring and made internal improvements to how Ledgerly sends email. These changes help us find and fix issues faster. You don't need to do anything.
