**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Filter profit & loss by client.** In Reports you can now narrow your profit & loss down to a single client, so you can see what each one actually earns you.
- **iPhone receipt photos upload now.** Expense receipts in HEIC format, the default for iPhone photos, used to fail to upload. They work now, so you don't have to convert them first.
- **Your 51st invoice is back.** If you had more than 50 invoices, one could go missing from your list between pages. It now shows up where it should.

Also: if you use the public API, the rate limit has gone from 60 to 300 requests per minute.
