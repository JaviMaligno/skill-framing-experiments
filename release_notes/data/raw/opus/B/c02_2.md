## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Two-factor authentication.** Add an extra layer of protection to your account with an authenticator app. Turn it on under **Settings → Security**.
- **"Paid on" in your CSV exports.** Exported invoices now show the date each one was paid, so reconciling your books is one less manual step.

## Fixes

- **Edits no longer disappear with two tabs open.** If you had Ledgerly open in two tabs, autosave could quietly drop some of your changes. That's fixed.
- **Spanish invoice footer.** It now reads "Factura" instead of "Fatura."
