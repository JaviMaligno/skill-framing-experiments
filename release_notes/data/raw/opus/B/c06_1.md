## What's new in Ledgerly

**New**
- **Sign in with Google (beta):** Team accounts can now log in with their Google Workspace account (single sign-on), so there's one less password to remember. It's still in beta, so tell us if anything feels off.

**Fixed**
- **Irish VAT numbers are accepted again.** Valid numbers like IE1234567FA were being rejected. They go through now.
- **Your dashboard shows the right month.** Around midnight, the revenue chart sometimes showed last month's figures as this month's. It now shows the current month.

**Also:** public invoice links have better protection against misuse. Your clients can still open and pay them as usual.
