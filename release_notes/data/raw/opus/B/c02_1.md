**What's new in Ledgerly this week**

- **Two-factor authentication:** you can now protect your account with a code from an authenticator app. Turn it on under **Settings → Security**.
- **"Paid on" in CSV exports:** exported invoices now show the date each one was paid, so reconciling with your bank or accountant is easier.
- **No more lost edits with two tabs open:** if you had the same invoice open in two tabs, autosave could sometimes throw away your changes. That's fixed.

Also: Spanish invoice footers now say "Factura" instead of "Fatura".
