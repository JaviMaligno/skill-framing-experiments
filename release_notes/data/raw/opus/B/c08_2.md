# What's new in Ledgerly

## New
- **Payment QR codes on invoices.** Your invoices can now include an EPC QR code (also called a Girocode). Your client scans it with their banking app and the transfer is filled in for them, so there are no IBANs to type and fewer payments with the wrong reference.
- **New Zapier trigger: "Invoice overdue."** You can start a Zap as soon as an invoice goes past its due date, for example to send a reminder, ping yourself in Slack or add a follow-up task.

## Fixed
- Large invoices (100+ lines) now generate their PDF without timing out.
- **Duplicate invoice** now gives the copy a fresh due date instead of the old one.
