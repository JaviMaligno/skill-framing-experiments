## What's new in Ledgerly

**Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** Bill a client in dollars and get paid in euros, or any other pair you need. The conversion is shown on the invoice PDF, so your client sees exactly what they're paying.

**Archive clients instead of deleting them.** You can now archive a client you're no longer working with. They disappear from your client lists, and their history is still there if you need it later.

### Fixes
- CSV imports now work with semicolon-separated files, which is the default in Spain, Germany and many other countries.
- The email preview now shows your client's real name instead of `{{client_name}}`.

Ledgerly should also feel a bit faster.
