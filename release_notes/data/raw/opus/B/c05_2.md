## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Get paid by SEPA Direct Debit.** Clients in Europe can now pay your invoices straight from their bank account through GoCardless. To set it up, go to **Settings → Payments** and connect your GoCardless account.
- **Fresher converted totals.** If you invoice in more than one currency, exchange rates now update every hour instead of once a day. Your converted totals stay closer to the current rate.

**Fixes:** The **Send** button works again for clients who don't have an email address saved, and in the UK the date picker now starts the week on Monday.
