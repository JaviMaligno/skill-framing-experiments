**Heads up:** if your password is shorter than 12 characters, you'll be asked to pick a new one the first time you log in after November 20, 2026. You can change it now in Settings to get it out of the way.

## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Sign in with Microsoft:** you can now log in with your Microsoft account. That's one less password to remember.
- **Your invoice drafts are safe:** before, if you were logged out for being away too long, the invoice you were working on could disappear without any warning. That doesn't happen anymore.
- **Stronger passwords:** all new passwords now need at least 12 characters.

Small fix: the Settings page now shows up in German again.
