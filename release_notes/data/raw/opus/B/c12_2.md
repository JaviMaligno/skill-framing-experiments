## What's new in Ledgerly

**Get paid in a different currency**
- You can now send an invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The PDF shows the conversion, so your client sees exactly what they owe.

**Archive clients instead of deleting them**
- Finished working with a client? Archive them. They won't show up in your client lists anymore, and you keep their history.

**Fixes**
- CSV import now works with files that use semicolons instead of commas. That's the usual format in Spain and Germany.
- The email preview shows your client's real name again instead of `{{client_name}}`.
