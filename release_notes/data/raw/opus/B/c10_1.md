## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Send e-invoices to public-sector clients.** You can now send invoices over the Peppol network, so government and public-sector clients get them straight into their systems, no portal uploads or emailed PDFs.

## Fixed

- **Credit notes now reduce what a client owes.** When you issue a credit note, it comes off the client's outstanding balance like it should.
- **"Mark as paid" works on iPhone and iPad again.** The button was stuck greyed out in Safari; you can tap it now.
