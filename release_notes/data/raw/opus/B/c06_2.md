## Sign in with Google for team accounts (beta)

Team accounts can now sign in to Ledgerly with their Google Workspace login, so there's no separate password to remember. It's in beta, so tell us if anything feels off.

## Fixes

- **Irish VAT numbers are accepted again.** Valid Irish numbers like IE1234567FA were being rejected. You can add them to clients and invoices now.
- **The dashboard shows the right month after midnight.** Around midnight UTC, the revenue chart sometimes showed last month's numbers as this month's. It now shows the current month.

Also: public invoice links are better protected, so nobody can hammer a link you've shared with your clients.
