## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Profit & loss by client:** You can now filter your profit & loss report by client, so you can see how much each one actually earns you.
- **iPhone receipt photos upload now:** Expense receipts saved as HEIC, the format iPhones use for photos, wouldn't upload before. They upload normally now.
- **Every invoice shows up:** If you had more than 50 invoices, the 51st was missing from your list. It's back, and so is the rest of the list.
- **More room on the API:** If you connect other tools through the Ledgerly API, you can now make up to 300 requests a minute instead of 60.
