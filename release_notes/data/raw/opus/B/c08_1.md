## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Payment QR code on your invoices.** Invoices can now include an EPC QR code (also called Girocode). Your client scans it with their banking app and the payment details are filled in for them: no typing out your IBAN, and fewer payments with the wrong reference.
- **New Zapier trigger: "Invoice overdue."** You can now start a Zap when an invoice goes past its due date. For example, send yourself a Slack ping or add a follow-up task automatically.

## Fixes

- Large invoices (100+ lines) now generate their PDF properly instead of timing out.
- "Duplicate invoice" now gives the copy a new due date instead of the old one.
