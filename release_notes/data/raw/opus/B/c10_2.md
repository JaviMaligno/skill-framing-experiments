## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Send e-invoices to public-sector clients.** You can now send invoices over the Peppol network, so government and public-sector clients get them in the format they need.
- **Credit notes now reduce what a client owes.** When you issue a credit note, it comes off the client's outstanding balance.
- **"Mark as paid" works on iPhone and iPad.** The button is clickable again in Safari on mobile.

Also: some under-the-hood improvements to keep things fast and reliable.
