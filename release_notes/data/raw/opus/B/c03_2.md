## What's new in Ledgerly

There's nothing you need to do for this update.

- **Dark mode for your dashboard.** Turn it on under **Settings → Appearance**.
- **Fixed: totals on German-format PDF invoices.** If your invoices use the German (de-DE) format, the PDFs were rounding totals to whole euros. They now show the exact amount, cents included.
- **New webhook event: `invoice.paid`.** If you've connected Ledgerly to other tools, you can now have them notified the moment a client pays an invoice.
