**Heads up: if you export to XLS, switch before December 1, 2026.** From that date the old XLS format won't be available any more. Choose XLSX or CSV when you export. Both open in Excel and other spreadsheet apps.

## What's new this week

- **Bill on the last day of the month:** Recurring invoices can now go out on the last day of each month. February, 30-day months and 31-day months are all handled for you.
- **One click, one reminder:** Clicking "Send reminder" twice used to email your client twice. Now they get just one.
- **SVG logos upload properly:** SVG logos larger than 1MB failed to upload before. They work now.
