**Heads up:** XLS export is going away on **December 1, 2026**. If you export invoices or reports as XLS, switch to **XLSX** or **CSV** before then. Both open fine in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers.

## What's new

- **Bill on the last day of the month:** recurring invoices can now go out on the last day of each month, whether that's the 28th, 30th or 31st.

## Fixes

- Clicking **Send reminder** twice no longer sends your client two emails.
- SVG logos larger than 1MB now upload without errors.
