## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Get paid by SEPA Direct Debit.** Your European clients can now pay you straight from their bank account through GoCardless. To set it up, go to **Settings → Payments**.
- **Fresher converted totals.** Exchange rates now update every hour instead of once a day, so totals in other currencies stay closer to the real rate.

**Fixed:** If a client had no email address saved, clicking **Send** no longer crashes. Also, UK users' date picker now starts the week on Monday.
