Here's what's new in Ledgerly this week:

## What's new

- **Payment QR codes on invoices:** Invoices can now include a payment QR code (EPC QR / Girocode), so clients can pay straight from their banking app.
- **Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger:** Start a Zap automatically when an invoice goes past its due date.
- **Duplicate invoice keeps the right due date:** Duplicating an invoice now sets a new due date instead of copying the old one.
- **Large invoices generate reliably:** Invoices with 100 or more lines no longer time out when creating a PDF.
- **Smoother app performance:** Behind-the-scenes updates to the app's code make it more consistent to use. Nothing you need to change.
