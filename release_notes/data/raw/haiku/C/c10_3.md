Release notes for this update:

## What's new

- **Send invoices to public-sector clients via Peppol.** You can now send e-invoices over the Peppol network to public-sector clients.

## Fixes

- **Credit notes now reduce the client's outstanding balance.** Balances were previously not reduced when you issued a credit note.
- **"Mark as paid" works on mobile Safari.** The button was greyed out and couldn't be tapped on iPhone and iPad.

## Improvements

- **Faster, more consistent tax calculations.** Tax calculations now run on a rewritten engine. Results are unchanged.
