**Ledgerly release notes**

**Action required**
- Sessions are being moved to a new database version. No action needed from you, but you may be signed out once after the update.

**New**
- Two-factor authentication is now available. Turn it on under Settings → Security.
- CSV exports now include the Paid on column.

**Fixed**
- Edits could be lost when autosave ran in two open tabs at the same time.
- The Spanish invoice footer now reads "Factura" instead of "Fatura".
