Here's what's new in Ledgerly this week:

## What's new

- **Payment QR codes on invoices:** Invoices now include a payment QR code (EPC/Girocode) that clients can scan to pay you directly from their banking app.
- **Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger:** Zaps can now start automatically when an invoice goes overdue.
- **Duplicated invoices keep the correct due date:** Duplicating an invoice no longer copies the old due date.
- **Large invoices generate reliably:** PDFs with 100 or more lines no longer time out during generation.
