**What's new in Ledgerly**

**Action required by 30 November 2026**
- Your bank feed is moving from Plaid to TrueLayer. Go to **Settings → Bank feeds** and reconnect your bank before 30 November 2026 to keep your transactions syncing.

**Improvements and fixes**
- Matched payments no longer appear twice in your Transactions list after reconnecting your bank.
- Invoice PDFs now show your IBAN in the footer.
- Faster, more reliable performance behind the scenes.
