## What's new in Ledgerly

- **SEPA Direct Debit payments:** Collect euro payments from clients by direct debit through GoCardless. Turn it on in Settings > Payments.
- **Fresher converted totals:** Exchange rates now refresh every hour instead of once a day, so amounts converted to your currency are more up to date.
- **Fixed a crash when sending:** The Send button no longer fails for clients who don't have an email address.
- **Fixed the date picker for UK users:** Weeks now start on Monday, not Sunday.
