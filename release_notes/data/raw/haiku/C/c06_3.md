## Release notes

- **Sign in with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in with their Google Workspace accounts.
- **Irish VAT numbers accepted:** Valid Irish VAT numbers in the IE1234567FA format are no longer rejected.
- **Dashboard revenue fixed after midnight UTC:** The dashboard no longer shows last month's revenue as current after midnight UTC.
- **Safer public invoice links:** Public invoice links now limit excessive requests.
