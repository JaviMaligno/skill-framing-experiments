**Ledgerly 2026-10-19 release**

## What's new

- **Payment QR codes on invoices.** Invoices now include a QR code that clients can scan with their banking app to pay you directly (EPC/Girocode format).
- **Zapier: "Invoice overdue" trigger.** Zapier can now start a workflow when one of your invoices goes past its due date, so you can send reminders or update other tools automatically.

## Fixes

- **Large invoices generate reliably.** Invoices with 100 or more line items no longer time out when creating the PDF.
- **Duplicated invoices get a fresh due date.** "Duplicate invoice" no longer copies the old due date, so check and adjust it on the new copy.
