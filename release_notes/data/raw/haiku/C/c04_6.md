**What's new in Ledgerly**

**Action required**
- **Legacy XLS export is ending on December 1, 2026.** Switch to XLSX or CSV before then. Exports to XLS will stop working after that date.

**Improvements**
- Recurring invoices can now be set to bill on the last day of the month.
- Tracking and performance improvements behind the scenes.

**Fixes**
- Clicking "Send reminder" more than once no longer sends duplicate emails.
- Logo uploads now work for SVG files larger than 1 MB.
