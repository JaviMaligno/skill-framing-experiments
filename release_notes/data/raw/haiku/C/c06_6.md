Here's what's new in Ledgerly this week:

**New and improved**
- Team accounts can now sign in with Google Workspace single sign-on (beta).

**Fixes**
- VAT numbers in the Irish format (for example, IE1234567FA) are now accepted.
- The dashboard revenue chart now shows the correct current month after midnight UTC instead of still showing last month.
- Public invoice links now limit how often they can be accessed.
