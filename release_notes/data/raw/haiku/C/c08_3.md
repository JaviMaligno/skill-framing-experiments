Here's what's new in Ledgerly this week:

## What's new

- **Payment QR codes on invoices:** Invoices can now show an EPC QR code (Girocode), so clients can pay by scanning it with their banking app.
- **Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger:** Zapier can now start a workflow automatically when an invoice goes past its due date.
- **Large invoices generate reliably:** Invoices with 100 or more lines no longer time out when you create the PDF.
- **Duplicated invoices get a fresh due date:** "Duplicate invoice" no longer copies the old due date.

## Improvements

- Ledgerly is now more responsive and stable behind the scenes.
