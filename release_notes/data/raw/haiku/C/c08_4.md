# Release notes

**Action required:** None.

- Invoices now include a payment QR code (EPC QR / Girocode) that your clients can scan to pay you directly from their banking app.
- New Zapier trigger: "Invoice overdue," so you can start automations when an invoice goes past its due date.
- Fixed a problem where generating PDFs for large invoices (100+ lines) could time out.
- Fixed the "Duplicate invoice" button copying the old due date. The copy now keeps the original due date.
