**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Archived clients are hidden from your lists, so your history stays intact.
- **Multi-currency invoicing.** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The conversion is shown on the PDF.
- **Fixed: CSV import with semicolon delimiters.** Files using semicolons (common in Spain and Germany) now import correctly.
- **Fixed: email preview showing a placeholder.** The client's name now appears in the email preview instead of `{{client_name}}`.
- **Faster, lighter app.** Loading is quicker, and the app is about 40KB smaller.
