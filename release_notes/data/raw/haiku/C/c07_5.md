**What's new in Ledgerly**

**Action required by 30 November 2026**
- Your bank feed is moving from Plaid to TrueLayer. Reconnect your bank in Settings → Bank feeds before 30 November 2026 to keep your transactions syncing.

**Improvements**
- Payments you've matched no longer show up twice in your Transactions list after you reconnect your bank.
- Your IBAN now appears in the footer of invoice PDFs.
- Payment matching is more accurate.
