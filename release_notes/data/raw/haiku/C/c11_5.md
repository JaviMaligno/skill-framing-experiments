**Ledgerly release notes**

**Action required: update your password by November 20, 2026**
- Passwords must now be at least 12 characters. If your current password is shorter, you'll be asked to change it the next time you sign in after November 20, 2026.

**What's new**
- You can now sign in with your Microsoft account.
- Passwords are stored more securely.

**Fixes**
- You're no longer signed out without warning, and your unsaved invoice drafts are kept.
- The Settings page now appears in German.
