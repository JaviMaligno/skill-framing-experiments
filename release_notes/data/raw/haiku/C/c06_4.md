**Ledgerly update: week of October 19, 2026**

## What's new
- **Single sign-on with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in with their Google Workspace login. Beta, so please tell us if anything looks off.

## Fixes
- **Irish VAT numbers:** Valid Irish VAT numbers (in the IE1234567FA format) are accepted again.
- **Dashboard revenue after midnight UTC:** The dashboard now shows the correct month's revenue as current after midnight UTC.
- **Public invoice links:** Public invoice links now have rate limiting, which protects them from abuse.
