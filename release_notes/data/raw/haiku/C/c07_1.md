**What's new in Ledgerly**

**Action required**
- Reconnect your bank in **Settings → Bank feeds** by **30 November 2026**. We're switching bank feed providers from Plaid to TrueLayer, and bank connections need to be set up again.

- Your IBAN now appears in the footer of invoice PDFs.
- Matching payments is more accurate.

**Fixes**
- Matched payments no longer appear twice in your Transactions list after reconnecting a bank.
