**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Pay by SEPA Direct Debit.** You can now collect payments by SEPA Direct Debit through GoCardless. Turn it on under Settings → Payments.
- **Date picker weeks now start on Monday for UK users.** The calendar used to start weeks on Sunday.
- **Sending to clients without an email address no longer fails.** The Send button now works for clients who don't have an email saved.
- **Converted totals update faster.** Exchange rates refresh every hour instead of once a day, so amounts shown in other currencies are more current.
