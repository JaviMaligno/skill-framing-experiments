**Ledgerly release notes**

## What's new

- **Sign in with Microsoft.** You can now sign in to Ledgerly with your Microsoft account.
- **Stronger passwords.** Passwords must now be at least 12 characters. If your current password is shorter, you'll be asked to change it the next time you sign in after November 20, 2026.

## Fixes

- You're no longer signed out without warning, and your unsaved invoice drafts are kept.
- The Settings page now shows German text correctly.
