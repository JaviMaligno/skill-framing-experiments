**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Archive clients:** Archived clients are hidden from your lists. Use archive instead of delete to keep their records.
- **Multi-currency invoices:** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The conversion is shown on the PDF.
- **Fixes:**
  - CSV import now works with files that use semicolons as separators, common in Spain and Germany.
  - Email previews now show the client's name instead of a `{{client_name}}` placeholder.
- **Faster and lighter:** Ledgerly loads faster and uses less data.
