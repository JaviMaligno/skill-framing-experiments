**Ledgerly update**

**Action required by November 20, 2026**
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters. If your current password is shorter, you'll be asked to change it the next time you sign in after November 20, 2026.

**What's new**
- You can now sign in with your Microsoft account.
- Passwords are now stored more securely.
- Ledgerly now runs on a newer version of its underlying technology, which should make it more reliable.

**Fixes**
- You stayed signed in longer than before. Unsaved invoice drafts are no longer lost when your session ends.
- The Settings page now appears in German.
