**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Pay in a different currency:** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The PDF shows the conversion.
- **Archive clients:** Archive a client instead of deleting it. Archived clients are hidden from your lists.
- **Fixes:**
  - CSV import now works with files that use semicolons as separators, common in Spain and Germany.
  - Email previews now fill in the client's name instead of showing `{{client_name}}`.
- **Faster and lighter:** Faster data loading and a smaller app.
