Here are the release notes for this update:

**What's new and improved**

- Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger, so you can start automations when an invoice goes past its due date.
- Payment QR codes: invoices now include a scannable QR code (EPC/Girocode) so clients can pay straight from their banking app.

**Fixes**

- Large invoices (100+ lines) now generate reliably instead of timing out.
- "Duplicate invoice" now sets a new due date instead of copying the old one.
