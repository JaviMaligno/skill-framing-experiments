**What's new in Ledgerly**

- **Archive clients:** Archived clients are hidden from your lists, and you can keep their records instead of deleting them.
- **Multi-currency invoices:** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The conversion is shown on the PDF.
- **CSV import fix:** Files with semicolons as separators (common in Spain and Germany) now import correctly.
- **Email preview fix:** Email previews now show your client's name instead of a `{{client_name}}` placeholder.
- **Faster, lighter app:** Pages load with less code, and client data loads more efficiently.
