Release notes are for Ledgerly's customers (freelancers), so I've left out the Rust rewrite, Terraform updates and logging change. The Peppol item and the Safari fix are the ones users will notice.

## What's new and fixed

- **Action needed:** If you invoice public-sector clients, you can now send invoices to them through the Peppol network. Check that each client's details are complete so invoices reach them.
- **Credit notes now reduce balances:** Credit notes correctly subtract from a client's outstanding balance.
- **"Mark as paid" works on iPhone and iPad:** The button is no longer greyed out in Safari on mobile.
