Ledgerly is now faster at reading dates, PDF totals show correctly in German locales, and the dashboard has a dark mode.

- **Dark mode for the dashboard:** Turn it on under Settings → Appearance.
- **New `invoice.paid` webhook event:** Webhooks can now notify you when an invoice is paid.
- **PDF totals fixed for de-DE:** PDF invoices no longer round totals to whole euros when your locale is German (de-DE).
- **Clearer Stripe webhook retries:** Retry attempts for failed Stripe webhook deliveries are now recorded, so you can see them in your logs.
