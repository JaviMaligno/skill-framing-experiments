Ledgerly 2.x update

**New**
- **Single sign-on with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in with Google Workspace SSO. This feature is in beta, so please share feedback with us as you try it.

**Improvements**
- **Rate limiting on public invoice links:** Public invoice links are now rate-limited to better protect against abuse.
- **React 19:** We've upgraded the app from React 18 to React 19.

**Fixes**
- **Irish VAT numbers:** Valid Irish VAT numbers in the IE1234567FA format are no longer rejected during validation.
- **Dashboard revenue after midnight UTC:** The dashboard chart no longer shows last month's revenue as current right after midnight UTC.
- **Staging deploys:** We fixed a flaky deploy pipeline on staging.
