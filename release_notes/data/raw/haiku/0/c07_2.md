# Ledgerly Release Notes

## Action required: bank feed provider change

We're moving our bank feed provider from Plaid to TrueLayer. **You'll need to reconnect your bank** in **Settings → Bank feeds** by **30 November 2026**. Bank feeds connected through Plaid will stop working after that date.

## New

- **IBAN in invoice PDF footers.** Invoice PDFs now show the IBAN in the footer.

## Improved

- **More accurate payment matching.** We've reworked the PaymentMatcher heuristics to improve how payments are matched to transactions.

## Fixed

- **Duplicate matched payments.** Matched payments no longer appear twice in the Transactions list after reconnecting a bank.

## Under the hood

- Node.js upgraded to 22.11.
- Added Datadog dashboards for queue depth monitoring.
