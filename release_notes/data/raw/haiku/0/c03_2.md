## Ledgerly Release Notes

**New features**

- **Webhooks: `invoice.paid` event.** Webhooks can now notify your systems when an invoice is paid (LED-77).
- **Dark mode for the dashboard.** Turn it on under **Settings → Appearance**.

**Improvements**

- **Stripe webhook retries are now logged.** Each retry attempt for a failed Stripe webhook delivery is recorded, so you can see what happened.

**Bug fixes**

- **PDF invoice totals with `de-DE` locale.** Totals now show their full decimal amounts instead of being rounded to whole euros.

**Internal**

- Replaced moment.js with date-fns.
- CI runners upgraded to ubuntu-24.04.
