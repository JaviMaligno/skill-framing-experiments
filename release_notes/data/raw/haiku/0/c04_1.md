**Ledgerly Release Notes: Week of October 19, 2026**

**⚠️ Deprecation notice**
- **Legacy XLS export is deprecated** and will be removed on **December 1, 2026**. Switch to **XLSX or CSV** export before then.

**New features**
- **Recurring invoices can now bill on the last day of the month.** Useful for month-end billing cycles.
- **OpenTelemetry tracing for the API gateway.** Requests are now traced end to end, improving observability and debugging.

**Bug fixes**
- **Send reminder no longer sends duplicate emails.** Clicking "Send reminder" twice previously sent two emails; a debounce now prevents the duplicate.
- **SVG logo uploads over 1 MB now work.** Previously, logo uploads failed for SVG files larger than 1 MB.

**Internal improvements**
- **EmailService extracted into its own module** to make the codebase easier to maintain and test.
