## Ledgerly release notes

### Action required: bank feed provider change

We're moving our bank feed provider from Plaid to TrueLayer. **You'll need to reconnect your bank in Settings → Bank feeds by 30 November 2026** to keep your transactions syncing.

### New
- **IBAN in invoice PDFs:** Your IBAN now appears in the invoice PDF footer.

### Improved
- **Payment matching:** We've reworked how payments are matched to invoices, which should make matching more consistent.

### Fixed
- **Duplicate matched payments:** Matched payments no longer appear twice in the Transactions list after reconnecting a bank.

### Under the hood
- Node.js upgraded to 22.11.
- Added Datadog dashboards for queue depth.
