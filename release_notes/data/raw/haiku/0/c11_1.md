# Ledgerly 

## New
- **Sign in with Microsoft.** You can now sign in to Ledgerly with your Microsoft account via OAuth.
- **Stronger password hashing.** Passwords are now hashed with argon2id instead of bcrypt.

## Changed
- **New minimum password length.** Passwords must now be at least 12 characters. If your current password is shorter, you'll be asked to change it at your next sign-in after November 20, 2026.
- **TypeScript upgraded to 5.7.**

## Fixed
- **Sessions no longer expire silently.** Previously, an expired session could cause unsaved invoice drafts to be lost. Your drafts are now protected.
- **German translation on the Settings page.** The missing German text on the Settings page now displays correctly.
