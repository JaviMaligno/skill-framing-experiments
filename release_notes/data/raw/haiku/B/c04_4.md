- **Recurring invoices can bill on the last day of the month.** Choose this when you set up a recurring invoice, which is handy for month-end clients.

## What's fixed

- **Send reminder sends one email.** Clicking it twice used to send two emails.
- **Large SVG logos upload.** SVG logos over 1 MB used to fail, and they now go through.
