# Ledgerly release notes, October 19, 2026

## What's new
- **Two-factor authentication.** Add a second step to your login with an authenticator app. Turn it on under **Settings -> Security**.
- **CSV exports include Paid on.** Each invoice in your export now shows the date it was paid.

## Fixed
- **Lost edits with two tabs open.** Autosave could overwrite your latest changes if Ledgerly was open in two tabs. That's fixed.
- **Spanish invoice footer.** It now says "Factura" instead of "Fatura".
