**Ledgerly release notes: week of 19 October 2026**

**Action needed: reconnect your bank by 30 November 2026**

Our bank feed provider has moved from Plaid to TrueLayer. To keep your transactions syncing, reconnect your bank under Settings → Bank feeds before 30 November 2026.

**What's new**
- Invoice PDFs now show your IBAN in the footer, so clients have your payment details on every invoice.

**What's better**
- Matched payments no longer show up twice in your Transactions list after you reconnect a bank.
- Payment matching is more accurate and consistent behind the scenes.

**Small stuff**
- Ledgerly now runs on Node 22.11.
