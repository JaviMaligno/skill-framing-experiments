# Ledgerly update: what's new this week

**Nothing you have to do.** Everything below is live when you log in.

## What's new

- **Payment QR codes on invoices.** Invoices now show a Girocode (EPC QR) so your clients can pay straight from their banking app by scanning it.
- **Zapier: "Invoice overdue" trigger.** Set up a Zap that fires the moment an invoice goes past its due date, and send a reminder, a Slack ping, or a row in your sheet.

## What's fixed

- **Large invoices generate again.** Invoices with 100+ lines were timing out while building the PDF. They now finish.
- **Duplicating an invoice keeps the right due date.** "Duplicate invoice" used to copy the old due date. The new copy now gets a fresh one.

Smaller things: onboarding is now tested more thoroughly, and parts of the app were tidied up behind the scenes. You won't notice a difference in daily use.
