**Action needed before 1 December 2026:** The legacy XLS export is being removed on 1 December 2026. If you export invoices as XLS, switch to XLSX or CSV before then.

## What's new

- **Recurring invoices can bill on the last day of the month.** Choose this when you set up a recurring invoice, and it will go out on the final day of each month, including months with 28, 30, or 31 days.
- **Logo uploads work for large SVG files.** SVG logos over 1 MB now upload without errors.
- **"Send reminder" sends one email per click.** A double-click no longer sends your client two reminders.
