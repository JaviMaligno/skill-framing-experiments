# Ledgerly release notes: week of October 19, 2026

**What's new**

- **Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** Set up an invoice in the currency your client uses and get paid in yours. The conversion now appears on the PDF, so both you and your client can see the exact amount.
- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Clients you're done with are hidden from your lists, and their invoice history stays put.

**Fixes**

- **CSV import works with semicolon files.** Spreadsheets saved with semicolons between columns, common in Spain and Germany, now import without errors.
- **Email previews show the client's name.** You'll no longer see a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder when previewing an email.

**Under the hood**

- Ledgerly loads client data faster, and the app is lighter to download.
