**Ledgerly update: bank feeds, faster matching, and a few fixes**

**Action needed by 30 November 2026**
- **Reconnect your bank.** We've switched bank feed providers from Plaid to TrueLayer. Go to Settings → Bank feeds and reconnect your bank before 30 November 2026, or your transactions will stop syncing.

**What's new**
- **IBAN on invoice PDFs.** Your IBAN now appears in the footer of every invoice PDF, so clients have the payment details they need.

**What's better**
- **No more duplicate payments.** Matched payments no longer show up twice in your Transactions list after you reconnect a bank.
- **Smarter payment matching.** We've reworked how Ledgerly matches incoming payments to your invoices.

**Small stuff**
- Under-the-hood speed and reliability improvements.
