# What's new in Ledgerly

## New
- **Sign in with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now log in with their Google Workspace account. It's still in beta, so tell us if anything feels off.

## Fixed
- **Irish VAT numbers:** Valid numbers in the IE1234567FA format were being rejected. They go through now.
- **Dashboard after midnight UTC:** The revenue chart was showing last month's total as this month's right after midnight UTC. It shows the right month now.

## Changed
- **Public invoice links:** These now limit how often they can be opened in a short window, to block abuse. Normal use shouldn't notice.
