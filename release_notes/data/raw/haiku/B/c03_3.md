## What's new in Ledgerly, 19 October 2026

- **Dark mode for the dashboard.** Turn it on under Settings → Appearance.
- **PDF invoices show exact totals in de-DE.** Totals were being rounded to whole euros when your locale was German; they now show the full amounts.
- **New `invoice.paid` webhook event.** Your integrations can be notified as soon as an invoice is paid.
- Failed Stripe webhook deliveries now log each retry attempt.
