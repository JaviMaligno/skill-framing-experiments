# Ledgerly Release Notes: October 19, 2026

**Action needed:** Public-sector clients can now receive invoices through the Peppol network. If you invoice government or public bodies, check that their Peppol details are on file before your next send.

## What's new

- **Send invoices to public-sector clients over Peppol.** Invoices can now reach public bodies through the Peppol network, the e-invoicing standard many governments require.
- **Better logs for invoice sending.** If an invoice doesn't go out, the details are easier to trace.

## What's fixed

- **Credit notes now reduce the client's balance.** Outstanding amounts were not going down when you issued a credit note. They now do.
- **"Mark as paid" works on iPhone and iPad Safari.** The button was greyed out on mobile Safari. It now works.

## Small stuff

- Under the hood, the tax calculations have been rewritten for speed. Your numbers stay the same.
- Infrastructure updates behind the scenes. Nothing to change on your end.
