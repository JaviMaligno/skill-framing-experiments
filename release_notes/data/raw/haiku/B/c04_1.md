## Action needed by 1 December 2026: move off XLS export

The legacy XLS export is being removed on **1 December 2026**. Switch to XLSX or CSV before then, and update any saved exports or automations that use XLS.

## What's new

- **Recurring invoices can bill on the last day of the month.** Pick this when you set up a recurring invoice and it will always go out on the month's final day.
- **Reminders send once.** Clicking "Send reminder" twice no longer emails your client twice.
- **Larger SVG logos upload.** SVG logos over 1 MB now upload without failing.
