## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Dark mode for your dashboard.** Turn it on under Settings → Appearance.
- **PDF totals fixed for German formatting.** Invoices in de-DE no longer round totals to whole euros. They show the exact amounts.
- **New `invoice.paid` webhook event.** Get notified the moment an invoice is paid. Pick it when you set up a webhook.

Smaller changes:
- Retries of failed Stripe webhook deliveries are now logged.
