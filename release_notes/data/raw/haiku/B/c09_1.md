Ledgerly update: what's new this week

**Heads up: nothing you need to do, but one thing to know**
- Images are moving to a new storage provider. Your existing files stay where they are, and you won't see any change.

**Now you can**
- **Filter profit & loss by client.** Pick one client in Reports to see how much they're worth to you.
- **Use the API more freely.** The limit is now 300 requests per minute, up from 60.

**Fixed**
- **iPhone photos on expense receipts.** HEIC photos from your iPhone now upload without trouble.
- **Missing 51st invoice.** Invoice lists now show every invoice, including the one that used to disappear after 50.

**Smaller changes**
- Old onboarding leftovers are gone, so setup should feel a bit lighter.
