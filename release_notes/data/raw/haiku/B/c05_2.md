## Ledgerly update: 19 October 2026

- **Take payments by SEPA Direct Debit.** Connect GoCardless under Settings → Payments, then let your European clients pay you by bank debit.
- **Send works for clients without an email address.** Before, the Send button could crash if a client had no email on file.
- **Week view starts on Monday for UK users.** The date picker no longer starts weeks on Sunday.
- **Converted totals are fresher.** Exchange rates now refresh every hour instead of once a day.
