# Ledgerly release notes: October 19, 2026

**Two-factor sign-in is here.** You can now turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app (TOTP) under **Settings → Security**.

## What's new
- **CSV export includes the "Paid on" column**, so you can see when each invoice was paid without opening it.

## What's fixed
- **Autosave no longer loses edits** when you have Ledgerly open in two tabs at once.
- **Spanish invoice footers now read "Factura"**, not "Fatura."

## Behind the scenes
- Faster, more reliable session handling after we moved our database to Postgres 16.
- Tax calculations are more thoroughly tested, which helps keep your numbers right.
