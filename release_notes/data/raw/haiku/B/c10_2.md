## What's new in Ledgerly, 19 October 2026

**Send e-invoices to public-sector clients over Peppol**
If a client is a public body that takes e-invoices, you can now send them your invoice over the Peppol network.

**Credit notes now reduce what the client owes**
Credit notes weren't taking their amount off the client's outstanding balance. They do now.

**"Mark as paid" works on mobile Safari**
The button was greyed out in mobile Safari, so you couldn't mark an invoice as paid there. It's clickable again.
