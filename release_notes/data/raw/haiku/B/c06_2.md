**Ledgerly update: week of October 19, 2026**

Nothing you have to do. Here's what's new when you log in:

## What's new

- **Sign in with Google Workspace (beta):** If your team uses Google Workspace, you can now sign in to Ledgerly with your work Google account. It's in beta, so tell us if anything feels off.
- **Rate limiting on public invoice links:** Public invoice links now have a limit on how often they can be opened, which helps keep them safe from abuse.

## What's fixed

- **Irish VAT numbers:** Valid Irish VAT numbers in the IE1234567FA format are accepted again. They were being rejected before.
- **Dashboard revenue after midnight UTC:** The dashboard no longer shows last month's revenue as this month's right after midnight UTC.
- **Staging deploys:** Test deploys to staging are more reliable.

## Under the hood

- Ledgerly now runs on React 19. You won't notice a difference, but the app should feel just as fast and steady as before.
