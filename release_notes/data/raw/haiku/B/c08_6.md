## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Payment QR code on invoices:** Your invoices can now show a payment QR code that clients scan with their banking app to pay you straight away.
- **Invoice overdue alerts in Zapier:** Zapier has a new "Invoice overdue" trigger, so you can set up a zap to ping you or your accounting tool when an invoice goes past due.
- **Large invoices generate reliably:** Invoices with 100 or more lines no longer time out when you create the PDF.
- **Duplicate invoice keeps the right due date:** Duplicating an invoice now sets a fresh due date instead of copying the old one.
- **Smoother onboarding:** Small behind-the-scenes improvements to the sign-up flow, so it should run more reliably.
