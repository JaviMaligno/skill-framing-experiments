# Ledgerly update: what's new this week

**Heads up: nothing you need to do.** Everything below is live now when you log in.

## What's new

- **Send invoices to public-sector clients through Peppol.** If your client is a government body or public agency, you can now send invoices through the Peppol network, the e-invoicing system many of them require.

## What's fixed

- **Credit notes now reduce what a client owes you.** Your client's outstanding balance drops when you issue a credit note, as it should.
- **"Mark as paid" works on iPhone Safari again.** The button was greyed out on mobile Safari and now responds when you tap it.

## Under the hood

- Faster, more reliable tax calculations. Your numbers stay the same.
- Invoice sending now logs more detail, so we can track down problems faster.
- Behind-the-scenes infrastructure updates.
