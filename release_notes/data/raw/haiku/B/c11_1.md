## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Sign in with Microsoft.** You can now log in with your Microsoft account.
- **Longer passwords.** New passwords must be at least 12 characters.
- **No more lost invoice drafts.** Your session used to expire without warning and could take an unsaved draft with it. Drafts now survive that.
- **Settings in German.** The Settings page is translated into German.
