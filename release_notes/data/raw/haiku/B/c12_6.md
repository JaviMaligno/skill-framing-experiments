## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** The conversion now shows on the PDF, so your client can see exactly what they're paying.
- **CSV import works with semicolon files.** Files that use semicolons instead of commas, common in Spain and Germany, now import without errors.
- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Archived clients are hidden from your client lists.
- **Email previews show the client's name.** The preview no longer shows a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder.
