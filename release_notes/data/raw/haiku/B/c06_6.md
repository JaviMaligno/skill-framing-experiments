## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Sign in with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now use single sign-on with their Google Workspace login. It's still in beta, so tell us if anything feels off.
- **Irish VAT numbers accepted:** Valid Irish VAT numbers in the IE1234567FA format no longer get rejected.
- **Dashboard revenue fixed:** After midnight UTC, the revenue chart no longer shows last month's total as the current month.
- **Public invoice links are rate-limited:** Invoice links now have a cap on how often they can be opened in a short time, which keeps them protected from abuse.
