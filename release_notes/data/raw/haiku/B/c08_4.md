# What's new in Ledgerly

**New**
- **Payment QR codes on invoices.** Invoices can now include a Girocode (EPC QR), so clients can pay by scanning it with their banking app. It works for euro bank transfers.
- **Zapier trigger: "Invoice overdue."** Start a Zap as soon as an invoice passes its due date, for example to send a reminder or log it in another tool.

**Fixed**
- **Large invoices generate again.** Invoices with 100+ lines were timing out when creating the PDF. They now come out without the wait.
- **Duplicated invoices no longer copy the old due date.** The copy won't carry over the original's due date, so you don't have to fix it by hand.
