**Ledgerly update: October 19, 2026**

Nothing you need to do. Everything below is ready when you log in.

## What's new

- **Payment QR code on invoices.** Your invoices now include a QR code (EPC QR, also called Girocode) that clients can scan with their banking app to pay you straight away.
- **Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger.** Start a Zap as soon as an invoice passes its due date, for example to send a reminder to your client.

## Fixes

- **Large PDFs generate again.** Invoices with 100 or more lines were timing out when you created the PDF. They now come out fine.
- **Duplicated invoices no longer copy the old due date.** Set the new one when you duplicate an invoice.
