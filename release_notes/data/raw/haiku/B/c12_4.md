# Ledgerly update: what's new this week

**Action needed:** None. Everything below is live when you log in.

## What's new

- **Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** The PDF now shows the conversion, so your client sees exactly what they're paying.
- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Archived clients disappear from your lists but keep their history.

## What's fixed

- **CSV import works with semicolon files.** Spreadsheets using semicolons (common in Spain and Germany) now import without errors.
- **Email previews show the client's name.** You'll no longer see a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder before sending.

## Under the hood

- Ledgerly loads faster and uses less bandwidth, with about 40KB of unused styling removed.
