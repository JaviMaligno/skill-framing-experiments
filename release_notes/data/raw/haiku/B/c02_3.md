## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Two-factor sign-in.** Add a second step to logging in with an authenticator app. Turn it on under Settings → Security.
- **CSV exports now show when each invoice was paid.** Look for the new Paid on column.
- **Fixed: lost edits when you had two tabs open.** Autosave no longer drops what you typed when Ledgerly is open in more than one tab.
- **Fixed: the Spanish invoice footer** now says "Factura" instead of "Fatura".
