# Ledgerly update: bank feeds, faster matching, and a few fixes

**Action needed by 30 November 2026:** Ledgerly is switching bank connections from Plaid to TrueLayer. Go to **Settings → Bank feeds** and reconnect your bank before then, or your transactions will stop syncing.

## What's new
- **Invoice PDFs now show your IBAN** in the footer, so clients have your payment details on every invoice.

## What's better
- **Matched payments no longer show up twice** in the Transactions list after you reconnect a bank.
- **Smarter payment matching.** Ledgerly pairs incoming payments with your invoices more reliably.
- **Faster, more up-to-date app.** Under the hood, we've updated the core software Ledgerly runs on.
