# Ledgerly update: what's new this week

**Action needed:** If your team uses Google Workspace, you can now turn on single sign-on (SSO) for your team accounts. It's in beta, so please test it with a small group first.

## What's new

- **Google Workspace sign-in (beta):** Team members can sign in to Ledgerly with their Google Workspace accounts. Turn it on from your team settings.
- **Public invoice links are rate limited:** Anyone opening a shared invoice link may now see a short "too many requests" message if they open it many times in a row. Normal use isn't affected.

## Fixes

- **Irish VAT numbers accepted:** VAT numbers in the IE1234567FA format are no longer rejected.
- **Dashboard shows the right month after midnight UTC:** Your revenue chart now shows the current month's revenue as soon as the month changes, instead of last month's.
- **Faster, more reliable updates to our test site:** Changes to our staging environment now deploy without intermittent failures. You won't notice this day to day.
- **Smoother performance under the hood:** We've upgraded the app's underlying framework (React 18 to 19). Everything should look and work as before.
