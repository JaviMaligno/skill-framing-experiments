# Ledgerly update: what's new this week

**Action needed:** Payment QR codes are now on invoices, and a new Zapier trigger is available if you connect Ledgerly to other tools.

## What's new

- **Payment QR code on invoices.** Your invoices can now show a scannable QR code (EPC QR / Girocode) so clients can pay straight from their banking app.
- **New Zapier trigger: "Invoice overdue".** Zapier can now start a workflow when an invoice goes past its due date, such as sending a reminder or logging it in another tool.

## What's fixed

- **Large invoices generate again.** Invoices with 100 lines or more were timing out while the PDF was being made. They now finish.
- **Duplicating an invoice keeps the new due date.** "Duplicate invoice" used to copy the old due date. The copy now starts with a fresh one.

## Small stuff

- Onboarding is now tested end to end, so it should stay smooth as we keep improving it.
- Behind-the-scenes cleanup of parts of the app, so it's easier to keep moving quickly.
