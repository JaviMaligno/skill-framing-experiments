## What's new in Ledgerly

**Sign in with Google Workspace (beta)**
Team accounts can now sign in with their Google Workspace login. It's in beta, so tell us if anything feels off.

**Fixes**
- Irish VAT numbers in the IE1234567FA format are accepted again. They were being rejected before.
- The dashboard now shows the right month's revenue after midnight UTC. It was still showing last month's figure.
- Public invoice links now limit how often they can be opened, which helps keep them safe from abuse.
