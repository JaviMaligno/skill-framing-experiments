# What's new in Ledgerly: 19 October 2026

- **Dark mode for your dashboard.** Turn it on under Settings → Appearance.
- **PDF invoices now show exact totals.** If your locale is German (de-DE), totals no longer round to whole euros. The cents are back.
- **New webhook event: `invoice.paid`.** Pick it when you set up a webhook to get notified the moment an invoice is paid.
