# Ledgerly release notes

**What's new**

- **Sign in with Google Workspace (beta).** If your team uses Google Workspace, you can now sign in to Ledgerly with your company Google account. It's in beta, so tell us if anything feels off.

**Fixes**

- **Irish VAT numbers now go through.** Valid Irish VAT numbers in the IE1234567FA format were being rejected. They're accepted now.
- **Dashboard revenue is right after midnight UTC.** Right after midnight UTC, the dashboard chart was showing last month's revenue as this month's. It now shows the correct month.
- **Public invoice links are rate-limited.** Public invoice links now have rate limits, which protects them from abuse.
- **Smoother staging deploys.** Our staging deploy pipeline was flaky. It's more reliable now.

**Under the hood**

- Ledgerly now runs on React 19. You won't see a difference day to day, but it keeps us current and ready for what comes next.
