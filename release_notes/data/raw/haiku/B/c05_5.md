# What's new in Ledgerly: 19 October 2026

- **Get paid by SEPA Direct Debit.** You can now collect payments by SEPA Direct Debit through GoCardless. Turn it on under Settings → Payments.
- **Fresher converted totals.** Exchange rates now update every hour instead of once a day, so amounts shown in your currency are more current.
- **Send button fixed.** It no longer crashes when a client has no email address saved.
- **Date picker fixed for UK accounts.** Weeks now start on Monday instead of Sunday.
