# What's new in Ledgerly: 19 October 2026

## What's new

- **Dark mode for your dashboard.** Turn it on under Settings → Appearance.
- **Webhook for paid invoices.** If you use webhooks, you can now subscribe to `invoice.paid` and get notified as soon as an invoice is paid, so you don't have to check for it yourself.

## Fixed

- **PDF totals in German format.** If your language is set to German (de-DE), your PDF invoices now show the exact total, cents included, instead of rounding to whole euros.
