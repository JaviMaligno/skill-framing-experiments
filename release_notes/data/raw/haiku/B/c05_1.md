## Ledgerly update: 19 October 2026

**New**
- You can now take payments by SEPA Direct Debit through GoCardless. Turn it on under Settings → Payments.

**Fixed**
- The Send button no longer crashes when a client has no email address.
- The date picker starts weeks on Monday for UK users, as it should.

**Smaller changes**
- Converted totals update hourly instead of daily, so they're closer to current exchange rates.
