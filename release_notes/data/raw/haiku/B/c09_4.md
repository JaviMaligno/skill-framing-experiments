Ledgerly update: what's new

**Action needed:** If you upload receipts from your iPhone, you can now attach them as-is. No conversion needed.

## What's new

- **Profit & loss by client:** Filter your profit & loss report to see one client at a time.
- **Faster API for integrations:** If you connect Ledgerly to other tools, you can now make up to 300 requests a minute, up from 60.
- **Images load from a new storage location:** Logos and other images now come from a new storage provider. You don't need to change anything.

## Fixes

- **iPhone receipts upload:** HEIC photos from iPhone now attach to expenses without errors.
- **Invoice list:** The 51st invoice no longer disappears from your list when you scroll past 50.
- **Onboarding:** Removed leftover settings from the old onboarding flow, so the setup screens are cleaner.
