## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Get paid by SEPA Direct Debit.** Turn it on under Settings → Payments to collect euro payments from your clients' bank accounts through GoCardless.
- **Send invoices without the crash.** The Send button no longer fails when a client has no email address saved.
- **Date picker starts the week on Monday for UK accounts.** It had been starting on Sunday.
- **Converted totals are fresher.** Exchange rates now refresh every hour instead of once a day.
