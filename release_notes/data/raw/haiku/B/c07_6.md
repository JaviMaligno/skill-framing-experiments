# Ledgerly update: what's new

**Action needed: reconnect your bank by 30 November 2026**

Our bank feed provider is switching from Plaid to TrueLayer. To keep your transactions syncing, go to **Settings → Bank feeds** and reconnect your bank before 30 November 2026.

## What's new

- **Your IBAN is on invoice PDFs.** It now shows in the footer, so your clients have what they need to pay you.

## What's fixed

- **No more duplicate payments.** After reconnecting a bank, matched payments no longer show up twice in your Transactions list.

## Smaller changes

- Payment matching works more reliably behind the scenes.
- We've upgraded the platform's underlying runtime for speed and security.
- We've added monitoring dashboards to keep an eye on background work.
