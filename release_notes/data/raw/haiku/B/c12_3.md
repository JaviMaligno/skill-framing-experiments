# Ledgerly update: what's new this week

**Heads up: nothing you need to do.** Everything below is live as of today, October 19.

## What's new

- **Get paid in a different currency.** Invoice in one currency and collect in another. The conversion is printed right on the PDF, so your client sees exactly what they're paying.
- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Clients you're done with move out of your lists, and their past invoices stay on record.

## What's fixed

- **CSV import works with semicolon files.** Spreadsheets saved with semicolons (common in Spain and Germany) now import without errors.
- **Email previews show your client's name.** The preview no longer displays a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder.

## Smaller stuff

- Pages load a little faster, with about 40KB of unused styling removed.
- Behind-the-scenes speed-ups to how Ledgerly loads client data.
