## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Profit & loss by client:** Filter the profit & loss report to one client at a time.
- **iPhone receipts:** Expense receipts in HEIC format, the default for iPhone photos, now upload. Before, they were rejected.
- **Invoice list:** Paging through invoices no longer skips the 51st one.
- **Public API:** The limit went up from 60 to 300 requests per minute.
