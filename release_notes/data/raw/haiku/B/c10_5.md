## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Send invoices to public-sector clients over Peppol.** If your client is a government body or public agency that takes Peppol e-invoices, you can now send them invoices through the network.
- **Credit notes now reduce what a client owes you.** Before, a credit note didn't come off the client's outstanding balance. It does now.
- **"Mark as paid" works on mobile Safari.** The button was greyed out on iPhone and iPad, and now you can tap it.
