**Ledgerly release notes: this week's update**

**Action needed: change your password if it's under 12 characters**
Starting **2026-11-20**, any password shorter than 12 characters will need to be changed at your next sign-in. Choosing a longer one now saves you the interruption later.

**What's new**
- **Sign in with Microsoft.** You can now log in with your Microsoft account, so there's no separate Ledgerly password to remember.
- **Stronger password protection.** Passwords must now be at least 12 characters, and they're stored with a newer, more secure method (argon2id).

**What's fixed**
- **No more lost invoice drafts.** If your session expired, your unsaved invoice drafts could disappear. Your work now stays put.
- **German on the Settings page.** The Settings page now shows in German instead of missing text.

**Small stuff**
- Behind-the-scenes update to TypeScript 5.7. You won't notice anything on screen.
