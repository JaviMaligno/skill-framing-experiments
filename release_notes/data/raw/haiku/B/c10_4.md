# Ledgerly update: October 19, 2026

**Action needed:** Nothing for most people. If you send invoices to a public-sector client, read the first item below.

## What's new

- **Send invoices to public-sector clients through Peppol.** Invoices can now go out over the Peppol network, which many government and public bodies require. Set this up per client when you create the invoice.

## What's fixed

- **Credit notes now reduce what your client owes.** Before, a credit note didn't lower the client's outstanding balance. It does now.
- **"Mark as paid" works on iPhone and iPad Safari.** The button was greyed out there and now responds.

## Smaller changes

- Invoice sending now logs more detail, so we can track down problems faster.
- Behind-the-scenes updates to our tax calculations and infrastructure. Nothing changes on your screen.
