**Action needed by 1 December 2026: XLS export is being removed.** On that date, Ledgerly stops exporting XLS files. Start using XLSX or CSV now.

## What's new and fixed

- **Recurring invoices can go out on the last day of the month.** When you set one up, pick the last day, and it will bill on the 28th, 30th, or 31st, whichever is the last day of that month.
- **"Send reminder" sends one email.** Clicking it twice no longer sends your client two.
- **Logo upload works for large SVG files.** SVG logos over 1 MB now upload without failing.
