# Ledgerly update: what's new

**Action needed: reconnect your bank by 30 November 2026**

Your bank feed now runs on TrueLayer instead of Plaid. To keep your transactions flowing, go to **Settings → Bank feeds** and reconnect your bank before 30 November 2026.

## What's new

- **Your IBAN is on invoice PDFs.** It now appears in the footer, so clients have the details they need to pay you.

## What's fixed

- **Matched payments no longer show up twice.** After reconnecting a bank, payments you'd already matched would appear twice in Transactions. That's gone.

## Smaller changes

- Under-the-hood improvements to how Ledgerly matches payments to invoices, so matching should feel more reliable.
- Ledgerly now runs on a newer version of Node (22.11) for faster, more stable performance.
