## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Profit & loss by client:** Filter the profit & loss report to one client to see just their numbers.
- **iPhone photos as receipts:** HEIC photos from your iPhone now upload as expense receipts.
- **Higher API limit:** The Public API now allows 300 requests a minute, up from 60, so busy integrations won't get held up.
- **Invoice list fixed:** The 51st invoice no longer gets hidden when you page through your invoices.
