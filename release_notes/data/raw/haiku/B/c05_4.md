## What's new in Ledgerly, 19 October 2026

- **Collect payments by SEPA Direct Debit.** Find it in Settings → Payments. It works through GoCardless, so your clients in Europe can pay straight from their bank account.
- **Fresher totals in other currencies.** Exchange rates now update every hour instead of once a day, so converted amounts are closer to today's rate.
- **Send button no longer crashes** when a client has no email address saved.
- **Date picker fixed for UK accounts.** Weeks now start on Monday.
