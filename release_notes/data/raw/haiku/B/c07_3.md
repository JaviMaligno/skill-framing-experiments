**Ledgerly release notes: week of October 19, 2026**

**Action needed: reconnect your bank by November 30, 2026**

Our bank feed provider is moving from Plaid to TrueLayer. To keep your transactions syncing, reconnect your bank in **Settings → Bank feeds** before **November 30, 2026**.

**What's new**

- **Your IBAN is on invoice PDFs.** It now appears in the footer of every invoice you send, so clients have what they need to pay you.

**What's fixed**

- **No more duplicate payments in Transactions.** Matched payments used to show up twice after you reconnected a bank. They now appear once.

**Smaller changes**

- Payment matching is more accurate and consistent behind the scenes.
- Our system runs on a newer version of Node (22.11), which keeps things fast and secure.
