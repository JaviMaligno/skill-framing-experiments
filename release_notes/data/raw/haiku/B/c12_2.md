## What's new in Ledgerly: week of 19 October 2026

- **Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** The conversion is shown on the PDF, so your client can see exactly what they're paying.
- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Archived clients are hidden from your lists.
- **CSV import works with semicolon-separated files,** the format common in Spanish and German spreadsheets.
- **Email previews show the client's name** instead of a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder.
