# Ledgerly release notes: week of October 19, 2026

**Action needed:** Spanish invoice footers now say "Factura" instead of "Fatura." Nothing to do on your side.

## What's new

- **Two-factor sign-in is here.** Turn it on under Settings → Security with an authenticator app.
- **CSV exports include a "Paid on" column,** so you can see when each invoice was paid.

## What's better

- **Autosave no longer loses your edits** when you have two tabs open at once.
- **Faster, more reliable sessions** after our database upgrade to Postgres 16. You shouldn't notice anything except fewer surprise logouts.
- **Tax calculations are better tested,** so your numbers are more dependable.
