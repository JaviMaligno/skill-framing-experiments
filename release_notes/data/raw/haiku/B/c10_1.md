Release notes are below. The Peppol e-invoicing and "Mark as paid" fixes are the ones you'll notice first, so I'll put them up top.

**Ledgerly update: October 19, 2026**

**What's new**

- **Send invoices to public-sector clients over Peppol.** If a client needs their invoices through the Peppol network, you can now send them straight from Ledgerly.

**What's fixed**

- **Credit notes now reduce what a client owes you.** Before, a credit note didn't bring down the client's outstanding balance. It does now.
- **"Mark as paid" works on iPhone and iPad Safari.** The button was greyed out on mobile Safari. It's clickable again.
- **Invoice sending is easier to trace.** We added more detailed logs to the invoice sender, so we can track down problems faster if one ever comes up.

**Under the hood**

- We rewrote the tax calculations for speed and reliability. Your numbers come out the same as before.
- We updated our infrastructure setup. You won't see any change from this.
