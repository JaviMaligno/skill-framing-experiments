# Ledgerly release notes

- **Action needed:** Team accounts can now try single sign-on with Google Workspace (beta). Turn it on in your account settings if you want your team to sign in with their work Google accounts.
- Irish VAT numbers in the IE1234567FA format are now accepted when you enter them.
- The dashboard chart now shows the correct current month's revenue after midnight UTC instead of still showing last month's.
- Public invoice links are now protected against excessive requests.
- Small reliability improvements behind the scenes, including faster, more stable updates to the app and performance improvements.
