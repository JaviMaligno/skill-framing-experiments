# Ledgerly release notes

- **Sign in with your Google Workspace account (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in to Ledgerly with Google Workspace single sign-on.
- **Irish VAT numbers now validate correctly:** Valid Irish VAT numbers in the IE1234567FA format were being rejected. They are now accepted.
- **Dashboard shows the right month after midnight UTC:** Right after midnight UTC, the dashboard showed last month's revenue as the current month's. It now shows the correct month.
- **Public invoice links are better protected:** Public invoice links now limit how many requests can be made in a short period.
- **Smaller fixes:** Fixed a few behind-the-scenes issues that could slow down or interrupt updates to Ledgerly.
