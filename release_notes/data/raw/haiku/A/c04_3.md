# Ledgerly update: what's new

- **Action needed:** The legacy XLS export is being retired. Download your data as XLSX or CSV instead before December 1, 2026, when XLS export stops working.
- Sending a payment reminder now sends one email, even if you click "Send reminder" twice.
- Recurring invoices can now be set to bill on the last day of the month.
- Fixed an issue where logo uploads failed for SVG files larger than 1 MB. They now upload correctly.
