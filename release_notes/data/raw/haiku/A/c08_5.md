# Ledgerly 2.14 update

- **Action needed:** None for this update.
- Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger lets you start automations as soon as an invoice goes past its due date.
- Payment QR code: invoices can now show a scannable QR code (EPC / Girocode) so clients can pay straight from their banking app.
- Fixed: large invoices (100+ lines) no longer time out when generating a PDF.
- Fixed: "Duplicate invoice" now gives the copy a new due date instead of keeping the old one.
- Smaller fixes and under-the-hood improvements to make Ledgerly more reliable.
