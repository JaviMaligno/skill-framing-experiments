# Ledgerly release notes

- **Action needed:** none for this update.
- **Pay in a different currency:** invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The converted amount is shown on the PDF.
- **Archive clients:** archive a client instead of deleting it. Archived clients are hidden from your lists.
- **Fixed CSV import** for files that use semicolons as separators, common in Spain and Germany.
- **Fixed email previews** that showed a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder instead of the client's name.
- **Faster, lighter app:** pages load more quickly and use less data.
