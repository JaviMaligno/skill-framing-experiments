## What's new in Ledgerly

- Two-factor authentication is now available. Turn it on under Settings → Security to add an extra step when you sign in.
- CSV exports now include the Paid on column, so you can see when each invoice was paid.
- Fixed a problem where edits could be lost when you had Ledgerly open in two tabs at once.
- Fixed a typo in the footer of Spanish invoices, which now reads "Factura" instead of "Fatura."
