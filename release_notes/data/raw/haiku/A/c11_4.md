# Ledgerly update: sign-in, security, and fixes

- **Action required:** If your password is shorter than 12 characters, you must change it the next time you sign in after November 20, 2026.
- You can now sign in with your Microsoft account.
- Passwords now require at least 12 characters, and they are stored with stronger protection.
- Fixed an issue where you could be signed out without warning and lose unsaved invoice drafts.
- Fixed a missing German translation on the Settings page.
- Performance and reliability improvements under the hood.
