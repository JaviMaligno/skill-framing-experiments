# Ledgerly 2.x: What's New

- **New webhook event:** Your integrations can now receive an `invoice.paid` notification the moment an invoice is paid.
- **Dark mode for your dashboard:** Turn it on under Settings → Appearance.
- Fixed: PDF invoices in the de-DE locale now show totals with cents instead of rounding to whole euros.
- Small fixes and speed-ups across the app, including more detailed logging of retried Stripe webhook deliveries.
