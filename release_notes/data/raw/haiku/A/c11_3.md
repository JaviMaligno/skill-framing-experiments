# Ledgerly release notes

- **Action needed by November 20, 2026:** if your password is shorter than 12 characters, you'll be asked to change it the next time you sign in.
- Passwords now need at least 12 characters.
- You can now sign in with your Microsoft account.
- Fixed a problem where you could be signed out without warning and lose invoice drafts you hadn't saved yet.
- Fixed a problem where part of the Settings page appeared in English instead of German.
- Security and performance improvements behind the scenes, including stronger password protection.
