## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Action required:** Passwords must now be at least 12 characters. If your current password is shorter, you'll be asked to change it the next time you sign in after November 20, 2026.
- You can now sign in with your Microsoft account.
- Fixed: you could be signed out without warning and lose unsaved invoice drafts. Your drafts are now kept.
- Fixed: the Settings page showed some text in English instead of German for German-language users.
