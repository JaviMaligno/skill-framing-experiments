# Ledgerly release notes

- **Action needed for Zapier users:** Invoice overdue is now available as a trigger, so your Zaps can start when an invoice passes its due date. (Check your existing Zaps for any that should use it.)
- Added a payment QR code to invoices, so clients can pay by scanning it with their banking app (EPC QR / Girocode).
- Fixed a problem where invoices with 100 or more lines could take too long to generate or fail to download as PDF.
- Fixed Duplicate invoice copying the old due date. The copy now starts with a due date you can set yourself.
- Various under-the-hood improvements to keep Ledgerly fast and reliable.
