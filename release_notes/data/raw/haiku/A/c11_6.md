# Ledgerly update: security, sign-in, and fixes

- **Action needed by November 20, 2026:** Passwords must now be at least 12 characters. If yours is shorter, you'll be asked to create a new one the next time you sign in after November 20, 2026.
- You can now sign in with your Microsoft account.
- Your session no longer ends without warning, and unsaved invoice drafts are no longer lost when it does.
- The Settings page now appears in German.
