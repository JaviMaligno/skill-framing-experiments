# Ledgerly 2.x: What's new

- **Action needed (webhooks):** If you use webhooks, you can now subscribe to the new `invoice.paid` event to be notified the moment an invoice is paid.
- **Dark mode for your dashboard:** Turn it on under Settings → Appearance.
- **PDF invoices in German formatting:** Totals on PDF invoices now show the exact amount with cents when your locale is de-DE, instead of rounding to whole euros.
- **Stripe webhook reliability:** Failed Stripe webhook deliveries are now retried, so fewer payment updates get lost.
- **Smaller fixes and improvements:** Date handling and other behind-the-scenes updates to keep Ledgerly fast and stable.
