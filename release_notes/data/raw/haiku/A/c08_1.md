# Ledgerly: This Week's Updates

- **Zapier:** New "Invoice overdue" trigger lets you start automations when an invoice goes past its due date.
- **Payment QR codes:** Invoices can now include a scannable QR code so clients can pay you straight from their banking app. Compatible with European (EPC/Girocode) payments.
- **Duplicate invoice:** Fixed an issue where duplicating an invoice kept the original due date. The new invoice now gets its own date.
- **Large invoices:** Fixed an issue where invoices with 100 or more lines could fail to generate PDFs or time out. They now generate reliably.
- **Performance and reliability:** Behind-the-scenes improvements to how parts of Ledgerly load and run.
