## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Dark mode for your dashboard:** Turn it on in Settings → Appearance.
- **New webhook event for paid invoices:** Webhooks can now send an `invoice.paid` event when an invoice is paid, so your other tools can react right away.
- **Fixed:** PDF invoices in the German (de-DE) format showed totals rounded to whole euros. They now show the exact amount, including cents.
