# Ledgerly update: bank connections, payment matching fixes, and invoice IBAN

**Action required by 30 November 2026:** reconnect your bank in Settings → Bank feeds. Ledgerly is switching bank connections to TrueLayer, so connections made through the previous provider will stop working after that date.

- Your IBAN now appears in the footer of invoice PDFs.
- Fixed an issue where matched payments could show up twice in the Transactions list after reconnecting your bank.
- Improved how payments are matched to invoices.
