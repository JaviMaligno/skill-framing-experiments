# Ledgerly release notes

- **Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** The PDF now shows the conversion, so your client sees exactly what they're paying.
- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Archived clients disappear from your lists, and their records stay safe.
- **Fixed CSV import for semicolon-separated files** (common in Spain and Germany). These files now import correctly.
- **Fixed email previews showing a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder.** The client's name now appears in the preview.
- **Faster, lighter app.** Smaller download and quicker loading across the app.
