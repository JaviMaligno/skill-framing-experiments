# Ledgerly update

- **Action needed:** To use SEPA Direct Debit, go to Settings → Payments and connect your GoCardless account.
- You can now collect payments by SEPA Direct Debit through GoCardless.
- Fixed a crash that could happen when sending an invoice to a client who had no email address.
- Converted totals now update hourly, so they show more recent exchange rates.
- Fixed the date picker so weeks start on Monday for UK users.
