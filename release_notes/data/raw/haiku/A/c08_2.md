# Ledgerly: what's new this week

- **Action needed:** Update any Zapier automations that send invoice data to Ledgerly before the next release. *(No deadline was provided. Add the date here before publishing.)*
- **New Zapier trigger:** "Invoice overdue" starts a Zap as soon as one of your invoices goes past its due date.
- **Payment QR codes on invoices:** Invoices now include a scannable Girocode (EPC QR) so clients can pay from their banking app.
- **Duplicate invoice fix:** "Duplicate invoice" now sets a new due date instead of copying the old one.
- **Faster PDFs for large invoices:** Invoices with 100 or more line items now generate their PDF without timing out.
