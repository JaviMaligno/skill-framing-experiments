# Ledgerly update: this week

- **Send invoices to public-sector clients over Peppol.** You can now send invoices through the Peppol network to public-sector clients.
- **Credit notes now reduce what clients owe you.** Issued credit notes were not lowering the outstanding balance on the client's account. Balances now update correctly.
- **"Mark as paid" works on iPhone and iPad.** The button was greyed out and could not be tapped in Safari on mobile. It is now active.
- **Smaller fixes and improvements.** Faster and more reliable tax calculations, updated behind-the-scenes infrastructure, and better tracking of invoice sending so issues are easier to resolve.
