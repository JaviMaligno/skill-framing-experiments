# Ledgerly release notes

- **Action needed:** None for this release. Your existing CSV imports keep working as before.
- **Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** The conversion is shown on the PDF so your client can see exactly what they're paying.
- **CSV import now works with semicolon-separated files**, common in Spain and Germany. Previously these files failed to import.
- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Archived clients are hidden from your lists, and their history stays intact.
- **Fixed:** the email preview showed a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder instead of the client's name.
- **Faster, lighter app.** Pages load more quickly, and several small performance and stability improvements are included.
