# Ledgerly release notes

- **Action needed: none.** Multi-currency invoicing is here: invoice in one currency and get paid in another, with the conversion shown on the PDF.
- Archive clients instead of deleting them. Archived clients are hidden from your lists.
- Fixed: CSV import now works for files with semicolon separators, common for Spanish and German spreadsheets.
- Fixed: the email preview no longer shows a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder; it shows the client's name.
- Faster app loading and smaller page downloads, with no change to how things look or work.
