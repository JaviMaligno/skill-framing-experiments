# Ledgerly update: bank feed upgrade, payment matching fix, and invoice IBAN

**Action required by 30 November 2026:** To keep your bank feed working, reconnect your bank in Settings → Bank feeds. Ledgerly now uses TrueLayer for bank connections, so existing connections need to be re-authorized before the deadline.

- Fixed matched payments appearing twice in the Transactions list after reconnecting your bank.
- Your IBAN now appears in the footer of invoice PDFs.
