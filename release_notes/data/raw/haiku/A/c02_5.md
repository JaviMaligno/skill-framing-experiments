# What's new in Ledgerly

- **Two-factor authentication:** You can now turn on two-factor authentication for your account under Settings → Security.
- **Autosave:** Fixed an issue where edits could be lost when the same invoice was open in two tabs.
- **CSV export:** Exports now include the Paid on column.
- **Spanish invoice footer:** Fixed a typo so the footer now correctly reads "Factura."
- **Under the hood:** Performance and reliability improvements.
