# Ledgerly update: new this week

- **Public API:** You can now make up to 300 requests per minute, up from 60.
- **Expense receipts:** iPhone photos in HEIC format now upload without errors.
- **Invoices:** Fixed an issue where the 51st invoice in your list was missing from view.
- **Reports:** Profit & loss can now be filtered by client.
- **Faster, cleaner app:** Image storage has moved to a new provider, so your images load as before, with no action needed on your part.
- **Onboarding:** Removed outdated setup options from the old onboarding flow.
