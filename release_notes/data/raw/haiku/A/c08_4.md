# Ledgerly update

- **Payment QR codes on invoices:** Invoices can now include a payment QR code (EPC/Girocode) that clients scan to pay you directly from their banking app.
- **Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger:** Zaps can now start automatically when an invoice goes past its due date.
- **Large invoices generate reliably:** Invoices with 100 or more lines no longer time out when you create the PDF.
- **Duplicate invoice keeps the new due date:** Duplicating an invoice no longer copies the old due date, so you can set a fresh one.
- **Smaller fixes and improvements:** Behind-the-scenes cleanup for faster, more consistent screens and a more reliable onboarding experience.
