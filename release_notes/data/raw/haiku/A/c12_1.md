# Ledgerly: What's New

- **Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** The converted amount now appears on your PDF invoice, so your client sees exactly what they're paying.
- **Fixed: CSV imports from Spain and Germany.** Files that use semicolons between columns now import correctly instead of failing.
- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Archived clients disappear from your lists but keep their history, so you can bring them back later.
- **Fixed: email previews showing `{{client_name}}`.** Previews now show the client's actual name.
- **Faster app loading.** Pages load more quickly and use less data.
