# Ledgerly update: bank connections, payment matching, and invoices

- **Action required by 2026-11-30:** reconnect your bank in Settings → Bank feeds. Ledgerly now uses TrueLayer instead of Plaid for bank connections, so existing connections need to be re-linked before the deadline.
- Fixed: matched payments no longer appear twice in the Transactions list after reconnecting a bank.
- Invoice PDFs now show your IBAN in the footer.
- Small fixes and performance improvements behind the scenes.
