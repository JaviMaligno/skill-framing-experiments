# Ledgerly 2.x update: stronger sign-in and fixes

**Action required:** If your password is shorter than 12 characters, you must change it the next time you sign in after **November 20, 2026**. Passwords need at least 12 characters from now on.

- You can now sign in with your Microsoft account.
- Your session no longer expires without warning, and unsaved invoice drafts are kept.
- The Settings page now appears in German.
- Your account data is protected with stronger password encryption.
