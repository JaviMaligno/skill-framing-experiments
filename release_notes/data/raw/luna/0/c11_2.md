## Ledgerly update

### Security & sign-in
- Passwords must now be at least 12 characters long. Starting November 20, 2026, users with shorter passwords will be asked to change them at their next login.
- Passwords are now protected with Argon2id hashing.
- You can now sign in with a Microsoft account.

### Fixes
- Session expiry now gives clearer feedback, and unsaved invoice drafts are preserved.
- Restored the German translation on the Settings page.

### Technical update
- Updated TypeScript to 5.7.
