## Ledgerly update

- **Google Workspace SSO (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in with Google Workspace.
- **More reliable staging deploys:** Fixed a flaky staging deployment pipeline.
- **Irish VAT validation:** Valid Irish VAT numbers in formats such as `IE1234567FA` are now accepted.
- **React 19:** Upgraded from React 18.
- **Dashboard timezone fix:** Revenue charts now show the correct current-month figures after midnight UTC.
- **Public invoice link protection:** Added rate limiting to public invoice links (LED-91).
