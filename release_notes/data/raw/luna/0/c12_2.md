# This week in Ledgerly

Invoices are now more flexible: bill in one currency and get paid in another, with the conversion clearly shown on the PDF.

We also fixed CSV imports for semicolon-delimited files, common in Spain and Germany, and email previews now display client names instead of a raw placeholder.

Clients can now be archived rather than deleted, keeping them out of lists without losing their records. Behind the scenes, GraphQL client lookups are more efficient, and we removed 40 KB of unused CSS.
