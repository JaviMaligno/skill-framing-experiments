# Ledgerly — This Week’s Updates

- **Invoice across currencies:** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The conversion is now shown on the PDF.
- **CSV imports:** Fixed imports for semicolon-delimited files, common in Spain and Germany.
- **Archived clients:** You can now archive clients instead of deleting them. Archived clients are hidden from lists.
- **Email previews:** Fixed previews that showed the raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder.
- **Behind the scenes:** Improved GraphQL client resolution with DataLoader and removed 40 KB of unused CSS.
