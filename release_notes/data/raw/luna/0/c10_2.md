## Ledgerly release notes

- **E-invoicing:** Send invoices to public-sector clients over the Peppol network.
- **Credit notes:** Fixed an issue where credit notes weren’t deducted from a client’s outstanding balance.
- **Mobile Safari:** Fixed the disabled “Mark as paid” button.
- **Behind the scenes:** Rewrote the tax engine in Rust with no change to its behavior, updated Terraform modules, and added structured logging to the invoice sender.
