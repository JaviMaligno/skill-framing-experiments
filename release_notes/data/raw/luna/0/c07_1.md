## Ledgerly release notes

- **Action required by 30 November 2026:** Ledgerly is moving its bank feed provider from Plaid to TrueLayer. Reconnect your bank in **Settings → Bank feeds** by this date to keep your feeds connected.
- Fixed an issue where matched payments appeared twice in Transactions after reconnecting a bank.
- Invoice PDFs now show the IBAN in the footer.
- Added Datadog dashboards to monitor queue depth.
- Refactored PaymentMatcher heuristics and upgraded Node to 22.11.
