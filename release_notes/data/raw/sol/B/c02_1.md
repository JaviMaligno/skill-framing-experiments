## What’s new in Ledgerly

- **Extra security for your account:** Set up two-factor authentication (TOTP) in Settings → Security.
- **No more lost edits across tabs:** Autosave now keeps your edits when you have two tabs open.
- **Payment dates in exports:** CSV exports now include the Paid on column.
- **A small Spanish fix:** Spanish invoice footers now say “Factura” instead of “Fatura.”
