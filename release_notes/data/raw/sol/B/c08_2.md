## What’s new this week

- **Automate overdue reminders:** Use the new “Invoice overdue” trigger in Zapier to start a workflow when an invoice becomes overdue.
- **Make paying easier:** Add an EPC QR / Girocode to invoices so clients can scan it to fill in payment details.
- **Download large invoices:** PDF generation no longer times out for invoices with 100+ lines.
- **Duplicate without the old due date:** “Duplicate invoice” no longer carries over the original invoice’s due date.
