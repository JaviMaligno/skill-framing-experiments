## What’s new in Ledgerly

- **Automate overdue follow-ups:** Use the new “Invoice overdue” trigger in Zapier.
- **Make paying easier:** Add an EPC QR / Girocode payment QR code to your invoices.
- **Download large invoices:** Fixed PDF generation timeouts for invoices with 100+ lines.
- **Duplicate without the old due date:** “Duplicate invoice” no longer copies the original invoice’s due date.
