## What’s new in Ledgerly

- **Google Workspace sign-in (beta):** Team accounts can now use single sign-on (SSO) with Google Workspace.
- **Irish VAT numbers:** Valid numbers in the `IE1234567FA` format are now accepted.
- **Accurate revenue charts:** Your dashboard no longer shows last month’s revenue as this month’s after midnight UTC.
- **Safer invoice links:** Public invoice links now limit excessive requests.
