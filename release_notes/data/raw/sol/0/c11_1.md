## What’s new
- **Sign in with Microsoft:** You can now use your Microsoft account to sign in to Ledgerly.

## Security
- **Stronger passwords:** Passwords must now contain at least 12 characters. If your password is shorter, you’ll be prompted to change it at your next login after November 20, 2026.
- Passwords are now hashed using Argon2id instead of bcrypt.

## Fixes
- Fixed an issue where sessions expired silently, causing unsaved invoice drafts to be lost.
- Restored missing German translations on the Settings page.

## Under the hood
- Updated TypeScript to 5.7.
