## What’s new
- **Sign in with Microsoft:** Use your Microsoft account to sign in to Ledgerly.
- **Stronger password security:** New passwords must contain at least 12 characters. If your current password is shorter, you’ll need to change it at your next login after November 20, 2026.
- Passwords are now hashed with Argon2id instead of bcrypt.

## Fixes
- Fixed an issue where sessions expired silently, causing unsaved invoice drafts to be lost.
- Added the missing German translation on the Settings page.

## Under the hood
- Updated TypeScript to version 5.7.
