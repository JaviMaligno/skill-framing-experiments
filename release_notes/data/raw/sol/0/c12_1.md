## What’s new

- **Multi-currency invoices:** Invoice in one currency and receive payment in another, with the conversion shown on the invoice PDF.
- **Archive clients:** Archive clients instead of deleting them. Archived clients stay out of your client lists.

## Fixes

- Fixed CSV imports failing on semicolon-separated files, commonly used in Spain and Germany.
- Fixed email previews showing `{{client_name}}` instead of the client’s name.

## Under the hood

- Batched GraphQL client lookups with DataLoader.
- Removed 40 KB of unused CSS.
