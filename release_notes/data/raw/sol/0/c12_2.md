## What’s new

- **Multi-currency invoicing:** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another, with the conversion shown on the invoice PDF.
- **Archive clients:** Archive clients instead of deleting them. Archived clients are hidden from lists.

## Fixes & improvements

- Fixed CSV imports failing on files with semicolon delimiters, commonly used in Spain and Germany.
- Fixed email previews showing `{{client_name}}` instead of the client’s name.
- Batched client data fetching to reduce redundant requests.
- Removed 40 KB of unused styles.
