## Ledgerly update: pay in one currency, invoice in another

- **Multi-currency invoicing:** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The conversion now appears on the PDF.
- **Archive clients:** You can now archive clients instead of deleting them. Archived clients are hidden from your client lists.
- **Fixes:** CSV import now works with files that use semicolons as separators, and the email preview no longer shows a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder instead of the client's name.
