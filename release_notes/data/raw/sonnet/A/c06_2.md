## Ledgerly update

- **Single sign-on with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in with their Google Workspace accounts.
- **Fixes:**
  - VAT numbers in the Irish format (for example, IE1234567FA) are now accepted instead of being rejected as invalid.
  - The dashboard chart no longer shows last month's revenue as the current month's right after midnight UTC.
- **Safer public invoice links:** Public invoice links are now protected against excessive repeated access.
