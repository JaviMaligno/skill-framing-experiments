**Ledgerly update: what's new**

- **Single sign-on with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in with Google Workspace.
- **Dashboard chart:** The chart no longer shows last month's revenue as the current month shortly after midnight UTC.
- **VAT numbers:** Valid Irish VAT numbers (such as IE1234567FA) are now accepted instead of being rejected.
- **Public invoice links:** Public invoice links are now better protected against excessive repeated access.
