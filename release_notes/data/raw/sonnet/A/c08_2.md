**Ledgerly update**

- **Overdue invoice alerts in Zapier:** the new "Invoice overdue" trigger lets you start automations as soon as an invoice becomes overdue.
- **Payment QR code on invoices:** invoices can now show an EPC QR code (Girocode), so clients can pay by scanning it with their banking app.
- **Fixes:** large invoices (100+ lines) no longer fail to generate as PDFs, and "Duplicate invoice" now gives the copy a fresh due date instead of the old one.
