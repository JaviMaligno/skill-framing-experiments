## Ledgerly release notes

- **Pay by SEPA Direct Debit:** you can now let clients pay you by SEPA Direct Debit. Turn it on in **Settings → Payments**.
- **Fresher converted totals:** exchange rates now update more often, so your converted totals are more up to date.
- **Fixes:** the **Send** button no longer crashes when a client has no email address, and the date picker now starts the week on Monday for UK users.
