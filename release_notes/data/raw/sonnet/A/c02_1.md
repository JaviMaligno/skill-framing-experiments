## Ledgerly release notes

- **Two-factor authentication:** You can now protect your account with an extra sign-in step. Turn it on under Settings -> Security.
- **CSV export:** Exports now include the Paid on column.
- **Fixes:** Edits are no longer lost when you have Ledgerly open in two tabs at once, and the Spanish invoice footer now reads "Factura" instead of "Fatura".
