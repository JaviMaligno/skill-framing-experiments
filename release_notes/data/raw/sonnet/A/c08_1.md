## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Payment QR code on invoices:** Your invoices can now include a payment QR code (EPC QR / Girocode) so clients can pay straight from their banking app.
- **Zapier:** There's a new "Invoice overdue" trigger, so you can start automations when an invoice goes overdue.
- **Fixes:** Large invoices (100+ lines) no longer fail to generate as a PDF. The "Duplicate invoice" button now sets a fresh due date instead of copying the old one.
