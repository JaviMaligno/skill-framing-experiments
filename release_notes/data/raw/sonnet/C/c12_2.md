## Ledgerly release notes

- **Multi-currency invoicing:** Invoice a client in one currency and get paid in another. The conversion now appears on the invoice PDF.
- **Archive clients:** You can now archive clients instead of deleting them. Archived clients are hidden from your client lists.
- **Fixes:**
  - CSV imports now work with files that use semicolons as separators, common in Spain and Germany.
  - The email preview now shows the client's name instead of the raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder.
