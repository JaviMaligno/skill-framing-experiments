## What's new in Ledgerly

- **Multi-currency invoicing:** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The conversion now appears on the PDF.
- **Archive clients:** Archive clients instead of deleting them. Archived clients are hidden from your client lists.
- **Fixes:**
  - CSV imports now work with semicolon-separated files, common in Spain and Germany.
  - The email preview no longer shows a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder; it now shows the client's name.
