## What's new

- **Payment QR code on invoices:** Your invoices can now include a payment QR code (EPC QR / Girocode), so clients can scan it with their banking app to pay.
- **Zapier trigger "Invoice overdue":** Set up automations in Zapier that start when an invoice becomes overdue, such as sending a reminder or notifying your team.
- **Large invoices now generate reliably:** Invoices with 100 or more lines no longer time out when you create the PDF.
- **Duplicate invoice fixed:** "Duplicate invoice" now gives the copy a fresh due date instead of the old one.
