## Release notes

- **Single sign-on with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in with Google Workspace.
- **Irish VAT numbers:** Valid Irish VAT numbers (like IE1234567FA) are now accepted instead of being rejected.
- **Dashboard revenue:** The dashboard chart now shows the right month's revenue as current after midnight, instead of last month's.
- **Safer invoice links:** Public invoice links are now better protected against excessive repeated visits.
