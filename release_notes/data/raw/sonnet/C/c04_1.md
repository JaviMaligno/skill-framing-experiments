## What's new in Ledgerly

**Action required**
- **Switch from XLS to XLSX or CSV before December 1, 2026.** The XLS export will be removed on that date.

**New**
- Recurring invoices can now be set to bill on the last day of the month.

**Fixes**
- Clicking "Send reminder" twice no longer sends your client two emails.
- Uploading a logo in SVG format over 1MB now works.
