## What's new

- **Two-factor authentication:** You can now add an extra layer of security to your account. Turn it on under Settings → Security.
- **CSV export:** Exports now include the "Paid on" column.
- **Fixes:**
  - Edits are no longer lost when Ledgerly is open in two tabs at once.
  - The Spanish invoice footer now reads "Factura" instead of "Fatura".
