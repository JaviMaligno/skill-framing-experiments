## What's new

- **Payment QR code on invoices:** Your invoices can now show a payment QR code (EPC QR / Girocode), so clients can scan it with their banking app and pay without typing in your details.
- **Zapier trigger "Invoice overdue":** You can now start a Zap automatically when an invoice becomes overdue, for example to send yourself a reminder or notify your client.
- **Fixes:**
  - Large invoices (100+ lines) no longer fail to generate as PDFs.
  - "Duplicate invoice" now gives the copy a new due date instead of the original one.
