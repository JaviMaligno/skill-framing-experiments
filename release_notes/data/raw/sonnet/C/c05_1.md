## What's new

- **Action needed:** none this week.
- **Pay by SEPA Direct Debit:** You can now accept SEPA Direct Debit payments from your clients. Turn it on in Settings → Payments.
- **Fresher converted totals:** Exchange rates now update more often, so totals converted into another currency are more up to date.
- **Fixes:**
  - The Send button no longer crashes when a client has no email address.
  - The date picker now starts the week on Monday for UK users.
