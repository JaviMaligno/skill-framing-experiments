## Release notes

- **Single sign-on with Google Workspace (beta):** Team accounts can now sign in with their Google Workspace login.
- **Irish VAT numbers:** Valid Irish VAT numbers (e.g. IE1234567FA) are now accepted instead of being rejected.
- **Dashboard revenue chart:** Fixed the chart showing last month's revenue as the current month after midnight UTC.
- **Public invoice links:** Added protection against excessive repeated access to your shared invoice links.
