# What's new in Ledgerly

- **Get paid by SEPA Direct Debit.** Turn it on in Settings → Payments, and clients can pay you by direct debit through GoCardless.
- **Fresher converted totals.** Exchange rates now update every hour instead of once a day.
- **Fixes**
  - The Send button no longer crashes when a client has no email address.
  - The date picker now starts the week on Monday for UK users.
