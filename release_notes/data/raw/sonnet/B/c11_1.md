**Action needed by 2026-11-20: update your password if it's under 12 characters**

Passwords now need at least 12 characters. If yours is shorter, Ledgerly will ask you to choose a new one the next time you log in after 2026-11-20. You can change it earlier in your account settings if you'd rather not do it mid-invoice.

## What's new

- **Sign in with Microsoft.** You can now log in with your Microsoft account, so there's one less password to remember.

## What's fixed

- **No more lost invoice drafts.** Your session used to expire without warning and take your unsaved invoice with it. That won't happen anymore.
- **Settings page in German.** The text that was missing on the Settings page is now translated.

## Behind the scenes

- Your password is now stored with stronger protection. You don't need to do anything.
