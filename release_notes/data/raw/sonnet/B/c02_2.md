## Two-factor authentication is here

You can now protect your account with a second step at login. Turn it on under **Settings → Security** and use any authenticator app.

## What's new

- **CSV export now includes the "Paid on" column**, so you can see when each invoice was paid without cross-checking.

## What's fixed

- **Edits no longer get lost when you have two tabs open.** Autosave could overwrite your changes; it doesn't anymore.
- **Spanish invoices now say "Factura" in the footer** instead of "Fatura".
