## Multi-currency invoicing

- **Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** The conversion now shows on the PDF, so your client sees exactly what happened.

## Archive clients

- **Archive instead of delete.** Archived clients disappear from your lists, so they're out of the way without losing anything.

## Fixes

- CSV import now works with files that use semicolons as separators, which is common in Spain and Germany.
- The email preview no longer shows a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder; it shows the client's name.
