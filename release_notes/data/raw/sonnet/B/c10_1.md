## Send invoices to public-sector clients over Peppol

You can now send e-invoices over the Peppol network, so invoicing government and other public-sector clients no longer means a separate portal or a PDF they have to re-key.

## Fixes

- Credit notes now come off your client's outstanding balance, so what you see owed is what's actually owed.
- "Mark as paid" works again on iPhone and iPad (Safari).
