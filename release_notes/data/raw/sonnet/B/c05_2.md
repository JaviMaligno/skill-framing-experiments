## What's new in Ledgerly

### Pay by SEPA Direct Debit
You can now get paid by SEPA Direct Debit. Turn it on in **Settings → Payments**; payments are handled through GoCardless.

### Fixes
- Clicking **Send** on an invoice no longer crashes when the client has no email address.
- The date picker now starts the week on Monday for UK users.

### Small improvements
- Converted totals use fresher exchange rates, updated every hour instead of once a day.
