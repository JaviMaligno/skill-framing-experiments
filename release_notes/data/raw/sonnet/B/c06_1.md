**Heads up for team accounts:** Single sign-on is now available in beta. Nothing changes unless you turn it on.

## What's new

- **Sign in with Google Workspace (beta).** Team accounts can now use single sign-on (SSO), so everyone logs in with their Google Workspace account.

## Fixed

- **Irish VAT numbers now work.** Valid numbers like IE1234567FA were being rejected. They're accepted now.
- **Dashboard revenue chart is right after midnight.** It was showing last month's revenue as the current month for a few hours after midnight UTC. It now shows the correct month.

## Also

- Public invoice links now have a limit on how often they can be opened in quick succession. That helps keep your invoices from being hammered by bots. Normal use isn't affected.
