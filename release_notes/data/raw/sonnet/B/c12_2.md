## Heads up: archive instead of delete

Nothing you need to do, but note that clients now get **archived** rather than deleted. Archived clients disappear from your client lists.

## What's new

- **Get paid in a different currency.** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another. The conversion now shows on the PDF, so your client sees exactly what happened.
- **Archive clients.** Done with a client? Archive them instead of deleting. They're hidden from your lists, and you don't lose anything.

## What's fixed

- CSV imports now work with semicolon-separated files, which are common in Spain and Germany.
- The email preview no longer shows a raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder. It shows the client's actual name.
