**Heads up for team accounts:** nothing you need to do, but SSO is new and in beta, so tell us if anything feels off.

## What's new

- **Sign in with Google Workspace.** Team accounts can now use single sign-on (beta), so everyone logs in with their work Google account.

## Fixed

- **Irish VAT numbers now go through.** Valid numbers like IE1234567FA were being rejected. They're accepted now.
- **Dashboard revenue chart shows the right month.** Just after midnight UTC, it was showing last month's revenue as the current month. No more.

## Also

- Public invoice links are now protected against too many rapid requests, so your invoices are harder to abuse.
