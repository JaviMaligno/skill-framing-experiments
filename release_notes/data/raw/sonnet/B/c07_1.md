**Action needed by 30 November 2026: reconnect your bank**

We've switched bank feed providers, so you'll need to reconnect your bank in **Settings → Bank feeds** before 30 November 2026. After that date, your bank feed will stop updating.

## What's new

- **Your IBAN now appears in the footer of your invoice PDFs**, so clients can see where to pay without asking.

## What's fixed

- **No more double entries after reconnecting your bank.** Matched payments were showing up twice in your Transactions list. They now appear once.
