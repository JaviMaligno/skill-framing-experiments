## Turn on two-factor authentication

You can now protect your account with a second step at login. Go to **Settings → Security** and set up two-factor authentication with an authenticator app.

## What's new

- **Two-factor authentication:** add an extra layer of security to your login with an authenticator app.
- **"Paid on" in CSV export:** your exported CSV now includes a **Paid on** column, so you can see when each invoice was paid.

## What's fixed

- **No more lost edits:** if you had Ledgerly open in two tabs, autosave could overwrite your changes. That's sorted.
- Fixed a typo in the Spanish invoice footer ("Fatura" is now "Factura").
