## What's new this week

### Invoice QR code for faster payments
Your invoices can now carry a payment QR code (EPC QR, also known as Girocode). Your clients scan it with their banking app, and the amount and your details are filled in for them.

### Zapier: new "Invoice overdue" trigger
You can now start a Zap the moment an invoice goes overdue. Use it to send a reminder, ping your team, or add a task to your to-do list.

### Fixes
- Large invoices (100+ lines) no longer time out when you create the PDF.
- "Duplicate invoice" now gives the copy a fresh due date instead of the old one.
