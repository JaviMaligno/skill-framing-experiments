## Heads up: SEPA Direct Debit is here

You can now collect payments by SEPA Direct Debit. To turn it on, go to **Settings → Payments** and connect GoCardless.

## What's new

- **SEPA Direct Debit:** Clients in the SEPA area can pay you straight from their bank account. Set it up under Settings → Payments.
- **Fresher converted totals:** Exchange rates now refresh every hour instead of every day, so totals in another currency stay closer to the real rate.

## Fixed

- The Send button no longer crashes when a client doesn't have an email address.
- The date picker now starts the week on Monday for UK users.
