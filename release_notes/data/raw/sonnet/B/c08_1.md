**Release notes**

## New

- **Payment QR code on invoices.** Your invoices can now show an EPC QR code (Girocode). Your client scans it with their banking app and the payment details fill in by themselves.
- **"Invoice overdue" trigger in Zapier.** You can now start a Zap when an invoice goes overdue, for example to send yourself a reminder or message the client.

## Fixed

- Large invoices (100+ lines) no longer time out when you generate the PDF.
- "Duplicate invoice" now gives the copy a fresh due date instead of the old one.
