# Ledgerly Release Notes

## Action required: reconnect your bank by 30 November 2026

We're changing our bank feed provider from Plaid to TrueLayer. To keep your transactions syncing, reconnect your bank in **Settings → Bank feeds** before **30 November 2026**.

## Improvements

- **IBAN on invoices:** Your IBAN now appears in the footer of invoice PDFs.
- **Payment matching:** We've refactored the logic that matches payments to transactions, which makes it easier for us to maintain and improve.

## Fixes

- Fixed an issue where matched payments appeared twice in the Transactions list after reconnecting a bank.

## Under the hood

- Upgraded to Node 22.11.
- Added Datadog dashboards to monitor queue depth.
