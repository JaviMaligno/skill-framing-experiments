# Ledgerly Release Notes

## New
- **Two-factor authentication (TOTP):** Protect your account with an authenticator app. Turn it on under **Settings → Security**.
- **Paid on in CSV export:** Exports now include the **Paid on** column.

## Fixed
- **Autosave with multiple tabs:** Fixed a race condition that could lose edits when Ledgerly was open in two tabs at once.
- **Spanish invoices:** Corrected a typo in the invoice footer ("Fatura" → "Factura").

## Under the hood
- Moved our sessions storage to Postgres 16.
- Increased unit test coverage of the tax module to 85%.
