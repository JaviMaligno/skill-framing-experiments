# Ledgerly Release Notes

## New

**Single sign-on with Google Workspace (beta)**
Team accounts can now sign in with Google Workspace SSO. This feature is in beta, so we'd love your feedback.

## Improvements

**Rate limiting on public invoice links**
Public invoice links are now rate limited to protect them from abuse and keep them reliably available for your clients. (LED-91)

## Fixes

- **VAT validation:** Valid Irish VAT numbers (for example, `IE1234567FA`) were wrongly rejected. They're now accepted.
- **Dashboard revenue chart:** Just after midnight UTC, the chart showed last month's revenue as the current month. It now rolls over correctly.

## Under the hood

- Upgraded the app to React 19.
- Fixed a flaky deploy pipeline on staging.
