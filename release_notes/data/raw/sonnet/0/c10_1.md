# Ledgerly Release Notes

## New
- **E-invoicing via Peppol:** You can now send invoices over the Peppol network, making it easy to invoice public-sector clients.

## Fixed
- Credit notes now correctly subtract from a client's outstanding balance.
- The "Mark as paid" button no longer appears disabled on mobile Safari.

## Under the hood
- We rewrote our tax engine in Rust. Tax calculations behave exactly as before.
- Added structured logging to the invoice sender, and updated our infrastructure modules.
