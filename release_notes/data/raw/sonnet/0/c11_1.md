# Ledgerly Release Notes

## Security

- **Stronger password requirements:** Passwords must now be at least 12 characters long. If your current password is shorter, you'll be asked to change it at your next login after **November 20, 2026**.
- **Safer password storage:** We've upgraded how passwords are stored, moving from bcrypt to argon2id, a more modern and more secure hashing method. No action is needed on your part.

## New

- **Sign in with Microsoft:** You can now log in to Ledgerly using your Microsoft account.

## Fixes

- **Invoice drafts no longer lost:** Fixed an issue where your session could expire silently and cause unsaved invoice drafts to be lost.
- **German Settings page:** Fixed a missing German translation on the Settings page.

## Under the hood

- Updated TypeScript to version 5.7.
