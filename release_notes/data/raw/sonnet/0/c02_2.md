# Ledgerly Release Notes

## New
- **Two-factor authentication (TOTP):** Protect your account with an authenticator app. Turn it on under **Settings → Security**.
- **Paid on in CSV export:** CSV exports now include the *Paid on* column.

## Fixed
- **Lost edits with multiple tabs:** We fixed a race condition in autosave that could lose edits when Ledgerly was open in two tabs at once.
- **Spanish invoice footer:** We corrected a typo in the footer ("Fatura" → "Factura").

## Under the hood
- Sessions now run on Postgres 16.
- We raised unit test coverage of the tax module to 85%.
