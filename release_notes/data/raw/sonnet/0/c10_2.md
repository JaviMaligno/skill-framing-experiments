# Ledgerly Release Notes

## New

**E-invoicing via Peppol**
You can now send invoices over the Peppol network to public-sector clients, so they arrive directly in the recipient's e-invoicing system.

## Fixed

- **Credit notes now reduce outstanding balances.** Credit notes weren't being subtracted from a client's outstanding balance. They are now.
- **"Mark as paid" works on mobile Safari.** The button was disabled on mobile Safari and now responds as expected.

## Under the hood

- The tax engine has been rewritten in Rust. Tax calculations behave exactly as before.
- Added structured logging to the invoice sender, which makes delivery issues easier to diagnose.
- Updated our Terraform modules.
