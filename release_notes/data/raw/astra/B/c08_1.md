## What’s new in Ledgerly

- **Automate overdue-invoice follow-ups:** Use the new “Invoice overdue” trigger in Zapier.
- **Let clients pay by scanning:** Add a payment QR code (EPC QR / Girocode) to your invoices.
- **Download large invoices without timeouts:** Fixed PDF generation for invoices with 100+ lines.
- **Duplicate invoices without the old due date:** The “Duplicate invoice” button no longer copies it over.
