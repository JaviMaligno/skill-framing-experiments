## What’s new in Ledgerly

- **Invoice in one currency, get paid in another.** The conversion is shown on the invoice PDF.
- **Archive clients instead of deleting them.** Keep their details without cluttering your client lists.
- **Import CSV files with semicolons.** Files using this format, common in Spain and Germany, now import without failing.
- **Email previews show your client’s name**, not a stray `{{client_name}}` placeholder.
