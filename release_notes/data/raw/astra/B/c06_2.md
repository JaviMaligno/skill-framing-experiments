## What’s new in Ledgerly

- **Google Workspace sign-in (beta):** Team accounts can now use single sign-on (SSO) with Google Workspace.
- **Irish VAT numbers accepted:** Valid numbers in the IE1234567FA format are no longer rejected.
- **Revenue charts show the right month:** Fixed a timezone issue that showed last month’s revenue as current after midnight UTC.
- **Invoice link protection:** Public invoice links now limit excessive requests to help prevent abuse.
