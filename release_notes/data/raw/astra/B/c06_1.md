## What’s new in Ledgerly

- **Google Workspace sign-in (beta):** Team accounts can now use single sign-on (SSO) with Google Workspace.
- **Irish VAT numbers accepted:** Valid numbers in the IE1234567FA format are no longer rejected.
- **Correct revenue after midnight:** The dashboard chart no longer shows last month’s revenue as this month’s after midnight UTC.
- **Extra protection for invoice links:** Public invoice links now limit repeated requests to help prevent abuse.
