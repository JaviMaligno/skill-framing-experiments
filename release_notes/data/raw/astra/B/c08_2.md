## What’s new in Ledgerly

- **Automate overdue follow-ups:** Use the new “Invoice overdue” trigger in Zapier.
- **Let clients scan to pay:** Add an EPC QR / Girocode payment QR code to your invoices.
- **Download large invoices reliably:** Fixed PDF generation timeouts for invoices with 100+ lines.
- **No more old due dates:** “Duplicate invoice” no longer copies the original invoice’s due date.
