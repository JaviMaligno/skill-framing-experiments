# Ledgerly release notes

## Action required: Reconnect your bank by November 30, 2026

We’re switching our bank feed provider from Plaid to TrueLayer. To continue receiving bank feeds, reconnect your bank in **Settings → Bank feeds** by **November 30, 2026**.

## What’s new
- Invoice PDFs now display the IBAN in the footer.

## Fixes
- Fixed an issue where matched payments appeared twice in the Transactions list after reconnecting a bank.

## Behind the scenes
- Refactored payment-matching heuristics.
- Updated Node.js to 22.11.
- Added Datadog dashboards to monitor queue depth.
