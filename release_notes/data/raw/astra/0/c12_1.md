## What’s new

- **Multi-currency invoices:** Invoice in one currency and get paid in another, with the conversion shown on the invoice PDF.
- **Archive clients:** Archive clients instead of deleting them. Archived clients are hidden from lists.

## Fixes

- CSV imports now work with semicolon-delimited files, commonly used in Spain and Germany.
- Email previews now show the client’s name instead of the raw `{{client_name}}` placeholder.

## Under the hood

- Batched GraphQL client resolution with DataLoader.
- Removed 40 KB of unused CSS.
