## Ledgerly release notes

### New
- **Sign in with Microsoft:** Use your Microsoft account to sign in to Ledgerly.
- **Stronger password requirements:** Passwords must now contain at least 12 characters. Users with shorter passwords will need to change them at their next login after November 20, 2026.

### Fixed
- Fixed an issue where sessions expired silently, causing unsaved invoice drafts to be lost.
- Restored missing German translations on the Settings page.

### Under the hood
- Switched password hashing from bcrypt to Argon2id.
- Updated TypeScript to version 5.7.
